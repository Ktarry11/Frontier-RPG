## What happened here

Deadwind was already a bad place: Karazhan on its crag, the leylines bunched under it, Morgan's Plot full of the
restless dead, ogres in the ravine. When the worlds merged, the leylines under the pass **tore**. Two things came out of
the wound and haven't stopped since.

- **Time broke.** Near the rupture, moments stick. A rockslide hangs in the air halfway down the gorge. A crow is stuck
  mid-wingbeat in a shard of stopped air. A tree aged a thousand years in a second and stands next to the sapling it
  was. Some things replay: a wagon crashes on the road below the Plot every few minutes, and the driver never learns.
- **The fallout.** Something leaked out with it: a sick, greenish-yellow glow that seeps from the ground, gets into
  water, settles as grey ash and crusts into glowing slag. Living things near it **mutate**. They grow tumours, extra
  limbs, eyes where eyes shouldn't be, sores that glow from inside. The worst of them have fused with **their own past
  and future selves**: a wolf with three heads that are the same wolf at three ages, an ogre wearing the body of the
  child it was.

The transplants have a word for this. Some of them have seen it before.

## The wheel

At the heart of the pass, over the rupture's mouth, stands **a rusted Ferris wheel with yellow gondolas**. It's the
only thing from Earth that has ever come through as an object, here or anywhere (Kyle: "JUST the wheel"). It came from
a city on Earth that was already a fallout zone, already empty, already wrong. The two wounds matched, and the wheel
fell through the gap. The transplants who recognise it don't like to talk about it.

It marks the dungeon's door. Nothing else from Earth is in Deadwind: no tower blocks, no cars, no signs.

**Getting there** (Kyle: "a tunnel through STV mountain that opened up"): the Integration cracked open a tunnel through
the mountains on Stranglethorn's north-east side, and Deadwind is at the other end. The tunnel itself is a short walk
with the fallout getting into the rock as you go, so the first sign of Deadwind is a glow in the wrong place.

**Karazhan** stays sealed: the tower on its crag, wrapped in stopped time, visible from most of the pass. Not a dungeon
in this pass.

## The people

**The liquidators** (Kyle picked this): Earth transplants who have seen a fallout zone before, or grew up next to one,
and volunteered to hold this one. They work with **Forsaken apothecaries**, because the rot barely touches the dead.
- The camp is Azerothian through and through: Forsaken iron and stone, beaked plague masks, apothecary tanks, wash
  troughs at the gate where everyone scrubs down before coming in, quarantine fences hung with bone wards, a pyre that
  never goes out (for the mutated dead), a sealed infirmary, sample racks, a board of names. There's an inn (every
  town has one) and a flight master on the camp's far side.
- Earth references run heavy here (it's a 55-60 zone, and they're the reason the liquidators came). The word
  "liquidator" is theirs.
- Horde-side: the Forsaken run the camp's supply line; the liquidators answer to nobody in particular, which is why
  they're tolerated.

## What lives there

- **The mutated wild**: Deadwind's wolves, crows, boars and bats, grown wrong.
- **The Deadwind ogres**: the ones who didn't die, swollen and fused, still in their compound.
- **The widows**: Deadwind's spiders, bloated, egg sacs glowing.
- **The stuck**: things caught in broken time, echoes that replay their last moment, things aged to bone in an instant,
  things with too many of themselves.
- **Karazhan's spill**: spectral servants and animated things that wandered out of the tower when it sealed.
- **The fallout horrors**: what the glow makes when it has enough to work with. Masses of fused animals, crawling
  slag-flesh, bloated walkers that burst.

## Tone (Kyle: "dark and disgusting")

Grotesque, not gory: tumours, extra limbs, fused bodies, sores, glowing pustules, exposed ribs, wrongness. Kyle picked
this so the image generator will actually draw it. Dark: dead grey-brown forest, black rock, sickly green-yellow glow,
bruise purple, rust. The only warm colour in the pass is the outpost's pyre, and the wheel's yellow paint.
