## The overlay: what landed, and why the two halves differ

**Stonetalon Peak is a volcano now.** The Cascades came down on Stonetalon the way the Rockies came down on Thousand Needles. The peak at the top of the range is a Mount St. Helens-style cone, dormant but not dead. When it landed, its lateral blast went **east**, the way St. Helens' blast went north.

**East: the blowdown.** The blast laid the eastern forest flat: grey trunks in rows, all pointing away from the peak, under a skin of ash.
- The Venture Co. were already logging Stonetalon. The blast did their felling for them, and they've been salvaging the blown-down giants ever since.
- Now they're running out of fallen timber and pushing west into the standing giants. That's the zone's conflict, and it's why the west is next.
- The Charred Vale becomes the blast zone.

**West: the giants.** Behind the ridge, sheltered from the blast, the forest kept its giants:
- **Coastal redwoods in the low western valleys**, damp, fern-floored and full of fog.
- **Giant sequoias up the slopes.**
- **The volcano's summit and crater lake** at the very top.

The Earth trees and Stonetalon's own great trees grow side by side, which shouldn't work.

**Earth references scale with level.** At 26-32 they're light (near the 30% curve's low end):
- The **landscape** is the Earth reference: transplants recognise the redwoods and the mountain.
- **Nothing from Earth appears as an object.** No roads, no signs, no ranger stations (standing rule).

### Stonetalon East (26-29): the blowdown

- **Sun Rock Retreat** (the town): a tauren hold on its rock, taking in refugees since Thunder Bluff fell. It has an inn, a flight master, shops, trainers in their themed pairs, and envoys from Orgrimmar, the Steamwheedle Cartel and the Forsaken. About 70% of the zone's quests are given here.
- **The Blowdown:** the blast zone. Grey fallen giants in rows, ash, snags and fireweed, with the Venture Co.'s salvage crews working it.
- **Windshear Crag:** the harpies' crags in the north-east, by the way to Ashenvale.
- **The Talondeep Path:** the tunnel north to Ashenvale.
- **The road east:** out to the Barrens.
- **The Grimtotem Compound:** the hardline clan's camp in the south, pressing on a weakened Sun Rock with Thunder Bluff gone.
- **The Mulgore Rim:** the south-east edge. The dark starts here. Closed border.
- **Cave, working name Windshear Mine:** the Venture Co.'s dig under the crags, around 27-30.

### Stonetalon West (29-32): the giants

- **The Venture Cutline:** where the goblins are cutting into the standing forest, the seam between the two zones.
- **Greatwood Vale:** coastal redwoods in the fog, ferns and creeks. Bears, elk and cougars.
- **The Sequoia Slopes:** the giant sequoias, and the greatest of them, the dungeon.
- **Stonetalon Peak:** the volcano at the top, with its crater lake and smoking rim, and the elementals it woke.
- **The west camp:** a small quest camp (quest givers only, no inn or flight master) for the far side.
- **Cave, working name the Ashvent:** a lava tube in the volcano's flank, around 30-32. Elementals.
- **The way toward Desolace:** cut but closed, until Desolace exists.

## The dungeon: inside a standing sequoia (30-33)

**It climbs UP.** The World-Root Hollow (a Frontier zone) already goes down under a giant tree's roots, so this one is its opposite.
- **Map 1, the trunk:** the hollowed inner chambers and growth rings, spiralling up.
- **Map 2, the crown:** out onto the branches and the canopy, ending in the open sky.
- **Who's in it (draft, on art sheet 4):** the Venture Co., who want this tree most of all, climbing it on ropes; bark beetles boring the heartwood; living sap; and up top, what nests in the crown.
- **Bosses (draft names, Kyle to change):** up to six, with 3-4 pulls of 4-5 mobs between them, and none of them flimsy.
  - **Map 1, the trunk:** Rigboss Gazzle (goblin foreman on a climbing platform), the Heartrot Queen (bark beetle queen) and Ambermaw (a sap elemental with a goblin trapped in it).
  - **Map 2, the crown:** Old Talonwing (a storm-roc nesting in the crown) and Topper Kezzik (the goblins' climbing shredder, come to top the tree).
  - **The Eldest:** the tree's own spirit, an optional last fight or the end.
- **Loot:** every boss gets a hand-made pool. It's checked against **all 97 specs of the 30 classes**, not the classic 9 (Kyle's standing rule, 15 Nov).
- **Its quests are given in Sun Rock**, not outside the door.

## People and factions

- **Who pays what:**
  - **Thunder Bluff:** the Sun Rock tauren and the main chains.
  - **Orgrimmar:** the Horde's interest, the Grimtotem and the road.
  - **Steamwheedle Cartel:** the Venture Co. are the Cartel's rogue rivals.
  - **Forsaken (one or two quests):** the dead tauren getting up again on the Mulgore Rim are exactly the sort of thing the Apothecarium wants a sample of.
- **Quest count:** about 30 per zone, with quest givers spread across many NPCs (not a few loaded ones). Every town NPC has a purpose.
- **Quest rules:**
  - Varied: deliver, craft, monster-drop starters, and a multi-zone gated chain.
  - No escorts.
  - Writing to the Ashenvale/Durotar bar, in plain speech with no AI tics.
- **Recruits:** none planned; Stonetalon can host one later. The recruit whose backstory mentions "the high country above Stonetalon" fits here when recruits are placed.

  - Its theme, look, location and way in are never written in a doc, commit or reply.
  - It won't look or play like either of the first two.
  - Its bosses pay "????".
- **Small secrets in both open maps.**
- **Built 15 Nov:** the skull zone (2 bosses, item level + 5) and the Frontier zone (3 bosses, item level + 8, every boss with an epic, trash epics at 1%), 7 quests between them (one elite each), 2 secret doors, 22 new secrets across the Stonetalon maps. None of the old secrets anywhere else moved.
- **"????"** now has one cryptic line on its journal page (Kyle: "Give a cryptic ass description too"). Its standing still comes only from Frontier zone bosses, never skull zones (tools/qa_unnamed_faction.py).

## Art

**What exists and gets reused:**
- **Aspen materials, made for the blowdown:** ash patch, char ground, deadfall, log litter, fireweed, fern floor, duff, granite, scree, talus.
- **Aspen props:** charred snags, burned stumps, snapped trunks, deadfall piles, boulders, pines.
- **Tauren buildings, for Sun Rock:** the full tau_ set, Camp Taurajo's and Freewind Post's.
- **Lava tiles**, for the Ashvent.
- **Creatures:** bears (six), fire elementals (four), earth elementals (two), harpies with a matriarch, goblin overseer and sappers, panthers (for recolour), a stag, a tauren brave.

**What's missing** (prompts in `docs/stonetalon_art_prompts.md`):
- **Giant tree props, the zone's centrepiece:** redwoods and sequoias at several sizes, with trunk bases you walk between and crowns that canopy over paths.
- **Tiles:**
  - redwood forest floor in fog (dark duff, sorrel, ferns);
  - the blowdown's grey ash plain;
  - pumice and volcanic rock;
  - crater lake water;
  - the Mulgore Rim's dark.
- **Props:**
  - blown-down giant trunks in rows;
  - Venture Co. logging (log decks, saw rigs, a shredder wreck, skid trails, a camp);
  - Grimtotem totems and tents;
  - harpy nests;
  - fumaroles and steam vents.
- **Creatures:**
  - Grimtotem tauren (warrior, shaman, hunter, an elder);
  - Venture Co. (logger, sawyer, shredder);
  - Earth beasts (cougar, elk, eagle, black bear cub);
  - elementals (ash, pumice);
  - the risen dead tauren of the Rim (seen from a distance);
  - bosses.
- **The sequoia dungeon interior:**
  - heartwood floors, growth-ring walls, sap, a spiral way up;
  - then branch walkways, the canopy and the sky.
