## The twist: Zin-Azshari is rising

The Integration shook the sea floor, and the old Highborne capital is coming up out of the water in pieces. Drowned
spires stand offshore, and the naga are coming up with them. Highborne ghosts walk the ruins, and they think it's ten
thousand years ago and you're trespassing.

It rises as you quest. Early on, only spires show offshore. A late chain on the East coast brings a drowned district up
onto the shore, and that becomes new ground with its own quests, rares and the vault's door. It's a save-flag swap,
the same way the Plaguelands towns work.

## The two hubs

**Talrendis Landing**: the arcane salvage camp, orcs and blood elves together.
- Orgrimmar provides the muscle and the blood elves provide the magisters. Together they're stripping Highborne magic
  out of the ruins, and neither side trusts the other.
- It has an inn and Azshara's one flight master.

**Timbermaw Hold**: the furbolgs' home, a caldera city.
- It's a huge hollow in the mountain, open to the sky, with lodges, totems and terraces climbing the inside walls and
  tunnels leading off it.
- It's larger than any town so far.
- It has an inn but no flight point.
- The Felwood Timbermaw Tunnel gets a long east passage that leads here, so the Timbermaw road through the mountains
  is real. The Winterspring and Moonglade ends stay shut.

## Reputation

- **Orgrimmar**: Talrendis Landing's orc-side quests.
- **The Sunreach Conclave** (new): the blood elf magisters reclaiming what their ancestors lost. It gets its own
  TBC-style shelf.
  - **Its home is open** (Kyle, 17 Nov: "This means we need a home for the sunreach eventually", then "leave it
    for now. Don't tie leader or anything to a quest so they can be moved", and "The elves can get an island later").
    For now its leader and quartermaster stand in Talrendis Landing as the Conclave's field camp.
    - Neither of them gives or takes a quest, so both can move to the Conclave's island when it's built.
    - The Conclave's quests come from other magisters in the Landing.
- **Timbermaw Hold** (exists since 17 Nov): the Hold's quests and the fel rot dungeon carry you past Honored.
  - Its quartermaster moves up from the tunnel to the Hold, or a second one stands there.
  - The tunnel's people stay in the tunnel.

## Caves and dungeons

- **The satyr den** (cave): a warren in the cursed forest, with fel fires, Highborne slaves bound to it and a satyr
  lord.
- **The naga grotto** (cave): the sea cave where the naga come up from Zin-Azshari, with tide pools, coral and drowned
  Highborne statues.
- **The fel rot under the Hold** (dungeon, 55-58, 5 bosses): whatever turned the Deadwood came up from below the Hold
  itself, so it's a satyr-and-demon dungeon under the furbolgs' feet.
  - Its door is a sealed lower gate inside the caldera, guarded by the Timbermaw.
  - Every boss pays Timbermaw rep.
- **The Vault of Zin-Azshari** (dungeon, 59-60, 6 bosses over two floors): the flooded halls, then Azshara's own
  vault. It's Scholomance-sized.
  - Its door appears when the drowned district rises at the end of the East coast chain.

Names for the two dungeons and their boss rosters are still to settle.

## Enemies

- Naga, coming up with the city.
- Highborne ghosts.
- Satyrs and demons: the Legion's leftovers and the fel rot.
- The blue dragonflight. The blues guard the leylines the salvage camp is tapping. They're not evil, just in the way.

## Story and people

- **About 90 quests** across the three maps and the Hold, roughly 30 a map.
  - About 70% are given in Talrendis Landing and the Hold.
  - Chains run out to Orgrimmar, Ashenvale, Felwood and other continents.
- **Earth shows up a lot** (it's 50s content): transplants among the salvage crews and as volunteers in the Hold, with
  Earth references common in the writing, like Felwood. There are no Earth objects in the world.
- **Two or three recruits**: a blood elf magister, an orc salvager and a transplant, in the Landing and the Hold.
- **Hidden content**: Kyle said "Surprise me". Where it is will never be written anywhere he reads, by the same rule as
  every other secret.
