## The World Tree, 42 to 51 (home-brew)

Kyle: "a new home brew off the coast of Northern Stranglethorn. Where the island is 42-44, then a massive tree ascends
up with actual layers that span from 44-48 (each tier has people living on it (the world tree)) - it started to grow
when the integration happened and it hasn't stopped. At the top of the tree is two zones that are level 46-49 and
48-51 (an entire aerial sky zone). The tree has one town in the middle of it and a new faction where the top of the
tree is basically sunlight, clouds, etc....and another new faction."

| Part | Levels | What |
|---|---|---|

| The tree's tiers | 44-48 | real layers going up, each tier lived on |

- It began to grow at the Integration and hasn't stopped.
- Two new factions: the town's, and the crown's.

**Kyle, 19 Nov 2026 (evening), names and the crown:** "The Middle Town — The Great Settlement" — "so the top will be
