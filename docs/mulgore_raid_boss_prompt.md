# Mulgore: the Thunder Bluff raid, boss sheet (test prompt)

Kyle, 18 Nov 2026: "I want you to test one for mulgore bro....give me a prompt for RAID BOSSES ---- for our Thunder
Bluff Raid.... 14 raid bosses....". The vision he asked to keep (15 Nov): Mulgore is "a version of hell... the black
void of permanent nighttime and nightmare... demons, like actual torture etc... taurens get mutilated... then they
stand up after they're killed and keep hunting... think inky black hell + zombies + torture + Gore.... basically
hellraiser", "like the eclipse in Berserk". Thunder Bluff fell in the Integration and Cairne has been missing since.

ChatGPT's own stated ceiling (Kyle relayed it, 18 Nov): very far on corpse fields, demonic transformations, distorted
bodies, flesh fused with architecture, skulls and bones, and body horror; blood-soaked "within limits"; sacrifice
"non-graphic"; no graphic dismemberment or exposed organs. The prompt is written to sit right up against that line.

**Designs are original.** Nothing here copies a known character (no nail-grid heads, no puzzle boxes, no Godhand), so
the sheet is ours: the Hellraiser and Eclipse feel comes from hooks, chains, ritual, a black sun and the wrongness, not
from borrowed looks. Boss names are working names. Whether the last boss is Cairne is Kyle's call, so the prompt leaves
the final boss's face hidden.

16 cells: the 14 bosses, then the last boss's second form (cell 15) and the raid's elite trash (cell 16).

**Style references to attach:** `pl_boss_abom_butcher.png`, `fw_boss_demon_lord.png`, `st_risen_tauren.png`.

