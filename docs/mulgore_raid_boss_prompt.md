# Mulgore: the Thunder Bluff raid, boss sheets (3 prompts)

Kyle, 18 Nov 2026: "give me a prompt for RAID BOSSES ---- for our Thunder Bluff Raid.... 14 raid bosses", then "we
want GORE for this....mulgore needs to be beyond fucked and these bosses are all in thunderbluff and they need to be
SICK - it's our first raid", "we need a memorable final boss", and "Chatgpt is fine - worst it can say is no. Go hard to
start and we can tone down IF needed!"

**The raid** (Kyle's shape): each rise has bosses. **Hunter Rise 3, Spirit Rise 3, Elder Rise 3, Central 5**, and the
final boss only unlocks in Central, as its 5th boss, after the other 13 are dead.

**The final boss is Cairne Bloodhoof, unmade** (Kyle picked it). He never escaped when Thunder Bluff fell. The demons
kept him alive for three years in the ruins of his own city and made him the eclipse's vessel. The fight is phases, and
he gets grosser each one: "Cairne starts to mutate and split disgustingly".

**The vision** (Kyle, 15 Nov): Mulgore is "a version of hell... the black void of permanent nighttime and nightmare...
demons, like actual torture... taurens get mutilated... then they stand up after they're killed and keep hunting...
think inky black hell + zombies + torture + Gore.... basically hellraiser", "like the eclipse in Berserk".

**Three sheets** (Kyle: "7+6 + Final boss"):

| Sheet | What | Image | Grid (cell) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Central's first four, Hunter Rise's three, and the raid's elite trash | 2048x1024 | 4 x 2 (512 x 512) |
| B | Spirit Rise's three and Elder Rise's three | 2048x1365 | 3 x 2 (about 682 x 682) |
| C | Cairne, unmade, in four phases, each grosser than the last | 2048x2048 | 2 x 2 (1024 x 1024) |

**Going hard, as Kyle asked.** ChatGPT's own stated line (Kyle relayed it): very far on corpse fields, demonic
transformation, distorted bodies, flesh fused into architecture, bones, body horror; blood-soaked "within limits";
sacrifice "non-graphic"; no graphic dismemberment or exposed organs. These prompts sit on that line: flayed hide,
exposed muscle and bone, hooks through flesh, blood everywhere, no organs spilled. If one refuses, take out the word it
balks at and re-roll.

**Designs are original** (no nail-grid heads, no puzzle boxes, no Godhand): the Hellraiser and Eclipse feel comes from
hooks, chains, ritual flaying, a black sun and the wrongness, not from borrowed looks. Boss names are working names.

**Style references to attach (at most 3):** `pl_boss_abom_butcher.png`, `fw_boss_demon_lord.png`,
