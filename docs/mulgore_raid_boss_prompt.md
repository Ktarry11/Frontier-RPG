# Mulgore: the Thunder Bluff raid, boss sheets (3 prompts)

Kyle, 18 Nov 2026: "give me a prompt for RAID BOSSES ---- for our Thunder Bluff Raid.... 14 raid bosses", then "we
want GORE for this....mulgore needs to be beyond fucked and these bosses are all in thunderbluff and they need to be
SICK - it's our first raid", "we need a memorable final boss", and "Chatgpt is fine - worst it can say is no. Go hard to
start and we can tone down IF needed!"

**The raid** (Kyle's shape): each rise has bosses. **Hunter Rise 3, Spirit Rise 3, Elder Rise 3, Central 5**, and the
final boss only unlocks in Central, as its 5th boss, after the other 13 are dead.

**The final boss is Cairne Bloodhoof, unmade** (Kyle picked it). He never escaped when Thunder Bluff fell. The demons
kept him alive for three years in the ruins of his own city and made him the eclipse's vessel. The fight is phases, and
he gets grosser each one: "Cairne starts to mutate and split disgustingly".

**The vision** (Kyle, 15 Nov): Mulgore is "a version of hell... the black void of permanent nighttime and nightmare...
demons, like actual torture... taurens get mutilated... then they stand up after they're killed and keep hunting...
think inky black hell + zombies + torture + Gore.... basically hellraiser", "like the eclipse in Berserk".

**Three sheets** (Kyle: "7+6 + Final boss"):

| Sheet | What | Image | Grid (cell) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Central's first four, Hunter Rise's three, and the raid's elite trash | 2048x1024 | 4 x 2 (512 x 512) |
| B | Spirit Rise's three and Elder Rise's three | 2048x1365 | 3 x 2 (about 682 x 682) |
| C | Cairne, unmade, in four phases, each grosser than the last | 2048x2048 | 2 x 2 (1024 x 1024) |

**Written as art direction, not trigger words** (18 Nov 2026). The first version said "gore", "flayed" and "blood
pouring", and ChatGPT pulled back. Its own advice, which Kyle relayed: "describe the actual visual characteristics you
want... visibly deteriorated anatomy, exposed skeletal structures, asymmetrical mutations, necrotic tissue". So every
prompt now describes what's on the body (torn hide over bone and sinew, hooks and chains embedded, fused bodies, dark
bloodstains, black ichor) instead of naming a category. Same monsters, more specific, and it's honest: nothing here
disguises what it is. If one still refuses, cut the single phrase it balks at and re-roll.

**Designs are original** (no nail-grid heads, no puzzle boxes, no Godhand): the Hellraiser and Eclipse feel comes from
hooks, chains, ritual flaying, a black sun and the wrongness, not from borrowed looks. Boss names are working names.

**Style references to attach (at most 3):** `pl_boss_abom_butcher.png`, `fw_boss_demon_lord.png`,
`st_risen_tauren.png`.

## SHEET C: Cairne, unmade, in four phases (MAGENTA KEY)

```
Generate ONE sprite sheet image, 2048x2048, a 2 columns x 2 rows grid = 4 separate boss sprites, each cell 1024x1024. Flat pure #FF00FF magenta background everywhere. Each sprite is ONE single figure, the SAME BOSS in four phases, full body, centred in its own cell with a wide margin, not touching or overlapping its neighbours or the image edge, nothing cropped. No ground, no floor patch, no blood pool under him, no cast shadow, no glow halo bleeding onto the background (light stays on the figure), no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames, no borders. Never use magenta or pink anywhere on the figure (flesh is ashen, raw red, bruised purple and bone, never magenta). Nothing modern or from the real world.

Painterly semi-realistic WoW Classic style: strong readable silhouette, soft dark outline, chunky shapes legible at small size, lit from the top-left by a thin cold light as if from the white rim of an eclipsed sun.

Camera: every phase stands full body facing three-quarters toward the lower-right, the same angle in all four cells.

Setting: the final boss of THE FALL OF THUNDER BLUFF. CAIRNE BLOODHOOF, the great old chieftain of the tauren, was never lost when his city fell: the demons kept him alive for three years in the ruins of his own hall and made him the vessel of the eclipse, the black disc ringed with white fire that hangs over Mulgore. Now it is hatching out of him. Each phase is GROSSER than the last as Cairne mutates and splits apart. BODY HORROR at full strength: his anatomy distorting and splitting open along his ritual scars, extra limbs and heads growing out of him, torn hide hanging loose over exposed sinew and bone, iron hooks and chains embedded in him, dark bloodstains, black ichor, the eclipse's white fire burning through every crack in him. Each phase more grotesque than the last. It must stay recognisably CAIRNE in every phase: a huge old tauren, long white braided beard, great curved horns, the remains of his chieftain's regalia and his runed spear. Palette: inky black, raw red, dried brown-red, bruise purple, bone white, tarnished bronze and gold, cold eclipse white, the faded red and turquoise of his chieftain's cloth.

The figure GROWS each phase: cell 1 is a huge tauren; each cell after it is bigger, and cell 4 nearly fills its whole cell. Keep that growth visible across the sheet.

Phases are numbered row by row, left to right, top to bottom:

1 CAIRNE, CHAINED: the old chieftain still standing tall and proud, gaunt from three years of torment, iron hooks driven through his shoulders and back with broken chains hanging from them, his hide carved with ritual scars, his white beard stained dark, his runed spear in his hands, his eyes burning the cold white of the eclipse, a small black eclipse ringed with white fire floating just above his horns like a crown. Tragic and terrifying.

2 CAIRNE, SPLITTING OPEN: the mutation begins. His body is swelling and splitting open along every ritual scar, the hide peeling back over sinew and bone, a second pair of long clawed arms growing out of his sides, one horn cracked off and a twisted black horn growing in its place, black ichor running down his body, the black eclipse sinking into his skull so white fire leaks from his eyes and mouth, his spear arm still gripping the spear.

3 CAIRNE, TWO OF HIM: he has split down the middle into two halves joined by stretched strands of sinew, one half still Cairne's own weeping face, the other half a grinning demonic head growing out of the wound, extra arms on both, hide hanging loose in torn sheets, his ribs and spine showing through, chains still embedded in him, dark stains everywhere.

4 CAIRNE, UNMADE: a towering mass of fused tauren bodies, torn hide and bone, the heads of his own dead people grown out of it and screaming, countless arms reaching out, the black eclipse burning white in the middle of his chest, and at the centre of it all Cairne's own old face, still recognisable, still weeping. The largest and most horrifying figure on the sheet.
```
