## The shape

Classic Feralas is an L: a long strip of coast down the west, and an arm running east along the bottom to Thousand
Needles. Frontier's Rockies run east to west and bleed into it, so you come down out of the Aspen Wilderness into the
far end of the east arm, walk west along it to the corner, and then turn north up the coast.

| Map | Levels | Shape | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The High Wilderness** (the east arm) | 44-46 | wide, east to west | the way down from the aspens, Camp Mojache's door, Woodpaw Hills, the Lower Wilds, the Grimtotem Compound, the Gordunni Outpost, Dire Maul's gate, the Writhing Deep's hive mouth |
| **The Forgotten Coast** (the corner and the south coast) | 46-48 | the corner of the L | the Fog Groves (the grafted rainforest and its settlers), the Ruins of Isildien, the naga beaches, Feathermoon's landing, Sardor Isle and the Isle of Dread across the shallows, the Frayfeather Highlands |
| **The Twin Colossals** (the north coast) | 47-49 | tall, north to south | the Twin Colossals and their giants, Rage Scar Hold, the Ruins of Ravenwind, the Dream Bough |

The map names are classic Feralas subzone names, used for the three maps. Kyle can rename any of them.

**Towns, each its own map:** Camp Mojache (the High Wilderness) and Feathermoon Stronghold (an island off the Forgotten
Coast, reached by boat from the landing). Each has an inn and the flight path for its part of Feralas. The Twin
Colossals gets a small group of quest givers at the Sentinels' watch and no flight path.

**The Writhing Deep (47-49):** the silithid hive under the east arm's south edge. A cave, a cave boss, and at the back
the tunnel the silithids are still digging, which comes out in the Dry Deep under the Abyssal Sands. That gives the Dry
Deep a second way in, and Feralas a way through to Tanaris without a flight.

**Dire Maul:** its great gate stands in the middle of the east arm with Gordunni ogres camped around it. It leads
nowhere yet; the dungeon comes later at 55-60.

## The people

**Camp Mojache** is bigger than it was. Tauren who got out of Mulgore came south through the Needles, and the camp took
them in. It answers to the leader at Camp Taurajo, it hunts the Grimtotem who moved on the empty ground, and it watches
the Gordunni at Dire Maul. Its quests give Orgrimmar reputation.

**Feathermoon Stronghold** is the Sentinels' fortress on its island, led by **Shandris Feathermoon**. Transplants are
factionless, so the night elves let you land and work for them. The Sentinels are the new faction: TBC-style like every
other (friendly supplies, honored a neck piece and a pattern, revered six ilvl 67 blues, exalted three ilvl 73 epics),
a direction rather than a cage (they lean toward hunters, rangers and archers, and serve every role). Rep comes from
the coast's quests and, later, from Dire Maul.

**The transplant settlers** live in the Fog Groves, the part of the forest that came from Earth: a few cabins of people
who woke up among trees they recognised. Earth references rise with level, so the coast carries more of them than the
east arm, and they stay a note, never the zone's theme.

**Enemies:** the Gordunni ogres (Dire Maul's gate, the Gordunni Outpost, the Ruins of Isildien), the Woodpaw gnolls,
the Grimtotem, the naga of the Forgotten Coast and its isles, the giants walking the Twin Colossals, the silithids
spreading out of the Writhing Deep, and the wildlife: yetis at Rage Scar Hold, hippogryphs in the Frayfeather
Highlands, chimaeras on the Isle of Dread, sprite darters, wolves, stags, bears and the forest's great cats.

## The story threads (draft, for the quests)
- **The dig.** The silithids aren't spreading at random; they're digging somewhere. Mojache's hunters follow the
  mounds south to the Writhing Deep, and the cave's back end shows where to: through to the Dry Deep. Leaves Feralas
  for Tanaris.
- **The refugees.** Who came south from Mulgore, who didn't, and what the Grimtotem took while the tauren were gone.
  Ties back to Camp Taurajo's leader and the survivors' list from Moonglade.
- **The Fog Groves.** Trees that are the wrong kind, fog that rolls in off the wrong sea, and the few people living
  under them. The night elves noticed first.
- **The Sentinels and the coast.** The naga coming up out of the sea at Isildien and Ravenwind, and what they want with
  the old Highborne ruins.
- **The Dream Bough.** A great tree in the north whose roots reach into the Dream. Moonglade's watchers want to know
  whether what's waking there has reached Feralas. Leaves Feralas for Moonglade.
- **Dire Maul.** The Gordunni at the gate, and what they're keeping people out of. Sets up the dungeon without opening it.
