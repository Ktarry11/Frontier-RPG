# Deadwind Pass: the story and the quest plan (about 80 quests)

Kyle, 18 Nov 2026: "let's look at deadwind pass for our final T0 token dungeon! And we want beautifully written quests.
Lots of lore....they should be exciting to do." His picks the same morning: two maps for the pass, the dungeon is
**the Exclusion Zone**, the camp is **Ariden's Watch**, and the liquidators get **their own faction**. The vision is
`docs/deadwind_brief.md`.

**Built 18 Nov 2026** (`docs/deadwind_build_log.md`): 63 quests over every arc below. Where the build moved from the
plan: the Watch's people grew (Dr. Aigerim Seitkali, Lisbeth Vane, Luiz Ferreira, Rine Lajuon, Baba Hanna and
Deathstalker Vessa Morrow joined, so nobody gives more than four), the dungeon's quests are all given in the camp,
Gristlemaw's by Harrow on Grull's behalf, and the last egg is a real choice between Morwen and Dr. Seitkali.

Rules carried over from Azshara: every kill target spawns where the quest says, every fetch item drops, every explore
point is walkable, dungeon and cave quests are given in the camp, a chain is a chain, no giver gives more than four,
and the rewards give every spec something. Earth comes up in well over a quarter of the quests here. The liquidators
are the whole reason the camp exists, and they talk about home all the time.

---------------------------------------------------------------------

## The heart of it

**What happened.** When the worlds came together, the leylines bunched under Karazhan tore open, right under the old
road through the pass. Two things came out of the tear. Time broke: moments stick, replay or age to dust in a second.
And something leaked, a sick green-yellow glow that gets into soil, water and flesh. The transplants call it **the
fallout**. The Forsaken, who were here first, call it **the Seep**, because that's what it does. (That's my pick for
the open question in the brief; veto it if you like.)

**The wheel.** Over the tear stands a rusted Ferris wheel with yellow gondolas. It's the only Earth object that ever
came through anywhere. It came from Pripyat, a city that was a fallout zone already, empty since 1986. The park it
stood in was meant to open on the first of May that year, and it never did. The wheel never turned for anyone. The two
wounds matched, so it fell through.

**The liquidators.** Transplants who've lived next to a fallout zone, or cleaned one up, came here on purpose. They
borrowed the Soviet word for the men and women who went into Chernobyl in 1986. They hold the line at Ariden's Watch
with the Forsaken apothecaries, who can walk where the living can't, and they're slowly, carefully losing.

**The question underneath every quest.** Is the tear healing, or growing? The quests answer it a piece at a time,
and the answer is worse than either: it's **remembering**. Every time something dies near the tear, the tear keeps a
copy, and the copies are what's crawling out. That's why the wolves have three heads of three ages and the ogres
wear their younger selves. The fallout is time, curdled.

**The ending.** Under the wheel, at the bottom of the Exclusion Zone, is the Wound's Heart: a knot of flesh, black
glass and stopped light that has been keeping every copy. Killing it doesn't close the tear. It stops it
remembering, so what dies in Deadwind can finally stay dead. The last quest goes back up to the Watch's board of
names, and every name on it gets a line.

## The people

**Ariden's Watch** (the camp: Forsaken iron, wash troughs at the gate, a pyre that never goes out, a board of names):
- **Chief Liquidator Halyna Bondar** (transplant, Slavutych, the town built for Chernobyl's workers). Her father was
  a 1986 liquidator, and she grew up with his dosimeter on the kitchen shelf. She runs the camp and gives the main
  arc. Her younger brother **Mykola** went down into the tear first, three months ago, and didn't come back.
- **Kenji Watanabe** (transplant, a decontamination foreman from the Fukushima clean-up). He maps the fallout, takes
  readings, and builds the Watch's dosimeters out of Seep-glass and cat gut. Quiet and exact.
- **Corporal Ray Delgado** (transplant, St. George in Utah, a downwinder's grandson). He runs the wire, the patrols
  and the quarantine fence, and he jokes too much.
- **Master Apothecary Morwen Ashgrave** (Forsaken). The Undercity's woman at the Watch. She wants samples, a lot of
  samples, and her reasons are only partly the Watch's.
- **Deathguard Harrow** (Forsaken), the gate captain, who has stopped counting.
- **Old Tobiah**, the pyrekeeper (Forsaken). He burns the mutated dead and talks to them while he does it.
- **Innkeeper Sela Crowe** (Forsaken; the inn and the hearth) and a bat handler for the flight path.
- **The recruits** (written on sheet 5 and placed here): **Taras** at the wash troughs with Kashtan, **Wes** on the
  quarantine fence, **Mariana** in the infirmary and **Neil** at the sample racks. They give no quests and talk about
  the camp, never about each other.

**Out in the pass** (a few givers where the story needs them):
- **Ariden** at his old camp on the gorge edge. He's the hermit the Watch is named for, a man who's been watching
  Karazhan for forty years, and he's stuck: the same evening plays over him, and he only half knows it. His quests
  are about the tower, and the last one asks you to let his evening end.
- **Grull the Younger**, an ogre fused with the child he was, in the wreck of the ogre compound. The child half is
  kind and frightened. He wants the chief who did this to his clan put down.
- **The Bridge Echo**, a liquidator's echo stuck on the broken bridge, mid-stride. Mykola's. He can say a few
  words each time the moment loops, and he points the way down.

## The arcs

### 1. A GLOW IN THE WRONG PLACE (the tunnel and Deadwind West's gate, about 8)
The way in is the tunnel that cracked open in Stranglethorn's north-east mountains. The first quest is given at
Grom'gol: a liquidator supply runner hasn't come back. You find his cart halfway down the tunnel, where the rock has
started to glow, and his dosimeter still clicking. At the far end, Corporal Delgado's fence and the first sight of the
pass: dead forest, a black tower on its crag, and over the middle of it all a Ferris wheel. Arriving at the Watch: the
wash troughs ("Everybody scrubs, everybody, I don't care if you're dead"), a dosimeter from Kenji, and Halyna's first
job.

### 2. THE DEAD FOREST AND MORGAN'S PLOT (Deadwind West, about 14)
- **The Watch's daily life:** samples for Morwen, tumour wolves and pustule boars off the supply road, Seep-glass for
  Kenji's dosimeters, wood for Tobiah's pyre (the dead forest burns green).
- **Morgan's Plot:** the dead don't stay down, and some never left. A wagon crashes on the road below the Plot every
  few minutes, and the **Wagon Driver** (a rare) has crashed it a thousand times. One quest is to stop him: kill the
  echo and the next crash doesn't come. Another is a grieving Forsaken who wants a name read off a crypt that's been
  aged to dust.
- **Earth:** Halyna explains the word "liquidator", Kenji tells you how Fukushima's workers wrote their names on the
  dosimeter sheets every day, and Delgado talks about his grandmother's town downwind of the Nevada tests.

### 3. THE OGRES' COMPOUND (Deadwind West, about 8)
The Deadwind ogres survived the tear, and that was worse. They've swollen and fused, and their chief is two ogres at
once: **Mog'grak the Twice-Born** (a rare), the chief and the boy he used to be. **Grull the Younger** gives the chain.
It's about putting his clan down kindly, recovering their ancestor totem from the bone heaps, and finally Mog'grak.
His last line is the arc's: "The little one in me is glad. The big one isn't sure yet."

### 4. THE WIDOWS' NEST (cave, about 4)
Deadwind's spiders have bloated, and their egg sacs glow. Morwen wants the eggs, alive. Halyna wants them burned.
You get to choose which of them to bring the last egg to (two quests, one reward each, the other locked out). The
cave boss, **the Silk Mother**, is a widow queen fused with her own cocoon.

### 5. BROKEN TIME (Deadwind East, about 12)
The east is where time broke. A rockslide hangs in the air over the gorge, a crow is stuck in a shard of stopped air,
and a tree aged ten thousand years in a second stands beside the sapling it was.
- **Kenji's survey:** reading the stopped places with a dosimeter that measures time instead of radiation, and he
  finds the stopped zones are growing.
- **The broken bridge:** the Bridge Echo, Mykola, stuck mid-stride. Each quest gets a few more of his words out of the
  loop, and the last points down: "It's not a hole. It's a mouth."
- **The stuck:** echoes, aged walkers, shard wraiths and the things with too many of themselves. **Old Three-Heads**,
  a wolf whose three heads are the same wolf at three ages, is a rare.
- **Ariden:** the hermit, his evening, and the tower.

### 6. KARAZHAN'S SPILL (Deadwind East, about 8)
The tower is sealed in stopped time, but things wandered out before it closed: spectral servants still serving
dinner, a stablehand ghost, flocks of books. Ariden's chain: Karazhan's librarian wrote down everything the tower ever
saw, and a page of it explains the tear. You gather the pages from the book flocks, and they're written by the same
hand at nine different ages. That hand is **Archivist Nine**, waiting in the Exclusion Zone. The last of Ariden's
quests: he asks you to stop his evening, and it does stop.

### 7. THE DROWNED SEEP (cave, about 4)
A flooded cave where the fallout pools and things grow in it. Kenji needs a reading from the bottom. **The Drowned
Thing**, a bloated mass of mud, roots and drowned animals, is the boss. The **Crow King** (a rare, many crows fused
into one) circles the cave mouth.

### 8. THE WHEEL (the rupture, about 8)
Everything turns toward the middle of the pass. The liquidators raise a **containment spire** at the rupture's edge
(Kenji's design, Morwen's runes, your materials), and the quests to build it take you round the wheel: seals on the
four gondola chains, the glow crater, the things that come up to stop you. When it's raised, Halyna gives the
dungeon's first quest and asks you to find Mykola.

### 9. THE EXCLUSION ZONE (the dungeon, 9 quests, all given at the Watch)
- **Gristlemaw**: a heap of Deadwind ogres moving as one body (Grull asks).
- **The Stopped Man**: a volunteer frozen mid-scream in stopped time. It's Mykola. Halyna's quest is to let him go.
- **Broodmother Vexis** (Morwen wants her heart).
- **The Glow-Eater** (Kenji: it's eating the fallout, and growing).
- **Archivist Nine**: the librarian at nine ages (Ariden's last page).
- **The Wound's Heart**: the knot that keeps the copies. Halyna and Kenji together. Kenji has a name for it from
  home: there's a mass of melted fuel under Chernobyl's reactor that they called the Elephant's Foot.
- **The T0 bracers token**, from the Heart.
- Two cleanup quests: the volunteers' lost kits (bring back every beaked mask you find), and samples from the
  deepest floor.

### 10. THE BOARD OF NAMES (the end, 2 quests)
Back at the Watch, Halyna adds Mykola's name to the board, and asks you to read the rest out loud at the pyre. Then
Tobiah lets the pyre go out for the first time since the camp was built, because nothing in Deadwind needs burning
twice any more.

## The rares
**Old Three-Heads** (the wolf at three ages), **the Wagon Driver** (the crash that keeps happening), **Mog'grak the
Twice-Born** (the ogre chief and his younger self) and **the Crow King** (many crows, one bird). Each has a quest and
a drop.

## The Liquidators (faction)
New faction, the camp's own. Quests at the Watch pay it, and so does every mutated thing in the pass to Honored. The
Exclusion Zone's bosses and trash pay it to Revered. The quartermaster's shelf follows the Conclave's shape:
- **Friendly:** supplies. Seep-washed bandages, an iodine tincture against the fallout (a real Earth habit), and a
  scrubbing kit.
- **Honored:** the Liquidator's tabard and two patterns.
- **Revered:** blues.
- **Exalted:** epics and two epic recipes.

## Build order
1. The two maps of the pass, Ariden's Watch, the tunnel from Stranglethorn, the two caves.
2. Creatures, rares, nodes, loot and the faction.
3. The Exclusion Zone.
4. The quests, arc by arc, then the four recruits placed in the camp.
