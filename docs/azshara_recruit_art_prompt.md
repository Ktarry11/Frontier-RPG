# Azshara's recruit: Mohammed Saleh (1 sprite and 1 portrait)

Kyle, 18 Nov 2026: "Make Mohammad there". Mohammed Saleh is Azshara's one recruit, a Chronomancer (Artificer) at
Talrendis Landing, by the inn door. His sister Layla keeps the inn now ("We never loose an innkeeper though for our
hearth"). Until this art lands he's drawn as the generic human the Landing already used for him.

Two prompts, the same as the sheet-4 recruits (`claude/recruit_art_prompts_sheet4.md`), so he matches them. Attach two
or three sheet-4 sprites as the style reference (Santiago, Sherif, Emiliano).

## Sprite

```
Generate ONE image, 1024x1024, ONE full-body character sprite centred on a flat pure #FF00FF magenta background with a wide margin, nothing touching the image edge. A single standing figure, FULL BODY head to boots, facing straight toward the viewer (front view, "facing down" on a top-down game map), relaxed idle pose, holding his staff low in one hand, slight 3/4 top-down camera angle, a tall narrow proportion roughly 1 : 1.6 (width : height). No ground shadow, no ground plane, no text, no labels, no frames. Painterly semi-realistic, WoW Classic art style, matching the attached references exactly in style, outline and level of detail, consistent lighting from the top-left, crisp clean edges, no magenta or pink anywhere on him.

He is an ordinary man from modern Earth who ended up in a fantasy world: his gear is class-appropriate fantasy gear, and he keeps one small personal touch from his old life. Strikingly good-looking in a grounded, lived-in way: strong bone structure, clear features, good posture, not airbrushed.

MOHAMMED SALEH, a Jordanian man, 41, medium build, warm brown skin, short dark curly hair going grey at the temples, a neat short beard, kind dark eyes with laugh lines. A CHRONOMANCER: deep teal and sand-coloured robes with brass trim and a small brass hourglass at his belt, a staff topped with a glass orb full of slowly turning sand. His personal touch: a worn brass door key on a leather cord round his neck, the front-door key of the guesthouse he ran in Aqaba.
```
