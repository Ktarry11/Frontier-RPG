  - Deathfire is 6 real stacks (CoA's cap); everything was divided by 10.
  - Skulltaker, Hellmaw, Implosion and Hellstorm spend every stack and hit harder per stack (+20%, +20%, +100%, +15%).

**Interface**
- Cast screen: real buff icons with hover durations, mana numbers, a secondary-resource bar, and each ability's real cost pool.
- Backpack tooltips show a "Currently Equipped" comparison.
- Reward cards say One-Hand or Two-Hand.
- Vendor trash shows its sell price.
- Turn-in rewards no longer overlap the text.
- A downed character's HP display is clamped at 0.
- **Difficulty menu:** the EXP pill became a "Difficulty" box that opens a tabbed window.
  - Tabs: Experience, Open World, Dungeons, Skull Zones and Frontier Zones.
  - Monster health and damage are set separately on each tab, 0.5x to 2x.
  - It saves per character, and the balance sims never see it.
