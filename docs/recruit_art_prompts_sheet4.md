## Why these 16 specs

97 specs, 68 recruits, one companion per spec. **29 specs had nobody.** Druid Feral stays untouched (Kyle's
do-not-touch pair with Restoration), which leaves 28, and these 16 take the ones that fit the new regions best. Only one
healer spec was left in the empty set (Bloodmage Fleshweaver), so it's in; the rest are melee and ranged damage.

Still empty after this sheet (12): Barbarian Headhunting, Bloodmage Sanguine, Chronomancer Artificer, Cultist
Godblade, Felsworn Infernal, Guardian Inspiration, Knight of Xoroth Hellfire, Starcaller Warden, Sun Cleric Piety,
Venomancer Stalking, Witch Doctor Shadowhunting, Witch Hunter Houndmaster.

## Why these levels and places

**No recruit existed above level 48.** Fourteen of these sixteen are level 51 to 58, in Felwood, Tempest Isle,
Onyxhollow, the Black Strand and the Plaguelands, so a party can still change at the top. Two fill the Arathi and
Stonetalon gap in the 30s. None stands in a hidden place.

The Plaguelands five are placed when those maps exist. The three from the Order of the Pale Flame join after a Pale
Flame chain. **The recruits-don't-know-each-other rule still holds:** they all serve the Order, but each one's text
talks about Rory and the Order, never about another recruit.
