# Drains: Drain Life, Siphon Life, Vampiric Embrace and the rest

Kyle, 15 Nov 2026, from a friend's playtest: "Drain life doesn't take life. Check to ensure drain life and drain mana
or siphon life work as intended. Vampiric embrace too", and "And coa drain skills as appropriate".

## What was wrong

| Ability | What it did | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drain Life (Warlock) | Shadow damage, no heal | Authored as plain `spell_damage`; nothing read a drain |
| Siphon Life (talent) | Nothing | Its proc listens for damage casts filtered to Corruption, and Corruption does its damage in DoT ticks. A tick didn't know who cast it, so no tick ever fired a proc |
| Vampiric Embrace (talent) | Healed 20% of ALL damage dealt by a cast, never from a DoT | No school filter; ticks, as above |
| Monster Drain Life, Life Drain, Siphon Soul | The monster healed itself, the player lost nothing | Authored as kind `heal` in data/creatures/ability_kits.json |
| Monster Mana Drain | An Intellect debuff, no mana moved | Authored as a plain debuff |
| Drain Mana (players) | Doesn't exist | Never added for Warlocks |

## What changed

- **`drains_pct`** (combat/abilities.py, an after-cast rider): the caster heals for that share of the damage the
  cast dealt. Drain Life carries 100, so it heals for all of it (Classic's Drain Life returns all of it). Its
  description now says so.
- **DoTs remember their caster.** A DoT an ability applies carries `source_caster`, `source_ability_id` and the
  engine (not saved). Each tick fires the caster's ON_DAMAGE_CAST with `is_tick` and the school it landed as.
  **Only procs that drain (`leech_pct`) or say `include_ticks` hear a tick**, so no other "on damage" proc in the
  game changes: a DoT can't suddenly trigger extra swings, stacks or the like.
- **`school_filter`** on a proc: only damage of that school (from the event, or the casting ability's
  `damage_school`). Vampiric Embrace now carries `"school_filter": "shadow"`.
- **Monster drains** (entities/monster.py): a kit ability of kind `heal` named as a drain (Drain Life, Life
  Drain, Drain Soul, Siphon Life/Soul/Vitality/Essence/Hope/Warmth/Light, Sap Drain, Blood Drain) is a hit with
  `lifesteal` 1.0: it deals its amount and heals the monster for what it dealt. A Mana Drain (or Drain Mana, Mana
  Siphon, Mana Leech) also takes 8% of the target's mana pool and gives it to the monster.

## Checked in real casts (tools/qa_drains.py)

- Drain Life at 40: heals exactly what it dealt.
- Siphon Life: a Corruption tick heals 40% of the tick; without the talent, nothing.
- Vampiric Embrace: Mind Blast heals 20%, Shadow Word: Pain ticks heal 20%, Smite (Holy) heals nothing.
- Soul Leech, forced to fire: Shadow Bolt heals 20%.
- All 24 `damage_and_heal` abilities across the thirty classes (Bloodmage's Vampiric Fang and Heartbreak,
  Necromancer's Harvest Plague, Reaper's Soul Strike, Witch Hunter's blades, Templar's Argent Blade and
  Titanstrike, Guardian's Final Verdict, Felsworn's Tyrant's Gaze, Cultist's Armageddon and Malevolence, Mage's
  Icy Penance and Frostfall, Pyromancer's Cleansing Flames, Witch Doctor's Spirit in a Bottle and the rest) hurt
  their target and heal somebody.
- A monster's Drain Life, Life Drain and Siphon Soul hurt their target and heal the monster for at least what
  they took; a monster's Mana Drain moves mana.
