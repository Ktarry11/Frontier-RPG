# Recruit art prompts, SHEET 5: 16 NEW characters (1 sprite sheet + 1 portrait sheet)

Kyle, 18 Nov 2026: "Let's build another 16 recruits. Character sheets and portraits. Keep in mind next zone will be
deadwind pass. And we can have some filler. Also will eventually have moonglade and winterspring." Then: "Make 5
characters attractive latino females." The roster goes from 85 to 101.

**11 men, 5 women.** The five women are all Latina (Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Peru, Mexico), aged 28 to 34 and genuinely
beautiful. The men follow sheet 4's mix (Kyle, 16 Nov: "White, Latinos, and one or two middle eastern"): seven white,
three Latino and one Iraqi, aged 33 to 46 and handsome in a grounded, lived-in way. Every one of them is an Earth
transplant (Kyle: "we can ONLY recruit earth transplants").

## Why these 16 specs

97 specs and 85 recruits. **Twelve specs had nobody.** Druid Feral stays untouched (Kyle's do-not-touch pair), so
**the other eleven are all here**, and after this sheet every spec but Feral has a companion. Those eleven are all
damage specs, though, and the game had **no tank above level 44 and only one healer above 48**. So the five filler
slots are three tanks and two healers at 56 to 60 (the rest of the sheet spreads down the levels), second copies of specs that already have a low-level companion:

| Filler | Spec | The existing one |
|---|---|---|
| Wes | warrior_protection (tank) | Dan, level 13 |
| Tobias | paladin_protection (tank) | Marco, level 10 |
| Ximena | primalist_mountain_king (tank) | Pasang, level 27 |
| Mariana | priest_holy (healer) | Ama, level 7 |

Nothing checks one-companion-per-spec (qa_recruits only counts zones), so two people can share a spec. Their kits will
be different anyway, because every kit comes from its owner's own story.

## Why these places

Kyle, 18 Nov 2026: "We can put recruits in older zones too! Don't think they ALL have to be end game." So it's
**nine at the top and seven spread down the levels**, each in a place with a gap: towns and open zones that had
nobody, or a level band that was thin (only six recruits under 10, thirteen in the 20s, twelve in the 40s).

- **Deadwind Pass, four (57 to 59).** They're the liquidators (`claude/deadwind_brief.md`): transplants who have seen
  a fallout zone before, or grew up next to one, and volunteered to hold this one. They work out of the liquidators'
  camp, with its working name Ariden's Watch. Deadwind's Earth references run heavy, and these four are a lot of the
  reason. Like the Plaguelands five, they're placed when the maps exist.
- **Moonglade, two. Winterspring, two (58 to 60).** They're placed when those zones are built.
- **Light's Hope Chapel, one (56).** Tobias goes live as soon as the art lands, so the top end gets a tank this week,
  not when Deadwind's done.
- **Older zones, seven (8 to 43)**, all live as soon as the art lands: Western Tirisfal, the Crossroads, Camp Taurajo,
  Arathi West, the Scarlet Monastery Grounds, Gurbashi Arena and Northern Tanaris.

None stands in a hidden place. **The recruits-don't-know-each-other rule still holds:** the four liquidators share a
camp, but each one's text talks about the camp, the wheel and the Forsaken, never about another recruit.
