# Feralas: the quest plan (about 90 quests)

For `docs/feralas_brief.md`. Ashenvale is the bar: people talking in their own voice, the useful thing first, real
lore, chains that build to something. About 70% of the quests come from the two towns; nobody gives more than five;
no quest giver stands at the place their quest sends you; the Writhing Deep's quests are given in Camp Mojache.

Rules carried over: every kill target spawns where the quest says, every fetch item drops (about 75% when a quest
needs several), every explore point is walkable, a chain is a chain, and the rewards give every spec something. Earth
scales with level: the east arm (44-46) is near silent, the coast (46-48) carries the most through the settlers, and
it stays a note, never the theme. No quest changes the world (Kyle, 18 Nov 2026).

Reputation by giver: Feathermoon's people pay **the Feathermoon Sentinels**, Mojache's pay **Orgrimmar**, the
settlers pay nothing but give good items, the Sentinels' Watch pays the Sentinels.

## The theme: everybody's digging

Feralas is quiet on top and busy underneath. The **silithids** are digging down and away, toward the Dry Deep. The
**naga** are digging up the Highborne ruins on the coast, and the **Gordunni** are digging up Isildien's dome. The
Sentinels work out that the naga and the ogres want the same thing: the way into Eldre'Thalas, the old Highborne city
the ogres call Dire Maul, and whatever is still alive in there. That sets up the dungeon without opening it.

## The people

**On the way in, the Aspen Descent:** **Scout Wapi Longstride** (tauren, Mojache's lookout at the foot of the pass).
The first quest: down the descent to Camp Mojache, like Moonglade's first quest in the tunnel.

**Camp Mojache** (tauren, grown with the refugees; answers to Elder Longtread at Camp Taurajo):
- **Chieftain Ahanu Swiftstrider**, the camp's leader, who took in everyone who walked south.
- **Hadoken Swiftstrider**, her brother, the camp's hunter: the Woodpaw and the Lower Wilds.
- **Elder Nayeli Cloudhoof**, who walked out of Mulgore with the refugees: the refugees' thread.
- **Brann Tallgrass**, the young tauren keeping the list of who got out and who didn't.
- **Krueg Skullsplitter** (orc), Orgrimmar's man at the camp: the Gordunni and Dire Maul.
- **Orwin Gizzmick** (goblin), the trader, who started buying silithid resin and wishes he hadn't: the dig.
- **Innkeeper Greul** (tauren), **Wind Rider Master Shyn**, a class trainer, a trader, the herb and skinning pairs.

**Feathermoon Stronghold** (night elves, the new faction):
- **Shandris Feathermoon**, General of the Sentinels, the faction's leader.
- **Latronicus Moonspear**, the stronghold's keeper of the Highborne ruins: the diggers.
- **Sentinel Selarin**, the coast watch: the Hatecrest naga and Sardor Isle.
- **Kindal Moonweaver**, the stronghold's druid: the Fog Groves, the hippogryphs.
- **Huntress Jalara Nightwind**, the Isle of Dread and the chimaeras that go for the boats.
- **Quartermaster Lyriel Dawnbough** (gives no quests, so she can move), **Innkeeper Shyria**, **Fyldren
  Moonfeather** at the roost.

**Cedar Hollow, the settlers** (transplants who woke up under trees they knew):
- **June Nakamura**, a ranger from Olympic National Park who knows exactly which trees these are.
- **Walt Hollis**, a retired logger from Forks, Washington, who won't cut a single one of them.
- **Rosa Ibarra**, an ER nurse from Tacoma, the hollow's only medic.
- **Sam Okonkwo**, a mycologist from Portland, who has noticed the fungus in the Fog Groves isn't from here either.

**The Sentinels' Watch, the north coast:** **Sentinel Aerwyn Starbrook** (the watch's captain), **Tahu Swiftrunner**
(a Mojache hunter tracking the Rage Scar), and **Dreamwatcher Sylanna** (the Cenarion Circle, sent from Moonglade to
the Dream Bough).

## The arcs

**THE REFUGEES (Mojache, about 14).** Who walked south out of Mulgore, and what they found when they got here: the
Grimtotem had moved onto the empty ground. Early quests are the camp's life, feeding a camp twice its size, the
Lower Wilds' crocolisks on the water, wolves on the hunting trails, the memorial pole. Brann's list of names, and the
ones he can't cross off. The Grimtotem Compound, where Pathfinder Harrok wears a totem carried out of Thunder Bluff.
It ends with Brann's list going north to Elder Longtread at Camp Taurajo (out).

**THE DIG (Mojache, about 14).** Orwin's resin, the mounds in the Hive Fields, the Zukk'ash coming up through the
forest floor, and the hunters working out the mounds run in a line. Into the Writhing Deep (the cave's quests): its
boss, and at the back the tunnel the silithids are still digging, which comes out in the Dry Deep under the Abyssal
Sands. It ends by carrying what you found through the tunnel to Onyxhollow (out).

**THE GORDUNNI AND THE WOODPAW (Mojache, about 10).** Hadoken's gnolls in Woodpaw Hills, raiding the camp's
hunting grounds. Krueg's ogres: the Gordunni Outpost, the gate of Dire Maul, Gash'tok, and the question nobody at the
camp can answer: what are the ogres keeping people out of? It passes to the Sentinels, who can.

**THE FOG GROVES (Cedar Hollow and Feathermoon, about 16).** The part of the forest that came from our world. The
settlers' life under the redwoods, the elk, the hollow's fog, Old Seven-Point. June's rangering, Walt's refusal, Rosa's
medicine from local herbs, Sam's fungus that shouldn't be here. Kindal watching from the stronghold, deciding what to
make of them, and in the end vouching for them. This is where most of Feralas's Earth lives.

**THE SENTINELS AND THE COAST (Feathermoon, about 22).** The Hatecrest naga coming up the beaches, Sardor Isle and its
Tidelord, the chimaeras of the Isle of Dread going for the boats, the Frayfeather hippogryphs, and Isildien's dome
with the Gordunni digging it out. Latronicus puts the diggers together: the naga and the ogres both want the way into
Eldre'Thalas. Shandris sends you north to see whether Ravenwind is the same.

**THE NORTH (the Sentinels' Watch, about 12).** Ravenwind's naga and Lady Szaressa, the same digging; the Rage Scar
yetis driven out of the hold by something; the giants walking the Twin Colossals and the Old Walker. The Dream Bough:
Dreamwatcher Sylanna came from Moonglade to see whether what's waking there has reached Feralas. It ends at Moonglade
with Dreamwatcher Lethiel (out).

## Out of Feralas
- To **Camp Taurajo**: Brann's list for Elder Longtread.
- To **Onyxhollow** through the Writhing Deep's tunnel and the Dry Deep: the dig.
- To **Moonglade**: the Dream Bough.
- To **Orgrimmar**: Krueg's report on the Gordunni and the gate.
- Into Feralas: a letter from Freewind Post's High Elder Namka Windhollow asking after the refugees who went south,
  and the Aspen Wilderness's scouts pointing west.

## The towns' rewards
Feathermoon's quartermaster is TBC-style like every faction, a direction (hunters, rangers, archers) and never a cage.
Quest rewards across the three maps give every spec something, checked against `data/items/spec_gear.json`.
