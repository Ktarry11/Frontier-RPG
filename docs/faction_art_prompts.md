# Faction card backgrounds: art prompts

Written by `tools/write_faction_art_prompts.py` (edit the script, not this file).

Kyle, 19 Nov 2026: background art for each faction's card, then "like a 'background card' but on the faction page, very opaque and unique for each one". So each faction's page is backed by its own painting, drawn solid (not a faint wash), with the page's text on a darker band over the painting's calm lower third. One landscape per faction, four to a sheet, each panel 1024x768. The secret faction gets none: its card says nothing on purpose. The three sky factions are drawn ahead of their zones.

Slice to `assets/ui/factions/bg_<faction id>.png` (the ids below).

## SHEET FC1: The Crimson Codex, Orgrimmar, The Darkspear Tribe, The Forsaken

```
Generate ONE image, 2048x1536, laid out as a strict 2 columns x 2 rows grid = 4 separate landscape paintings, each panel exactly 1024x768. COUNT THEM: exactly 2 across and 2 down. Each panel is COMPLETELY FILLED by its painting, edge to edge, with no border, no frame, no gutter, no grid lines, no text, no letters, no logos, no banners with writing, no numbers. Painterly semi-realistic fantasy art in the style of World of Warcraft loading screens: rich, atmospheric, soft painted edges, a sense of place.

These are BACKGROUNDS for a game's faction card, so the composition matters: the place and its people fill the upper two-thirds of each panel; the lower third is calmer and darker (ground, shadow, mist or water) so white text can be read over it; a soft natural vignette toward the edges; no single face close to the viewer, figures small or seen from behind; nothing bright or busy in the lower third. Each panel is the faction's HOME and what it stands for, readable at a glance. Nothing from our real world appears unless the panel says so.

Panels, left to right, top to bottom:

1 THE CRIMSON CODEX: Kael's Bastion deep in the Crystalline Underdark: a blood elf arcanist citadel of crimson and gold built into a vast cavern of glowing blue and violet crystal, floating tomes and arcane sigils of light, robed magisters on a high balcony, the cavern falling away into darkness below.; 2 ORGRIMMAR: Orgrimmar at dusk: the orc capital of red stone and ship timber in a canyon, spiked iron walls, war banners of red and black, smoke from forges, wyverns circling, the great gate's tusks in silhouette, warm firelight against a red sky.; 3 THE DARKSPEAR TRIBE: The Echo Isles at sunset: jungle troll huts on stilts over a turquoise lagoon, carved loa totems, torches and voodoo lanterns, palm trees, a war canoe pulled up on white sand, a witch doctor's green smoke rising.; 4 THE FORSAKEN: The ruined throne city of Lordaeron above the Undercity's gate: broken pale towers, sickly green light glowing up from below, purple banners, plague cauldrons, bats against a grey-green sky, Forsaken sentries in silhouette.
```

Slice order: `bg_crimson_codex`, `bg_orgrimmar`, `bg_darkspear_tribe`, `bg_the_forsaken`
