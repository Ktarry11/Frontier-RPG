Generate ONE sprite sheet image, 2048x2048, a 6 columns x 6 rows grid = 36 separate creature sprites, each cell about 341x341. Flat pure #FF00FF magenta background everywhere. Each creature is ONE single figure, centred in its own cell with a wide margin, not touching or overlapping its neighbours or the image edge. No ground, no dirt patch, no water under it, no cast shadow, no glow halo bleeding onto the background, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames, no borders. Never use magenta or pink anywhere on the creatures themselves.

Match the attached reference creatures EXACTLY in style, camera angle, outline and level of detail: they are from the same set. Painterly semi-realistic WoW Classic style: strong readable silhouette, soft dark outline, chunky simple shapes legible at small size, consistent soft lighting from the top-left.

Camera: every creature faces three-quarters toward the lower-right, full body, feet, tail or base included and nothing cropped. Flying creatures hover with their lowest point near the bottom of the cell. Consistency of angle across all 36 is critical.

Setting: THE WORLD TREE, a colossal living tree grown out of a reef off the Stranglethorn jungle coast. Its golden sap makes the beasts that drink it big, glowing-eyed and wrong; rot is eating it in places; pirates and savage trolls climb it; high up, storms, wyverns and harpies; and finely made bronze-and-crystal machines are starting to fall out of the sky. Palette: jungle greens and browns, amber-gold sap glow in the eyes and veins of sap-drunk beasts, black-violet rot, Bloodsail red and black, Bloodscalp red war paint on green-blue troll skin, storm blue and silver, bronze, pale stone and glowing blue crystal for the machines.

Creatures keep their RELATIVE scale: a moth, a murloc and a gear-drone are small, a troll and an ape are big, the rare beasts and the rot-treant are the largest sprites on the sheet. Do not blow each one up to fill its own cell.

Creatures are numbered row by row, left to right, top to bottom:

Row 1 - ROOTWATER ISLE: 1 a sap-drunk crocolisk, huge, gold veins glowing under its scales; 2 a murloc tangled in root fibres with glowing gold eyes; 3 a giant reef crab with roots grown into its shell; 4 a Bloodsail sap-tapper pirate with a mallet and a sap bucket; 5 a Bloodsail cutthroat with a cutlass and a red bandana; 6 a Bloodsail powder-monkey with a lit grenade.

Row 6 - FALLEN FROM THE SKY: 31 a damaged scout construct, a flying machine of bronze and pale stone with a cracked blue crystal eye; 32 a gear-drone, small and round with spinning blades; 33 a crystal-shard sentinel, a walking frame of bronze around a glowing blue crystal; 34 a storm wisp, a ball of blue lightning; 35 the Rootmaw, a colossal ancient sap-drunk crocolisk covered in barnacles and roots (a rare); 36 the Rot-Mother, an enormous swollen rot-treant crowned with fungus and black blossoms (a rare).
