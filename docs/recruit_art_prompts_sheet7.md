**16 men, all Earth transplants** (Kyle: "we can ONLY recruit earth transplants"): white, Latino and Middle Eastern.
Big, broad and solid men built by real physical work, with thick beards, hairy forearms, a bit of grey on some of them
and lived-in handsome faces. They're powerful and fit, never obese and never gym-sculpted.

## Why these specs and these places

The roster has 111 recruits. The thin spots are tanks and healers at 51 to 60 (three of each), healers and ranged at
21 to 30 (two healers, one ranged) and melee at 31 to 40. So ten of these go to 51 to 60, mostly to the Ascendant
Bastions and the Fading Firmament, which are the next zones built, and they're placed when those maps exist. Five go
to 21 to 30 and one to 31 to 40. Every spec here has only one companion so far, and Druid Feral stays untouched as
before. Final zones get picked when each one is placed.

## SHEET 7A: SPRITES, ALL 16  (paste this whole block)

Attach two of the existing recruit sprites as the style reference (`assets/npcs/recruits/mariana_priest/down.png` and
`assets/npcs/recruits/tobias_paladin/down.png`).

```
Generate ONE sprite sheet image, as large as you can make it (ideally 2048x2048 or bigger), a grid of 4 columns x 4 rows = 16 separate full-body character sprites. Flat pure #FF00FF magenta background everywhere. Each character stands fully inside its own cell with a wide margin and does NOT touch or overlap neighbours or the image edge. Every character is a single standing figure, FULL BODY head to boots, facing straight toward the viewer (front view, "facing down" on a top-down game map), relaxed idle pose, arms at sides or holding their weapon low, slight 3/4 top-down camera angle. Each figure is a tall proportion roughly 1 : 1.5 (width : height), broader than usual because these are big men, and all 16 are the SAME height in the cell. No ground shadow, no ground plane, no text, no labels, no grid lines. Painterly semi-realistic, WoW Classic art style, matching the attached references, consistent lighting from the top-left, crisp clean edges, no magenta tint on the characters themselves (avoid pink/magenta colours in clothing or skin). These are ordinary men from modern Earth who ended up in a fantasy world: their gear is class-appropriate fantasy gear, but each keeps one small personal touch from their old life.

ALL SIXTEEN ARE RUGGED, HANDSOME "BEAR" TYPE MEN: big, broad-shouldered, barrel-chested and genuinely muscular from a lifetime of hard physical work, thick strong arms and forearms with visible body hair, full or heavy beards, a strong masculine presence. Solid and powerful, NOT obese, NOT a fat belly, and NOT a sculpted bodybuilder either: real working men's bodies. Weathered, lived-in, very handsome faces with warm eyes and laugh lines, ages 38 to 49, some with grey in their beards. Where the armour allows, show their arms or a bit of chest hair at an open collar. Every one is a different man and you could tell any two apart at a glance.
```
