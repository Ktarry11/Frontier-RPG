# Faction leaders and the story's own: art prompts (sheets FL1 and FL2, 12 each)

Kyle, 22 Nov 2026: "I need faction leaders. Keeping it 12 to improve quality of each sprite". Front-only like the
townsfolk (only the character-creation looks walk), twelve to a sheet so each figure gets a big cell.

**The first leader sheet (28 Oct 2026) already gave these their own art** in `assets/npcs/leaders/`: MerchantTusk
(the banker in every capital), Thrall, Sylvanas, Gazlowe, Elder Longtread, Elder Kang'ari and Codex-Master Ilarien. Since
then the Plaguelands added Flamekeeper Rory Callahan and Commander Eligor Dawnbringer, and Ilaren Vey and Leandro Arrieta
got theirs. None of them is on these sheets.

**FL1** is every faction leader still standing in a generic look (seven), the Aetherians' First Speaker, and the four
people who lead Caelora's story from zone to zone. **FL2** is twelve more of Caelora's named people.

**Not on these sheets:** Baron Revilgaz, Shandris Feathermoon and Rabine Saturna are Blizzard's own characters, and I
won't write prompts that recreate them; authentic WoW art is the cleaner route for a private build. The ???? faction
gets nothing, and Cairne isn't drawn.

## SHEET FL1: FACTION LEADERS AND CAELORA'S LEADS, 12 (paste this whole block)

```
Generate ONE sprite sheet image, 2048x1536, a grid of 4 columns x 3 rows = 12 separate full-body character sprites (each cell 512x512). Flat pure #FF00FF magenta background everywhere. Each character stands fully inside its own cell with a wide margin and does NOT touch or overlap neighbours or the image edge. Every character is a single standing figure, FULL BODY head to feet, facing straight toward the viewer (front view, "facing down" on a top-down game map), a confident idle pose, slight 3/4 top-down camera angle. All 12 are drawn at the same scale as the attached references, so a troll is taller than a dwarf and every figure's feet sit at the same baseline in its cell. No ground shadow, no ground plane, no text, no letters, no labels, no grid lines. Match the attached references EXACTLY in style: crisp detailed pixel art with a clean dark outline, the same head size, rich colours, consistent lighting from the top-left. No magenta or pink anywhere in the characters. These are LEADERS: each one is more detailed, more distinctive and more finely dressed than ordinary townsfolk, with one clear signature detail you could recognise from across a town, but still dressed for their people and their place, not for a parade.
```
