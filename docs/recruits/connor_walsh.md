## Who he is

Connor has never been frightened of weather in his life. He's cheerful, weatherproof and steady in a crisis, and he treats every storm like an old friend.

He sings badly in two languages and speaks Irish to the wind and English to people. He's not sure which one listens better.

What annoys him: people who give orders they wouldn't follow, anyone posh about a pint and a forecast he could have done better. What he loves: a big sky, a dog in the bow, a fiddle on a Friday and the moment the rain comes.

## Before

He grew up in a stone cottage at the end of a lane on Inis Mór, with stone walls and wind and not a tree to be seen. His father built the half-decked boat he fished, and Bran rode in the bow. His father taught him to read the sky before he could read a book.

His mother knitted every jumper he ever owned. He's wearing the last one.

## The Integration and what it cost

In 2017 a squall came over and he laughed at it, the way he does, and a young lad on his boat went over the side because he trusted him. Connor got him back, just. He's never told anyone how close it was.

He came through on the ground in his oilskins, which was lucky, because it was raining. He climbed the tree to the top because the higher he goes, the more the weather feels like home. The first storm on the Upper Boughs knocked three climbers flat, and he stood in the middle of it laughing with sparks running down his arms.

## Now

He stands on Highrest's windward edge and watches the storms come across from the Stormsplit, and he tells the camp which ones to worry about. He hasn't been wrong yet.

Three of the Windwardens' scouts are lost in the storm over the Stormsplit, and nobody will go after them. He's been watching that storm for days.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to sail something on this world out into weather nobody else will go into.
- **Hope:** a proper pint, and he's told the trolls exactly how to brew one.
- **Dream:** Bran in the bow again, and his father at the tiller.
- **Fear:** that laughing at the weather will cost somebody else.
- **Fear:** that he's never once been frightened, and should have been.

## How he's changed

At home the fearlessness was a gift his father gave him. Here it nearly cost a lad his life, and the storms are bigger. Part of what the player gives him is somebody to be frightened with.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to bring the lost scouts home, and to learn to respect the weather without losing his love for it.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. Three Windwarden scouts are lost in the storm over the Stormsplit. He asks you to sail into it with him and bring them home.
2. On the way he tells you about 2017, and the lad who trusted him. He tells you to grab a rope.
3. In the storm he finds the scouts, and he's properly frightened the whole way.

**Ending (happy):** they bring every one of the scouts back, and for the first time in his life he was frightened, and it felt right. That night he writes to the lad's mother, though he's no way to send it. Then he brews the first proper pint on the tree. It's dreadful, and the trolls love it.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Connor's Lobster Gauge.** A brass lobster gauge off an Aran half-decker, notched to the legal size and gone green at the edges. It was in his oilskin pocket when he came through. He gives it to you after the storm, and says it never once let him keep anything undersized.

## Voice notes

- Aran English with Irish in it. "Grand", "right so", "God love him", "a chara" to people he likes and "Dia linn" when something's close.
- He sings in a fight, badly.
- His fighting voice is a cheerful shout over the wind.
- **He won't talk about** the lad who went over until Friend.
- **Habits:** sniffs the air, reads the sky out loud and calls every gale a fine breeze.

- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with funny, easygoing people and anyone who lives outdoors, and with anybody from the sea. He clashes with bossy and posh types, and laughs at them.
