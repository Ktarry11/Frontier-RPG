## Who he is

Dan is a shield. That's the whole arrangement, he'll tell you. He stands in front and the rest of you get on with it.

He's plain-spoken. He doesn't do speeches or fancy words. He says what's needed and stops. If something's daft, he says
it's daft.

He's loyal. On the floor you trusted your crew with your life, every shift, and you didn't talk about it because it
sounded soft. He's the same with a party. He'll never say he cares about you. He'll just never let anything get past
him to you.

He's funny, in a dry Yorkshire way. He deflates anyone who takes themselves too seriously, including himself.

He's carrying a lot he doesn't talk about. The plant closed and he was out of work for four years, and most of that is
a subject he'll walk away from.

What annoys him: people who don't pull their weight, management types, and being thanked. What he loves: a crew, a
proper pint, a hard shift done right, and his daughter.

## Before

He grew up in Attercliffe, in Sheffield. His dad came over from Enugu in the 1960s and worked the steel for thirty
years, and his mum was a dinner lady from Rotherham. Dan went onto the floor at 18, the same plant as his dad.

He worked 22 years on a four-man crew: Dan, Gaz, Little Mick and Pete. Heat, noise and a kind of trust you can't
describe without sounding daft. They went for a pint every Friday for nine years straight.

He married Sharon at 23. Jade was born when he was 24. He and Sharon split when Jade was 12, without much fuss. He saw
Jade every weekend and never missed one.

The plant closed in 2019. The crew went their separate ways. He was out of work for four years. He stopped going out,
stopped answering the phone, and spent a lot of time on the sofa. Jade came round every Sunday and made him eat. She's
the reason he got through it, and he's never told her.

## The Integration and what it cost

He'd just started a new job, driving a forklift in a warehouse. He landed in Durotar in his hi-vis vest and made his way
to Orgrimmar because it looked like the only place with work.

He stopped being idle. He'd call that the entire adjustment. He's wrong, but he's wrong in a direction that works.

Jade was in Leeds, working as a paramedic. She married a lad called Craig Dawson the year before, so she's a Dawson on
the lists now. The lists at the hub are alphabetical, and he's got to the D's about forty times.

## Now

He lives in Orgrimmar, near the forges, because the heat and the hammering feel like home. He takes the front of
whatever goes out, and he's never once asked to be paid differently for it.

He's been trying to put together a crew out of whoever's in the tavern. It isn't the same, and he won't say so.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a crew. Four men, long shifts, a Friday.
- **Hope:** to find Jade.
- **Dream:** a pint with Jade, where he finally tells her she got him through.
- **Fear:** going back to the sofa. Being useless again.
- **Fear:** that Jade's been looking for "Okafor" and he's been looking for "Dawson", and they've missed each other.
- **Fear:** letting something get past him.

## How he's changed

In Sheffield he was a steelworker, then he wasn't anything for four years, and that nearly finished him.

Here he's useful again, and that's saved him. He's quieter about his feelings than ever, and better at showing them. Part
of what the player gives him is a crew again.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find Jade.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He asks you to help him go through every list at every hub he can reach, for Dawson and for Okafor. You go with him.
2. At Ratchet a medic remembers a young Englishwoman, a paramedic, who worked the docks for a month two years ago and
   then went off with a caravan. Nobody knows where.
3. You follow the caravan's route as far as it goes. The trail stops in the Southern Barrens, at a camp that was
   abandoned long ago.

**Ending (still unknown):** there's no answer. Dan stands in the empty camp for a while. Then he says she's a paramedic,
she's tough, and she's out there somewhere doing the job, and that'll have to do. He leaves a note at every hub, under
both names, saying where he is. He stops trying to rebuild the old crew. He says he's got one now, and he means the
party. He still checks the D's.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Dan's Hi-Vis Vest.** The orange high-visibility vest from his first week at the warehouse, the one he landed in. It's
faded and torn, and he wore it under his armour for months. He gives it to you after the empty camp. He says he's on a
proper crew now, so he doesn't need to be seen.

## Voice notes

- Short, plain and dry. Contractions, Yorkshire speech ("love", "lad", "nowt", "summat"), and no speeches.
- He says what's needed and stops.
- He calls the party "the crew" once he trusts you.
- He swears like a steelworker, mildly and often, and never in front of kids.
- **He won't talk about** the four years out of work until Friend, or Jade until Companion.
- **Habits:** goes first through every door, checks everyone's kit before a pull, buys the first round, and folds the
  hi-vis vest neatly every night.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** always goes first and hates anyone else pulling. Plain about what went wrong. Grumbles about
  grinding but does it. Gets protective of the party's weakest member.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with working people and anyone who pulls their weight. Clashes with posh people and
  anyone who acts like management. Would make a fine crew with Marco and Adrián, the other men who stand in front.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is being afraid he's useless. A skill idea to look at then: a passive
  where the party takes less damage for every ally still standing, because a crew holds together.
