## Who he is

Esteban is precise. Surgery is doing a violent thing for a good reason, with absolute precision, while the person on the
table trusts you entirely. Blood magic, he says, is the same thing with the formalities removed.

He believes in consent. He spent twenty years explaining consent forms properly even when nobody wanted to listen. He
still names the price before every spell and asks you to agree to it, and he asks you not to pretend afterwards that he
didn't.

He's calm. Unsettlingly calm. He has a surgeon's bedside manner, and it doesn't change whether he's stitching a cut or
draining a man's blood.

He's dry and quietly funny. He makes very understated jokes and waits to see if you caught them.

He's disappointed in the people his magic attracts. Most bloodmages he's met want power and don't care who pays. He
finds the honesty of the magic a relief and the company it keeps a continual letdown.

What annoys him: people who don't ask, people who don't listen, sloppy work, and anyone who uses the word "just" about
someone else's body. What he loves: a clean piece of work, Chilean wine, classical music, and a patient who goes home.

## Before

He grew up in Valparaíso, in a house on one of the steep hills above the harbour, with lifts that creak up the slopes.
His father was a dock worker and his mother a seamstress. He was the first in the family to go to university.

He trained in Santiago and came home to Valparaíso to work. For nineteen years he operated on the city's worst injuries:
car crashes, stabbings, port accidents. He did a lot of his work under X-ray, and he wore a radiation badge on his collar
every day to count the dose.

He never married. He had a few relationships. They ended because he was always at the hospital, and because, one
partner said, he talked about everything as if he were explaining a procedure. He's close to his sister Paz and her
children. He was their favourite uncle, the one who explained exactly how bones heal.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was scrubbed in, in the middle of an operation, when it happened. He landed in the Stranglethorn jungle in his
theatre clothes, with his radiation badge still clipped to his collar. Its little window has since gone completely
black.

He had no crisis. He had an inventory, and he conducted it in the first month. He found the skill transferred entire.

What he underestimated was how much of his old restraint had been supplied by the hospital rather than by him. He found
that out in his second year, and he didn't enjoy it.

The Bloodsail would pay anything for a man who could keep their injured alive and wouldn't ask about the injuries. One
night their captain was dying and there was a prisoner chained below who was dying too. Esteban took the prisoner's blood
to save the captain, and he didn't ask the prisoner. The prisoner died. Esteban left the Bloodsail the next morning.

He hasn't found Paz on any list.

## Now

He lives in Orgrimmar, near the Valley of Spirits, where the orc and troll healers have started letting him work with
them. He still treats anyone who comes to him. He asks how they got hurt, every time, on the record, and then he treats
them anyway.

He's writing a standard of practice for blood magic: rules, written down, binding on anyone who claims the discipline.
Nothing like it exists here and the lack of it appals him. He's drafted eleven clauses. He expects nobody to adopt them
and keeps drafting.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** that someone, anyone, adopts his standard.
- **Hope:** that Paz and the children are safe.
- **Dream:** to train young bloodmages properly, the way he was trained in surgery.
- **Dream:** to go a whole year without doing something he has to forgive himself for.
- **Fear:** that without the hospital he's not a good man, only a careful one.
- **Fear:** the night with the prisoner happening again.
- **Fear:** becoming the kind of bloodmage he despises.

## How he's changed

In Valparaíso he was a respected surgeon with a system around him: rules, forms, committees, colleagues who'd stop him.

Here there's nothing around him but himself. He found out in year two that he wasn't as good as he'd thought, and he's
been building his own rules ever since. He's more honest than he was, and harder on himself. Part of what the player
gives him is somebody whose consent he can actually ask for and get.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to make blood magic answer to rules.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A group of bloodmages is bleeding prisoners in a jungle camp to sell their power. He asks you to help him stop them.
2. You break the camp and free the prisoners. One of them is very badly hurt, and Esteban can save him only with blood
   magic. He kneels down and asks the man if he agrees. The man says yes.
3. He takes the camp's leader to Orgrimmar to be judged, along with his eleven clauses.

**Ending (happy):** the healers of the Valley of Spirits read his clauses. They argue about them for three days, change
two, and add one of their own. Then they adopt them for every bloodmage who wants to work in Orgrimmar. Esteban teaches
the first class of three young bloodmages. On the first day he tells them about the prisoner on the Bloodsail ship,
because he wants them to know why the first clause is there.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Esteban's Radiation Badge.** The badge that sat on his collar for nineteen years of surgery under X-ray, its window
long since gone black. He was wearing it in theatre the day the worlds joined. He gives it to you after the clauses are
adopted. He says it counted the dose for years, and now the clauses do.

## Voice notes

- Calm, measured and polite. Contractions, careful words, and medical explanations nobody asked for.
- His English is excellent, formal and very precise, with a soft Chilean accent.
- He names the price before every procedure and asks for an answer.
- He doesn't swear. When something's done badly he says "that's regrettable", and it sounds worse than swearing.
- **He won't talk about** the prisoner until Friend, or Paz until Companion.
- **Habits:** washes his hands before every fight, asks "may I?" before healing, touches the badge on his collar, and
  takes notes on every wound he treats.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** asks before he uses blood magic on anyone, every time. Coldly furious at cruelty to prisoners.
  Explains every injury in detail after a fight. Calm in a wipe.
- **Wave 3, with others:** respects careful, honest people. Has a lot in common with Ryan, who also names the price
  first. Clashes with reckless people and other bloodmages who don't ask. Gentle with frightened people.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is not trusting himself without the hospital. A skill idea to look at
  then: once a fight, with the party's agreement, he takes some of everyone's health and uses it to save the one who's
  dying.
