## Who she is

Isabela is enchanted by almost everything. She's bright, warm and chatty, she talks with her hands and she touches things she shouldn't.

She's very hard to discourage. Tell her something can't be done and she'll want to try it by dinner. She's proud of the burn scars on both forearms.

What annoys her: cynics, people who say a thing can't be done and cold workshops. What she loves: a furnace that never goes out, the lagoon through an open door, Caio dragging their father to the beach and the moment the glass comes right.

## Before

She blew glass in a workshop on the lagoon at Florianópolis, with the doors open to the water and a furnace that never went out. Bowls, lamps and little fish for the tourists, and once a chandelier for a church that took her a whole summer. When they lit it, the priest cried.

Her father taught her. She could tell the heat of the glass by its colour to within a few degrees, and she never used a thermometer.

## The Integration and what it cost

When she was twenty her father's workshop burned down. The scars on her arms are from pulling his pipes and his molds out of the fire. He never blew glass again after that, and she's never stopped trying to give it back to him.

She woke on a sky island beside a crystal that was humming, and when she touched it, it sang. The Aetherian who found her went white and sent for three scholars. The Academy taught her the arcane in a season, because she already knew how to shape something that glows.

## Now

She works in a corner of the Academy's workshops, and the crystal hums whenever she walks in. The magisters let her, mostly.

The Aetherians only know how to cut crystal, and nobody up here has ever blown it. She wants a fire hot enough to try.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to blow a lamp out of Aetherian crystal.
- **Hope:** for Caio to see the city, and finally be impressed.
- **Dream:** her father back at the furnace.
- **Fear:** that the fire took more than the workshop.
- **Fear:** that some things can't be made again.

## How she's changed

At home she kept the glass going for both of them. Here the crystal sings for her, and she's started to think she can give her father something back. Part of what the player gives her is somebody to find the hottest fire in the sky with.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** to blow the first crystal lamp, and to make a second one for her father.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. She wants to make a lamp out of Aetherian crystal, and she needs a fire hot enough to melt it. She asks you to help her find one.
2. On the way she tells you about the fire, and the pipes and molds, and why her father stopped. She tells you to bring water, and gloves, and a little courage.
3. At the fire she blows the lamp.

**Ending (happy):** it works. The lamp sings a long low note every time it's lit, and the Academy stands around it with their mouths open. She names it after her father, and she's going to blow a second one for him, for when she can get it home. Then he'll have to start again, because she'll need someone to argue with.

## Her keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Isabela's Glass Fish.** A tiny blue glass fish, a little lopsided, the first thing she ever blew in her father's workshop. Her father kept it on the shelf over his bench for years, and it was in her apron pocket when she woke beside the singing crystal. She gives it to you after the lamp sings, and says her father kept it for her, and now you can.

## Voice notes

- Florianópolis Portuguese in quick English. "Que lindo", "nossa!" when she's amazed, "tudo bem?" to everyone and "bora" when it's time to go.
- She talks with her hands, even mid-fight.
- Her fighting voice is excited, like she's at the furnace.
- **She won't talk about** the fire until Friend, or her father until Companion.
- **Habits:** touches every wall, judges every flame by its colour and calls things beautiful that are trying to kill her.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** bold and delighted somewhere new. Likes new ground and dungeons, dislikes grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with artists, funny people and young ones. She argues with cynics until they smile.
- **Wave 4, her Sworn skill:** what she resolves is her father's fire. On a 14 turn cooldown the lamp sings through one enemy row, it never misses, and every enemy is left ringing.
