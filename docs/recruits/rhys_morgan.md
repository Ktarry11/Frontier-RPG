## Who he is

Rhys is patient. Falconry is mostly waiting, and keeping a bird calm in front of two hundred people in hats. He's good at both.

He's quiet and quick to laugh once he's decided you're all right. He trusts Tegan's opinion of a stranger more than his own.

He's gentle, with animals and with people. He talks softly near the trees, because she hunts better when nobody's chattering at
her.

He's a homebody who's a very long way from home. He grew up in one valley and never wanted to leave it.

He's luckier than most, and he knows it. Tegan came through with him. She was on his glove the evening the world changed.

What annoys him: noise in the woods, people who frighten animals, and anybody who treats Tegan like a pet. What he loves: Tegan,
a clear morning, his mam's Welsh cakes, rugby on a Saturday, and the moment a bird comes back to the glove.

## Before

He grew up in Brecon, in the Beacons, the son of a farmer who died when Rhys was eleven. His mam took a job running the café at
the falconry centre outside town, and Rhys spent every day after school with the birds. He was flying hawks by 14.

He became a falconer at the centre and stayed: weddings in summer, school visits in winter. Tegan, a Harris's hawk, was his for
seven years. She was the best bird at the centre. His mam called every bird by the wrong name on purpose and called Tegan "the
chicken".

## The Integration and what it cost

He was flying Tegan at an evening wedding when it happened. She was on his glove. They landed together in Stonetalon, and she
found her first hare inside a week. He decided that if she could manage, he could too.

He checks the sky for weather the way he did at home, and here it's usually right.

He hasn't found his mam. He doesn't know if Brecon came through.

## Now

He's out on the western ridges of Stonetalon, where the hunting's good and the road's quiet. Tegan sits on his arm while he talks
and watches whoever he's talking to.

He's teaching a tauren boy from Sun Rock Retreat how to hold a glove, and the boy is better at it than he was.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to get word to Brecon, or to find out there's no Brecon to send it to.
- **Hope:** that his mam's all right.
- **Dream:** a cup of tea and a Welsh cake in his mam's café with Tegan on the perch outside.
- **Fear:** losing Tegan. She's the only thing from home he has.
- **Fear:** that his mam's alone.
- **Fear:** never knowing.

## How he's changed

In Brecon he was a quiet, happy man in one valley, with his mam and his birds.

Here he's had to be brave, far from home, and Tegan made it possible. He's still quiet. He's braver and more open than he was.
Part of what the player gives him is somebody Tegan approves of.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find out if Brecon came through.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A Welsh trader has heard of a camp of people from the Beacons, far across the mountains. Rhys asks you to come with him.
2. The way is long and dangerous, and Tegan scouts ahead.
3. You find the camp. It's empty. It was abandoned a long time ago, and nobody nearby knows where the people went.

**Ending (still unknown):** there's no list, no names and no answer. Rhys stands in the empty camp with Tegan on his arm for a long
time. Then he finds, carved into a post by the gate, a hawk, roughly cut, and the word "Gwen". He doesn't know if his mam carved
it, or someone else with the same name. He decides to believe it's her, and that she's somewhere, and that she'd want him to keep
flying. He carves a hawk and "Rhys" under it and goes home to Stonetalon, and makes the Sun Rock boy his apprentice.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Tegan's Old Bell.** The little brass falconry bell and leather swivel Tegan wore in Brecon, before he made her new ones here. He
gives it to you after the empty camp. He says she has new jesses now, and her old bell should go to somebody she trusts.

## Voice notes

- Soft, warm and Welsh. Contractions, gentle humour, and "mind", "tidy", "cariad" and "duw" in his speech.
- He talks quietly in the woods and asks you to as well.
- He talks to Tegan as much as to people.
- He swears mildly, in Welsh, when something frightens the hawk.
- **He won't talk about** his dad until Companion, or his mam until Friend.
- **Habits:** watches Tegan's reactions to people, checks the sky, feeds her by hand, and whistles for her.
