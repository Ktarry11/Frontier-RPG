## Who he is

Callum is relaxed about things that kill people. He's been bitten more often than anyone should be and files all of it
under professional development. His forearms are a map of the learning curve.

He's patient and kind with animals, and he explains them to people constantly. The animal isn't malicious. It's frightened,
and you're enormous.

He's funny, in a laid-back Aussie way. Everything is "no worries" until it very much is a worry, and then he goes quiet and
fast.

He believes the thing that bites back is the thing that trusts you. He's yet to meet an animal that wasn't worth the
conversation.

He's lonely in a way he doesn't notice. He's always been more comfortable with animals than people.

What annoys him: people who kill snakes on sight, people who poke things with sticks, and anyone who calls an animal evil.
What he loves: a calm taipan, a cold beer, his dog, and the moment a frightened animal stops being frightened.

## Before

He grew up in the suburbs of Brisbane, catching lizards in the backyard. His dad was an electrician and his mum ran a
childcare centre. His sister Kirsty went into law and still can't believe he handles snakes for a living.

He started at a reptile sanctuary in the hinterland at 22 and stayed sixteen years. A sanctuary, a hook, and school groups
who all wanted to know the same thing about the same snake. He did the snake shows, raised the hatchlings and took the
bites. He was bitten by a taipan once and nearly died, and he talks about it like a holiday.

He had girlfriends who didn't last, because he was always at the sanctuary and the house was full of tanks. He had a blue
heeler, Biscuit, who went everywhere with him.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was in the snake house, mid-feed, when it happened. He landed in the Barrens with his snake hook in his hand.

He spent eighteen months poisoning himself on purpose, alone, documented badly. What he got out of it was a working
relationship with things that kill people. Same relationship he had before. Better participants.

He misses Biscuit more than he misses most people, and he's embarrassed about that. He hasn't found his family.

## Now

He lives in the Southern Barrens, within reach of the Wailing Caverns, where the serpents are a lot more than serpents and
hold firm views about intruders. He goes in regularly and comes out reliably. The one time he brought something out alive,
the Crossroads made him promise not to do it again. He promised.

He'd like to be bitten by whatever's at the very bottom of the Caverns and survive it, and he knows exactly how that sounds.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to meet whatever's at the bottom of the Caverns and understand it.
- **Hope:** that his family's fine, and that someone's looking after Biscuit.
- **Dream:** a sanctuary of his own here, where people come to learn that the creatures aren't evil.
- **Fear:** an animal he can't understand.
- **Fear:** having to kill something because people were afraid of it.
- **Fear:** that he's better with animals than people because he never tried with people.

## How he's changed

In Queensland he was a laid-back snake man, happiest in the snake house, a little lost everywhere else.

Here he's turned himself into something that can survive any bite, and he's gone further into the dark than almost anyone.
He's lonelier and braver. Part of what the player gives him is a person who's as interesting as a snake.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to reach the bottom of the Caverns and meet what lives there.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He asks you to come all the way down the Wailing Caverns with him, past everything, to the very bottom.
2. At the bottom is a great serpent, old and enormous. It's sick. The dream-sickness in the caves has got into it, and it's
   in agony and half mad.
3. It bites him. He survives it, just. Then he understands what it is.

**Ending (sad):** it can't be healed. It's suffering and it's dangerous, and it'll kill everyone who comes down. Callum asks
you to hold it while he does it, quickly, the way he'd do it for an animal at the sanctuary that couldn't be saved. He sits
with it afterwards for an hour, talking to it, the way he talked to every animal he ever lost. He says that was the most
interesting thing he's ever met, and he's the one who had to kill it. He keeps going back into the Caverns, but he's
quieter about it.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Callum's Snake Hook.** A steel snake hook with a worn grip, the one he used every day at the sanctuary and had in his hand
when he landed. He gives it to you after the bottom of the Caverns. He says he handles things with his own hands now, and
he'd rather you had something for keeping your distance.

## Voice notes

- Easy, cheerful and very Australian. Contractions, "mate", "no worries", "reckon", and understatement about danger.
- He explains animals constantly, accurately and affectionately.
- He goes quiet and fast when something's genuinely dangerous.
- He swears casually and constantly, never at an animal.
- **He won't talk about** the taipan bite until Companion, or Biscuit until Friend.
- **Habits:** picks up anything with scales, checks under rocks, talks to his scorpion, and rolls up his sleeves to show
  people the scars.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** holds the line against anything venomous, cheerfully. Explains every beast you fight. Upset by
  killing animals for no reason, and very upset if the party's cruel to them. Loves caves full of serpents.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with easy-going people, and with Rosa and Bakyt, who also know animals. Lovely with

- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the serpent he had to kill. A skill idea to look at then: once a fight,
  he takes every poison on the party into himself and shrugs it off.
