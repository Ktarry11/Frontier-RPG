## Who he is

Pieter is the most tedious man on the dock, and proud of it. Twenty-eight years of harnesses, exclusion zones and the deeply unglamorous
business of nobody dying today.

He's been shouted at by thousands of men in a hurry and outlasted all of them. He's patient, stubborn and unbudgeable.

He runs the same arithmetic he always has: a precaution costs less than the thing it prevents, every time, on any world. That's why he stands in
front. He's the one wearing the armour.

He's blunt and gruff, with a dry Afrikaans humour. He's kinder than he looks.

He counts. He's pulled nine men out in three years, and he keeps the number because somebody should.

What annoys him: men in a hurry, bosses who say "they chose the job", and people who say accidents happen. What he loves: a well-rigged
harness, a braai, rugby, his wife's stamppot, and a whole shift where nothing happens.

## Before

He grew up in Pretoria, the son of a mine safety officer, and he went into the same line of work. At 23 he met Anneke, a Dutch nurse on a working
holiday, and followed her back to Rotterdam. He spent 28 years in port safety there: a clipboard, a hard hat, and a reputation as the most
tedious man on the dock.

He fought for every rule. Some of the men he saved never knew it. Some of them hated him for it. He didn't mind.

He and Anneke had two daughters, Lotte and Sanne, both grown and in Rotterdam. He carried his father's old groundsman's folding knife every day.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was on the docks, checking a crane's harness, when it happened. He landed in Tanaris, in the heat, with his father's folding knife on his belt.

He adjusted instantly, and he's slightly ashamed of how instantly. His entire profession turned out to apply directly to a world with monsters
in it. His colleagues in Rotterdam would have found it hilarious.

He hasn't found Anneke or the girls.

## Now

He works the goblin dig sites in Northern Tanaris, where men and goblins work deep pits and high scaffolds with no harness, and the Cartel's
position is that they chose the job. Pieter's pulled nine men out in three years.

He wants rules that outlive him. He's written a set for the dig sites and the Cartel foreman has ignored all of them. He submits them again every
season. Four seasons now.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** rules that outlive him.
- **Hope:** that Anneke and the girls are safe.
- **Dream:** a braai in the back garden in Rotterdam, with nothing to worry about.
- **Fear:** the tenth man.
- **Fear:** that nobody will ever listen until someone dies.
- **Fear:** being too slow.

## How he's changed

In Rotterdam he was a tedious, beloved, hated safety man, a husband and a father.

Here he's still that man, with a mace. He's angrier than he was, because the stakes are higher and nobody listens. Part of what the player gives
him is somebody who wears the harness.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to have his rules adopted.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The Cartel is digging a deep new pit in Northern Tanaris, fast, with no shoring and no harnesses. Pieter warns them it'll collapse.
2. It does, during a shift, with workers inside. You go in with him, through the collapsing tunnels, to get them out.
3. You get nine of them out. The tenth is trapped too deep.

**Ending (sad):** the tenth man, a young goblin called Fizzik who used to bring Pieter tea, dies before you can reach him. Pieter stays at the
collapse until the next morning. Then he walks into the Cartel office and puts his rules on the desk for the fifth time. This time the foreman
signs them. Pieter says that's always how it goes: they listen after the tenth. He adds Fizzik's name to the top of the rules, and he makes every
new worker read it before they go into a pit.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Pieter's Father's Knife.** A groundsman's folding knife, the handle worn pale, his father's from the mines outside Pretoria, carried every day in
Rotterdam and on the day he landed. He gives it to you after the rules are signed. He says his father would want it carried by somebody who
listens.

## Voice notes

- Blunt, gruff and dry. Contractions, safety jargon, and Afrikaans: "ja", "nee", "lekker", "jislaaik" when something's alarming.
- He speaks in rules and numbers: "two points of contact", "nine men in three years".
- He tells people off for unsafe things constantly.
- He swears in Afrikaans when someone does something stupid near a drop.
- **He won't talk about** Anneke and the girls until Companion, or the men he didn't save until Friend.
- **Habits:** checks everyone's gear, counts the party, refuses to let anyone climb without care, and keeps a clipboard.
