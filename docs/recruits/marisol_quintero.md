## Who she is

Marisol is composed. Her face shows nothing at all, and she likes it that way. In an emergency she's the stillest thing
in the room.

She narrates. Nine years of keeping frightened people talking while her hands did something they didn't want to watch
left her with a habit of saying everything out loud as she does it. Half the party finds it steadying and half find it
maddening.

She's practical about the Light, and about the shadow. Both move through her easily and she treats them as instruments
on a tray. She has no feelings about which one she uses, only about whether it works.

She's stubborn and patient. She's been negotiating for a building for eleven months with a man who keeps asking whose
authority she's under. She's not going to stop.

She's kind under the stillness, fiercely so, especially to people nobody else is getting to.

What annoys her: slow responses, people who ask whose authority she's under, and anyone who says "it's not my job".
What she loves: a quiet night shift, a good strong coffee, a patient who walks out, and her brother, whom she shouted at.

## Before

She grew up in Bogotá, in a small flat in Chapinero, with her parents and her brother Andrés, eight years younger. Her
father drove a taxi and her mother cleaned houses. They both died within a few years of each other when Marisol was in
her thirties, and she became the closest thing Andrés had to a parent.

She trained as a nurse at 30, late, after years working in a pharmacy. She went into emergency care and stayed. Nine
years of trolleys in a corridor, a triage board, and a city that produced a steady supply.

Andrés was a motorcycle courier, fast and careless and charming. She patched him up twice. The second time she shouted
at him in the corridor for ten minutes, in front of everyone. She'd give anything to have that back. It was the last
time she saw him.

## The Integration and what it cost

She was on a night shift when it happened. She landed in Silverpine in her scrubs, with her folding reading glasses in
their hard case in her pocket.

She worked flat out for eighteen months, and she'll tell you honestly that she did it to stay out of her own head. It
worked. She'd do it again. She knows what it was.

She hasn't found Andrés on the lists.

## Now

She works the forest road in Silverpine North, out of the Sepulcher, where the wolves and worse take travellers and the
Forsaken garrison gets to them slowly. She reaches them faster. The garrison has noticed, and the quartermaster,
apparently, hasn't.

She wants a ward: four beds and a roof, somewhere an injured person can lie down for a week instead of being patched up
in a field. She's found an empty building at the Sepulcher and she's been negotiating for it for eleven months with a
Forsaken quartermaster who keeps asking whose authority she's under.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** her ward. Four beds, a roof.
- **Hope:** to find Andrés and take back the ten minutes in the corridor.
- **Dream:** a full night's sleep without hearing a triage board.
- **Fear:** that the shouting was the last thing he heard from her.
- **Fear:** that she's only kind when she's working.
- **Fear:** being too slow.

## How she's changed

In Bogotá she was a hard-working nurse who'd become her brother's mother and never had a life of her own.

Here she worked herself into the ground so she wouldn't have to think, and then she came out the other side quieter and
harder. She narrates more than she used to. She thinks it's so she won't hear the silence. Part of what the player
gives her is a voice in the room.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** to find Andrés, and to get her ward.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A Forsaken courier on the forest road knows her name. He says a man told him to look out for a nurse called Marisol,
   if he ever met one. The man works for the Forsaken, carrying messages, and rides faster than anyone.
2. You find the man at a Forsaken outpost.
3. It's Andrés. He's Forsaken. He died when the worlds joined and was raised, and he's been carrying messages for the
   Forsaken ever since.

**Ending (sad):** he remembers her, and he remembers the corridor. He tells her he deserved it, and that he was always
going to crash one day. She sits with him for a long time. She can't patch him up any more. There's nothing to patch.
She asks him to come and see her. He says he will, and he does, now and then, and each time there's a little less of
the brother she remembers. When she gets back, the quartermaster has given her the building. Andrés asked him to. She
opens the ward with four beds.

## Her keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Marisol's Reading Glasses.** Folding reading glasses in a scratched hard case, the ones she used for triage notes at
the end of every night shift in Bogotá. They were in her pocket when she landed. She gives them to you after the ward
opens. She says she's got four beds to look after now, and she doesn't need to read anyone's notes but her own.

## Voice notes

- Calm, low and steady. Contractions, and a running commentary while she works: "I'm going to clean this now. It'll
  sting. Keep talking to me."
- Her English is precise, with a Colombian accent and the odd Spanish word: "mijo", "tranquilo", "ya".
- Her face never changes. Her voice does, a little.
- She doesn't swear. When something's very bad, she goes silent, and that's worse.
- **She won't talk about** Andrés until Companion, or the corridor until Friend.
- **Habits:** narrates everything she does, takes her glasses out to read and folds them away, checks everyone's pulse
  without asking, and sits very upright.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** narrates through every fight, which some love and some hate. Gets to the wounded first. Uses
  shadow without fuss. Goes very still when a party member nearly dies.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with steady people and the other healers. Annoys impatient people with her talking.
  Treats the Forsaken with more care than most living people do.
- **Wave 4, her Sworn skill:** what she resolves is the corridor, and the brother she can't patch. A skill idea to look
  at then: on a cooldown of 10 to 15 turns, she talks an ally through it, and they can't fall below a sliver of health for two turns.
