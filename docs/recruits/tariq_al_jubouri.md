## Who he is

Tariq is enormous and gentle. He's softly spoken, slow to anger and generous to a fault, and he doesn't like to hurry anyone, least of all himself.

He believes a gym is where boys learn they're strong without hurting anyone. He'd rather carry something for you than talk about it.

What annoys him: bullies, show-offs who lift with their egos and anyone who rushes a beginner. What he loves: a fan that works, Nadia's kubba, Zainab out-lifting the boys and two biscuits instead of one.

## Before

He lifted for Iraq at two Asian Games, and when he retired he coached boys at a gym in Karrada with a tin roof and a fan that never worked. There was a wall of photographs of his boys.

He gave blood every eight weeks from the day he turned eighteen. The nurse at the blood bank knew him by name, and she always gave him two biscuits instead of one.

## The Integration and what it cost

In 2006 Hamid was in the market at Karrada when the bomb went off. Tariq gave blood that day for six hours, until they sent him home, and he's given it every eight weeks since as if it could still reach him.

He woke on a sky island north of Aurelion and lifted a fallen cart off an Aetherian child before anyone had asked his name. The Ascendancy asked him to join within a week, and he said no very politely. The first time he was cut in a fight, the blood didn't run. It stopped, and it pulled him back up.

## Now

He carries things for the embassy's staff whether they ask or not, and the houses' guards have started training with him in the mornings.

The Ascendancy keeps trying to take his trainees, and he doesn't like it at all.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a gym in Aurelion for anyone at all, with a fan that works.
- **Hope:** someone to give two biscuits to, the way the nurse did.
- **Dream:** Zainab lifting in his gym, beating everyone.
- **Fear:** that the blood never reached Hamid.
- **Fear:** his trainees being turned into soldiers.

## How he's changed

At home he gave his blood to strangers and his strength to boys. Here the blood gives back, and the boys are Aetherians and trolls. Part of what the player gives him is somebody standing beside him, so he doesn't have to carry it alone.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to open the gym, keep the Ascendancy out of it, and tell somebody about Hamid.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The Ascendancy keeps trying to take his trainees. He asks you to stand with him while he opens a gym in Aurelion for anyone at all.
2. On the way he tells you about Hamid, and the six hours at the blood bank. He makes you tea every morning instead of thanking you.
3. On the opening morning the recruiters come to the door, and the two of you stand in it until they leave.

**Ending (happy):** the gym's open, with a fan that works and a sign over the door that says everyone is welcome, and Aetherians, trolls and Earthborn lift together. On the first morning he gives every one of them two biscuits. He tells them about Hamid, and they listen, and nobody hurries him.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Tariq's Donor Card.** A creased blood donor card from Baghdad, its back filled edge to edge with dates eight weeks apart. It was in his lifting belt when he woke. He gives it to you on the gym's first morning, and says there's no more room on it.

## Voice notes

- Baghdadi Arabic under soft English. "Habibi" to people he likes, "inshallah", "yalla" only when it truly can't wait, and "shwaya shwaya", little by little.
- He speaks slowly and never finishes anyone's sentence for them.
- His fighting voice is very low and very calm.
- **He won't talk about** Hamid until Friend, or Nadia and Zainab until Companion.
- **Habits:** carries things for people, offers tea and biscuits, and tells everyone there's time.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** steady at the front, patient with the party and happiest with a full one. Likes quests and a full party, dislikes grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with young people, patient ones and honest ones. He keeps away from bossy types and show-offs.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is Hamid. On a 14 turn cooldown he gives his own blood to the whole party at once, and it gives him strength back.
