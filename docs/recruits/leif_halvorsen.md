## Who he is

Leif spent twenty-five years cutting down trees, and he loves them more than anybody you'll meet. He'll tell you there's
no contradiction in that, and he'll tell you slowly.

He's quiet, dry and patient. He says less than he means and means all of it. He reads a tree the way some people read a
face, where it leans, where it's rotten and where it'll go when it falls.

The woods chose him here, and he can't work out why. The primal power came up out of the ground in Ashenvale the week
he arrived, and the druids told him the forest picked him. He cut down forests for a living, and he thinks it's either a
joke or a sentence.

What annoys him: waste, people who talk in the woods, and anyone who cuts a living tree when there's deadwood lying
about. What he loves: first light in a stand of old trees, black coffee, his daughter's voice, and the creak a tree makes
right before it goes.

## Before

He grew up in Coos Bay, where everybody's dad worked in timber. His grandfather came over from Norway and worked the
mills. Leif went into the woods at twenty-one as a choker setter, then a faller, then a climber who topped spar trees and
pruned the big ones nobody else would go up.

In 2016 his falling partner, Travis, was killed by a widowmaker, a dead branch that came down out of a tree Leif was
cutting. It wasn't anyone's fault. Leif went back to work the next week and hasn't stopped thinking about it since.

He married Dawn at twenty-five. They split at forty, friendly enough. Kaylee fights wildfires now, and he's proud of her
in a way he doesn't have words for.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was up a Douglas fir outside Myrtle Point, sixty feet up with his spurs in, when it happened. He came down out of a
gold tree in Ashenvale three years ago with his saw still on his belt and nothing to cut with it.

He spent two years with the druids and the woodcutters there, learning that the forest would rather he didn't. When the
Conclave sent word round Azshara that they needed timber for an island and somebody who knew wood, he got on the boat.

He misses the sound of a saw. He hasn't started one here.

## Now

He works the Red Hollow in Duskfall Woods, bringing in deadwood for Dawnrest's roof and the Spire's scaffolds, and only
deadwood. The elves wanted him to fell the old red trees for the beams. He took one look and said no, and showed them
what was already lying on the ground instead.

The bears in the Red Hollow leave him alone, mostly. He doesn't push it.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to understand why the woods picked him.
- **Hope:** to hear from Kaylee that she's all right, which he can't, and he says so.
- **Dream:** a morning in an old stand of trees with nothing to do but look at them.
- **Fear:** a widowmaker. Something coming down out of the canopy on somebody standing where he told them to stand.
- **Fear:** that the woods are right to be wary of him.

## How he's changed

In Oregon he was a good logger who never asked whether he should be one. Here the forest answered him back, and he's had
to sit with what that means. Part of what the player gives him is somebody to stand where he tells them, and trust it.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to give something back to the woods.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The oldest trees in the Red Hollow are dying from the inside, and the Conclave wants to burn the stand before the rot
   spreads into the rest of Duskfall.
2. Leif asks for a few days first. He climbs the biggest of them, the way he used to, and he asks you to be his ground
   man, the one who watches the canopy and shouts.
3. Near the top he finds what's eating them, a nest of grubs fat on the old magic leaking up out of the ground, and you
   fight it off the trunk together while he hangs on.

**Ending (happy):** the stand lives, and nobody burns it. Leif climbs down, sits with his back against the old tree's
trunk, and tells you about Travis, all of it, for the first time. He says that's why the woods picked him: he knows what
it costs when one comes down.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Leif's Diameter Tape.** A logger's tape for measuring a trunk's width, in a dented steel case, with TRAVIS scratched
on the back. It was clipped to his belt when he came down out of the gold tree. He gives it to you after the Red Hollow,
and says he doesn't need to measure them anymore.

## Voice notes

- Pacific Northwest, slow and dry. "Yep", "you bet", "pretty much", and long pauses he doesn't apologise for.
- He talks about trees the way other people talk about animals.
- His fighting voice is the same as his normal one, which unsettles people.
- **He won't talk about** Travis until Friend, or Kaylee until Companion.
- **Habits:** looks up before he steps under anything, checks which way a tree leans before he camps beside it, and never
  sits with his back to the woods.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** steady and quiet in a fight, good on new ground, impatient with grinding and noise. He likes the
  wild places and the beasts in them.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with outdoors people, workers and the quiet. He's wary of the loud and the reckless,
  and he has a soft spot for anybody Scandinavian.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the widowmaker. On a 14 turn cooldown he calls a tree down on one enemy
  row, and it lands exactly where he said it would.
