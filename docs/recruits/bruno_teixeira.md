## Who he is

Bruno is cheerful. He's huge, very hard to knock down, and he smiles more than a templar should. Rory says so, and Bruno tells him somebody in the Order
has to.

He fights like the bell's about to go: all in, all the time, and grinning while he does it.

He's loyal. He walked out of the Scarlet Crusade beside Rory Callahan, and he'd follow Rory anywhere.

He's carrying one night he won't talk about unless Rory does first: the night the Crusade burned the transplant camp, and he and Rory walked away.

He's a born coach. He's teaching the Bulwark's recruits to fight with their hands, and he's delighted with every one of them, however bad.

What annoys him: bullies, cheap shots, zealots, and people who quit in the first round. What he loves: a good fight, his mother's feijoada, samba on a
Saturday, his recruits, and the moment a beginner lands a clean punch.

## Before

He grew up in Curitiba, raised by his mother after his father left. At 15 he walked into a gym above a tyre shop and met his coach, Seu Valdir, who made
him a fighter. Twenty-three fights, eighteen wins, in small halls with loud crowds. He was better than his record says.

His mother came to every fight and covered her eyes for all of them. He always looked for her in the crowd before the bell.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was in the gym, wrapping his hands for training, when it happened. He landed in the Plaguelands with a roll of black hand-wrap in his pocket, the one
from his last fight, which he'd won.

He joined the Scarlet Crusade in the first months, because it was the only army winning, and because they took in big men who could fight. They taught
him the Light, and that's the part he kept.

The rest he left on the road out of Hearthglen, the night they burned the transplant camp. He and Rory Callahan walked out together, and the Order of the
Pale Flame started that night. He doesn't talk about it unless Rory does first.

He hasn't found his mother.

## Now

He's in the Order's quarter of the Bulwark, training anyone who'll stand in front of him. He wants the Order to be big enough that nobody ever has to
choose between the Crusade and nothing again.

The roll of black hand-wrap on his belt is from his last fight at home. He won it.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** an Order big enough that nobody has to choose the Crusade.
- **Hope:** that his mother's somewhere, covering her eyes at somebody's fight.
- **Dream:** one more fight in Curitiba, with his mother in the crowd, and her watching for once.
- **Fear:** what he did in the Crusade before he walked away.
- **Fear:** meeting someone he trained, on the other side.
- **Fear:** that the smile is a cover.

## How he's changed

In Curitiba he was a cheerful heavyweight with a loving mother and a coach who believed in him.

Here he joined the wrong side, walked away, and found the Light anyway. He's still cheerful, and there's something under it now. Part of what the player
gives him is somebody who asks about that night and waits.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to grow the Order, and bring people out of the Crusade.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. Word comes that a Scarlet unit is preparing to purge a transplant settlement in the Plaguelands, and the man leading it is Kleber, a young Brazilian
   Bruno trained to box in the Crusade's camp.
2. You and Bruno go to stop it, and Bruno insists on facing Kleber first and asking him to walk away, the way Bruno did.
3. Kleber won't.

**Ending (sad):** they fight, the way Bruno taught him, and Bruno wins, and Kleber dies. The settlement is saved. Bruno sits with Kleber afterwards for a
long time. Then he tells you about the night of the camp, all of it, without waiting for Rory. He says he taught Kleber to fight and the Crusade taught
him the rest, and that he should have gone back for him. The Order grows that winter, by forty Scarlet defectors who heard what Bruno did. He trains
every one of them, and he still smiles, but less.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Bruno's Hand-Wrap.** A roll of black fighter's hand-wrap from his last fight in Curitiba, which he won, tucked in his belt for three years. He gives it to
you after Kleber. He says he's done fighting for wins. He fights for the Order now, and you should have the wrap from the last one he won for himself.

## Voice notes

- Big, warm and cheerful. Contractions, boxing talk, and Brazilian Portuguese: "valeu", "bora" (let's go), "meu irmão", "tá bom".
- He coaches everyone, all the time.
- He talks about Rory often, fondly.
- He swears cheerfully in Portuguese, and never in front of recruits.
- **He won't talk about** the night of the camp until the quest, unless Rory does, or his time in the Crusade until Friend.
- **Habits:** shadowboxes, wraps his hands before fights, coaches beginners, and grins at danger.
