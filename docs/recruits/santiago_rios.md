## Who he is

Santiago is a perfectionist about line work, in a way that makes the Ashclaim's smiths nervous. Every rune's a line you can't take back, he says, same as a
tattoo, just a lot louder.

He's confident and bright, with a big grin and a lot of charm. He knows he's good, and he's usually right.

He's an artist first. He wants to design a rune nobody's ever cut before, and he's filled three notebooks trying.

He's responsible in a way his swagger hides. He was putting his little brother through school, and he never once complained about it.

He's gentle with nervous people. Nine years of tattooing first-timers taught him to talk people through pain.

What annoys him: sloppy lines, people who rush art, and smiths who don't respect a design. What he loves: a perfect line, bandeja paisa, reggaeton in the
shop, his mother's laugh, and the moment a client sees their tattoo for the first time.

## Before

He grew up in Medellín, the eldest son of a bus driver who died when Santiago was fifteen, and Gloria, who sold arepas from a cart. He started drawing on
everything, then on people. He opened a shop in Laureles with his cousin at 22, and it had a waiting list that ran for months: three hundred clients' worth
of designs in a folder.

He put his little brother Julián, ten years younger, through school. Julián was the clever one, at a good school in Bogotá. Santiago was proud of him
in a way he never said.

His mother never approved of the tattoos, and had a small one done anyway, by him: a tiny sun on her wrist.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was at his station, mid-tattoo, when it happened. He came through with his tattoo machine's grip in his pocket.

Runes started showing up in his work within the month. The first rune he cut glowed, and he spent a week trying to work out what he'd done right. He
worked it out, and his arms are proof.

He hasn't found his mother or Julián. Julián was in Bogotá that day, a long way from Medellín.

## Now

He's on the Ashen Reach, near the works, cutting runes into anything that'll hold still, and into his own arms. They burn when he fights.

The tattoo machine grip on his hip is from his station at the shop, and he still cleans it every night.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a rune nobody's ever cut before.
- **Hope:** to find Julián and his mother.
- **Dream:** his brother graduating, and him in the crowd, crying and pretending he isn't.
- **Fear:** a line he can't take back, cut wrong.
- **Fear:** that Julián is alone, somewhere far from anyone who knows him.
- **Fear:** running out of skin.

## How he's changed

In Medellín he was a confident, talented tattoo artist and a quiet, devoted big brother.

Here his art has become power, and he's made it beautiful. He's still confident, and lonelier. Part of what the player gives him is somebody to show his
designs to.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to design a new rune, and to find his brother.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He's close to a new rune, a rune of finding, that he thinks could point the way to someone you've lost. He needs a rare stone from the Ashen Reach's
   worst ground to cut it into.
2. You get the stone with him, and he cuts the rune: one perfect line after another, for a whole night.
3. It works. It glows and points. He tries it for Julián.

**Ending (still unknown):** the rune points far away, off the edge of every map anyone has, and then it fades. Julián is out there, somewhere, but nowhere
anybody can reach. Santiago doesn't know if that means alive. He decides it does. He cuts the rune of finding into his own forearm, a new line he can't take
back, so it'll glow if he ever gets close. He gives the design to the Ashclaim's smiths, so other people can use it to look for the people they've lost.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Santiago's Machine Grip.** The black and steel grip from the tattoo machine at his station in Laureles, cleaned every night for three years. He gives it to
you after the rune of finding. He says he cuts with steel and light now, and you should have the thing that drew his first lines.

## Voice notes

- Confident, quick and charming. Contractions, art talk, and paisa Spanish: "parce" (mate), "qué más", "bacano", "pues".
- He talks about runes like tattoos: lines, shading, flow.
- He talks nervous people through pain, gently.
- He swears cheerfully in Spanish, often.
- **He won't talk about** his father until Companion, or Julián until Friend.
- **Habits:** cleans the machine grip, sketches designs, traces runes on his arms, and critiques everyone's tattoos.
