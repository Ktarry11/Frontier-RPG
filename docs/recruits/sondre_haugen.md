## Who he is

Sondre is good-natured. He was 24 and had never had anything bad happen to him, and you can still see the boy he was:
easy, open, quick to smile, good with teenagers and boats and weather.

He's frightened of himself. The sky here started answering when he spoke to it, and the first time it went wrong it
went very wrong. He's polite about being afraid. You can still see it.

He's careful with everyone. He keeps a little distance, because he thinks distance is safer for other people. He's
first awake every morning and he's made the fire before anyone else is up, and he offers that as proof he's fine.

He's bossy in exactly one way: if he says get down, you get down. Teaching sailing taught him that the asking is the
part that kills people.

He's funny when he forgets to be careful, in a dry Norwegian way. He likes long walks, cold water, and being
outdoors in weather that would make other people go in.

What annoys him: people who don't listen in an emergency, people who show off with magic, and being treated like he's
breakable. What he loves: wind on water, a good knot, mornings, and teaching someone to do something properly.

## Before

He grew up in Bergen, where it rains most days of the year and nobody minds. His father was a ferry captain and his
mother a dentist. His sister Ingrid is four years younger and much braver than him, he says.

He learned to sail at seven and never stopped. At 20 he started teaching sailing to teenagers on the Hardangerfjord
every summer, and he studied marine biology in Bergen in the winters. He was good at it. Teenagers listened to him
because he never shouted and he was never frightened.

He was seeing a boy from Voss that last summer, Jonas, who worked in the café by the boathouse. It wasn't serious yet.
They'd talked about him coming to Bergen in the autumn.

The season ended in August. He expected to do a fifth summer.

## The Integration and what it cost

He landed in Tirisfal in a cold grey drizzle, and he told everyone afterwards that at least the weather felt like
home. He had his dinghy shackle key on a lanyard round his neck. It's the little key for undoing the shackles on a
sail, and he'd worn it every day for four summers.

He'll say the first year was fine. Anybody who was there will tell you it wasn't.

In the second year the wind did something he hadn't asked for, and a barn went over with people inside. Nobody died. A
girl of about fifteen broke her leg, and he carried her out himself. He hasn't been the same since. He's never been able
to explain why, when nobody died.

He describes his family in the present tense, deliberately, every time, like a man holding a door open with his foot.
He doesn't talk about Jonas at all.

## Now

He lives on the high open ground near the Scarlet Crypt, where he can see weather coming for an hour before it
arrives. He's started going out alone to practise somewhere there's nothing to break. The people who care about him
think that's the worst possible way to handle it, and they're probably right.

He sleeps badly.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** control. Not power. He has plenty of power, and that's the whole problem.
- **Hope:** that his family is together in Bergen, wherever Bergen is now.
- **Dream:** to teach sailing again, on any water at all.
- **Dream:** to go a whole day without being afraid of his own hands.
- **Fear:** hurting someone he loves with something he didn't mean to do.
- **Fear:** that nobody will ever stand close to him again once they've seen what he can do.
- **Fear:** having to stop using the present tense.

## How he's changed

In Norway he was easy-going and lucky, and he knew it. He'd never been tested.

Here he's been tested every day, and he's become careful, quiet and very kind. He's braver than he thinks, because he
keeps going out there. Part of what the player gives him is somebody who stands next to him in a storm and doesn't step
back.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find out if his family made it, and to stop being afraid of what he is.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A trader tells him there's a settlement of Norwegians up north, on a cold coast, and that some of them are from
   Bergen. It's a long, dangerous road. He goes.
2. On the way a storm comes, a real one, and you're caught in the open with a caravan. He has to use everything he's
   got, without any distance, to keep the wind off everybody.
3. He does it, and nobody's hurt. You reach the settlement.

**Ending (sad):** the settlement keeps a book of everyone from Bergen who came through and everyone who didn't make it.
His parents and Ingrid are in the second part. They landed together, in a bad place, in the first week. He sits with the
book for a long time. The next morning he talks about them in the past tense for the first time. On the road home he
isn't afraid of the wind any more. He says he held off a storm for a whole caravan, so he can probably manage a barn.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Sondre's Shackle Key.** The little dinghy shackle key he wore on a lanyard every day for four summers on the
Hardangerfjord, and every day since. It's worn smooth. He gives it to you after Bergen. He says he'll get another one
when he finds a boat.

## Voice notes

- Polite, calm and plain. Contractions, short sentences in a crisis, longer ones when he's relaxed.
- His English is very good and a little formal, with Norwegian rhythms.
- He gives instructions like a sailing instructor: once, clearly, and early.
- He talks about his family in the present tense until the quest.
- He doesn't swear. When he's frightened he goes very quiet.
- **He won't talk about** the barn until Companion, or Jonas until Friend.
- **Habits:** watches the sky, holds the shackle key when he's nervous, makes the fire every morning, and steps back
  from people when his hands crackle.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** shouts "get down" before big hits and is usually right. Holds back in tight spaces, because
  he's frightened of hitting the party, and opens up in the open. Shaken by friendly casualties far more than anyone
  else. First up every morning.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with calm, outdoorsy and kind people. Feels protective of anyone younger. Uneasy
  around reckless casters. Quiet people suit him and he doesn't mind silence.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the fear of his own power. A skill idea to look at then: once a fight,
  he throws everything at the enemies without holding back, and none of it can touch the party.
