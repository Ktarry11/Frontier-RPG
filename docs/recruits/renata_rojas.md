## Who she is

Renata falls off things for a living, and she always lands. She's fearless, quick and funny, and she'll do any fall twice if the first take wasn't clean.

She has a professional's respect for safety. She checks everyone's straps every time and then jumps first. She doesn't like being told what she can't do, and she doesn't like cynics.

What annoys her: skipped checks, bossy directors and anyone who says it can't be done. What she loves: a clean take, a crash mat, the moment just before the jump and a crowd that doesn't know it's her.

## Before

She lived in a rooftop flat in Roma Norte with a crash mat in the living room. For nine years she doubled for actresses who didn't want to get hurt, off buildings, out of cars, down stairs and through windows made of sugar glass. She did a high fall off a hotel roof for a telenovela finale, and the director made her do it twice because the first one was too clean.

Her mother refused to watch any of her films. Her brother told her later that their mother watched them all, alone at night, with the sound off, covering her eyes.

## The Integration and what it cost

On a car stunt in 2021, a rigger skipped his checks because they were behind schedule, and Beto went through a windscreen that should have been sugar glass. He lived, but he can't work anymore. She's checked everybody's straps ever since.

She woke on a sky island right on the edge and nearly went over before she'd opened her eyes. The Aetherians found her climbing their skiff rigging for fun and wrote her down as a ward who kept escaping, which wasn't fair, because she always came back.

## Now

She runs messages between the islands faster than the skiffs, and she never takes the bridges. She walks the rims where the falls pour off as if they were pavement.

There's a drop off the edge of the Expanse into the cloud, and she's been looking at it for months.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to dive off the edge of the Expanse into the cloud and come up somewhere else, on purpose.
- **Hope:** for somebody to watch it, and she knows that's the hard part.
- **Dream:** Beto on set again, even just to shout at her.
- **Fear:** a skipped check.
- **Fear:** that nobody's ever watched her fall for her own sake.

## How she's changed

At home every fall was for someone else's face. Here the falls are hers, and there's nobody to double for. Part of what the player gives her is somebody watching who isn't being paid to.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** to dive the edge on purpose, with somebody watching who knows what they're looking at.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. She wants to dive off the edge of the Expanse into the cloud and come up somewhere else. She asks you to be her eyes.
2. Before the jump she tells you about Beto and the windscreen. You check her straps first, and she tells you to double check them.
3. She stands on the edge, counts down from three and dives.

**Ending (happy):** she comes up through the cloud exactly where she said, screaming the good kind of scream the whole way. It's the first time anybody's watched her fall who wasn't being paid to. She writes Beto a letter about it that she can't send, and she keeps it until she can.

## Her keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Renata's Red Bandana.** A red bandana, faded at the folds, that she tied round her wrist before every fall for nine years. She gives it to you after the dive, and says you're the first person who watched her jump for free.

## Voice notes

- Chilanga Spanish, fast and funny. "Güey" to people she likes, "qué padre" when something's great, "aguas" for a warning and "¡ándale!" when she's going.
- She counts down from three before anything risky.
- Her fighting voice is a stunt coordinator's, short calls and a laugh.
- **She won't talk about** Beto until Friend, or her mother until Companion.
- **Habits:** checks everybody's straps, stands on edges and does everything twice if it wasn't clean.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** bold in a fight and first through any gap. Likes new ground and bosses, dislikes grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with sporty, funny and honest people. She clashes with bossy types and cynics, and dares them to prove it.
- **Wave 4, her Sworn skill:** what she resolves is Beto's windscreen. On a 14 turn cooldown she checks it twice and then goes, and she lands exactly where she said on whatever moves next.
