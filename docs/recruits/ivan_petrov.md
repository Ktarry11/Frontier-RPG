## Who he is

Ivan is a man of routine. Eleven years of the same route, the same four stops in the dark every winter morning, and he
loved it. He likes things on time, in order, and where they're supposed to be.

He's careful in a way nobody expects from a man with a two-handed axe. Eleven years of a heavy thing on rails with other
people's children stepping off the kerb. He never hit one. He uses his weapon the same way: slow start, committed middle,
and knowing exactly how long it takes to stop.

He's blunt and grumpy and very funny. He complains about everything in a deadpan voice, and he means about half of it.
He's said more than once that this world has very low standards.

He's a family man, all the way through. He talks about his girls all the time, in the present tense.

He gets things done that nobody asked him to. He started directing Orgrimmar's traffic at the worst hour, unasked, and
the orcs started doing what he said.

What annoys him: lateness, chaos, people who don't look before they step out, and anyone who stands behind him in a
fight. What he loves: a timetable, strong black tea, a good hockey game, and his wife and daughters.

## Before

He grew up in Novosibirsk, in a concrete block on the edge of the city. His father worked in a factory and his mother
was a nurse. He did his military service, worked in a warehouse, and at 27 got a job driving trams. He found that he
loved it.

Eleven years on Route 13, through the dark Siberian mornings. He knew every stop, every regular, every pothole. He never
had an accident. He was proud of that the way other men are proud of their cars.

He married Natasha, a pharmacist, at 26. Sonya was born when he was 29 and Katya at 32. He drove the morning route so
he could be home for dinner. On Sundays he took the girls skating.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was on the morning route, between the third and fourth stops, when it happened. He landed in Durotar in his driver's
jacket, with the key to his tram cab still in his pocket.

He expected to be useless, a tram driver in a world of warriors. What he found was that nobody here knows how to commit
to a movement and then stop it, and it's apparently a rare skill.

He hasn't found Natasha or the girls on any list. He talks about them in the present tense, every day.

## Now

He lives in Orgrimmar, where the traffic's worse than anything Novosibirsk ever managed and nobody's in charge of it. He's
started directing it at the worst hour, and the orcs do what he says now.

He goes down into Ragefire Chasm under the city, because the cultists down there keep dragging people off the streets
above, and somebody has to go and get them back.

He wants a route. He's asked three separate caravan masters whether anybody runs a fixed schedule, and all three laughed.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a route. A fixed schedule, on time, every time.
- **Hope:** to find Natasha, Sonya and Katya.
- **Dream:** a Sunday on the ice with his girls.
- **Fear:** hitting someone. It's never happened. It's the thing he thinks about most.
- **Fear:** that his family's alive and he's not with them.
- **Fear:** chaos winning.

## How he's changed

In Novosibirsk he was a happy, ordinary man with a route, a wife and two girls.

Here he's had to become a fighter, and he's good at it, and he doesn't much like it. He's become the man who brings order
wherever he goes, because it's the only way he can stand being away from them. Part of what the player gives him is a
schedule: an outing, a return, an outing.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to run a route.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He talks a caravan master into letting him run a fixed schedule between Orgrimmar and the Crossroads, on time, for one
   month. The caravan master bets him it'll fail in a week.
2. The route keeps getting hit: raiders, beasts, a bridge that's out. You help him clear the road and fix the weak points.
3. On the last run of the month, a Russian woman with two girls is waiting at the Crossroads stop.

**Ending (happy):** it's Natasha, Sonya and Katya. They've been living at a camp in the Barrens and heard about a big
Russian running the Orgrimmar road on time. The girls are taller. Ivan stops the caravan exactly at the marker, gets
down, and doesn't say anything at all for a long time. He takes them home to Orgrimmar. The caravan master pays the bet,
and the orcs call it Ivan's road. He runs it every week, and he's never late.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Ivan's Cab Key.** The heavy brass key to the driver's cab of his tram on Route 13, still in his pocket when he landed.
He turned it in an imaginary lock before every run of his route here. He gives it to you after the girls come home. He
says he's got a proper route now and he doesn't need to pretend.

## Voice notes

- Gruff, short and deadpan. Contractions, complaints, and very dry jokes.
- His English is blunt and Russian-flavoured, with dropped words when he's tired. He says "normal" when he means fine.
- He gives instructions like traffic directions: stop, go, wait, left.
- He talks about his family in the present tense.
- He swears in Russian, often and with great feeling.
- **He won't talk about** the fear of hitting someone until Companion, or the girls' birthdays until Friend.
- **Habits:** checks the time by the sun, stands where he can see everyone, counts the party before moving, and turns an
  imaginary key before every fight.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** wants everyone in position before a pull, and grumbles when they aren't. Hits like a falling
  tram and stops exactly when he means to. Hates lateness and loves a fixed plan. Complains constantly and never quits.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with practical, punctual people, and with fathers. Clashes with chaotic, careless people.
  A natural grumpy friend for Dan.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the fear of hitting someone. A skill idea to look at then: once a fight,
  a huge two-handed strike that can't hit an ally even in a crowd, and that stops dead on the enemy.
