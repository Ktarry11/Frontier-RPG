## Who she is

Clara reads trees. Give her a core the width of a pencil and she'll tell you every dry summer, every fire and every bad winter the tree lived through, four hundred years back.

She's precise, curious and stubborn about evidence. She talks very fast when she's excited, which is most of the time, and she apologises for it and keeps going.

What annoys her: guesses dressed up as facts, and anyone who carves their name into bark. What she loves: a clean core, a cold morning in the forest, her bicycle and the moment a date comes out right.

## Before

She had a tiny lab under the forestry building with a wall of cores in labelled straws. She rode her bicycle up into the Black Forest every weekend and came back with mud to her knees.

She once dated a beam in a farmhouse to a winter three hundred years gone, and the farmer cried. She's never been prouder of anything.

## The Integration and what it cost

Her grandmother died during a field season in Romania, and Clara didn't go home for the funeral because the cores wouldn't wait. She's told herself for years it was the right decision, and she doesn't believe it anymore. Her grandmother used to bring her pinecones when she was small and ask her how old they were.

She came through on the ground with her increment borer in her bag and climbed the World Tree on purpose, because it's the biggest tree anyone has ever seen. It has no rings at all, and it's driving her mad.

## Now

She sits on the moss at Mossroof with her notebook open and her borer in her lap. She patches up climbers between measurements, and she asks every one of them how high they've been.

The first time she put her hand on the bark to steady herself, it warmed under her, and a scratch on her wrist closed. The grovekeepers say the tree likes her, and she's trying very hard to treat that as data.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to know how old the tree is, properly, with evidence.
- **Hope:** to tell her old department, just to see their faces.
- **Dream:** a lab of her own on the tree, with a wall of cores and a window.
- **Fear:** that some questions don't have answers.
- **Fear:** that she's still choosing the work over the people.

## How she's changed

At home she could hide in the work. Here the tree heals people through her, and that's harder to hide behind. Part of what the player gives her is somebody who goes into the dark with her and doesn't ask her to stop measuring.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** to read the tree's oldest wound, and to put her grandmother where she belongs.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. She's worked out that the tree only lays a ring where something's hurt it. The oldest wound is in a fallen limb deep in the trunk, and she asks you to take her down to it.
2. On the way she tells you about Romania, and the funeral she missed. She asks you not to let her stop to measure things, and then she stops to measure things.
3. At the wound she reads it, every ring, all the way back to the first one.

**Ending (happy):** she counts every wound the tree ever took, and the first one is older than any story the trolls tell. She writes it out by hand and reads it aloud at Mossroof, and the elders cry. She writes her grandmother's name at the top of the page, where a dedication goes.

## Her keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Clara's Increment Borer.** A slim steel tree-core drill in a worn canvas sleeve, its handle wrapped in tape. It's the only reason anyone at Mossroof believed she was a scientist. She gives it to you after the reading, and says she's counted the only rings that matter.

## Voice notes

- German English, quick and exact. "Genau" when something's right, "Moment" when she's thinking, and "ach" when it isn't.
- She apologises for talking and doesn't stop.
- Her fighting voice is very precise, like she's reading numbers.
- **She won't talk about** her grandmother until Companion.
- **Habits:** touches bark, writes in her notebook mid-sentence and asks everyone how high they've climbed.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** careful and curious, and happiest somewhere new. Likes new ground and quests, dislikes grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with scholars, honest people and patient ones. She clashes with showoffs and reckless people, and corrects them politely.
- **Wave 4, her Sworn skill:** what she resolves is the funeral she missed. On a 14 turn cooldown the tree answers her at last, and the whole party is healed.
