## Who he is

Federico believes a promise is a contract and a contract is sacred. He's never once broken his word, and he reads everything twice.

He's formal, patient and utterly reliable, with a dry wit that sneaks up on people. He doesn't raise his voice, because he's never needed to.

What annoys him: a promise made carelessly, sarcasm aimed at someone who can't answer back, and anybody who signs without reading. What he loves: a well drafted clause, piano scales through a wall, a good mate in the morning and his grandson's inky hands.

## Before

He kept an office with green shutters on Calle Sarandí. People came to him to sign the biggest papers of their lives, houses and marriages and wills, and he read every word aloud before anyone signed, because they deserved to know what they were promising.

Graciela taught piano in the next room. He did his best work to the sound of children playing scales badly. On Saturdays Bautista played with his seal and stamped his own hand.

## The Integration and what it cost

He keeps a list of the promises he made at home and couldn't keep. The worst is one he made to Martín, that he'd retire and spend his Saturdays with Bautista. He kept saying next year.

He came through on the ground with his notary's seal in his pocket and climbed the tree with a party that needed someone to hold the line. The first time he promised to keep a stranger alive, the light came up through his arm, and he kept that promise. The templars of Heartwatch gave him a shield.

## Now

He stands at Heartwatch's gate with his shield and his seal. Every climber who leaves promises to come back, and he writes their names in a ledger and crosses them off when they do.

He still stamps the oaths the climbers swear, and nobody's told him it doesn't count here. The trolls and the climbers have never had a proper agreement, and it worries him more than the monsters do.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to write the tree's first proper contract and have both sides read it aloud.
- **Hope:** to work through his list of broken promises, one line at a time.
- **Dream:** a Saturday with Bautista, any Saturday at all.
- **Fear:** that next year never comes.
- **Fear:** a promise he makes here that he can't keep.

## How he's changed

At home a promise lived on paper. Here it comes up through his arm as light, and he's had to accept that it was never really the paper. Part of what the player gives him is a witness who keeps both sides honest.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to write the tree's first contract, and to start keeping the promise about Saturdays in whatever way he can.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The trolls and the climbers are close to blood. He asks you to help bring both sides to the table.
2. On the way he tells you about his list, and about Martín and the Saturdays. He asks you to stand witness.
3. At the table both sides read every clause aloud, and then they sign.

**Ending (happy):** he stamps the contract with his old seal, which nobody in the world will ever accept, and it's the best work he's ever done. That night he crosses the first line off his list. It isn't the one about Saturdays, but he starts writing Martín a letter about it anyway.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Federico's Notary Seal.** A brass notary's seal on a short chain, its die worn soft by nineteen years of stamping. Nothing on this world will accept its mark. He gives it to you after the signing, and says you witnessed the best work it ever did.

## Voice notes

- Rioplatense Spanish, formal and exact. "Usted" until Friend, "che" once he relaxes, and "bárbaro" for anything that went well.
- He reads things aloud, signs and menus and warnings.
- His fighting voice is level and slow, like he's reading a clause.
- **He won't talk about** the Saturdays until Friend, or Graciela until Companion.
- **Habits:** writes names in the ledger, checks that everyone understood the plan and keeps every word he gives.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** steady at the front and careful with every plan. Likes quests and dungeons, dislikes abandoned quests and grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with honest, patient and formal people. He clashes with reckless and sarcastic ones, very politely.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the promise about Saturdays. On a 15 turn cooldown he gives the party his word, and for a few turns nobody falls and nothing stops them.
