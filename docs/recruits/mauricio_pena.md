## Who he is

Mauricio is at ease with death. He's gentle with the grieving, funny with everyone else and an incorrigible host.

He's warm and unflappable and quietly funny, and nothing frightens him, including the dead, who still listen to him. He answers every question at length, with diagrams.

What annoys him: cynics, people who rush a goodbye and a cheap coffin. What he loves: marigolds, a full table, Lupita singing, being teased by his daughters and a goodbye done properly.

## Before

His family has buried Guadalajara for three generations, from a funeral home with blue tiles on the front. He washed the hearse every morning himself. Every Day of the Dead he built the biggest altar in the barrio, with marigolds from the door all the way to the street, and he walked the whole barrio with a cart of marigolds and gave them away.

Lupita sang at the services, and people chose them for her voice. Not one of his daughters would touch the business, and he's glad.

## The Integration and what it cost

When his father died, Mauricio ran the funeral himself, every detail, and he never once stopped to grieve him. He was too busy being good at it.

He woke on a cloud above the World Tree, decided he'd died and was very pleased with the arrangements. Then a man with crystal on his face asked him to fill in a form. The Aetherians wrote him down as a ward, and the first time something died near him, he felt it go and asked it politely to stay a while, and it did.

## Now

He keeps the peace among the Earthborn at the Annex. He's the one they go to when somebody's grieving, and the one the Aetherians go to when they want to know why.

The Earthborn have nowhere to put their dead and nothing to remember them by, and he's asked the survey's gardeners to find him something orange.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to build a proper altar for everyone who came through and everyone they left behind.
- **Hope:** marigolds, or anything orange enough.
- **Dream:** dinner with Lupita and the girls, and being teased about all of it.
- **Fear:** that he's only good at grief when it's someone else's.
- **Fear:** that his father never got a son at his funeral, only a director.

## How he's changed

At home death was the family's work. Here it's everyone's, and the Earthborn have nobody else to do it for them. Part of what the player gives him is somebody who sees through the host to the son.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to build the altar, and to put his father on it at last.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The Earthborn have nowhere to remember their dead. He asks you to help him gather what a proper altar needs, starting with something orange.
2. On the way he tells you about his father's funeral, and how good he was at it. You tell him he'll put his father on it too, and he says you see right through him.
3. On the night every Earthborn at the Annex brings something to put on the altar.

**Ending (happy):** the altar's covered in photos, a ring, a ticket stub and a child's drawing. At the end he puts his father's picture in the middle and cries in front of everyone, twenty years late. The Aetherians stand at the back and take notes, and he lets them.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Mauricio's Paper Marigold.** A marigold folded from orange paper, the kind he made by the hundred every November. It was pinned to his collar the morning he woke on the cloud. He gives it to you after the altar night, and says it belongs with somebody who helped him remember gently.

## Voice notes

- Tapatío Spanish, warm and courtly. "Ay", "mi amigo", "a sus órdenes" when he meets you and "ni modo" when something can't be helped.
- He describes things as if he's arranging them.
- His fighting voice is calm and polite, as if he's showing someone to their seat.
- **He won't talk about** his father until Friend, or Lupita and the girls until Companion.
- **Habits:** feeds everyone, straightens collars and asks the dead to wait.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** unhurried in a fight and gentle with anyone hurt. Likes the undead and quests, dislikes grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with sentimental people, hosts and honest ones. He disagrees with cynics, gently and at length.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the grief he never let himself have. On a 14 turn cooldown he asks the dead to come, every enemy is struck, and the party is healed by what it takes.
