## Who she is

Isla is still. She's the stillest person you'll ever meet with a weapon in her hands. Stalking deer and shooting biathlon both come down to a slow heart and
a steady breath.

She's sharp and level, and quietly kind once the shooting stops. She doesn't waste words, or shots.

She stayed well clear of the Crusade. She joined the Order of the Pale Flame after Rory Callahan walked out of it, because she'd watched the Order do the
work cleanly.

She's a daddy's girl, in a fierce, quiet way. Her dad raised her alone in a cottage above the glen, and taught her to shoot, and he was going to watch her
at the World Cup.

What annoys her: waste, carelessness with weapons, fanatics, and noise in the hills. What she loves: a still dawn in the glen, her dad's laugh, a clean shot,
whisky by the fire, and the moment the deer lifts its head.

## Before

She grew up above Glen Affric, the only child of the estate's gamekeeper, Alasdair, after her mum died when she was twelve. He taught her to stalk and shoot,
and gave her a rifle at fourteen. She took up biathlon at university, skiing and shooting, and she was good, very good.

She worked as a stalker on the estate and trained every spare hour. The month before the Integration she qualified for the World Cup, and her dad booked the
time off to come and watch. He'd never been abroad.

## The Integration and what it cost

She was on the hill at dawn, stalking a stag, when it happened. She landed in the Plaguelands with her biathlon rifle's sling still over her shoulder, and no
rifle.

She went a year on her own, hunting what needed hunting in the Plaguelands. The Order was the first group of people she met here who didn't waste a shot.

She hasn't found her dad. She'd like to hear him laugh again. She keeps that one to herself.

## Now

She's at Light's Hope Chapel with the Order's camp, keeping watch over the eastern road. She wants Stratholme cleared, properly, and she'll be there when it
is.

The steel buckle on her crossbow strap came off her biathlon rifle sling, and she's never thought about replacing it.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to find her dad.
- **Hope:** Stratholme cleared.
- **Dream:** her dad in the stands at the World Cup, shouting louder than anyone.
- **Fear:** that he's alone somewhere, getting old, with nobody.
- **Fear:** missing when it matters.
- **Fear:** that she'll never hear him laugh again.

## How she's changed

In the Highlands she was a quiet stalker and a rising athlete, her dad's pride.

Here she's a hunter of the dead, steadier and harder, and lonelier. Part of what the player gives her is someone she can be quiet with.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** to find her dad.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A Highland voice in a camp of Scottish transplants far to the east of the Plaguelands, an old gamekeeper who tells terrible jokes, someone says. Isla
   packs that night.
2. The road there goes past Stratholme, through the worst of the Scourge. You clear it with her, shot by shot.
3. You reach the camp. He's there.

**Ending (sad):** her dad is alive, and old, and very ill. He's been dying slowly of something the camp's healers can't treat, and he's been holding on. He
sees her and laughs, the big laugh she's wanted to hear for three years, and says he knew she'd come, because she never misses. She stays with him for nine
days. He tells her terrible jokes the whole time. He dies on the tenth morning, peacefully, with her holding his hand. She buries him on a hill above the
camp that looks a bit like the glen. She goes back to Light's Hope and keeps watch on the eastern road, and sometimes she laughs at one of his jokes, out
loud, by herself.

## Her keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Isla's Sling Buckle.** The worn steel buckle from her biathlon rifle sling, moved onto her crossbow strap and never replaced. Her dad bought her the sling
when she qualified for Scotland. She gives it to you after the hill above the camp. She says he'd want it carried by someone who doesn't miss either.

## Voice notes

- Level, dry and Highland. Contractions, short sentences, and Scots: "aye", "wee", "och", "dinnae fash" (don't worry).
- She talks about everything in terms of breath and stillness.
- She's quietly kind after a fight, never during.
- She swears precisely, in Scots, when someone wastes a shot.
- **She won't talk about** her dad until Companion, or her mum until Friend.
- **Habits:** breathes slowly before every shot, watches the horizon, touches the buckle, and laughs at bad jokes she remembers.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** still and deadly at range, never wastes a shot. Calm in a wipe. Cold toward fanatics and the Scourge. Quietly kind to the wounded
  after.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with calm, competent people, especially Ratih and Rhys. Close to Bruno and Joaquín in the Order. Tolerates loud people
