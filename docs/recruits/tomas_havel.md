## Who he is

Tomas moves heavy things through small gaps. For eighteen years he hung weight over the heads of people who never once
looked up, and his job was leverage, timing, and never being the reason something fell. He swings a two-handed sword the
same way.

He's cheerful and a bit of a show-off. He arrived forty feet up, which should have killed him, and he's never once managed
to tell that story without it coming out as a brag. He's given up fixing the delivery and just gets it over with early.

He hates shouting. He's worked with people who shout during, and he didn't enjoy any of them. Plan first, then work.

He can't walk past bad rigging. People here build fast and tie off badly, and he physically can't leave it alone. He
fixes it whether anyone asked or not.

He's easy-going about most things and stubborn about safety. He's never once been the reason something fell, and he
intends to keep it that way.

What annoys him: shouting, bad knots, and people who look up only after it's too late. What he loves: a good knot, a
clean lift, a beer after the get-out, and things that stay up.

## Before

He grew up in Brno, the son of a welder and a nurse. He moved to Prague at 20 and started working as a stagehand, then
climbed into the grid and never came down. Eighteen years of rock tours, opera houses and arenas, up at four in the
morning, hanging lights and speakers and scenery.

He hurt his shoulder on a truss in 2014 and worked through it because the show was that night. It still tells him when the
weather's turning, and he still works through it.

He's never settled with anyone. There was a lighting tech, Dominik, who asked him first and had a yes inside the week. It
lasted two years on the road and ended when Dominik wanted a house and Tomas wanted the next tour. Later there was a woman
in Prague, Klára, a stage manager, whom he hadn't asked yet. The crew ran a book on which way he'd go. He refused to settle
it, mostly because he liked watching the money change hands.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was up in the grid of a Prague concert hall, at four in the morning, when it happened. He arrived forty feet up, in the
air over Fenris Isle, and landed in a tree. He had a steel carabiner clipped to his belt loop, scratched to bare metal.

Three years is long enough that he's started describing Dominik and Klára in the past tense without noticing. His friends
here have noticed.

He hasn't found his parents.

## Now

He lives on Fenris Isle, fixing whatever scaffolding he can reach, because people here build fast and tie off badly. He's
paid in food and the occasional argument.

He wants something that stays up. He's been looking at the way people here hold their roofs on with an expression they
find difficult to read.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to build something that stays up. Properly.
- **Hope:** that his parents are together in Brno, wherever Brno is.
- **Dream:** a full crew again, a big lift, and a beer after.
- **Fear:** being the reason something falls.
- **Fear:** that he never settled because he was always looking up.
- **Fear:** his shoulder giving out halfway through a lift.

## How he's changed

In Prague he was a happy wanderer, always on the next tour, never committing to a person or a place.

Here he's stayed on one island for three years, which is the longest he's been anywhere. He's starting to like it. Part of
what the player gives him is a crew.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to build something that stays up.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. Fenris Isle needs a proper bridge to the shore. The old ones keep falling down. He wants to build one that won't.
2. You help him get the timber and the iron, from dangerous places, and keep the things in the water away while he works.
3. The bridge is tested by a storm on the night it's finished.

**Ending (happy):** it holds. In the morning, people walk across it without looking down, which is the best compliment a
rigger can get. He sits under it with a beer and says it's the first thing he's ever built that'll still be there when he
leaves. Then he says he isn't leaving, and he seems surprised to hear himself say it. He starts calling Fenris Isle home.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Tomas's Carabiner.** A steel climbing carabiner, scratched to bare metal, clipped to his belt loop through eighteen years
of rigging and on the night he fell forty feet into this world. He gives it to you after the bridge. He says it caught him
a hundred times, and he'd like it to catch you.

## Voice notes

- Easy, cheerful and practical. Contractions, rigging terms, and jokes about heights.
- His English is good, picked up on international tours, with a Czech accent and crew slang.
- He gives instructions before the work, never during.
- He swears in Czech when a knot's bad, which is often.
- **He won't talk about** Dominik or Klára until Friend, or his parents until Companion.
- **Habits:** checks every knot he passes, looks up before he walks under anything, rolls his bad shoulder, and clips the
  carabiner on and off when he's thinking.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)
