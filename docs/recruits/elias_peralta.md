## Who he is

Elias is calm. He's the calmest person in most rooms, and it's not something he works at. It's just how he is.

He's practical about the Light. It arrived in his second year, during nothing in particular, while he was fixing a cart. He's never once
presented it as a sign. He treats it as a tool that showed up, and he's been learning its tolerances ever since.

He's gentle and protective. He takes what's aimed at other people with an air of mild apology, as if he's sorry to be in the way.

He's put something off. He says he coped well, and everybody agrees. He's also said, once, that he thinks he simply hasn't got to it yet, and
that it's still sitting somewhere waiting for him.

What annoys him: people who rush repairs, people who make a show of faith, and bad radio. What he loves: a bike running sweet, bachata, his
grandmother's sancocho, a full church on Sunday, and Yamila's laugh.

## Before

He grew up in Santo Domingo, in a crowded house with his grandmother, his mother and a dozen cousins. His grandmother took him to church every
Sunday, and he kept going after she stopped being able to ask, because it was a habit and he liked the singing.

He fixed motorbikes for twelve years with his cousin Junior: a workshop with a tin roof, four bikes in pieces at any time, and a radio they argued
over.

He'd been seeing Yamila, a hairdresser, for two years. He hadn't told his mother about her, for reasons that seemed important at the time and
seem stupid now. He was going to propose. He had the ring in the workshop drawer.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was under a bike in the workshop when it happened. He landed in Tanaris with a mechanic's telescoping inspection mirror in his hand.

He fixed things for about eighteen months and let everything else wait. The Light came in the second year, while he was fixing a cart.

He hasn't found Yamila or his family. He hasn't really looked, because looking would mean getting to it.

## Now

He's at the Drownbell, on the coast of Northern Tanaris, at the front of any party, taking what's aimed at other people. The inspection mirror
lives in his gauntlet. He uses it to see round corners, and more often to check on the people behind him without turning round.

He wants to build something that'll outlast him, and he means a building. He's drawn up a shelter for a crossing on the coast where travellers
get caught out by storms: four walls, a roof and a stove. He's shown the drawing to nobody, because he knows the first question will be who it's
for.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to build the shelter.
- **Hope:** to find Yamila and give her the ring he left in the drawer.
- **Dream:** a Sunday with Yamila at his grandmother's table, his mother finally meeting her.
- **Fear:** getting to it.
- **Fear:** that he waited too long to tell anyone anything.
- **Fear:** a storm catching travellers with nowhere to go.

## How he's changed

In Santo Domingo he was a calm, easy-going mechanic who kept his love a secret from his family, and put off the big things.

Here he's become a shield and a light, and he's still putting off the big things. Part of what the player gives him is someone who asks who
the shelter's for.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to build the shelter, and to get to it.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He shows you the drawing. You ask who it's for. He says it's for Yamila, so if she ever comes along that coast in a storm, there'll be
   somewhere to go.
2. You help him gather the stone and timber and build it, and a storm hits the night it's finished, and travellers come in out of it.
3. Among them is a Dominican woman with a little boy.

**Ending (sad):** it's Yamila. She's alive, and she's married to a Dominican fisherman, and the boy is theirs. She thought Elias was dead. She
looked for him on every list for a year, and then she stopped. She cries, and holds his hands, and tells him she's happy. He tells her he's glad,
and he means it, and it breaks his heart. In the morning, when the storm's over, he gives her the ring, as a gift for the boy. Then he sits in his
shelter alone and finally gets to it, all of it, three years of it. You sit with him.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Elias's Inspection Mirror.** A mechanic's round telescoping inspection mirror from the workshop in Santo Domingo, kept in his gauntlet for
checking on people behind him. He gives it to you after Yamila. He says he'd rather look at what's in front of him now.

## Voice notes

- Calm, warm and gentle. Contractions, mechanic's metaphors, and "hermano" for everyone.
- His English is relaxed and Dominican, with Spanish: "tranquilo", "dime", "qué lo qué", "mi pana".
- He apologises for being in the way while standing in front of you.
- He doesn't swear much. When a part breaks, he does, cheerfully.
- **He won't talk about** Yamila until Friend, or his grandmother until Companion.
- **Habits:** checks on people with his mirror, fixes anything broken, hums bachata, and stands in front.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)
