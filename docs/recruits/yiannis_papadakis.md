the island's healer (Kyle: "We always want at least a Healer at the start of the game"). Waves 2, 3 and 4 build on this

## Who he is

Yiannis is the man on the beach. When the sea gives somebody up on the Waking Shore, he's the one who wades in and gets
them, and he's usually singing.

He's patient, warm and devout in the old island way, with an icon of Saint Nicholas in his hut and a joke for every
priest he's ever met. He improvises mantinades, the rhyming couplets old Cretans trade at weddings, about everyone he
pulls out of the water.

He sings to the sea when it's rough. He started doing it at sixteen, the winter his brother drowned, and he's never
stopped.

What annoys him: people who turn their back on the sea, waste, and anyone who rushes a meal. What he loves: a calm
morning on the water, a long lunch, his girls, and the moment somebody he's pulled out coughs and starts breathing.

## Before

He grew up in the old harbour at Chania, the son of a fisherman and the grandson of another. He dived for octopus from the
age of nine, beat them soft on the harbour stones, and sold them to the tavernas on the front.

When he was sixteen, he and Manolis took the boat out on a morning their father said was too rough. Yiannis came back.
He's spent thirty years being careful with the sea, and he's never once been angry with it.

He married Eleni at twenty-four. They have two girls, Maria, who's nineteen and wanted to study in Athens, and Katerina,
who's fifteen and wanted to be a diver like her father.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was out on the water before dawn when it happened, lifting pots off Akrotiri. He woke in the surf of a strange beach
two years ago, before the Conclave had a single tent up, and pulled himself out.

The first person he pulled out after that was an elf girl the tide brought in. She wasn't breathing. He did what he'd
learned to do on the harbour, and while he did it, light came into his hands. She breathed.

He talks to Eleni and the girls every night, out loud, on the beach. He doesn't care who hears.

## Now

He keeps the Waking Shore. Every arrival the sea gives up, he's there, wading in, wrapping them in a blanket and singing
them a couplet about how they looked coming out of the water. The Conclave's rangers bring him their wounded, because
the Light comes when he asks for it.

He still dives. There's a reef off Brinecoil Cove with octopus on it the size of a dinner table, and the naga don't like
him being there.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** that nobody the sea gives up on his shore is ever lost.
- **Hope:** to sing for his brother again, properly, out loud.
- **Dream:** a long lunch in the sun with Eleni and the girls.
- **Fear:** the morning the sea brings someone in and he isn't there.
- **Fear:** a storm he can't sing down.

## How he's changed

On Crete he was careful, quiet with grief and very good at his job. Here he found the Light in his hands, and with it a
reason to wade into the sea every time it calls. Part of what the player gives him is someone to row the boat.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to bring everyone in.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A storm season comes in, the worst since the Conclave took the island, and the lookouts see a wreck on the reef off
   Brinecoil Cove with people clinging to it.
2. Nobody will row out in that. He asks you to, and on the way he tells you about the morning with Manolis.
3. You fight off what's come up out of the deep to feed on the wreck while he brings them over the side, one at a time,
   singing the whole way.

**Ending (happy):** everyone comes in. On the beach, with the arrivals wrapped in blankets round the fire, he sings a
mantinada for Manolis, the first he's sung for his brother out loud in thirty years. Then he cooks everybody octopus.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Yiannis's Komboloi.** A string of worn amber worry beads on a silk cord, his brother's, which Yiannis has carried every
day for thirty years. It was in his pocket when he woke in the surf. He gives it to you after the storm, and says
Manolis would want it to keep moving.

## Voice notes

- Warm, slow and generous. "Ela", "my friend", "re", "Panagia mou" when he's startled, and food in nearly everything.
- He rhymes without meaning to and laughs when he catches himself.
- His healing voice is soft and sing-song, the way you'd talk to a child in the water.
- **He won't talk about** Manolis until Friend, or the night he talks to his family until Companion.
- **Habits:** wraps everyone in a blanket, feeds the party before they ask, crosses himself before a fight and after it,
  and checks the sky every morning.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** a gentle, steady healer who likes questing and new ground and hates grinding. He never panics,
  and he hates a fight left before everyone's out.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with patient people, hosts, the devout and anybody Greek. He worries over the
  reckless and feeds the young.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is Manolis. On a 13 turn cooldown he sings to the sea, and the whole party
  is healed and keeps healing.
