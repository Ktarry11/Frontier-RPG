## Who he is

Cristóbal is brave. Reckless with himself, never with anybody else. He's the one who goes back in.

He's loud and warm and friendly with everyone. He calls everybody hermano within a minute of meeting them, and he means
it. He laughs at everything, especially himself.

He's a joker about the things that frighten him. Hellfire found him, a firefighter, and he says it's a joke at his
expense, and he's decided to laugh at it.

He's a natural leader without trying. People follow him because he's always in front and always smiling.

He's soft about his mother. He talks about her cooking, her nagging, her house on the hill, constantly.

What annoys him: people who leave fires burning, people who stand back and watch, and anyone who tells him to be careful.
What he loves: his mother's empanadas, a fiesta, dancing cueca badly, and the moment a fire line holds.

## Before

He grew up in Valparaíso, in his mother's house on one of the steep hills above the port. His father left when he was
four. His mother cleaned offices and raised him alone, and sent him off every morning with empanadas.

He joined the forest fire brigade at 23. Twelve years of fighting fires in the hills: a brigade station, a motorbike that
broke down every week, long dry summers. In 2024 the hills above Valparaíso burned for days. He spent four days on the
line above his mother's house, without sleeping, while the city burned below. The house was still standing when he came
down. He's never told anyone how close it was.

He danced cueca at every fiesta and was very bad at it, which he says is the point. He had girlfriends, many of them,
none of them for long. His mother despaired.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was on duty at the station when it happened. He landed in the Barrens in the middle of a grass fire, wearing his yellow
brigadista helmet. He was the only one who walked out of that fire. The others who came through with him didn't.

The fire inside him scared him for a month. Then he started using it to light other people's camps.

He hasn't found his mother. He writes her a letter every week and keeps them all.

## Now

He lives at Camp Taurajo, drilling a dozen young tauren on how to cut a firebreak. When the grass goes up in the dry season,
he's the one Taurajo sends for. The tauren refugees from Mulgore are wary of fire, and he's earned their trust one fire at
a time.

He wants every town in the Barrens to have a fire brigade, and he's started training the first one.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a fire brigade in every town in the Barrens.
- **Hope:** that his mother knows he's alive.
- **Dream:** his mother's empanadas, and her telling him to be careful.
- **Fear:** the people who didn't walk out of the grass fire with him.
- **Fear:** the hellfire getting away from him.
- **Fear:** his mother alone in that house if the hills burn again.

## How he's changed

In Valparaíso he was a fearless, cheerful firefighter with a mother who worried and a lot of girlfriends.

Here he's become a teacher and a leader, and the fire is inside him now. He's still loud and brave. He's more careful with
other people's lives than ever. Part of what the player gives him is someone who'll go in with him.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to train a brigade that can hold a fire without him, and to get word to his mother.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The dry season comes early and a firestorm rolls across the Barrens toward Camp Taurajo. He's the only one who knows how
   to stop it.
2. You help him and his young tauren brigade cut the breaks and hold the line, and go back in for the people caught on the
   wrong side.
3. The line holds. The brigade did most of it.

**Ending (happy):** Camp Taurajo is saved. The young tauren he trained held the line without him for the last hour, while
he was pulling people out. A week later a Chilean trader brings a letter from a camp across the sea. His mother is alive.
She's heard there's a crazy Chilean fighting fires in the Barrens, and she knows it's him, and she tells him to be careful.
He sends her all the letters at once, three years of them, in a sack.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Cristóbal's Brigadista Helmet.** His yellow wildfire helmet, scorched and dented, the one he walked out of the grass fire
in on his first day. He gives it to you after the firestorm. He says the brigade has helmets of their own now, and his mother
would want someone careful to have it.

## Voice notes

- Loud, warm and fast. Contractions, "hermano" for everyone, and big laughs.
- His English is enthusiastic and Chilean: "po", "cachai", "weón" between friends, "hermano" always.
- He calls fire by name, like a person.
- He swears cheerfully and constantly in Chilean Spanish.
- **He won't talk about** the people in the grass fire until Friend, or the 2024 fire until Companion.
- **Habits:** checks the wind, sniffs for smoke, puts out every campfire properly, and dances badly at every inn.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** charges in and pulls people out. Laughs through everything. Furious at anyone who leaves a fire
  burning. Fearless in fire-based fights.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with everybody, especially loud, brave people, and with Matías and Esteban, the other
  Chileans. Teases serious people until they laugh. Very protective of the young.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the grass fire, and the people he couldn't bring out. A skill idea to look
  at then: on a cooldown of 10 to 15 turns, he walks into the worst of it and pulls the lowest-health ally out, shielding them.
