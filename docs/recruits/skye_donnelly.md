## Who she is

Skye knows fire as a thing with moods. Eleven seasons with a volunteer brigade outside Ballarat. She's the only caster most people
have met who treats her own magic as something to be managed, not owned.

She's very funny and swears a great deal. She's also completely dependable at the moment it matters, and those two things surprise
people.

She's practical and brave. She found a fire in her first week here and put it out, and says that sorted her head out for a year.

She has a rule about her fire that she tells nobody, and she's never broken it.

She's an optimist about people. She has six volunteers in her brigade and four turn up, and she describes this as going extremely
well, and she means it.

What annoys her: arsonists, people who play with fire, and people who don't turn up when they said they would. What she loves: a
quiet shed with a kettle, a cold beer after a burn, dogs, and a fire line that holds.

## Before

She grew up in Ballarat, the daughter of a postie and a primary school teacher. She trained as a vet nurse and joined the local CFA
brigade at 22. For eleven seasons it ate her entire summer and most of her friendships: a tanker, a pager, and a shed with a kettle
in it where the crew sat out the nights when nothing happened.

She married Dave, a mechanic, at 25. They divorced at 29, amicably. She says the fire brigade was most of the reason, and she'd do it
again. Her parents retired to Queensland. Her brother Jake drives road trains up north.

## The Integration and what it cost

She was in the brigade shed, cleaning the tanker, when it happened. She landed in Thousand Needles with her helmet torch clipped to
her belt.

She found a fire in her first week and put it out. The harder part was discovering she could start one. She's thought about that
more than she lets on. The rule she tells nobody is this: she never starts a fire she couldn't put out herself.

She assumes her parents and Jake are fine, on grounds she admits are entirely vibes.

## Now

She goes where the burning is, which in this world is most places. Lately that's the Drowning Floor, the flood cave under Southern
Thousand Needles, where something keeps setting the oil seeps alight under the water. A pyromancer in a flooded cave makes everyone
laugh, including her.

She's building a brigade: actual volunteers, actual training, actual kit, for a world that burns constantly and has nobody organised
about it.

The helmet torch is still clipped to her belt. The battery died two and a half years ago. She clicks it on sometimes out of habit and
laughs at herself every time.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a brigade for every region, properly trained.
- **Hope:** that her family's fine, vibes notwithstanding.
- **Dream:** a quiet night in a shed with a kettle and the crew, with nothing burning.
- **Fear:** breaking her rule.
- **Fear:** a fire she can't put out.
- **Fear:** that she's more fire than firefighter now.

## How she's changed

In Ballarat she was a cheerful vet nurse who gave her summers to fire and lost a marriage to it.

Here she can make fire, and it frightened her more than anything she'd ever fought. She's kept her rule and kept her sense of humour.
Part of what the player gives her is a crew that turns up.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** to find what's setting the Drowning Floor alight, and to train a brigade that works.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The fires under the water in the Drowning Floor are getting worse, and the flood will carry burning oil into the canyons. She's
   the only one who understands what she's looking at.
2. You go down with her and find the cause: something deep in the cave feeding on the fire and spreading it on purpose.
3. To stop it she has to start a fire she isn't sure she can put out.

**Ending (happy):** she does it, with you and four of her volunteers holding the line above, the way she trained them. It works. The
fire goes exactly where she meant it to and dies when she says. She sits on a wet rock afterwards, shaking, laughing and swearing. Her
brigade, all six, turn up for the next training day. She says it's the best turnout in the history of firefighting, and she isn't
joking.

## Her keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Skye's Helmet Torch.** The yellow, smoke-stained torch from her CFA helmet, dead for two and a half years, that she still clicks on out
of habit. She gives it to you after the Drowning Floor. She says she doesn't need to see in the smoke any more, she can make her own
light, and you should have something to laugh at.

## Voice notes

- Loud, quick and very Australian. Contractions, swearing, "mate", "reckon", "she'll be right", and briefings one sentence long.
- She talks about fire as if it were a cranky animal.
- She swears constantly and creatively, and goes quiet and precise when it matters.
- **She won't talk about** her rule until Friend, or Dave until Companion.
- **Habits:** checks the wind, clicks her dead torch, puts out every campfire properly, and makes tea at every stop.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** funny and sweary through fights, calm and precise when it counts. Furious at anyone who plays with fire.
  Puts out every fire the party leaves behind.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with funny, practical people, and especially with Cristóbal and Liam, fellow firefighters. Loses
