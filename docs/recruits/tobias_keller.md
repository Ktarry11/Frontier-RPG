## Who he is

Tobias is patient. He worked on the crew that's been repairing Cologne Cathedral for seven hundred years and will never finish, and he loved it. He replaced
stones carved by men who died in the middle ages.

He says very little, and all of it's true. He's quiet, methodical and quietly devout.

The Light came to him the way good mortar sets: slowly, and it holds.

He's playful in one hidden way. He carved a little face into every stone he ever replaced, somewhere nobody would ever look. He's still doing it.

He loves singing, deep and low, in a choir. He's started one at Light's Hope, and it has three people in it.

What annoys him: shoddy work, rushing, and people who lean on walls before the mortar's set. What he loves: good stone, the cathedral's bells, Kölsch with
his crew, a choir, and the moment a new stone fits exactly.

## Before

He grew up in Bonn, the son of a carpenter, and apprenticed as a stonemason at 17. At 24 he joined the Cologne Cathedral works crew and stayed sixteen
years: a crew of twelve who ate lunch together on the roof, and a cathedral that would never be finished.

He lived in a flat in the Südstadt and sang bass in a church choir. He never married. He says the cathedral was his wife and the crew his family, and the
choir his Sunday.

He carved a little face into every stone he replaced, somewhere hidden: a gargoyle's ear, the back of a pinnacle. His crew knew and never told anyone.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was up on the cathedral roof, with his crew, setting a stone, when it happened. He came through alone into the Plaguelands, which was very bad luck, with
his stonemason's chisel and mallet in his apron.

The Argent Dawn took him in because he could fix a wall. He found the Light on a scaffold, and he still prays best with a trowel in his hand.

He hasn't found his crew, or his family.

## Now

He's at Light's Hope Chapel, on the scaffold by the chapel wall, repointing mortar. There's a little carved face on one of the new stones already. He won't
say which one.

He wants Light's Hope to stand for a thousand years, and he'd like to be the one who made it.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** Light's Hope standing for a thousand years.
- **Hope:** to find his crew.
- **Dream:** lunch on the cathedral roof with all twelve of them, and the bells.
- **Fear:** a wall he built failing when it matters.
- **Fear:** that his crew landed somewhere with nothing to build.
- **Fear:** a choir of three forever.

## How he's changed

In Cologne he was a quiet, devoted mason in a crew that was his family, working on something bigger than all of them.

Here he's alone, and he's found the Light, and he's building something bigger than himself again. He's still quiet. He's lonelier. Part of what the player
gives him is a crew.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find his crew, and to make Light's Hope's walls hold.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. Scourge forces are massing to attack Light's Hope, and the eastern wall won't hold. Tobias says he can make it hold, if he gets the right stone, from a
   quarry deep in Scourge country.
2. You fight your way there and back with him, hauling the stone.
3. He rebuilds the wall in three days, without sleeping. The attack comes. The wall holds.

**Ending (sad):** among the Scourge that broke against his wall were things that had been men. One of them wore a stonemason's apron with the Cologne
works crest on it. After the battle he finds three more. His crew came through together, into the Plaguelands, and the Scourge took them. He lays them to
rest in the chapel yard and carves twelve little faces into the cornerstone of the wall they couldn't break. He says that wall will stand a thousand years,
and now he knows why.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Tobias's Chisel.** The stonemason's steel chisel from his apron on the cathedral roof in Cologne, with which he's carved a little hidden face into every stone
he's ever set. He gives it to you after the battle. He says he has one more face to carve, his own, next to theirs, and he'll do it with a new chisel.

## Voice notes

- Quiet, steady and plain. Contractions, very few words, and builder's terms.
- His English is careful and German: direct, understated, with "genau" (exactly), "na ja", "Mahlzeit" at meals.
- He sings bass under his breath while he works.
- He doesn't swear much. When work's shoddy, in German, quietly.
- **He won't talk about** his crew until Companion, or the hidden faces until Friend.
- **Habits:** checks every wall, carves faces in secret, hums hymns, and won't let anyone lean on fresh mortar.
