## Who he is

Facundo is the most patient man in the Expanse. He listens more than he talks, and he never rushes anything, a watch or a wound or a person.

He's gentle and precise, with a dry humour he keeps for people who've earned it. He can strip a pocket watch to its last screw and put it back together blindfold.

What annoys him: anyone who forces a mechanism, loud rooms and people who are late and call it fashionable. What he loves: a quiet bench, good light, the tick of something running true and arguing with Rocío about whether clocks should run fast or slow.

## Before

His grandfather's shop was a narrow place between a bakery and a shoe repairer, under a sign nobody had painted in fifty years. He sat at the bench by the window with a loupe in his eye. The whole wall of clocks struck noon a few seconds apart, and he never once fixed it.

Rocío taught mathematics at the secondary school. She said he was the only man in Argentina who was never late.

## The Integration and what it cost

His grandfather died while Facundo was away in Buenos Aires at a watch fair. He came back for the funeral and wound the old man's pocket watch at the graveside, and he's wound it at the same minute every morning since.

He woke on a sky island with his loupe round his neck and the watch still ticking. The Aetherian scholars found it so interesting they took it apart, and he made them put it back together in front of him. The first time he held a bleeding man's wrist, he felt the moment slip, and he wound it back a few seconds.

## Now

He tends the wounded who come off the Golem Fields, and he sits among the golems with his loupe in his eye. They're the most beautiful mechanism he's ever seen.

There's a golem core on the Fields that's ticking wrong, and it's hurting the golem that carries it. He can hear it from Halcyon Rest.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to understand the golems' cores.
- **Hope:** to know whether his grandfather's watch keeps the same time at home.
- **Dream:** a morning without counting the seconds.
- **Fear:** that he wasn't there when it mattered.
- **Fear:** that winding the watch is the only thing holding him together.

## How he's changed

At home he fixed time for other people. Here he can turn a few seconds back on a wound, and it's made him notice how many seconds he's been counting. Part of what the player gives him is a quiet room to work in, and somebody to keep the world off him while he does.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to set the golem's core right, and to stop counting.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The golem core on the Fields is ticking wrong. He asks you to keep the wards off him while he sets it right.
2. While he works he tells you about his grandfather, and the watch fair, and the graveside. He works slowly, and you let him.
3. The core ticks true, slow and even.

**Ending (happy):** the golem lies down afterwards, gently, and goes to sleep, and he sits with it until dark. He keeps the smallest gear that came loose and carries it with his grandfather's watch. For the first time in years, he isn't counting the seconds.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Facundo's Loupe.** A jeweller's loupe on a worn cord, its brass rim rubbed bright by his thumb. It was round his neck when he woke on the island. He gives it to you once the golem's asleep, and says he doesn't need to look so closely at time anymore.

## Voice notes

- Mendocino Spanish, quiet and exact. "Che", "tranqui", "despacito" when you're rushing and "qué lindo" for anything well made.
- He speaks in short careful sentences, and every one of them is finished.
- His fighting voice is a whisper, like he's working.
- **He won't talk about** his grandfather until Friend, or Rocío until Companion.
- **Habits:** winds the watch at the same minute, listens to things before touching them and takes pulses without asking.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** careful in a fight and patient with anything broken. Likes quests and new ground, dislikes grinding and abandoned quests.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with patient people, makers and quiet ones. He keeps away from reckless and loud types.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is counting the seconds. On a 15 turn cooldown he winds the whole party back a little, and what was done to them comes undone.
