## Who he is

Jun-seo's body learned things his mind never had a say in. Nine years of the same two motions ten thousand times a day on a Busan
production line. Here that's a weapon: he moves without deciding to, and it's fast.

The only thing that frightens him about it is how little of it he chooses.

He's quiet, patient and dry. He has deep smile lines he doesn't use often. When he does smile, it's worth it.

He doesn't know how to rest. His first year here he worked: anything, for anyone, as many hours as there were. In the second year
somebody made him sit down, and he found out he had no idea what to do with a day.

He's trying to learn to be slow. He's picked woodcarving because it can't be hurried, and he's catastrophically bad at it, and
that's the point.

What annoys him: rushing, bosses who shout, wasted effort, and being thanked for working. What he loves: a quiet morning, instant
noodles, baseball on the radio, his mother's kimchi, and the moment a piece of wood looks a little bit like what he meant.

## Before

He grew up in Busan, near the shipyards, the only son of a fish-market porter and a seamstress. He wasn't good at school and his
parents needed money, so at 20 he went into the yards, and at 34 he was on the welding line.

Nine years on the line, night shift mostly: a shift bell, a welding mask, and two square metres of floor he could draw from memory.
He sent money home every month without fail. He never married. He never had time, and he told himself he'd start when his parents
were looked after.

He used to whittle as a boy, sitting with his grandfather. He forgot about it for twenty years.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was at the end of a night shift, flipping up his welding shade, when it happened. He landed in Stranglethorn with the cracked shade
still on his head.

His parents are in Busan or they're not. The hub for that city came down in the far north, past the ice, and the arithmetic of that
isn't something he's prepared to do out loud.

## Now

He runs the cargo hoist at a goblin camp in Central Stranglethorn, hauling crates up from the river. It's two motions, ten thousand
times. He's aware of the joke and he keeps the job anyway.

In the evenings he carves wood, very slowly, very badly.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to be slow at something.
- **Hope:** that his parents are looked after, wherever they are.
- **Dream:** to carve something good enough to give his mother.
- **Fear:** that his body does things he didn't choose, and one day it'll hurt someone he didn't mean to.
- **Fear:** a day with nothing in it.
- **Fear:** that he spent his whole life working and never lived it.

## How he's changed

In Busan he was a good, tired son on a production line, who never chose anything for himself.

Here he's become dangerous, fast and free, and none of it was his choice either. He's trying, slowly, to choose. Part of what the
player gives him is somebody who lets him take his time.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find out about his parents, and to learn to live slowly.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He decides he wants to go north, toward the far hub where the Busan lists are, and asks you to come as far as the road goes.
2. The road north is long, and on it his body reacts before he does, in an ambush, and saves you. It frightens him more than the
   ambush.
3. You reach the end of the road: the edge of the ice. Nobody's crossed it. The hub is on the other side.

**Ending (still unknown):** he can't get there. Nobody can, yet. He stands at the edge of the ice for a long time. Then he takes out
his knife and a piece of wood and sits down and starts carving, right there, slowly, on purpose, for an hour. He carves a small,
lumpy bird. He says it's for his mother, for whenever he can get it to her. He goes home to the hoist and keeps carving, slower and
slower, and gets a little less bad at it.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Jun-seo's Welding Shade.** The flip-up welder's shade from the shipyard in Busan, cracked across one lens, the one he was wearing at
the end of his last night shift. He gives it to you after the ice. He says he doesn't need to protect his eyes from his work any more,
and he'd like to look at things properly.

## Voice notes

- Quiet, dry and short. Contractions, simple sentences, and long pauses.
- His English is plain and careful, learned late, with Korean words: "aigo" (oh no), "hwaiting" (come on), "gwaenchana" (it's okay).
- He says "it's easier than the factory" about almost everything.
- He doesn't swear in English. In Korean, quietly.
- **He won't talk about** his parents until Companion, or the fear of his own body until Friend.
- **Habits:** moves with the same efficient motions, carves wood at every rest, sits very still when he's tired, and never sits down
  first.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** fast and efficient in a fight, frightened afterwards by what he did without thinking. Works through every
  rest unless stopped. Hates grinding because it feels like the line.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with quiet, patient people, and with Haruto, who teaches him to slow down. Uncomfortable with
  loud, bossy people. Kind to other workers.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is moving without choosing. A skill idea to look at then: on a 10 to 15 turn
  cooldown, he chooses every strike deliberately for a few turns, and each one lands where it hurts most.
