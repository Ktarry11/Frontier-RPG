## Who he is

Álvaro is the calmest man on the tree. Twenty years of opening hives taught him that panic is what gets you stung, and he's never once panicked about anything.

He's warm and unhurried, and he tells long stories about his bees that wander off and come back. He stands very still when everyone else is running, and the swarms on Rootwater Isle seem to respect it.

What annoys him: loud people near a hive, and anyone who swats. What he loves: the first light on a hillside, a pickup with the windows down, honey straight off the comb and Marisela laughing at him.

## Before

He grew up on his uncle's farm in the hills above Cartagena, coffee and plantains and a long line of hives along the fence. His grandfather taught him the bees, and the old rule that you tell them the news every morning or they leave.

Marisela sold the honey off the back of the pickup on the coast road, and she sold it better than he ever kept it. The pickup had no second gear, and neither of them ever fixed it.

## The Integration and what it cost

His uncle died the week before the Integration, and Álvaro never told the bees. He was standing in the hives at dawn with the smoker in his hand when it happened, and he still wonders whether Marisela found the smoker on the ground.

He came through on the ground and climbed the tree with the first troll crews. The first wasp he met was the size of his hand, and it walked around on his arm and flew off. The venomancers took one look at that and taught him the rest.

## Now

He keeps the swarms off the climbers on Rootwater Isle. When something with a stinger blocks the path, they send for the beekeeper, and he always goes and he always walks back.

There's a hive of gentle sap-bees on the isle that the big wasps are tearing apart, and he goes and sits by it most evenings without saying anything.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to find a hive on this world that makes real honey, and keep it.
- **Hope:** to get word home somehow, the way he gave the bees their news.
- **Dream:** a hillside of his own hives, here, and Marisela selling the honey.
- **Fear:** that he left the bees without a word and they've gone.
- **Fear:** that Marisela thinks he walked away.

## How he's changed

At home the calm was just how he was. Here it's the thing that keeps people alive, and he's had to learn that it matters. Part of what the player gives him is somebody to tell the news to, out loud, before he tells the bees.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to save the sap-bees, and to tell them everything he never told the ones at home.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The wasps on Rootwater Isle are tearing the sap-bees' hive apart. He asks you to help him hold them off while he moves the queen.
2. On the way he tells you about his uncle, and about the morning he didn't tell the bees. He asks you to walk slowly when you get close, because they can feel your heart.
3. With the hive safe, he sits with the bees and tells them all of it, his uncle and Marisela and the farm and the pickup.

**Ending (happy):** the queen is laying again, and he takes the first comb of honey on this world out of that hive. It tastes of the tree. He feels lighter than he has in three years, and he starts telling the bees the news every morning again.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Álvaro's Hive Tool.** A beekeeper's hive tool, flat steel with a hooked end and the paint long worn off the grip. It was in his back pocket the morning he came through. He gives it to you once the hive is safe, and says the bees know your news now, so you should have it.

## Voice notes

- Caribbean Colombian Spanish, slow and warm. "Tranquilo", "mijo" to people he likes, "ajá" when he's listening, and "¡Ave María!" when he's surprised.
- His stories wander off and always come back.
- His fighting voice is quieter than his normal one.
- **He won't talk about** his uncle until Friend, or Marisela until Companion.
- **Habits:** stands very still, hums to anything with wings and offers everyone honey he hasn't got yet.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** patient in a fight, calm when it goes wrong and fond of beasts. Likes quests and beasts, dislikes grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with patient, gentle people and anyone who works outdoors. He keeps away from loud, reckless types, but never says so.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the news he never gave. On a 14 turn cooldown he stands very still in front of the party, every enemy comes for him, and the first two that try it get stung.
