## Who he is

Sebastián is a host. It's who he is before it's what he does. If you sit down near him, he'll feed you. If you stand
near him, he'll make you sit down.

He's cheerful, about everything, including blood, fire and meat. He's completely unbothered by gore and doesn't
understand why other people are. The Forsaken find his blood magic deeply unsettling, mostly because he's so happy
about it.

He's sociable and loud. He talks to everyone and remembers what they like to eat. He argues about football like it's
life and death, and with him it is.

He's generous to a fault. He'll give you his last piece of bread and tell you he's not hungry. He never sits down at
his own table, and he's never noticed.

Under the noise he's lonely in a way he doesn't let himself feel. He grew up with twenty people round a table every
Sunday, and here he cooks for whoever turns up.

What annoys him: people who won't eat, people who rush food, and anybody who supports Boca Juniors. What he loves: a fire,
a full table, a good argument about football, and the moment someone tastes something and stops talking.

## Before

He grew up in Palermo, in Buenos Aires, in a flat above the family's steakhouse. His grandmother, Nélida, ran the grill
for thirty years. He was in the kitchen from the age of eight, and at 22 she handed him the tongs.

He ran the grill for twelve years: forty tables, a wood fire and a reputation. He knew the meat, the blood and the fire
better than anyone, and he could tell when a steak was ready from across the room.

Every Sunday he cooked asado for the whole family, twenty people or more. He never once sat down.

He's had a few loves. A girlfriend from school who married someone else, and a long relationship with a sommelier
called Tomás, who left for a job in Mendoza a year before the Integration. Sebastián says it was the wine region's
fault. He still thinks about him sometimes, and it doesn't make him sad any more.

He supports River Plate, like his father and his grandfather, and he'll tell you about the 2018 final if you give him
the chance. Don't give him the chance.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was lighting the grill for a Saturday lunch when it happened. He landed in Western Tirisfal, among the Forsaken,
with his grandmother's chimichurri recipe in his wallet. It's on a card in her handwriting, stained with oil, and he's
carried it since she gave it to him.

The Forsaken don't eat much. That was the hardest thing he's ever had to accept.

Blood magic came easily, because he's been reading blood on a grill his whole life and he knows when it's ready.

It cost him his family, as far as he knows. Twenty people, and he hasn't found one of them on a list. He doesn't talk
about it. He talks about food instead, and he's very good at it.

## Now

He lives by a fire pit he dug himself in Western Tirisfal, with a grill, a stack of wood and a farmer who's never sure
whether to be grateful or afraid. He waves at every passing Forsaken, and some of them have started waving back.

He's working on a recipe the Forsaken can actually taste. He thinks he's close.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to open a grill in Brill that the living and the dead will both come to.
- **Hope:** to find even one of his family.
- **Dream:** to cook asado for twenty again, and this time sit down.
- **Dream:** to see a Forsaken taste something and smile.
- **Fear:** an empty table.
- **Fear:** that if he stops cooking for people he'll have to feel how much he misses them.
- **Fear:** forgetting how his grandmother's food tasted.

## How he's changed

In Buenos Aires he was the heart of a big, loud family and a busy kitchen. He never had a moment alone.

Here he's had a lot of moments alone, and he's filled them with cooking for strangers. He's become braver and stranger.
He's made friends with the dead. Part of what the player gives him is a table to cook for that keeps coming back.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find his family.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He hears of a camp of Argentines in Tanaris, in the desert, and that there's a family from Buenos Aires
   there who run a grill. He's sure it's someone he knows.
2. The road is long and dangerous, and he cooks for every camp on the way, because he can't help it.
3. He finds the camp. The family running the grill are from Buenos Aires, but they're strangers. They've never heard of
   the Cruz family. They've seen lists from three other camps, and none of them have his people on them either.

**Ending (still unknown):** there's no answer, and the trail goes nowhere else. He stays the night anyway and cooks
with the strangers. On the way home he decides he isn't going to keep waiting for his family to turn up before he
lives. He opens the grill in Brill. On the first Sunday he cooks asado for twenty: the party, a few farmers and three
Forsaken who've started coming every week. Then he sits down at his own table for the first time in his life. He
doesn't know if his family is alive. He keeps a chair empty for them every Sunday.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Nélida's Chimichurri Card.** His grandmother's recipe, in her handwriting, stained with oil, carried in his wallet
for twelve years and through the Integration. He gives it to you after the first Sunday in Brill. He knows it by heart,
he says, and he wants it somewhere safe.

## Voice notes

- Loud, warm and fast. Contractions, lots of "my friend", and food in almost every sentence.
- His English is good with a strong Buenos Aires accent and the odd Spanish word: "che", "dale", "boludo" for friends.
- He describes people by what they'd eat.
- He swears cheerfully in Spanish, and never at anyone.
- **He won't talk about** his family's absence until Friend, Tomás until Companion, or anything at all when the food's
  nearly ready.
- **Habits:** feeds everyone, sniffs the air near a fire, checks every kill for what's edible, and stands at every meal.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** cooks at every rest and won't let anyone skip a meal. Cheerful in gruesome places that bother
  everyone else. Chats to friendly undead. Gets sad and quiet when a quest giver mentions their family.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with nearly everyone, especially loud people and anyone who'll argue about football.
  He'd win over the grumpiest person in the party with food. Clashes with fussy eaters.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is never sitting down. A skill idea to look at then: a passive where,
  after a rest, the whole party starts the next fight stronger, because they ate properly and so did he.
