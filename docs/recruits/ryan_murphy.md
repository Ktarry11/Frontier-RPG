# Ryan Murphy

Wave 1 profile, draft 1 (19 Nov 2026). Built on his recruit entry. Waves 2, 3 and 4 build on this page.

## Who he is

Ryan is honest now. He wasn't for most of his life, and he'll tell you that before you can work it out for yourself.
Three years on he's more honest than he's ever been and no better a man, and he says so.

He's charming. He knows how to read a room and what people want and what they'll settle for. He was very good at his
job because of it. He uses it on demons now, which he says is the same skill.

He's funny, in a dark, self-mocking, very Irish way. He's the first to make a joke about himself and the last to let
anybody else make it.

He's careful with money and with promises. He pays exactly on time, every time. He keeps every deal to the letter. He
says it isn't virtue, it's the only thing stopping the arrangement running the other way.

He doesn't like himself much, and he doesn't ask anyone else to. He drinks a bit too much and he knows it.

What annoys him: people who pretend they're better than they are, people who say "it's just the system", and being
trusted too quickly. What he loves: a well-drafted contract, a good whiskey, honest people, and his son, whom he hasn't
looked for.

## Before

He grew up in Cork, in a terraced house with his grandmother, Nora. His mother left when he was small and his father
wasn't around. Nora raised him on stories, the rosary and very strong opinions about right and wrong.

He was clever and hungry, and he got a scholarship to study in Dublin, then a job in Boston at 26. He was good at the
work. He closed deals that took things from people who never learned his name: factories closed, pensions cut, a town in
Ohio that lost its biggest employer on a Friday afternoon. He drank after work with men who did the same and agreed with
them that it was the system. He didn't believe it even as he said it, and that's the only real difference between him
and them.

He married Dana, a nurse, at 27. Conor was born when Ryan was 29. Ryan missed most of it. Dana divorced him when Conor was
eleven, and she was right to. He saw Conor every second weekend and was late half the time.

Nora died when Ryan was 40. On balance he's glad she didn't live to form an opinion of what he became.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was at his desk on a Friday afternoon, closing a deal, when it happened. He landed on the Echo Isles in a suit, with
the gold watch he bought with his first bonus still on his wrist.

He hasn't had an adjustment. He's had a diagnosis, and he took it the way a man takes one he'd already privately made.
Demons turned out to be clients: read what they want, work out what they'll take, close.

Conor was 19 when it happened, at college in Boston. He landed somewhere on this world and might well be looking. Ryan
hasn't put his name on a single list at any hub in three years. He can give you the reason in one sentence and won't.
The sentence is this: if Conor's looking, he deserves to find something better than his father.

## Now

He lives on the Echo Isles, where nobody's under any illusions about anybody. He finds it restful.

His demons are on contracts he drafted and can recite clause by clause. He pays on time, exactly on time.

He's wanted out of the business for fifteen years without once taking a step toward it, and he's fluent in the reasons.
What he does instead is keep the contracts clean and the terms honest and tell himself that's different. Some days it
is.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** that Conor's all right, and better than him.
- **Hope:** to get out of the business for good.
- **Dream:** to have one honest conversation with his son.
- **Dream:** to be the kind of man Nora wanted him to be, even for one day.
- **Fear:** finding Conor and seeing his own face looking back, disappointed.
- **Fear:** that he can't change, only describe himself more accurately.
- **Fear:** a contract he can't keep.

## How he's changed

In Boston he was successful, rich, and lying to himself about it every day.

Here he stopped lying. He's honest about what he is and what he does. It hasn't made him good. It's made him clearer.
Part of what the player gives him is somebody who sees him clearly and stays anyway.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to stop hiding from his son.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He finally writes his name on a list at the hub, with a note: "Conor Murphy, your father is on the Echo Isles."
2. While he waits, a demon he's under contract to tries to bend the terms, and he has to choose between breaking his
   word to it and letting it hurt people. He breaks the contract. You fight it with him.
3. A letter comes. Conor will meet him in Orgrimmar.

**Ending (sad):** Conor is 22, tall, polite and steady. He's working as a medic with a Horde unit. He listens to everything
Ryan says. Then he tells him he's glad his father's alive, he's been fine without him, and he doesn't want a father
now. He shakes Ryan's hand and goes back to his unit. Ryan doesn't argue. On the way home he says that was the most
honest thing anyone's ever said to him, and that the boy turned out better than him without any help at all. He keeps
writing to Conor once a month. Conor doesn't write back, but the letters don't come back either.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Ryan's First-Bonus Watch.** A heavy gold watch he bought with his first bonus in Boston, the day he decided he was
the kind of man who wore one. It was on his wrist when he landed. He gives it to you after Orgrimmar. He says he's out of
the business now, and a man who's out doesn't need to know what time the markets open.

## Voice notes

- Charming, quick and dry. Contractions, a Cork accent worn down by Boston, and jokes at his own expense.
- He talks about everything in terms of deals: price, terms, what something will cost.
- He tells you the price before you start.
- He swears casually and cheerfully, never at people he respects.
- **He won't talk about** Conor until Companion, or Nora until Friend.
- **Habits:** checks his watch when he's lying, reads every contract and notice twice, never takes the last drink, and
  goes quiet at the word "son".

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** reads what every quest giver really wants. Haggles over rewards. Cynical about noble quests and
  then does them properly anyway. Calm about demons and wary of anyone who trusts them.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with honest, blunt people and anyone else who knows they're no saint. Clashes with
  self-righteous people, and with naive ones until they surprise him. Has a lot in common with Esteban, who also names
  the price first.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is hiding from what he did. A skill idea to look at then: on a 10 to 15 turn cooldown,
  he pays the price himself, taking the damage meant for the party and turning it into power for his demon.
