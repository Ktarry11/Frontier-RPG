## Who he is

Omar is unbothered. He's spent his whole working life very calm around things that sting, and he's the only person who walks into the Royal Hive without
flinching.

He's amused by almost everything. He says the bugs here are bigger, and otherwise it's the same job. He means it as a joke, and he also means it.

He's methodical. A poison you understand is half an antidote, so he catalogues everything that lives in the hive, carefully, like a pest survey.

He's warm and easy, with a big laugh and a lot of stories about the strangest jobs he ever had, starting with the museum storeroom.

He's searching, quietly. He asks every healer he meets about Mariam, because she'd have ended up near the sick.

What annoys him: panic around insects, people who use too much poison, and being called an exterminator. What he loves: a clean survey, koshari from the
cart on his street, football on a Friday, Mariam's laugh, and the moment a frightened family's house is safe again.

## Before

He grew up in Shubra, in Cairo, the eldest son of a man who started a pest control business in 1987 with one van. Omar joined at 23 and spent fifteen years
fumigating apartment blocks, restaurants, and once, memorably, a storeroom of the Egyptian Museum, where he found a colony of beetles eating a crate
labelled "do not open".

He met Mariam, a pharmacist, when he came to fumigate her pharmacy. She told him exactly which chemicals she didn't want near her stock, and she was right
about all of them. They were engaged, and the wedding was set for the spring after the Integration.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was on a job, fumigating a restaurant kitchen, when it happened. He came through with his fogging rig on his back. Most of it broke on landing; the
brass nozzle survived.

He found out venom listens to him here. He says it's the same job.

He hasn't found Mariam, or his family. He asks every healer he meets.

## Now

He's inside the Royal Hive, in the first chambers, cataloguing what lives there. He's studying all of the hive's poisons, because a poison you understand
is half an antidote.

The brass nozzle hanging from his belt came off his old fogging rig, and he still checks it for blockages.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to find Mariam.
- **Hope:** an antidote for every poison in the hive.
- **Dream:** the wedding, in the spring, with his father dancing badly.
- **Fear:** that Mariam was in the pharmacy, and the pharmacy didn't come through.
- **Fear:** a poison he can't understand.
- **Fear:** that he's become what he used to get rid of.

## How he's changed

In Cairo he was a cheerful, competent pest controller about to be married.

Here he's become a venomancer, which he says is the same job, and he's carried on as if nothing's changed, because that's how he copes. Part of what the
player gives him is somebody who sees that it has.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find Mariam.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A sickness from the Royal Hive's stings is spreading through the nearby camps, and nobody can treat it. Omar's catalogue has the answer, if he can get a
   sample from the deepest chamber.
2. You go deep into the hive with him, further than anyone's gone.
3. You bring the sample out, and he makes the antidote, and a party of healers arrives from the south to collect it for the camps.

**Ending (happy):** leading the healers is a woman with a pharmacist's bag, who looks at his antidote and tells him exactly which part of it is wrong, and
she's right. It's Mariam. She's been working in the camps for two years, sending notes to every hub asking after a pest controller who'd be "near things
that sting". He laughs until he can't breathe. They fix the antidote together. He asks her if the spring wedding's still on, and she says it's always
spring somewhere.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Omar's Fogging Nozzle.** The brass nozzle from his fumigation rig in Cairo, the only part that survived the landing, checked for blockages every day for
three years. He gives it to you after Mariam. He says he doesn't need to fog anything any more, and you should have the part that came through.

## Voice notes

- Calm, warm and amused. Contractions, stories, and Egyptian Arabic: "ya basha", "maalesh" (never mind), "khalas", "yalla".
- He talks about every monster like a pest infestation.
- He tells people not to panic, cheerfully.
- He swears in Arabic when someone uses too much poison.
- **He won't talk about** Mariam until Companion, or the fear about the pharmacy until Friend.
- **Habits:** catalogues every creature, checks the nozzle, laughs at danger, and asks every healer the same question.
