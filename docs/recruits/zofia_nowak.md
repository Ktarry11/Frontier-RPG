## Who she is

Zofia doesn't let anyone talk down to her twice. Eight years of people shouting at her down a phone about things she couldn't fix left her with very little
patience for it.

She's guarded and determined. She tells you plainly what she will and won't talk about, and she means it.

She's honest about what she did. The Cult of the Damned found her in the first winter and promised she'd never be powerless again, and for a while they
kept the promise. Then she saw what it cost, and she walked out with what they'd taught her.

She's practical to the bone. She wants every Cult house in the Plaguelands burned, and then she'd like to finish her accounting course, and she isn't
joking about either.

She has a sly sense of humour, learned from a grandmother who cheated at cards.

What annoys her: people who talk down to her, people who shout, cults, and anyone who calls her a victim. What she loves: a balanced ledger, pierogi,
card games, her grandmother's cheating, and the moment a Cult house burns.

## Before

She grew up in Łódź, raised by her grandmother, Babcia Halina, in a small flat after her parents split and moved away. Her grandmother taught her to play
cards and cheated every single time, and Zofia learned to cheat back.

She worked at an insurance company's call centre for eight years: eight hours a day of people shouting about things she couldn't fix. She was studying
accounting at night and was two exams from finishing.

She wore a headset all day, and she still has the plastic clip from it.

## The Integration and what it cost

She was at her desk, on a call, when it happened. She landed in the Plaguelands in the first winter, with her grandmother, who'd come through in the same
flat. They were starving, and her grandmother was sick.

The Cult of the Damned found them. They offered Zofia power and her grandmother food and medicine. Zofia said yes, for both of them. The first year isn't
something she'll talk about, and she'll tell you that plainly.

Then she saw what it cost, and she left Andorhal at night, alone, with three of the Cult's books. She thought her grandmother had died in the Cult's care,
the winter before. She was told so.

## Now

She hunts the people who recruited her, in the north of the Western Plaguelands, and she knows where most of the Cult houses are.

The headset clip pinned to her collar is from her desk at the call centre, and she wears it like a medal.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** every Cult house burned.
- **Hope:** to finish the accounting course.
- **Dream:** a card game with Babcia Halina, both of them cheating.
- **Fear:** what she did in the first year.
- **Fear:** that she's still the Cult's, underneath.
- **Fear:** that her grandmother died because Zofia said yes.

## How she's changed

In Łódź she was a tired, smart young woman shouted at all day, raised by a grandmother who loved her.

Here she sold herself for power to save her grandmother, and walked away with the power and without her grandmother. She's harder and angrier. Part of what
the player gives her is somebody who doesn't talk down to her.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** to burn the Cult house where she and her grandmother were taken in.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. She finally tells you where it is: a farmhouse in the north of the Plaguelands, still in use, where the Cult takes in starving transplants.
2. You fight your way in with her. Inside are the Cult's servants, the dead they've raised to work.
3. One of them is an old woman with Babcia Halina's face, mindless, shuffling cards in her hands without knowing why.

**Ending (sad):** her grandmother didn't die in the Cult's care. The Cult raised her and kept her working. Zofia lays her to rest herself, with her own hands
and the power the Cult gave her, and the old woman goes still holding the cards. Then Zofia burns the farmhouse to the ground. She keeps the deck of cards.
She says her grandmother cheated at the last hand, because she had five aces in that deck, and she laughs, and then she doesn't.

## Her keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Zofia's Headset Clip.** A black plastic headset clip from her desk at the call centre in Łódź, pinned to her collar like a medal for three years. She gives it
to you after the farmhouse. She says she isn't answering anyone's calls any more, and she'd rather you had it than the Cult's symbols.

## Voice notes

- Sharp, guarded and dry. Contractions, short answers, and a sly humour that shows up slowly.
- Her English is good and direct, with Polish: "kurwa", "no" (yes, well), "babcia".
- She states what she won't talk about, firmly.
- She swears in Polish when anyone mentions the Cult.
- **She won't talk about** the first year at all, or her grandmother until Friend.
- **Habits:** touches the headset clip, shuffles cards, tears Cult symbols off anything she finds, and does sums in her head.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** uses Cult magic with grim efficiency. Cold fury in Cult and Scourge places. Bristles at being talked down to. Quietly clever.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with sharp, honest people, and with Marta, another woman from Łódź whom she never met there. Has a lot in common with
  Rui and Owen, who also bargained with something in the dark. Clashes with condescending people.
- **Wave 4, her Sworn skill:** what she resolves is the bargain she made for her grandmother. A skill idea to look at then: on a 10 to 15 turn cooldown,
  she turns the Cult's corruption back on an enemy, and it spreads to everything near it.
