## Who she is

Camille is abrasive on purpose. It keeps people at the first layer, and almost nobody gets past it. She's fast, sharp and
extremely funny when she decides to be.

She knows exactly how much space she has and takes all of it. Nine years of weaving through Lyon traffic taught her that.
She fights the same way.

She's loyal to a degree she'd deny under torture. The few people who get past the first layer find out she'd ride through
anything for them.

She won't take a message she hasn't read. She learned that the hard way in her second month and has been insufferable about
it ever since.

She keeps busy so she doesn't have to think. She's been mapping every road between the camps, by hand, by riding them.
Somebody should, she says. The real reason is that it's the only thing here that feels like the job, and the job was the
last part of her life that worked.

What annoys her: slow people, sentimental people, people who say "be careful", and anyone who asks how she's feeling. What
she loves: speed, a well-tuned machine, a good map, strong coffee, and her brother, though she'd never say so to his face.

## Before

She grew up in Villeurbanne, outside Lyon. Her father was a postman and her mother a hospital cleaner. She and Théo, three
years younger, fought constantly and were inseparable.

She started couriering at 23 and never stopped: a fixed-gear frame she built twice, a radio in one ear, and a city she could
cross in twenty-two minutes. She fixed the company's bikes too, because nobody else could do it properly. Théo joined two
years later because she did.

She had a girlfriend for three years, Margaux, a pastry chef with early mornings that never matched Camille's shifts. It
ended a year before the Integration, badly, mostly because Camille wouldn't talk about anything. She knows that now.

On the morning of the Integration, Théo was riding the Part-Dieu road. She was supposed to be, and she'd swapped the shift
with him the night before so she could sleep in. She's said this out loud twice and regretted it both times.

## The Integration and what it cost

She was in bed when it happened. She landed in the Barrens in her pyjamas, with her bicycle-spoke wrench still on the
keyring she'd been holding.

She made herself useful immediately and let nobody near her for two years. It worked, in the sense that she survived it.
She knows it wasn't the only option. She says the other options were for people with more patience than her.

She thinks Théo is dead, on the road she should have been on. She's never looked for him on the lists, because she doesn't
want to see it written down.

## Now

She's on the roads, carrying things between places faster than they've any right to arrive. Lately she's been mapping the
Wailing Caverns, every tunnel, because nobody has a proper map and people keep getting lost and dying down there.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a complete map of every road and every tunnel she can reach.
- **Hope:** she doesn't let herself hope about Théo.
- **Dream:** a ride through Lyon at dawn, with Théo slipstreaming behind her, complaining.
- **Fear:** that she killed her brother by swapping a shift.
- **Fear:** letting someone close and losing them.
- **Fear:** slowing down.

## How she's changed

In Lyon she was a fast, funny, prickly courier who'd driven off the people who loved her by never talking.

Here she drove everyone off for two years, then slowly let a few people back in. She's still prickly. She talks a little
more. Part of what the player gives her is someone who doesn't ask her to talk and stays anyway.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** to find out what happened to Théo, though she'd never say it that way.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A goblin trader complains about a French courier who works for a rival company and delivers faster than anyone, and won't
   take a message he hasn't read. Camille goes very quiet.
2. You go with her to find him, along a road she's mapped and he apparently has too.
3. He's been ambushed on a road in the Barrens, and his cargo's being taken.

**Ending (happy):** it's Théo. He's alive, older, and just as annoying. He landed somewhere else entirely, became a courier
again because what else would he do, and he's been reading every message for three years for exactly the reason she does.
She gets him out of the ambush. Then she tells him about the swapped shift, and he laughs at her and says he was always
going to take the Part-Dieu road because it's the best one. They argue about the fastest route to the Crossroads the whole
way back. She doesn't stop smiling, and she'd deny it under torture.

## Her keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Camille's Spoke Wrench.** A slim silver bicycle-spoke wrench on a battered keyring, the one she used to true wheels in
Lyon. It was in her hand when she landed. She gives it to you after Théo. She says she'd like to know someone else has it,
which is the most sentimental thing she's ever said, and she tells you never to mention it.

## Voice notes

- Fast, sharp and sarcastic. Contractions, short sentences, and "yes?" at the end of half of them.
- Her English is fluent with a strong French accent and French swearing: "putain", "merde", "allez".
- She insults people she likes and is icily polite to people she doesn't.
- She refuses to talk about feelings, loudly.
- **She won't talk about** Théo until Friend, or Margaux until Companion.
- **Habits:** reads every message and notice, draws maps of everywhere she goes, fidgets with her wrench, and is always the
  first one moving.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** fast and first, impatient with slow parties. Maps every dungeon. Sarcastic through every fight.
  Furious if anyone gets left behind, while pretending she isn't.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on fast with sarcastic, quick people, and clashes with slow, sentimental or preachy ones.
  Secretly very fond of anyone loyal.
- **Wave 4, her Sworn skill:** what she resolves is the swapped shift. A skill idea to look at then: on a 10 to 15 turn cooldown, she
  moves so fast she acts twice, and takes an ally's next hit for them.
