## Who he is

Marco is steady. He doesn't hurry, he doesn't shout and he doesn't panic. He stands where he's needed and stays there.

He fixes things. Locks first, and then everything else. A neighbour's argument, a debt that's gone bad, a boy who's
started running with the wrong people. Nobody ever asks him to. He just notices and does something about it, quietly,
and doesn't mention it afterwards.

He's careful with his reputation. He spent thirty years building one in a neighbourhood where the authorities were
often worse than the problem, and he never once spent it on himself.

He's warm, in an old-fashioned way. He calls younger people "ragazzo" and "ragazza". He insists on paying for things.
He brings food to people who are sick and he remembers saints' days.

He's stubborn about one thing: he puts off what hurts. He looked for people to be useful to in his first fortnight here,
and everything else, his family above all, he put off. At 63 he's decided that's a legitimate way to handle it. It
isn't, and part of him knows.

What annoys him: bullies, people who take advantage of the old, and anyone who makes a promise they don't keep. What he
loves: a good coffee, a well-made lock, his family, and a street where people know each other.

## Before

He grew up in the Quartieri Spagnoli, the son of a cobbler. He apprenticed to a locksmith at 15 and had his own shop by
32, a narrow place with a bench, a key-cutting machine and a door that was always open.

For twenty years he was the man the neighbourhood sent for when something was wrong. He opened the doors of people who'd
locked themselves out, and the doors of old people who hadn't answered in a while. He settled arguments because somebody
had to, and because everybody owed him a favour and he never called one in.

He married Carmela at 28. She ran a haberdashery two streets away and laughed at him for being so serious. They had two
daughters. Giulia became a pharmacist and Sara a primary school teacher. Sara had a son, Matteo, the February before the
Integration. Marco held him four times.

He was going to retire that summer and hand the shop to his apprentice.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was in the shop, cutting a key, when it happened. He landed in Tirisfal with the key still in his hand, and walked to the Undercity because it had doors. He kept
it.

He looked for people to be useful to and found them within a fortnight. The Light found him already doing the work. He
thinks of the whole thing as a formality that arrived late.

Everything else he put off. He hasn't looked for his family on the lists. He can give you reasons, and they're good
reasons, and none of them are the real one. The real one is that while he doesn't look, they're all alive.

## Now

He lives in the Undercity, in two rooms off the Trade Quarter, among the Forsaken and the few living people who work
there. The quarter has quietly worked out who to fetch when something's wrong. Forsaken have a lot of locks.
He fixes locks, and he fixes other things, and nobody has ever asked him to.

He has about half a quarter there now, a few passages where people, living and dead, know to come to him. He's
counting.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a quarter again, a few streets where people know to come to him.
- **Hope:** that Carmela, the girls and the baby are together.
- **Dream:** to hold Matteo a fifth time.
- **Dream:** to sit in a café again, with the newspaper, and have people stop to say good morning.
- **Fear:** looking for his family and finding out.
- **Fear:** that Matteo is growing up and won't know who he is.
- **Fear:** getting too old to stand in front of people.

## How he's changed

In Naples he was the most respected man on his streets. He was settled, trusted and nearly ready to rest.

Here he started again at 60, and he's found that he's still that man, the Light just made it official. What's changed
is the fear. He was never afraid of anything in Naples. Here he's afraid of a list. Part of what the player gives him is
somebody who'll stand next to him while he reads it.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to stop putting it off.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He asks you to come with him to the hub to read the lists. He's never done it. You find Sara's name on a list from a
   camp of Neapolitans at Tarren Mill.
2. The road there is dangerous, and he won't let you go ahead of him.
3. You find the camp.

**Ending (happy):** Sara is there, and so is Matteo, who's three now and hiding behind her legs. Sara cries. Matteo
doesn't know who Marco is, and Marco kneels in the street and lets the boy look at him for as long as he wants. Giulia
isn't there. Carmela isn't there either; Sara hasn't found her. Marco holds Matteo for the fifth time that evening, and
the sixth, and stops counting. He moves Sara and the boy to the Undercity, where he has half a quarter to give them. Matteo is the only
child in the Trade Quarter, and the Forsaken are very careful around him.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**The Key He Was Cutting.** A half-cut brass key from his shop in Naples, still in his hand when he landed. It doesn't
open anything. He gives it to you after Sara and Matteo come to the Undercity. He says he's finished it now, in his way.

## Voice notes

- Few words, slow and certain. Contractions, short sentences, and long pauses.
- His English is careful and Italian in rhythm. He says "ragazzo", "allora" and "piano, piano".
- He never raises his voice. When he's angry he gets quieter.
- He doesn't swear in English. In Neapolitan, under his breath, when something's badly made.
- **He won't talk about** his family until Companion, or the lists until Friend.
- **Habits:** checks every lock and door he passes, keeps the half-cut key in his waistcoat pocket, stands at the front
  without being asked, and always pays for the drinks.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** stands in front, says little, and never steps back. Notices which party member is struggling
  and quietly helps. Hates cruelty to the weak in any quest. Patient in a wipe.
- **Wave 3, with others:** a natural father to the younger recruits, which some like and some don't. Gets on with steady
  people. Quietly disapproves of show-offs and loud talkers.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is putting things off. A skill idea to look at then: once a fight, he
  takes every hit aimed at the party for a turn, and the hits that land on him are much weaker.
