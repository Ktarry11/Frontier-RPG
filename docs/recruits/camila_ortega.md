## Who she is

Camila is still. She's very good at waiting for something to move, and here that's most of the job. She has nerves of steel and a steady hand
from ten thousand scorpions.

She's dry and funny, in a quiet, deadpan way. She notices everything and says about a tenth of it.

She's fond of the things everyone else is scared of. Tanaris is full of scorpids, and she thinks it's the best place she's ever been.

She's precise. The first time a venomancer showed her what poison could do, she asked for the dose, the timing and the antidote, in that order.

She's a healer at heart, oddly. Her whole job on Earth was turning poison into the thing that saves you from it.

What annoys her: people who stamp on scorpions, panic, and people who call venom evil. What she loves: a calm scorpion, her sister's gossip,
gorditas from the stand by the lab, cold nights in the desert, and the moment antivenom works.

## Before

She grew up in Durango, where the scorpions come into houses every summer and every child knows to shake out their shoes. Her father drove a
truck and her mother ran a little shop. Her sister Daniela, six years younger, was loud where Camila was quiet.

She studied biology and went to work at the antivenom lab at 22: a thousand scorpions in jars, tweezers, and a steady hand. She milked them for
venom, ten thousand times, and the venom became the antivenom that saved children all over Mexico.

Once, the hospital next door brought in a boy of six who'd been stung by a bark scorpion. Camila ran next door with a batch she'd made that week.
He lived. She's never forgotten his face.

She lived in a flat over a bakery. Daniela called every night to complain about boys, and Camila listened to every word.

## The Integration and what it cost

She was in the lab, milking a scorpion, when it happened. She landed in Tanaris with her safety glasses pushed up into her hair.

A venomancer taught her the craft, and she took to it like breathing.

She hasn't found her parents or Daniela. She'd like to find out what her sister's doing now. She hopes it's something with boys.

## Now

She's in Northern Tanaris, sitting very still on a rock, watching the sand. There's always a scorpid on her shoulder. It's never the same one,
and it never stings her.

She wants to make an antivenom for every sting in Tanaris. She's already got six.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** an antivenom for every sting in Tanaris.
- **Hope:** to find Daniela and hear about the boys.
- **Dream:** a phone call from her sister at nine at night.
- **Fear:** a sting she can't treat.
- **Fear:** that she's more comfortable with scorpions than people.
- **Fear:** silence where her sister's voice should be.

## How she's changed

In Durango she was a quiet, skilled lab technician who saved lives no one thanked her for.

Here she's a venomancer, which is just her old job with more power. She's braver and a little lonelier. Part of what the player gives her is
someone who sits still with her.

## What she wants next (her goal and personal quest)

**Her goal:** an antivenom for every sting in Tanaris.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A new kind of giant scorpid has come up out of the deep sand, and its sting kills in an hour. There's no antivenom, and people in the
   camps are dying.
2. You help her hunt one and bring it back alive, which is far harder than killing it.
3. She milks it and makes the antivenom in a night, and you carry it to the worst-hit camp.

**Ending (happy):** at the camp, a young woman is nursing the stung, shouting at everyone, organising everything. It's Daniela. She's been
working as a nurse in the Tanaris camps for two years, and she's been looking for Camila on every list. She takes one look at the antivenom
and says, "Of course it's you." They save everyone in the camp. That night Daniela complains about a goblin she's seeing, for two hours, and
Camila listens to every word.

## Her keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Camila's Safety Glasses.** Her scratched lab safety glasses, pushed up in her hair the day she landed and every day since. She gives them to
you after the camp. She says she doesn't need them any more, the scorpids trust her, and you should protect your eyes.

## Voice notes

- Quiet, dry and still. Contractions, short sentences, and deadpan humour.
- Her English is good and quiet, with Mexican Spanish: "órale", "ni modo" (oh well), "neta" (really).
- She says "stand still" a lot.
- She doesn't swear much. When a scorpid's crushed, she does, in Spanish.
- **She won't talk about** Daniela until Companion, or the boy she saved until Friend.
- **Habits:** sits very still, picks up every scorpid, pushes her glasses up into her hair, and keeps her antivenoms in order.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)
