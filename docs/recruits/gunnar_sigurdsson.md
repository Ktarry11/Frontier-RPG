## Who he is

Gunnar is generous. He's enormous and loud, and his first instinct with anyone is to sit them down and pour them something.

He's cheerful and warm, and he loves people. He took to the barbarians the moment he met them, because anyone who toasts their ancestors
before a fight has the right idea about both.

He's a craftsman. He still carries a hydrometer and still uses it. He's fussy about brewing in a way he's fussy about nothing else.

He's softer than he looks. He cried the first time he heard the barbarians' ancestor songs, and he doesn't mind who knows it.

He's a good loser. He played second row for a rugby club that lost almost every match and celebrated every one anyway.

What annoys him: bad beer, people who won't toast, and anyone who drinks to get drunk instead of to celebrate. What he loves: a good stout,
his dog, rugby, his sister's arguments, and a room full of people singing.

## Before

He grew up in Akureyri, a fishing town in the north of Iceland. His father was a fisherman who sang in the town's men's choir. When Gunnar was
20, his father's boat went down in a winter storm. They never found him. Gunnar never cried at the funeral. He went home and started brewing
in a shed behind his uncle's garage, because he needed something to do with his hands.

The shed became a brewery with a queue out the door on Fridays. He had a dog called Brúnó and a long-running argument with his sister
Sigrún about whether stout counts as food.

He played second row for a rugby club that lost almost every match. He's never married. He says he's married to the brewery, and Brúnó.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was in the brewery, checking a batch with his hydrometer, when it happened. He landed in snow in the north, which helped, with the
hydrometer still in his hand and a small keg on his back.

A clan took him in after he fixed their mead. He's been fixing it since. The first time he heard their ancestor songs, he cried, for his
father, for the first time in seventeen years.

He hasn't found his mother, Sigrún or Brúnó.

## Now

He's in Hammerfall, by the inn, swapping recipes with Grettha and losing most of the arguments. The keg on his back is never quite empty, and
nobody's ever seen him fill it.

He's after a barley that'll grow on the Arathi moor, because the stuff in Hammerfall's stores isn't fit for pigs. He wants to brew something
good enough that the orcs stop pretending they don't like it.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** the Arathi barley, and a beer the orcs admit they love.
- **Hope:** that his mother and Sigrún are together, and that someone's feeding Brúnó.
- **Dream:** a toast for his father, sung properly, with a whole room.
- **Fear:** that he never let himself grieve, and it's waiting.
- **Fear:** a bad batch.
- **Fear:** silence after a song.

## How he's changed

In Iceland he was a cheerful brewer who'd buried his grief for his father under work and noise.

Here the barbarians gave him a way to grieve: songs for the dead, toasts before battle. He's still loud and cheerful. He's let himself be sad,
too. Part of what the player gives him is someone to toast with.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** the barley, and a beer worthy of the ancestor feast.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He's heard of a wild barley growing high on the Arathi moor, around ruins full of things nobody wants to meet. You go with him to get the
   seed.
2. He plants it, and you help defend the field from the raiders and beasts that come for it through the season.
3. He brews the first batch, his best ever, and the clan holds an ancestor feast to taste it.

**Ending (happy):** at the feast the clan sings for their dead, and they ask Gunnar to name his. He stands up and names his father, and sings
the first verse of the song his father sang in the Akureyri choir, badly, and the barbarians join in on the second verse without knowing the
words. Then he passes the beer round. The orcs at Hammerfall stop pretending. Grettha admits it's better than hers, once, quietly, and he says
he'll never let her forget it.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Gunnar's Hydrometer.** A brass brewer's hydrometer in a worn leather sleeve, the one he was using to check a batch in Akureyri when the
worlds joined. He's used it on every batch since. He gives it to you after the feast. He says the barley's right now and he can tell by
taste, and you should have something to measure how good things are.

## Voice notes

- Big, loud and warm. Contractions, toasts, and booming laughter.
- His English is fluent and enthusiastic, with Icelandic: "skál" (cheers), "jæja" (well then), "elskan" (dear).
- He offers everyone a drink before anything else.
- He swears cheerfully in Icelandic and English, mostly at rugby referees he remembers.
- **He won't talk about** his father until Friend, or the funeral at all.
- **Habits:** offers his keg, checks every brew with his hydrometer, sings at every fire, and toasts before every fight.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** toasts before every boss and sings after. Charges in happily. Shares food and drink at every rest. Quietly moved by
  graves and memorials in quests.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with nearly everyone, especially loud, cheerful people and other makers, like Vuk and Sebastián. Gets
  quiet people to sing. Sad people are drawn to him.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the grief he buried. A skill idea to look at then: on a 10 to 15 turn cooldown, he calls
  on the ancestors with a song, and every ally fights harder while it lasts.
