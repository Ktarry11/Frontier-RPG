## Who he is

Orlando talks constantly and listens harder than anybody notices. A barber is a priest with better gossip, he says, and
everybody tells him everything.

He's warm, loud and funny. He compliments people outrageously and means every word. He says "with love" after every insult.

He builds rooms. Everything good in his life on Earth came from having a place where people sat down and talked, so the first
thing he did here was make one. Wherever the party stops becomes a shop for as long as they're there.

The spirits took to him immediately, and he treats them the way he treated his chair: sit down, tell me what's wrong, I'm
going to fix it and we're going to talk the whole time.

He's hiding something from himself. He's had three offers of a building for a proper shop. He's turned all three down and
can't explain why to anybody, including himself, and his hands shook on the last one.

What annoys him: rude people, bad haircuts, silence, and anyone who won't let him help. What he loves: salsa on a radio, a
full shop on a Saturday, his mother's arroz con gandules, and the moment someone who walked in miserable walks out laughing.

## Before

He grew up in Santurce, in a house full of aunts and cousins, the middle child of a bus mechanic and a school cook. His little
sister Yari was his best friend. He started sweeping up at a barbershop at 14 and was cutting hair by 18.

He opened his own shop at 22: two chairs, a radio always on, and a Tuesday regular called Don Beni who came in for nineteen
years, eight of them at Orlando's shop, and never once needed a haircut. Everybody told Orlando everything. He knew every
wedding, every fight and every secret in Santurce, and never repeated one.

He'd been seeing a nurse called Gabriel for a year before the Integration. It was going well. Gabriel had started leaving a
toothbrush at his place.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was cutting Don Beni's hair, which didn't need cutting, when it happened. He landed on Fenris Isle with his pearl-handled
straight razor in his sleeve pocket.

He was running a chair inside a month, in a tent, with a borrowed blade. It was the most effective adjustment anybody made,
and the reason is simple: he rebuilt the room.

His whole family were on the island. He says they're fine. He has a reason he believes this. A Puerto Rican trader told him
once that the island came through whole, somewhere across the sea. It's thin, and everybody lets him have it.

## Now

He lives at the Shadowpine Altar on Fenris Isle, where the trolls taught him the spirits and let him keep a chair by the fire.
He cuts anybody's hair, any race, free, and he's never been refused twice by the same person.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to get home to the island.
- **Hope:** a proper shop, with a floor and a mirror.
- **Dream:** a Sunday at his mother's table with the radio on and Yari stealing his food.
- **Fear:** that if he takes a building, it means he isn't going home.
- **Fear:** a room that goes quiet.
- **Fear:** that his family is fine without him.

## How he's changed

In Santurce he was the heart of a neighbourhood, surrounded by family, happy and a little complacent.

Here he's rebuilt the room again and again, wherever he goes, and found out how much he needs it. He's braver and more
spiritual than he was. Part of what the player gives him is a regular.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find out if he can get home.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He tracks down the Puerto Rican trader who told him the island came through. The trader is in Booty Bay, and he's got
   more to say.
2. The island did come through whole, family and all, far across the sea. No ship has ever reached it. The sea between is
   full of things that sink ships, and the trader's ship was the only one that ever got close.
3. Orlando insists on trying. You go with him to the farthest point any ship will sail, and the sea turns you back, and nearly
   sinks you.

**Ending (sad):** he can't get home. His family is probably alive, all of them, on an island he can never reach. He stands at
the rail for a long time. Then he goes back to Fenris Isle and takes the next building he's offered. His hands don't shake.
He calls the shop Casa Rivera, after his mother's house, and keeps a chair empty in the corner for Don Beni. He says
everybody who sits in it is family now, because that's the only family he can reach.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Orlando's Straight Razor.** A barber's straight razor with a pearl handle, the one he used every day in Santurce for eleven
years, and had in his sleeve the day he landed. He gives it to you once Casa Rivera is open. He says he's got a new one for
the shop, and this one cut the hair of everybody he loved.

## Voice notes

- Fast, warm and loud. Contractions, endless chat, compliments, and "with love".
- His English is good with a Puerto Rican accent and Spanish everywhere: "mi amor", "nene", "wepa", "ay bendito".
- He talks to the spirits exactly like customers.
- He swears in Spanish cheerfully and often.
- **He won't talk about** the island until Companion, or Gabriel until Friend.
- **Habits:** fixes everyone's hair, hums salsa, sets up a chair wherever he stops, and touches the razor in his sleeve.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** chats through every fight and talks to the spirits out loud. Makes a little shop at every camp. Loves
  a full party and hates silence. Gets upset by loneliness in quest givers.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with nearly everyone, and draws out quiet people. Bonds with fellow talkers and hairdressers,

- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is not being able to go home. A skill idea to look at then: a passive where
  allies near him at the start of a fight are steadier and harder to frighten.
