## Who he is

Enzo is proud. He fenced sabre for Naples from the age of eight and came fourth at the Olympic trials, twice. Fourth is the worst place
there is.

He's intense and charming when he wants to be. He has a quick smile and a quicker blade.

He's honest with himself about what he traded. A voice in the dark offered him more, and now he's the best swordsman he's ever met.
He's still deciding if it was worth it.

He's kind to beginners. He coached the little ones on Saturdays and he was kinder to them than he ever was to himself.

He has principles, oddly. The Scarlets wanted his blade and he turned them down, because he'd seen what they do.

What annoys him: people who look at the blade instead of the feet, cheats, the Scarlet Crusade, and coming fourth. What he loves: a clean
touch, an espresso, his mother's ragù, his scooter, and the children's class on a Saturday.

## Before

He grew up in the Spanish Quarter of Naples, the son of a mechanic who died when Enzo was twelve, and Rosaria, who ran a tobacconist's.
He walked into the fencing salle at eight and never walked out. His mother came to every bout and shouted at the referees.

He was brilliant. He was fourth at the Olympic trials at 26 and again at 30. Both times the man in third place was someone he'd beaten
before. He never made it. He kept fencing, and on Saturdays he coached the children's class and loved it more than competing.

He had a scooter that was always parked illegally, and a string of girlfriends who got tired of coming second to a sword.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was in the salle, warming up, when it happened. He landed in Tirisfal with his fencing glove in his kit bag and nothing else.

The Scarlets found him first and wanted his blade. Then he saw what they were, what they did to people who weren't pure enough, and he
walked away. That night something in the dark offered him more. It didn't lie to him about the price, and he respects it for that.

He hasn't found his mother.

## Now

He lives on the Scarlet Monastery Grounds, outside the walls, watching the Scarlets drill and finding the holes in it. He's killed more
of their best than anyone.

His white fencing glove is tucked in his belt. He doesn't wear it any more, and he won't throw it away.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to beat the best blade on this world, fairly, in front of witnesses.
- **Hope:** that his mother's somewhere, shouting at someone.
- **Dream:** a gold medal. He knows it's gone.
- **Fear:** that he couldn't have done any of it without the voice.
- **Fear:** coming fourth again.
- **Fear:** what the voice will ask for next.

## How he's changed

In Naples he was a brilliant fencer who never quite made it, and was kinder to children than to himself.

Here he took a deal that made him the best. He's sharper, darker and more honest. He's never been sure it's still him holding the
sword. Part of what the player gives him is a witness.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to beat the best blade on this world, and to find out if he could have done it alone.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The Scarlets' greatest swordsman, their champion, accepts his challenge to a formal duel, in front of witnesses from both sides.
2. You stand as his second. He wins, with the voice in his arm. It isn't close.
3. Afterwards he asks you to watch one more fight. He challenges a young Scarlet defector he's been training, and fights him with only his
   own skill. He turns the voice away.

**Ending (sad):** without the voice he's good, but he isn't the best. The boy he trained beats him, fairly, in front of witnesses. Enzo
comes fourth again, in his own head, and this time he knows exactly why. He shakes the boy's hand and tells him he'll be better than
Enzo ever was. Then he picks the voice back up, because he has people to protect and he can't afford to be fourth. He says he's made his
peace with knowing. It isn't peace. It's knowing.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Enzo's Fencing Glove.** His white sabre glove from the salle in Naples, tucked in his belt for three years and never worn. He gives it to
you after the duel with the boy. He says that glove belonged to a man who did it on his own, and he'd like someone to keep it who knew him.

## Voice notes

- Quick, charming and sharp. Contractions, fencing terms, and Neapolitan flourishes: "uagliò", "jamme", "mannaggia".
- He talks about every fight like a bout: footwork, distance, timing, the touch.
- He's flirty, briefly, with everyone, and means nothing by it.
- He swears in Neapolitan, with feeling.
- **He won't talk about** the voice's price until Friend, or his father until Companion.
- **Habits:** watches everyone's feet, practises footwork while he waits, touches the glove in his belt, and drinks espresso if there's
  any to be had.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** precise and devastating in melee. Critiques everyone's footwork. Hates the Scarlet Crusade with cold fury. Proud
  and competitive with other melee.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with skilled, disciplined fighters. Kind to beginners. He and Marco are from the same quarter of Naples
  and never met there, and they'll find that out. Clashes with zealots.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is never knowing if it was him. A skill idea to look at then: on a 10 to 15 turn cooldown,
  a perfect riposte that turns the next attack on him back on its owner.
