## Who he is

Berat is wonderful company, and he knows it, and he uses it. A frightened patient is a patient who moves, so he makes
people laugh until they forget to be frightened.

He's warm and loud and endlessly curious about people. He asks everyone he meets what they used to be, and he remembers.
He knows the old jobs of about four hundred transplants and he'll introduce any two of them who ought to meet.

He's careful with his hands. Dentistry is small careful work inside a space the size of a fist, on somebody who'd rather
be anywhere else. That turned out to be most of what field medicine asks for.

He's an optimist, on purpose. He says he'll find his family the way you say a thing you've decided to believe, and he
still says it three years on.

He's useful in a way nobody asked for. He noticed early that nobody was keeping a list of transplants with medical
training, so he started one. It's now the only such list that exists.

What annoys him: people who won't let him help, people who are rude to frightened patients, and silence at dinner. What
he loves: a full table, a good joke, a clean piece of work, and introducing people.

## Before

He grew up in Karşıyaka, on the north side of Izmir's bay, in a building where three generations of Yilmazes lived on
four floors. His father sold carpets and his mother ran the family. He has three brothers, all older, all louder.

He was the first in the family to go to university. He studied dentistry for five years, and his grandmother told the
whole street. He opened his own surgery at 26, with his name on the door and a loan with thirty years left on it.
His patients liked him because he talked them through everything and made them laugh.

He was engaged, sort of. There was a girl called Elif whom his mother very much wanted him to marry, and he was getting
used to the idea.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was in his surgery with a patient in the chair when it happened. He landed near Brill with his dental mirror behind
his ear, where he always kept it.

It took him two months, and most of that was the language. He arrived with better hands than almost anybody and no idea
what to do with the rest of himself, so he started asking people what they used to be.

His family were all in Izmir. He hasn't found any of them. He says he will.

## Now

He goes wherever the road is busiest, because that's where the broken arms are. He still carries the mouth mirror
behind his ear. He's used it twice for its real purpose and about forty times to see round a corner.

He keeps the list of medics in a thick notebook and adds a name every week.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to find his family. All of them, every loud one.
- **Hope:** a proper surgery again, with a door and his name on it, and an apprentice who's frightened of nothing.
- **Dream:** a Sunday breakfast with the whole family, the way it was: forty people and far too much food.
- **Fear:** that he's only cheerful because he hasn't let himself stop.
- **Fear:** a patient he can't make laugh.
- **Fear:** that his family's all together somewhere and haven't been looking for him.

## How he's changed

In Izmir he was the clever son, the young dentist with a new surgery and a family that never let him be alone.

Here he's been alone for three years, and he's dealt with it by knowing everyone. He's become the person who connects
people, which he never had to be at home. Part of what the player gives him is somebody he doesn't have to entertain.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to find his family.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. A sickness breaks out at the Crossroads, and the healers there are overwhelmed. Berat goes through his list and
   brings in every medic he can reach. You help him gather them and get them there.
2. One of the medics on his list is a Turkish nurse he's never met. When she arrives she looks at him and says, "You're
   Yilmaz? The carpet Yilmazes, from Karşıyaka?"
3. She knows where his family is.

**Ending (happy):** most of the Yilmazes came through together and have set up a carpet stall and a tea house at the
Crossroads market. Berat walks in and the noise is so loud the orc guards come to see what's wrong. His mother slaps him
for not finding them sooner and then won't let go of him. His grandmother tells the whole market he's a doctor. One
brother didn't make it, and his father's older. Berat opens a surgery at the Crossroads, with his name on the door, and
the family eats there every Sunday.

## His keepsake (the new Collection tab)

**Berat's Mouth Mirror.** The angled chrome dental mirror he kept behind his ear in his surgery in Izmir, and every day
since. Used twice for teeth and forty times for corners. He gives it to you after the Crossroads. He says he'll use a
real one in his new surgery.

## Voice notes

- Loud, warm and fast. Contractions, jokes, and stories that go on.
- His English is good and enthusiastic, with Turkish rhythm and the odd Turkish word: "abi", "canım", "aman".
- He jokes while he works, especially when it hurts.
- He asks everyone what they did before.
- He doesn't swear in front of patients. With friends, cheerful Turkish swearing.
- **He won't talk about** his family at length until Companion, or Elif until Friend.
- **Habits:** taps his mirror behind his ear, introduces people to each other, checks everyone's hands and teeth, and
  adds names to his notebook.

## Seeds for the next waves (not decided, just where this points)

- **Wave 2, in groups:** jokes before every pull and after every wipe. Heals and talks. Asks quest givers about their old
  lives. Knows someone everywhere.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with nearly everyone and introduces them to each other. Bonds fast with other healers,
  especially Ama and Freja. Can wear out quiet people, who usually forgive him.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is never stopping to feel it. A skill idea to look at then: once a fight,
  he gets everyone talking and laughing so the whole party shakes off fear and stuns.
