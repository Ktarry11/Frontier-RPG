## Who he is

Raúl is loud, cocky and better company than he lets on. He takes stupid risks with a grin, and never once with somebody else's neck.

He's never met a horse he couldn't talk round, and he talks to raptors the same way. He's generous with his time, his food and his stories, and stingy with nothing except the one story that matters.

What annoys him: anybody stuck up, anybody formal and anybody who calls rodeo cruel without ever having been near a ranch. What he loves: a loud crowd, a good count, a cold beer and his mom's kitchen shelf.

## Before

He grew up in a trailer outside Tucson and rode the circuit from Prescott to Cheyenne. He had a pickup with a hitch and a saddle worth more than the trailer. He's got a silver buckle for every one he won and a scar for every one he didn't.

His mom never watched him ride, not once. She kept every buckle on a shelf in her kitchen anyway, polished, with the date written underneath.

## The Integration and what it cost

In 2019 a bull called Diablo Blanco threw him and came round for him on the ground, and Chuy stepped in front of it. Chuy took the horn. He walked again, but he never fought another bull, and Raúl has been riding to prove it was worth it ever since.

He came through on the ground wearing his biggest buckle, the national one, and climbed the tree because someone told him the trolls respect a man who keeps his trophies, and it turns out they do. He rode a raptor on a dare in his first month and stayed on for nine seconds.

## Now

He's in the middle of the Great Settlement's market most days, polishing his buckle and arguing about technique with any headhunter who'll stop. The headhunters adopted him in a week.

He tells everyone about the raptor. He doesn't tell anyone about Chuy.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** to ride a wild wyvern off the Upper Boughs and stay on.
- **Hope:** to win something on this world worth putting on a shelf.
- **Dream:** to tell his mom he finally gets why she never came.
- **Fear:** that Chuy's horn wasn't worth it.
- **Fear:** being the reason somebody else gets hurt.

## How he's changed

At home the next ride was always the answer. Here he's started to notice he never asked the question. Part of what the player gives him is somebody shouting the count who isn't there for the show.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to stay on the wild wyvern, and to stop riding to pay a debt nobody asked him to pay.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. There's a wild wyvern at the top of the tree nobody can ride. He asks you to be at the landing, shouting the count.
2. On the climb he tells you about Diablo Blanco, and Chuy, and the horn. He asks you to make the count loud.
3. He rides it, all the way down to the landing.

**Ending (happy):** he stays on, nine, ten, he doesn't know how long, and when he gets off he doesn't want to celebrate. He wants to call Chuy. That's when he knows he doesn't need to prove anything anymore, and he hangs up the big buckle.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Raúl's National Buckle.** A big silver rodeo buckle with a bull rider in relief and a national title round the rim, polished thin at the edges. He was wearing it when he came through. He gives it to you at the landing, and says you're the only shelf he trusts.

## Voice notes

- Arizona English with Spanish all through it. "Friend" to everyone, "órale" when he's impressed, "ándale" to hurry you and "no manches" when he can't believe it.
- He counts seconds out loud when he's nervous.
- His fighting voice is a loud, happy holler.
- **He won't talk about** Chuy until Friend, or why his mom never came until Companion.
- **Habits:** polishes the buckle, sizes up every animal in sight and bets on things that can't be bet on.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** bold in a fight, loud about it and hungry for the big one. Likes bosses and beasts, dislikes grinding and a full party.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with funny, sporty people and anyone reckless enough to keep up. He clashes with formal, posh types and teases them until they laugh.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is Chuy's horn. On a 14 turn cooldown he picks the biggest thing in the fight and stays on it, and the hit always lands hard.
