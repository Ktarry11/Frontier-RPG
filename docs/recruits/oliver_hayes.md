## Who he is

Oliver notices everything about people and says only the gentle half of it. He's kind, careful and a little shy, and he can't walk past a pair of glasses that need adjusting.

He hates getting anything wrong, and he double checks the smallest things. He's quietly brave when it counts, and he'd never call it that.

What annoys him: loud people in small rooms, being rushed and anyone who says nothing can be done. What he loves: a cup of tea, his choir, his cat asleep in the frames display and the moment someone sees clearly for the first time.

## Before

He had a little shop with a green door on a steep street in Clifton, up the hill by the suspension bridge. He had a test chart, a cardigan and a kettle that never got a rest. He sang in a choir on Thursdays, badly, at the back, which is where they put him.

He once caught something serious behind a schoolboy's eye during a routine test, and the boy's mother sent him a card every Christmas after.

## The Integration and what it cost

His dad lost his sight to glaucoma when Oliver was fifteen. Nobody caught it in time, and it was entirely catchable. That's why he became an optician. His dad used to ask him to describe things, and he got very good at it.

He woke on a sky island with his spectacles still on. The lensmaster at Lenswatch asked him about eyes for nine hours on his first day, and Oliver answered every question and then asked for a cup of tea. The light came the first time he held a lens up to a wounded guard, and it went straight through the glass into the wound.

## Now

He grinds lenses with the lensmaster and argues happily about light. He sees the Earthborn about their eyes once a week, because nobody else up here knows how.

The lensmaster's apprentice is going blind from the crystal light, the same slow way his dad did. The Aetherians don't think anything can be done, and Oliver can't stop thinking about her.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a proper pair of glasses for an Aetherian, which nobody's ever tried.
- **Hope:** his cardigan back, and he knows that isn't going to happen.
- **Dream:** to describe this whole world to his dad.
- **Fear:** missing something he should have caught.
- **Fear:** being told nothing can be done, and believing it.

## How he's changed

At home he caught things early so nobody else would lose what his dad lost. Here the light goes through his lenses into people, and the stakes feel bigger. Part of what the player gives him is somebody who helps him try, even when he might be wrong.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to save the apprentice's sight, and to stop carrying his dad's eyes alone.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. He thinks he can make the apprentice a pair of glasses, but he needs a lens cut from crystal deep in the Keel. He asks you to help him get it.
2. On the way he tells you about his dad, and the test nobody did. He brings the callipers, and you bring everything else.
3. Back at Lenswatch he grinds the lens and fits the glasses.

**Ending (happy):** she puts the glasses on and reads the lens plates across the workshop, and then she cries, and so does he, and so does the lensmaster, who'll deny it. That night he tells her about his dad, and she asks him to describe Bristol. He does, for two hours.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Oliver's Pen-Torch.** A slim pen-torch with its clip bent from years in a breast pocket, and it's still bright. It's looked into a thousand eyes, and once it caught something in time. He gives it to you after the apprentice sees, and says you helped him catch it again.

## Voice notes

- Bristol English, soft and polite. "Sorry", "lovely", "cheers" and "gert" when something's very big.
- He apologises before saying anything important.
- His fighting voice is calm and clinical, like he's doing a test.
- **He won't talk about** his dad until Companion.
- **Habits:** looks into everyone's eyes, cleans his spectacles when he's nervous and makes tea for anybody upset.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** careful in a fight and quietly brave when it counts. Likes quests and dungeons, dislikes grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with patient, gentle people and scholars, and with anyone musical. He keeps away from loud, bossy types.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is his dad's eyes. On a 13 turn cooldown he catches what everyone else missed, takes every curse and magic off the party, and heals the harm it was still going to do.
