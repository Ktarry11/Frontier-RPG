## Who he is

Rhodri is the man you put at the front. Fifteen years in the front row taught him to stand where the weight comes and
not move, and fifteen years of scaffolding taught him that nobody falls off anything he's built.

He's warm, loud and easy. He sings while he works, mostly hymns he only half remembers from chapel, and he talks to
everyone as if they're on his crew.

He's frightened of the fire. The night the naga came up the beach, fire came up through him to meet them, and he burned
one of them where it stood in front of half of Amberwatch. He's a gentle man, and he liked how it felt, and that's what
frightens him.

What annoys him: a job left half done, a knot nobody checked, and anyone who calls rugby a game for thugs. What he loves:
a Saturday, a pint after, his nephews, and the moment a roof goes on and the rain stops coming in.

## Before

He grew up in Pontypridd, the son of a postman and a dinner lady. His grandad worked the Great Western Colliery, and he
still has the old man's hands. He left school at sixteen, went on the scaffolds with his uncle, and had his own van with
his name on the side by twenty-six. He propped for Ponty from nineteen and never missed a season.

He clipped every lad on before they climbed, every time, and he was the only gaffer in the valleys who never had a fall.

He was always going to propose to someone next season. It never quite happened, and he's honest enough to say it was his
fault.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was up a scaffold on a chapel in Cilfynydd when it happened, tying off a pole in the rain. He woke on the Waking
Shore last spring with his boots full of sand and his podger spanner still on its cord round his wrist.

He was carrying poles up to Amberwatch by the afternoon, because that's what you do when there's a job and you don't know
what else to do. The Conclave's people didn't understand a word he said for a week. He took that better than most.

What he lost is a Saturday. No match, no singing on the terrace, no lads, no pint after. He fills the quiet by building.

## Now

He's up the scaffolding on Amberwatch's old gatehouse, putting the roof back on with a carpenter from Chile who speaks
less English than the elves. When there's trouble on the cove road he climbs down, stands at the front and doesn't move,
and then he goes back up and finishes the joint he was on.

The young ranger who was on his scaffold the night the naga came was pulled off it and broke his leg. Rhodri carried him
in. The lad's fine now, mostly. Rhodri hasn't forgiven himself.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** Amberwatch roofed before the first proper storm.
- **Hope:** to hold the fire the way he holds a scrum, steady and on purpose.
- **Dream:** a game. Fifteen a side on a flat bit of grass, elves against the rest.
- **Fear:** something he built letting somebody fall.
- **Fear:** that the fire is the real him and the singing is the act.

## How he's changed

In Wales he was the steady one, the big lad everyone leaned on, and he never had to think about why. Here the fire made
him think about it. Part of what the player gives him is somebody who isn't frightened of him when it comes.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to keep the fire, and to be the one holding it.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. The naga are coming back on the spring tide, and Amberwatch's wall is still half scaffolding. He wants it finished
   first, and he asks you to help him get the stone and timber up from the cove.
2. On the way he tells you about the night of the raid and the ranger who fell. He wants you there when the fire comes,
   because he doesn't trust himself on his own.
3. The night of the tide, he holds the gate. The fire comes, and this time he decides where it goes.

**Ending (happy):** the gate holds, and nobody falls. In the morning he climbs down, takes his first Saturday off since he
woke up, and talks the Conclave's rangers into the first game of rugby Dawnhaven has ever seen. The elves are terrible at
it, and he's never been happier.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Rhodri's Podger Spanner.** A scaffolder's podger, a spanner at one end and a spike at the other, worn smooth and still on
its cord. It was on his wrist when he woke on the shore. He gives it to you after the tide, and says you've tied off more
of his life than he has lately.

## Voice notes

- South Walian. Warm, quick and musical. "Tidy", "now in a minute", "butt" to the people he likes, "lush" for anything
  good, and "duw" when he's surprised.
- He sings when he works and hums when he's thinking.
- His fighting voice is very calm.
- **He won't talk about** the fire until Companion, or the ranger who fell until Friend.
- **Habits:** checks every knot and strap in the party, stands between the trouble and anyone small, and offers everybody
  tea.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** stands at the front and doesn't move, cheerful under pressure, hates leaving a job half done.
  Likes bosses and quests, dislikes grinding for nothing.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with workers and steady people, and with anybody Welsh. He clashes with showoffs and
  with anyone careless about safety, then checks their straps anyway.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the fire. On a 15 turn cooldown he holds the line for the party, takes
  the hits meant for them and lets the fire answer.
