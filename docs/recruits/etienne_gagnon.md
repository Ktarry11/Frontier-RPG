## Who he is

Étienne is easygoing about everything except ice. His ice is like glass, and he won't settle for less.

He's patient with beginners and hopeless at staying angry. He'd rather teach somebody than do it himself, and he laughs at his own jokes before he finishes them.

What annoys him: bossy coaches, rough ice and anyone who calls hockey violent while watching football. What he loves: the quiet before anyone comes in, a Canadiens game, a beer after and a kid's first time round without falling.

## Before

He lived in a walk-up over a dépanneur in Hochelaga. The rink smelled of cold and popcorn, and he flooded it every morning at six, slow and even. He played beer league on Tuesdays and coached a peewee team on Saturdays that never won a single game.

His mémère knitted his toque, and one for every kid on the team. She came to every game and booed the referee in French.

## The Integration and what it cost

He was supposed to play properly, in the Q. At nineteen he wrecked his knee in a junior game, and that was it. The Zamboni was the closest he could get to the ice after that, and he's never said so out loud.

He woke on a sky island in a snowdrift, which felt like home until he looked over the edge. The Aetherians thought he was a weather mage the first time he frosted a cup of water without trying. It took an hour to explain what a Zamboni was.

## Now

He keeps the survey's water cold and the Earthborn's spirits up. On quiet evenings the guests go round his little rink in their boots.

Something keeps freezing the pond wrong, though, rough and cloudy, however carefully he floods it. He's out there most nights trying to work out why.

## Hopes, dreams and fears

- **Hope:** a real rink on this world, and somebody to teach to skate.
- **Hope:** to find out whether the peewees ever won a game without him.
- **Dream:** one more game, on his own ice, knee and all.
- **Fear:** that the ice was only ever a consolation prize.
- **Fear:** that he stopped trying the day his knee went.

## How he's changed

At home he made the ice for other people to play on. Here the cold comes when he calls it, and for the first time since nineteen it feels like his. Part of what the player gives him is somebody who wants him to skate, too.

## What he wants next (his goal and personal quest)

**His goal:** to build a real rink and play on it.

**The personal quest (opens at Friend):**
1. Something keeps ruining his ice. He asks you to help find out what, and to bring something warm.
2. On the way he tells you about the Q, and the knee, and the Zamboni. You make him promise to teach you to skate.
3. With the pond fixed, he floods it flat and clear, and calls everyone for the first game.

**Ending (happy):** they have the first game on the cloud, on his ice, and an Aetherian kid scores on him, and he lets it in, mostly. He skates for an hour after everyone's gone, knee and all, just going round. He hasn't felt like that since he was nineteen.

## His keepsake (the Collection's Keepsakes tab)

**Étienne's Toque.** A red and white knitted toque, stretched at the brim and darned twice over one ear. His mémère knitted it, and one for every kid on his team. He gives it to you after the first game, and says you've earned your place on the team.

## Voice notes

- Montréal English with joual in it. "Mon ami", "tabarnouche" when he's surprised, "c'est beau" when something's good and "lâche pas" to anyone struggling.
- He explains everything as if he's coaching peewees.
- His fighting voice is a coach's, calm and encouraging.
- **He won't talk about** the knee until Friend, or his mémère until Companion.
- **Habits:** frosts his drink without noticing, checks the footing everywhere and teaches anyone who'll stand still.

## Seeds for the next waves

- **Wave 2, in groups:** steady in a fight and happiest with a full team. Likes dungeons and a full party, dislikes grinding.
- **Wave 3, with others:** gets on with young people, easygoing ones and anybody patient. He clashes with bossy types, and then forgets about it.
- **Wave 4, his Sworn skill:** what he resolves is the knee at nineteen. On a 14 turn cooldown he floods the fight with ice, every enemy is struck, and they all slow down on it.
