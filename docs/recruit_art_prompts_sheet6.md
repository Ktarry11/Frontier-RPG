## Why these specs and these places

The roster has 99 recruits, and the gap was plain: **levels 45 to 52 had no tank at all, one melee and two healers.**
That's exactly the World Tree's tiers and the sky. So eleven of the sixteen go up the tree and into the sky, five of
them tanks and three healers, and the other four start the Aetherian start zone off with a full party, the healer first
(Kyle: "ensure a Healer recruit in the start of the zone").

- **The Aetherian start zone (1-24), four.** Earthborn the Aetherians found on the islands north of Aurelion (Kyle, 20
  Nov: the low-level zones are north of the capital, and the last of them runs into the city). A healer in the first
  map, then a tank, a melee and a ranged as the levels climb. Placed when those maps are built.
- **The World Tree, five.** Rootwater Isle, the Lower Boughs, the Great Settlement, the Middle Boughs and the Upper
  Boughs: live now.
- **The Sunward Crown and Lenswatch, three.** Two at the Annex with the survey's other guests, one at Lenswatch: live
  now.
- **The Celestial Expanse and Aurelion, four.** Placed when those are built.

Each spec here is a second copy of one that already has a companion somewhere else in the levels (nothing checks one
companion per spec, and every kit comes from its owner's own story anyway). Druid Feral stays untouched, as before.
