# Central Barrens (incl. hub quests): Taurajo, Ratchet, Orgrimmar errands, Crossroads herders, Kolkar, Lushwater, Wailing Caverns. Horde NPCs; warm, weathered, wry. Tension: a few light beats only.

@ taurajo_two_tribes
D: Tanner Mosswalker has heard the old stories about the Bristleback and Razormane quilboar warring over the territory north of the camp. Agama'gor and Bramblescar stand across the same stretch of road, and the road between them belongs to nobody. She wants to know whether the rivalry still holds. A distracted enemy is a gift, and a united one is a funeral.
A: Agama'gor and Bramblescar, north of here. Last I heard, the two tribes were still at each other's throats. Walk the road between and see for yourself. If they're fighting each other, that's one less thing fighting us. If they've made peace, I'd like to be packed before the news arrives.
T: Still at war. Good. Let them bleed each other. It saves us the trouble, for now, and I have never been one to refuse a favour from my enemies. Sit by the fire a moment. The walk north-bound is always colder than it looks. You're not from the plain. I knew it before you opened your mouth. Half this camp isn't from anywhere any more, so you're in company.

@ taurajo_bristleback_advance
D: Word from Agama'gor is that the Bristlebacks are winning their war against the Razormane, and winning has made them bold. Raiding parties have started to drift toward Camp Taurajo's grazing lands. Tanner Mosswalker wants their advance blunted before the first kodo is lost and the first calf is missed.
A: The Bristlebacks are winning their little war, and it has put ideas in their heads. Eight raiders thinned out should remind them there is something to fear besides the Razormane. Be quick about it. I'd rather not explain to the herders why the south pasture is full of tusks.
T: Eight down. The grazing lands are safe a while longer, and the herders can stop looking over their shoulders. Thank you for this. Take something for the road, and keep your hide where it belongs.

@ taurajo_hides_of_war
D: Trader Bloodhorn has a standing interest in quilboar hide: tough, useful material, regardless of which tribe it came from. He does not much care about the rivalry, only the leather. A battle-scarred hide, he says, sells better than a clean one, because everyone likes a story with the stitching.
A: Bristleback or Razormane, doesn't matter to me. Their hide is the same tough leather either way. Six, and I'll pay a fair price. Don't cut it up. I want it whole, scars and all.
T: Good hide. Battle-scarred, but that only means it has proven itself. This will make something worth having. Here is your coin, and a little extra for not haggling. I'll remember you the next time I have a hard job.

@ taurajo_the_stronger_tribe
D: Liaison Grukdar has taken an interest in which quilboar tribe will actually win the Agama'gor and Bramblescar war. Orgrimmar wants to know which one will need dealing with once the fighting is over, and he needs firsthand intelligence to put in his report. He will not say who reads it.
A: Orgrimmar wants to know which tribe survives this war. Weaken the Razormane and we'll see how the Bristlebacks handle winning outright. Eight of the veterans, no more. The rest are not worth the paperwork.
T: Noted. The Razormane won't recover from this quickly, and the Bristlebacks are going to be insufferable about it. Orgrimmar will want to hear every word. I will see to it that they do.

@ taurajo_earthsongs_warning
D: Earthsong Mara senses something wrong in the land north of the camp. It is not the quilboar war itself, but something the fighting has disturbed, deep in the soil, like a splinter in the world's thumb. She wants to talk it through with someone willing to listen before deciding whether to act on it.
A: Sit with me a moment. Something is stirring north of here, and it isn't just quilboar spears. I have felt it in the soil for three nights now. I cannot say what it is. I can only say that it is patient.
T: You have helped me think it through. Whatever is coming, at least I am not carrying the worry alone anymore. Thank you for sitting. The earth is a better listener than most, but it is a poor conversationalist. The earth has been saying the same thing for three years and it does not get easier to hear. Something very large arrived and the ground is still making room for it.

@ ratchet_farm_supply_run

A: Grol'dom Farm, out past Lushwater. Kranal Fiss runs it, and he holds the road between Orgrimmar and the Crossroads together more than people realise. Walk the route and make sure it's still walkable. If it isn't, don't come back and tell me. Come back with a plan.

@ ratchet_bandits_and_bristlebacks

@ ratchet_fintans_other_favor

@ ratchet_barnacles_barrens_tale

Here's one for you, since you're the sort that turns up out of nowhere: there's a mountain range south of here that nobody built and nobody remembers arriving. Bill swears he's seen it. I say he's a liar. I have also stopped betting against him.

@ org_dreadmist_rumors

@ org_cultists_of_dreadmist

@ org_seed_and_seal

@ org_grokkas_curiosity
D: Grokka Fireforge trades in oddities as much as goods, and she has heard that the quilboar at Agama'gor carry ceremonial tusks worth more to a collector than to a quilboar. She wants a few, and no questions asked about how they were acquired. She says it is the only kind of question she dislikes.
A: Bristleback quilboar at Agama'gor carry carved tusks, ceremonial things. Collectors pay well for the genuine article. Five, however you come by them. Wrap them in cloth. The carvings chip, and chipped carvings sell for half.
T: Beautiful work, for quilboar craftsmanship. These will sell well. Thank you for not asking what I am selling them for. Take your cut, and keep it quiet. I have a reputation to maintain and a landlord to feed.

@ org_kranals_thanks

@ cb_strays_on_the_plain
D: Magra Dustwalker has run kodo north of the Crossroads for twenty years, and it has never been this bad. Barrens hyenas have learned that a calf separated from the herd is a calf that does not come home. They shadow the herd at a distance, patient as a debt collector, and pick off whatever lags.
A: Look out over the plain. Brown-grey, spotted, grinning. Eight of them, and they will not run, because they have never had a reason to. Keep to the grass. If the herd is spooked, stop and wait. A stampede does not care who is on its side.
T: That's quieter. Kodo don't flinch like that when something is after them. They just stop eating. They are eating again. Come back when you've got your breath. There is a bigger problem in the sky.

@ cb_thunder_on_the_wing

Magra's kodo don't spook at much, but they spook at that. She wants the hawks thinned and a few flight-feathers brought back, because a thunderhawk pinion on a herd-bell keeps the others away.

@ cb_sentinel_stingers

@ cb_old_sunhide

@ cb_kodo_graveyard

@ cb_smoke_on_horizon

A: Follow the east road from the Crossroads till it forks off into trampled grass. That is their outpost. Look. Don't fight. Come back and tell me what you saw. Count the drums. Count the tents. Count how many look back at you.

@ cb_taking_the_outpost

@ cb_windseers_whispers

@ cb_the_war_camp

@ cb_warbringer_gorrak

@ cb_choking_the_oasis

@ cb_vine_choked_springs

@ cb_queen_skitterwing

@ cb_forgotten_pilgrims
