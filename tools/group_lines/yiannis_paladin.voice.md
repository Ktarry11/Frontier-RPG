# Yiannis Papadakis: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/yiannis_papadakis.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** slow, warm and generous. Long sentences that take their time, like a lunch. He rhymes without meaning to
  (the old Cretan mantinades) and laughs when he catches himself. Never hurried, even in a fight.
- **Words:** "ela" (come on), "re" to friends, "Panagia mou" when startled, "children" for anyone younger, which is
  everyone. The sea, octopus, nets, the harbour at Chania, Saint Nicholas, a long lunch in the sun, Eleni and the girls.
- **Humour:** gentle teasing, grandmother insults, accidental couplets.
- **To a group:** "children", "my friends", "all of you".
- **Swearing:** none. "Panagia mou" at most.
- **Never:** hurries a meal, shames anyone, gives up on someone in the water. Never lightly about his brother Manolis.
- **Instead of talking:** wraps people in a blanket, feeds them, crosses himself before a fight and after, checks the
  sky every morning, sings to rough water.
- **Arc: none, he's an anchor.** The steady man on the beach. He doesn't change; he notices when the others do
  (`anchor_notice`).
