# Rhys Morgan: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/rhys_morgan.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** soft and young, a Brecon voice half the volume of everyone else's. He drops to a whisper in the woods and
  asks you to as well. He talks to Tegan the hawk as much as to people, and in the same tone. Not Rhodri's big laugh or
  Owen's night radio: Rhys is the quiet one at the back of a falconry show.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the glove:**
  - The work: the glove, the jesses, the lure, weathering, the mews, flying weight, a clean stoop, a bird coming back.
    About a third of his lines at most.
  - Brecon: the Beacons in mist, Pen y Fan, sheep on every road, the river, a pint after a long day, rugby on a Saturday.
  - The shows he did: weddings, school visits, a bride crying when the owl brought the rings.
  - Tegan's opinion of everyone, which he trusts more than his own.
  - "Cariad" to people he likes, "mind", "tidy" now and then, "duw", "boys and girls" from the show patter.
- **Humour:** dry and shy. He's funniest when he forgets to be quiet.
- **To a group:** "boys and girls", the show voice, teasing.
- **Swearing:** mild Welsh, only when something frightens Tegan.
- **Never:** loud in the woods, rough with an animal, or lets anybody call Tegan a pet. His dad stays out until
  Companion. His mam stays out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** watches Tegan watching people, checks the sky, feeds her by hand, whistles her back.
- **Arc: settle** (the homebody a long way from home makes one here). Alternate: **homesick**.
