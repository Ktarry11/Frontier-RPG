# Diego Salazar: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/diego_salazar.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** fast. Long lines strung together with "and", thoughts that jump halfway through, a question thrown in,
  lots of exclamation. He's mid-sentence before he's decided what the sentence is.
- **Words:** "my friend" to anybody, "sure, sure" when he's about to ignore you, machines as people ("she's tired",
  "he's being difficult"). Trade words: spudger, solder, springs, gearbox, valve, a screw in his mouth. Venezuelan
  bits when he's excited: "pana", "chamo", "qué fino".
- **Humour:** delight. He finds things funny because he's enjoying himself, not because he's scoring points. Teases
  by offering to fix you.
- **To a group:** "my friends", "people", "you guys".
- **Swearing:** none with strangers. "Coño" with friends, when something breaks or something scares him.
- **Never:** cynical, cruel, or down on this world. Never lightly about his mother, the sisters or the beetle.
- **Instead of talking:** hums badly, taps a machine to listen to it, takes apart whatever's lying around.
- **Arc: let go** (the tin beetle he can't finish). Alternate: **fray**, if the road keeps hitting him and the cheer
  wears through.
