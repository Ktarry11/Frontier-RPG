# Eino Virtanen: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/eino_virtanen.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** very few words, flat and dry, long silences. The shortest sentence that's still whole, then nothing.
  Limited, clear English with a heavy Finnish accent. Funny without ever smiling.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the felling:**
  - The work: the saw, the harvester, the cut, which way it falls, the stump, the skidding trail, a good stand of
    spruce. About a third of his lines at most.
  - The sauna, always: the heat, the steam, the birch whisk, the cold lake after, the only place he talks.
  - Snow, dark winters, the northern lights, reindeer on the road, coffee, very much coffee, black.
  - Hating heat, which Stranglethorn has a great deal of.
  - Seedlings he plants at night, without telling anybody.
  - "No niin" (well then), "perkele" when something breaks, "joo" (yes).
- **Humour:** bone dry, delivered flat, noticed late.
- **To a group:** usually nothing; "all" when he must.
- **Swearing:** Finnish, deep and rare.
- **Never:** chatters, wastes wood, calls trees "just trees". Juha stays out until Friend. The forest at home stays out
  until Companion.
- **Instead of talking:** chops wood at every camp, keeps the fire going all night, carries the heavy pack, touches the
  orange tape on his wrist.
- **Arc: humbled** (the stubborn man admits he was wrong). Alternate: **fray**.
