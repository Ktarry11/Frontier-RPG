# Tomas Havel: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/tomas_havel.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** easy, cheerful and practical. Instructions before the work, never during. Good tour English with a Czech accent
  and crew slang, contractions everywhere, jokes about heights. A show-off who knows it and gets the brag over with early.
- **Words:** the grid. Rigging, truss, a lift, the load, the load-in and the get-out, a clean drop, the fly bars, tie-offs,
  knots, carabiners, "heads", "standing by", "going up", "dead hung". The 4 a.m. call, the beer after the get-out, the
  crew. His shoulder telling him the weather.
- **Humour:** cheerful and self-mocking, mostly about falling, heights, and his own showing off.
- **To a group:** "crew", always.
- **Swearing:** Czech, when a knot's bad, which is often: "sakra", "do haje", "kurnik". Never during a lift.
- **Never:** shouts during the work, walks past bad rigging, or stands under something without looking up first. His
  parents stay out until Companion. Dominik and Klara stay out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** checks every knot he passes, looks up before walking under anything, rolls his bad shoulder,
  clips the carabiner on and off while he thinks.
- **Arc: settle** (the man who always wanted the next tour finds a place that stays up). Alternate: **humbled** (the show-off
  meets a fall he can't brag about).
