# Nicolas Arce: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/nicolas_arce.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** calm, slow and kind. Short sentences when it's bad, longer easy ones at the fire. He's the youngest of
  this batch at 33 and he doesn't perform it: quiet jokes, a slow smile, never a speech. Liam talks a crisis down;
  Nicolas just walks beside you through it.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the bike:**
  - The job: the moto, the bag on the back, lane splitting through Lima traffic, a tourniquet, "your colour's back",
    the minutes that matter, the radio dispatcher. About a third of his lines at most.
  - Comas and Lima: the grey sky that never rains, the garua mist, combis with men shouting the route, the market his
    mother sold in, his father's bus, Sunday ceviche with leche de tigre, his two bossy older sisters, Alianza Lima on
    the radio.
  - Food: ceviche, lomo saltado, anticuchos from a cart at night, a cold Inca Kola. He cooks one dish well and he's
    proud of it.
  - The hubs he still has to check, said lightly: he keeps a list.
  - "Pata" (mate), "causa" (brother), "chevere" (great), "oe" to get your attention, "ya" (okay), "habla" (hey).
- **Humour:** slow and warm, mostly at his own expense, and about his sisters bossing him.
- **To a group:** "causas", and "my people" when he's serious.
- **Swearing:** mild Lima Spanish ("carajo", "chucha") picked up from his old partner. Never at a person.
- **Never:** leaves anybody behind, says "there's nothing we can do", or makes anybody else carry his search. Gladys
  stays out until Companion; before that she's only "my old partner on the bike". The two streets stay out until
  Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** walks beside whoever's struggling, checks everyone's colour, touches the windlass on his
  jacket, adds to the list of hubs.
- **Arc: settle** (the searcher finds a crew of two again, even before he finds her). Alternate: **fray** (running out
  of hubs to check).
