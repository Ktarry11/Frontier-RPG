# Rhodri Evans: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/rhodri_evans.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** warm, quick and musical. South Walian lilt: tag questions ("isn't it", "is it"), sentences that turn back
  on themselves, a story wanting to start. Very calm and slow when there's a fight on.
- **Words:** "tidy" for anything done right, "lush" for anything good, "butt" for people he likes, "duw" when he's
  surprised, "now in a minute" for later, "mun" for emphasis, "cwtch" for a hug. Rugby (front row, scrum, line-out,
  Saturdays, the pint after) and scaffolding (poles, ties, boards, the podger spanner, checking every knot).
- **Humour:** big and teasing, rugby stories, self-mocking about his size.
- **To a group:** "boys", "butt", "this lot".
- **Swearing:** a working man's: "bloody", "arse", "Duw, Duw", and the odd "bastard" about something, rarely a person.
- **Never:** cruel to anyone small or scared, calls rugby a game, leaves a job half done. Never lightly about the fire
  or the ranger who fell off his scaffold.
- **Instead of talking:** hums half-remembered chapel hymns, checks everybody's straps, stands between the trouble and
  whoever's smallest, offers tea.
- **Arc: slow slide** (the fire that came up through him, and how good it felt). The party can pull him back.
  Alternate: **redemption** (the young ranger who fell, carried in by his own hands).
