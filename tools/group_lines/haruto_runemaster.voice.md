# Haruto Nakamura: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/haruto_nakamura.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** formal, spare and exact. Few words, each one chosen, and full stops where other people would ramble.
  Careful English learned late, with Japanese politeness in it. Contractions, but never slang.
- **Words:** the studio. Ink, the stone it's ground on, paper, the brush, a stroke, the grip, form, the hand. Runes as
  strokes cut into the world, and a careless cut fails on whoever stands closest. Tea made properly. Early mornings.
  His master's eleven years of correction.
- **Humour:** so dry it takes an hour to land. A formal sentence with a joke folded inside it.
- **To a group:** "all of you", once in a while "students" when they've earned the teasing.
- **Swearing:** never. "That was careless" is the worst thing he says, and it lands hard.
- **Never:** raises his voice, hurries a stroke, or talks about himself unasked. Hrunn stays out until Companion. Kenji
  stays out until Sworn. His hand stays out of every line.
- **Instead of talking:** corrects someone's grip, grinds ink before dawn, stands very straight, pours tea in silence.
- **Arc: thaw** (the private master who learns to say what he feels out loud). Alternate: **let go** (the discipline handed
  on to someone else's hand).
