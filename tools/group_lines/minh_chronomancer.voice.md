# Minh Tran: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/minh_tran.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** quiet, calm and formal. Sequence first, always: what happened, then what happens next, then what he wants.
  Aviation English, clear and slightly clipped, contractions kept careful. Never lets his voice carry his pulse.
- **Words:** the radar room. Scope, sector, traffic, separation, vectors, holding pattern, handover, the frequency,
  "say again", "copy", "stand by", "you have control". Iced coffee, a clear sky, the night shift, three hours' sleep.
  Time slips in the riverbed, water from years ago.
- **Humour:** quiet and slightly formal, when he's relaxed, which is rarely. An incident report about something silly.
- **To a group:** "everyone", and "all stations" when he's teasing.
- **Swearing:** never. When something goes wrong he gets calmer, and that is how you know.
- **Never:** acts before he knows the sequence, raises his voice, or says "it'll be fine" without checking. Linh stays
  out until Companion. The handover stays out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** counts things in order, adjusts his glasses before he speaks, keeps watch while others sleep,
  appoints himself to things nobody asked him to do.
- **Arc: let go** (the man who has to hand over the watch and trust it to hold). Alternate: **burnout** (twenty years with no
  hour off, catching up with him).
