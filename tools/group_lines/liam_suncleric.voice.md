# Liam Hartigan: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/liam_hartigan.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** warm, easy and storytelling, very Blue Mountains. Long friendly sentences at the fire; in a crisis his voice
  drops lower and slower and turns into plain instructions. "Breathe out loud for me."
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the ambulance:**
  - The job: the truck, the roster, the night shift, triage, pupils, airways, "stay with me", the quiet drive back from
    one that went well. About a third of his lines at most.
  - Home: Katoomba, the fog coming up the valley, the Three Sisters, his mum the nurse, his dad the builder, his little
    brother who still owes him fifty bucks.
  - The good stuff: a barbecue, a cold beer, backyard cricket, a story told well, a mate who laughs at the right bit.
  - Sleep, or the lack of it. He gets up at every noise.
  - The sun answering him "like a good crew, when called and all at once".
  - Grom's Rest and the two kids there, mentioned lightly and fondly.
  - "Mate", "too right", "fair enough", "good on ya", "she'll be right" (only when it really will be).
- **Humour:** gentle and self-mocking; hates being called a hero and turns it into a joke.
- **To a group:** "team", the word a paramedic uses when he means it.
- **Swearing:** casual, Australian, and never in front of kids.
- **Never:** panics, freezes, or accepts being called a hero. Jess stays out until Companion. 2019 stays out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** counts breaths, checks pupils after a fight, gets up at every noise, puts himself between
  danger and anyone small.
- **Arc: belonging** (the man who always went in alone finds a crew to go in with). Alternate: **burnout** (the no-sleep
  catching up).
