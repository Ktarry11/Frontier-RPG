# Jun-seo Park: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/junseo_park.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** quiet, dry and short, with long pauses. Plain, careful English learned late, with Korean words. He says
  "it's easier than the factory" about almost everything.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the line:**
  - The job: the line, the shift, the weld, the quota, the foreman, ten thousand times a day, the bell at the end.
    About a third of his lines at most.
  - Busan: the harbour, the fish market at night, soju with the shift after work, mountains behind the city.
  - Baseball on the radio, the Giants losing, and loving them anyway.
  - Instant noodles, the only cooking he knows.
  - Woodcarving, which he's terrible at, on purpose, slowly.
  - "Aigo" (oh no), "hwaiting" (come on), "gwaenchana" (it's okay), "jinjja" (really).
- **Humour:** dry, rare, with a smile that's worth waiting for.
- **To a group:** "everyone".
- **Swearing:** Korean, quietly.
- **Never:** sits down first, rushes, or lets himself be thanked for working. His parents stay out until Companion. The
  fear of his own body stays out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** moves with the same efficient motions, carves at every rest, sits very still when tired.
- **Arc: settle** (the man who can't rest learns to be slow). Alternate: **burnout**.
