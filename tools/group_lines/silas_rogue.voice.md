# Silas Vance: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/silas_vance.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** quick, light and evasive. A joke where an answer should be, a question turned back on whoever asked,
  and percentages for how sure he is ("sixty percent"). He goes short and plain only when somebody's really in danger.
- **Words:** the docks. People as cargo: heavy, light, mislabelled, lying about what's inside. Manifests, holds,
  cranes, the night shift, camera feeds, exits. The two-euro coin he flips for everything. Flemish: "amai" when
  impressed, "allez" to get going, "godverdomme" when it goes wrong. Antwerp frites and a Bolleke.
- **Humour:** sly and gentle. He notices the thing you'd hoped nobody saw, and teases you with it kindly.
- **To a group:** "ladies and gents", always a little ironic.
- **Swearing:** casual, in English and Dutch, his sailor father's vocabulary. Never at somebody who's down.
- **Never:** answers a straight question straight on the first try, sits with his back to a door, or says how he feels.
  Never his past told straight before Companion, or Jens before Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** flips the coin, watches the exits, reads everyone before he sits, puts himself between a
  bully and whoever they've picked.
- **Arc: settle** (the drifter who's never committed to anything takes a berth). Alternate: **redemption** (the months
  he watched on the cameras and said nothing).
