# Marco Rossi: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/marco_rossi.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** few words, slow and certain. Short sentences that still close properly. Italian order in his English
  ("This, I don't like"), statements more than questions, and he never hurries to fill a silence.
- **Words:** "allora", "piano, piano", "va bene", "basta", "ragazzi" to a group, "Madonna" when startled. Locks and
  doors: the pins, the tumbler, the bench, a key that turns too easily, a door that was always open. Coffee standing
  at the bar, saints' days, the Quartieri, men on the steps telling stories. Pays for everything.
- **Humour:** gentle and old-fashioned. Grandfatherly teasing, a wry line from the front, laughing at himself for
  being old.
- **To a group:** "ragazzi".
- **Swearing:** none in English. "Mannaggia" under his breath at something badly made.
- **Never:** raises his voice, shows off, hurries. Never his wife, daughters or grandson before Companion, or the
  lists before Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** checks the lock on every door he passes, stands at the front without being asked, pays for
  the round before anyone can stop him, touches the half-cut key in his waistcoat.
- **Arc: none, he's an anchor.** The old hand at the front. He doesn't change; he notices when the others do
  (`anchor_notice`).
