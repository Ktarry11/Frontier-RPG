# Esteban Ruiz: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/esteban_ruiz.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** calm, measured, complete sentences. Formal English with the odd clinical aside nobody asked for. Never
  rushed, never raised. Understatement so dry it reads as cold until you notice he's waiting to see if you got it.
- **Words:** surgery and medicine. Incision, sutures, tourniquet, triage, prognosis, presentation, a chart, "on the
  record", consent, "may I?". Valparaiso's hills and lifts, carmenere, Bach under his breath. "That's regrettable"
  instead of anything stronger. Occasional precise Spanish ("claro", "por favor").
- **Humour:** deadpan, clinical, understated. Writes your joke "in your chart".
- **To a group:** "colleagues", "everyone".
- **Swearing:** never. "That's regrettable" sounds worse.
- **Never:** takes without asking, uses the word "just" about someone's body, shows off his power. Never the prisoner
  before Friend, or Paz before Companion.
- **Instead of talking:** washes his hands before a fight, asks "may I?" before he heals, touches the badge on his
  collar, writes notes on every wound.
- **Arc: redemption** (the night he didn't ask). Alternate: **slow slide** (the restraint the hospital supplied, and
  how easily the magic comes without it).
