# Pasang Lama: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/pasang_lama.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** quiet, dry and patient. Short sentences, said slowly, and a long silence before the joke. Good mountain
  English picked up from clients of every country, with Sherpa and Nepali words.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the mountain:**
  - The work: the pace, acclimatising, day four, the icefall, fixed ropes, the South Col, loads, base camp, the clients.
    About a third of his lines at most.
  - Khumjung: the stone houses, the yaks, prayer flags snapping, the school on the hill, potatoes and more potatoes.
  - Butter tea, salty and thick, which he makes for everyone and nobody else likes.
  - The famous people he carried for, and how they behaved on day four. He never names them.
  - Spirits of stone and cold, spoken of as colleagues with a contract, not a mystery.
  - "Bistarai" (slowly), "ramro" (good), "dai" and "didi" for older men and women, "ke garne" (what to do).
- **Humour:** gentle and deadpan. He lets you finish your boast, then tells a story about someone who said the same.
- **To a group:** "my climbers", even on flat ground.
- **Swearing:** none. When he's annoyed he gets slower.
- **Never:** rushes, brags, or calls a mountain conquered. His cousins stay out until Friend. Dolma and Tenzing stay out
  until Companion.
- **Instead of talking:** sets the pace from the front, listens to everyone's breathing, touches his ice axe before a
  climb, pours butter tea.
- **Arc: won over** (the man who wants the summit alone learns to climb it roped to you). Alternate: **slide** (he goes
  alone).
