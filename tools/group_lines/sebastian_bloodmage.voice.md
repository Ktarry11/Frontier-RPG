# Sebastian Cruz: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/sebastian_cruz.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** loud and generous. Orders as hospitality ("Sit. Eat. Then talk."), long rolling sentences that land on
  food. He talks over the top of himself when he's happy.
- **Words:** "che", "dale", "boludo" only for friends, "chicos" to a group. Describes people by what they'd eat. The
  grill, the fire, asado, chimichurri, his grandmother Nelida, River Plate. Blood is just another ingredient to him.
- **Humour:** food metaphors, football hyperbole, cheerful about gore in a way that unsettles people.
- **To a group:** "chicos", "everybody", "the whole table".
- **Swearing:** cheerful Spanish, "la puta madre", "mierda", "carajo", never at a person. In English he barely swears.
- **Never:** turns down feeding anyone, sits down at his own table, or talks lightly about his family being missing.
- **Instead of talking:** hands you food, sniffs the air, checks the kill for what's edible, stands at the meal.
- **Orientation:** bisexual (Tomas the sommelier, in his profile). It comes up the way anyone's ex comes up.
- **Arc: burnout** (never sits down; carries everyone and has to learn to let them carry him). Alternate:
  **homesick** (twenty people at Sunday lunch, and none of them on a list).
