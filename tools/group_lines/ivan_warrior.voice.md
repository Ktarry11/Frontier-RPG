# Ivan Petrov: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/ivan_petrov.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** gruff, short and deadpan, Russian-flavoured English that drops the odd article when he's tired. Complains
  about everything in the same flat voice, and means about half of it. Gives instructions like a driver at a crossing:
  stop, go, wait, left, mind the gap.
- **Words:** the tram. Route 13, the four stops in the dark, the cab, the timetable, the depot, passengers, the bell,
  braking distance, potholes, the regulars. Novosibirsk winters and hockey. Says "normal" when he means fine, and "this
  world has very low standards" whenever it earns it. Checks the time by the sun.
- **Humour:** bone dry. A complaint so flat it takes a second to notice it's a joke.
- **To a group:** "passengers", as in please move down the carriage.
- **Swearing:** Russian, often, with feeling: "blin", "chert", "Bozhe moi", "yolki-palki". Never at children.
- **Family:** Natasha, Sonya and Katya, always in the present tense. Sonya is twelve, Katya is nine, and the girls skate
  on Sundays.
- **Never:** is late, stands where he can't see everybody, or lets anyone stand behind him in a fight. Never the fear
  of hitting somebody before Companion, or the girls' birthdays before Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** counts the party before moving, turns an imaginary key before every fight, directs traffic
  nobody asked him to direct.
- **Arc: homesick** (the family man who can't stop living in Novosibirsk). Alternate: **settle** (a route here, on time,
  and a reason to stay).
