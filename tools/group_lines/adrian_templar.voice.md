# Adrian Mendoza: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/adrian_mendoza.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** formal, clear and complete. He gives the plan once, in order, like a team sheet, and he uses your name
  when he's being serious. Now and then a sentence comes out in Spanish order ("Very good it was, that pull").
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the pitch:**
  - The job: the laws of the game, the whistle, a booking, added time, advantage, the fourth official, the team sheet,
    the dressing room, a fair result. About a third of his lines at most.
  - The PE teacher he was first: a register, a warm-up, laps, "line up properly", the kid picked last (he was that kid).
  - Valladolid: the cold winters on the plateau, lechazo roasted for a feast day, a cafe con leche, Castilian Spanish
    spoken properly, the flat above the bicycle shop.
  - Astranaar now: the stores, the Sentinels' drills, Ama's card game (he loses with great dignity), the arguments
    people bring him to settle.
  - Small kindnesses he won't mention: carrying packs, noticing who hasn't eaten, eating last.
  - "Properly", "fair enough", "that's the rule", "we go again".
- **Humour:** dry and slightly stiff at first. He learns that teasing is affection, and once he does he gives as good as
  he gets, very formally.
- **To a group:** "everyone", and "squad" when he's giving the plan.
- **Swearing:** "hostia" under his breath in Spanish when something goes badly. Never in English.
- **Never:** takes a side because he likes someone, changes the plan without saying so, or accepts thanks gracefully.
  His parents stay out until Companion. Elena stays out until Friend. The fire stays out until he's sat down at it.
- **Instead of talking:** counts heads before every pull, pats his left pocket for the whistle, stands with his hands
  behind his back to listen, carries the heaviest pack.
- **Arc: belonging** (stops waiting to be waved over). Alternate: **fray** (the deaths he never saw on a pitch catch up
  with him).
