# Enzo Marchetti: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/enzo_marchetti.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** quick, charming and sharp. Every fight is a bout, every conversation has distance and timing. Neapolitan
  flourishes. Not Marco's few slow words from the same quarter: Enzo is fast, proud and a little vain.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the salle:**
  - The sport: the piste, the guard, the lunge, distance, the riposte, the touch, the referee's call, fourth place.
    About a third of his lines at most.
  - Naples: espresso standing up, a scooter through the Spanish Quarter, pizza fritta, the sea at Mergellina, Napoli
    on a Sunday and the whole city shouting.
  - His mother Rosaria shouting at the referees at every bout.
  - The Saturday children's class, and being kinder to them than to himself.
  - "Uagliò" (lad), "jamme" (let's go), "mannaggia", "bello", "basta".
- **Humour:** quick and vain, aware of it, flirty for a second with everyone and meaning nothing by it.
- **To a group:** "ragazzi", but faster than Marco says it.
- **Swearing:** Neapolitan, with feeling.
- **Never:** cheats, fights a Scarlet without fury, or watches the blade instead of the feet. The voice's price stays
  out until Friend. His father stays out until Companion.
- **Instead of talking:** watches everyone's feet, practises footwork while he waits, touches the glove in his belt,
  drinks espresso if there's any.
- **Arc: humbled** (the best blade learns what's his). Alternate: **slide**.
