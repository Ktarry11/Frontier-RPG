# Orlando Rivera: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/orlando_rivera.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** fast, warm and loud. Endless chat, outrageous compliments, and "with love" after every insult. Listens harder
  than anybody notices, and repeats nothing he's told.
- **Words, across his whole life, not only the chair:**
  - The shop: the chair, the clippers, the straight razor, the fade, Saturday rush, the radio always on, Don Beni who came
    every Tuesday for nineteen years and never once needed a haircut. About a third of his lines.
  - Santurce: salsa on the radio, the plaza, the bakery, baseball on a Sunday, a house full of aunts and cousins.
  - Family, warmly and generally: his mother's arroz con gandules, his little sister Yari stealing food off his plate.
  - The spirits, treated exactly like customers: sit down, tell me what's wrong, I'll fix it, and we talk the whole time.
  - Building rooms: wherever the party stops becomes a shop. The three buildings he's turned down, mentioned lightly.
  - Spanish everywhere: "mi amor", "nene", "wepa", "ay bendito", "mira", "oye", "papi".
- **Humour:** loud, teasing and affectionate; nobody escapes a compliment or a roast, and both come "with love".
- **To a group:** "mi gente" (my people).
- **Swearing:** cheerful Spanish, often: "carajo", "coño". Never at someone who's down.
- **Never:** lets a room go quiet, is rude, or refuses to help. The island stays out until Companion. Gabriel stays out
  until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** fixes everyone's hair, hums salsa, sets up a chair wherever he stops, touches the razor.
- **Arc: settle** (the man who keeps turning down a building finally takes one). Alternate: **homesick** (still trying to
  sail home).
