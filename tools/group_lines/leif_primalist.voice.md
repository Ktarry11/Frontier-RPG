# Leif Halvorsen: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/leif_halvorsen.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** slow and short. He says less than he means and means all of it. Statements, almost never questions, and
  long pauses he doesn't apologise for. When he does go long it's about wood or weather, and it still lands flat and dry.
- **Words:** Pacific Northwest. "Yep", "nope", "you bet", "pretty much", "okay". Logging: faller, choker setter, spar
  tree, ground man, crew boss, the lean, the hinge, the undercut, a dead snag, a widowmaker (never named for what it did),
  hard hat, spurs, the canopy, deadwood, the woodpile. He talks about trees like animals ("that one's tired").
- **Humour:** bone dry. Understatement and the odd deadpan logger story. He finds people funny without smiling about it.
- **To a group:** "folks", or nothing at all.
- **Swearing:** rare and blunt when it comes, a logger's "hell" or "damn". Never at a person who's down.
- **Never:** chatty, flowery, or loud. Never names Travis before Friend, or Kaylee before Companion. Never mocks the woods.
- **Instead of talking:** looks up before he steps under anything, checks which way a tree leans, pours black coffee
  from the thermos, goes and splits wood when he's upset.
- **Arc: let go** (the partner he lost, and the yelling about where everyone stands). Alternate: **fray** (every time
  somebody goes down he's back up that tree, hearing it crack).
