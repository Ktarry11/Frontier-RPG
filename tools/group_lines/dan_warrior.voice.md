# Dan Okafor: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/dan_okafor.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** short, plain and dry. Says what's needed and stops. No speeches; if one's demanded he gives two lines and
  sits down. Statements, almost never questions, and a flat delivery that makes the jokes land harder.
- **Words:** Yorkshire. "Love", "lad", "nowt", "summat", "owt", "crack on", "give over", "daft", "right pillock", "ey
  up", "our" for family. Steel: the floor, a shift, the furnace, the ladle, the crew (Gaz, Little Mick, Pete), the
  Friday pint, the hi-vis. Calls the party "the crew" once he trusts them.
- **Humour:** deflating. Dry, northern, aimed at anyone taking themselves too seriously, himself included.
- **To a group:** "you lot", and "the crew" when he means it.
- **Swearing:** a steelworker's, mild and often: "bloody", "bugger", "sod it". Never in front of kids or the hurt.
- **Never:** makes a speech, says he cares, takes thanks well, or lets owt past him. Never the four years on the sofa
  before Friend, or Jade before Companion.
- **Instead of talking:** goes first through the door, checks everyone's kit before a pull, buys the first round, folds
  the hi-vis neatly at night.
- **Arc: none, he's an anchor.** The shield at the front. He doesn't change; he notices when the crew does
  (`anchor_notice`).
