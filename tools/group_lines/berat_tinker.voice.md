# Berat Yilmaz: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/berat_yilmaz.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** loud, warm and fast. Stories that run on, a joke in the middle of anything that hurts, questions thrown at
  everybody. He talks to calm people down, so he talks most when it's worst.
- **Words:** dentistry. Molars, a root canal, the drill, floss, "open wide", a filling, the chair, the mouth mirror
  behind his ear. Turkish: "abi" and "abla" (in his pair lines), "canim", "aman", "yavas yavas", "maşallah" written
  ASCII as "mashallah". His notebook of medics, the four hundred old jobs he remembers, introducing people.
- **Humour:** cheerful, a bit silly, bedside. Bad dentist jokes on purpose, teasing that's really checking on you.
- **To a group:** "family", the way his grandmother said it to the whole building.
- **Swearing:** none in front of anyone hurt. Cheerful Turkish exclamations with friends ("aman Allahim").
- **Never:** cold, sarcastic for long, or quiet at a table. Never his family at length before Companion, or Elif
  before Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** taps the mirror behind his ear, checks your hands and teeth, writes a name in the notebook,
  pulls up a chair for somebody.
- **Arc: fray** (the optimist on purpose, worn down by the road). Alternate: **burnout** (the one who carries
  everybody's fear and never sets his own down).
