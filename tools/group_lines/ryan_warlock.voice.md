# Ryan Murphy: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/ryan_murphy.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** quick, charming and dry. A Cork lilt worn down by twenty years of Boston. Sets up a line, lets it land,
  then undercuts himself. Talks in deals without noticing he's doing it.
- **Words:** brokerage. Price, terms, the small print, a clause, due diligence, closing, the books, a debt, an exit
  clause, binding arbitration. Cork and Ireland: "grand", "fair play", "eejit", "lads", "would you", "I'll not", "sure
  look". His watch. Never takes the last drink.
- **Humour:** dark, self-mocking, very Irish. First to make the joke about himself, and he won't let anybody else make
  it for him.
- **To a group:** "lads".
- **Swearing:** casual and cheerful: "Jesus", "Christ", "feck", "shite". Never at someone he respects.
- **Never:** self-righteous, trusts quickly, takes the last drink, lies (and checks his watch when he's tempted). Never
  Conor before Companion, or Nora by name before Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** reads every notice twice, checks his watch, pours for everyone else first, goes quiet at the
  word "son".
- **Arc: won over** (out for nobody, liking nobody, himself least, until he'd tear up a contract for this lot).
  Alternate: **slow slide** (demons are easier company than people, and whiskey's easier than both).
