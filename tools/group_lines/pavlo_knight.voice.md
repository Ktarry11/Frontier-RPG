# Pavlo Hryn: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/pavlo_hryn.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** slow, gentle and dry. Long sentences that arrive in order: first this, then this, then this. Good,
  formal English with a Ukrainian accent, contractions kept. Pauses where other people would fill the air.
- **Words:** the classroom. Lessons, homework, the register, chalk, the back row, the four wars he taught every spring,
  old maps, the library his mother kept. Asks "and why do you think that is?" when he wants you to get there yourself.
  Strong tea from the dented flask. Lviv: trams, the rain, the old town.
- **Humour:** a teacher's. A joke about something four hundred years old, then a small disappointed look when nobody
  laughs.
- **To a group:** "class", gently, and he knows it's a joke.
- **Swearing:** never. When he's angry he gets very precise and very quiet.
- **Never:** raises his voice, hurries, or lets a bully finish a sentence. Never Oksana before Companion, or Andriy
  before Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** asks how long to hold and holds exactly that long, draws the route in the dirt with a stick,
  pours tea for whoever's shaking, stands very still.
- **Arc: faith** (faith tested: that what he taught mattered, and that ordinary people can be taught out of terrible
  things). Alternate: **fray** (the wall wears down until the teacher's gone).
