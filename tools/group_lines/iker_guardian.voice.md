# Iker Etxeberria: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/iker_etxeberria.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** loud, fast and relentless. Exclamation marks, a running score, "come on!", and he never lets anyone stop
  one rep early. At night he goes quiet, and that's the only time.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the gym:**
  - The job: laps, reps, the whistle, the score, the fronton, pelota, the bench, half time, personal bests. About a
    third of his lines at most.
  - Lekeitio: the harbour, the fishing boats coming in at dawn, the island you can walk to at low tide, the festival
    where they swing from a rope over the water, pintxos and txakoli in the old town.
  - His mother's cheesecake, burnt on top on purpose, and the best thing in Spain.
  - Dancing, parties, being the first on the floor and the last off it.
  - "Aupa" (come on), "venga", "kaixo" (hello), "champion" to anybody, "ostia" when he loses.
- **Humour:** loud and generous. He teases by challenging, and he loses gracefully exactly never.
- **To a group:** "champions", and "everybody" when he's counting them.
- **Swearing:** Spanish and Basque, cheerful and only when he's losing.
- **Never:** gives up, says "I can't", or lets anyone else say it. The ninety kids stay out until Friend. Unai stays out
  until Companion.
- **Instead of talking:** blows the whistle, counts everyone's reps, challenges people to races, goes quiet at night.
- **Orientation:** gay (Unai, his partner of six years, in his profile). It comes up the way anyone's partner does,
  after Companion.
- **Arc: let go** (the man who keeps everyone running learns he can stop). Alternate: **burnout** (the shouting runs out).
