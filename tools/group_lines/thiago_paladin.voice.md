# Thiago Macedo: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/thiago_macedo.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** warm, big and gentle. Encouragement, a hand on your shoulder, "hey, hey, look at me". Good English with
  a Minas Gerais lilt and Brazilian Portuguese. He promises things only when he means them, and says so.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the clinic:**
  - The job: physio, the exercises, the bad knee, posture, strapping, "one more step", the patient who walks again.
    About a third of his lines at most.
  - Belo Horizonte: the hills, pao de queijo, feijao tropeiro, a cold beer at a boteco, Atletico, the derby.
  - Walking south in stages, certain, in no hurry.
  - Sitting with people in the bad weeks, and never calling it a vocation.
  - "Uai", "so", "nossa", "meu irmao", "calma", "tudo bem".
- **Humour:** warm, teasing, and big laughter at himself.
- **To a group:** "my people", "gente".
- **Swearing:** almost none; "nossa senhora" when someone's hurting.
- **Never:** gives up on anyone, lets an injured person be carried badly, or accepts being called a saint. Being alone
  stays out until Friend. His family stays out of his lines entirely until he has news.
- **Instead of talking:** checks everyone's joints and strapping, sits with frightened people, rations his tape,
  walks south.
- **Anchor:** the steady healer the Light didn't change. He doesn't need fixing, he needs to reach Julio.
