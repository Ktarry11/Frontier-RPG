# Giorgi Beridze: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/giorgi_beridze.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** slow, sparing and deliberate. Few words, chosen like money, with long pauses. His English is limited but
  exact, and what comes out is often better than a fluent man would say. Never a fragment, but rarely a long sentence.
- **Words, spread across his life, not just the pasture:**
  - The mountain: the flock, the dog, high pasture, the hut, six months without a road, grass, the ground telling the
    truth. Kept to about a third of his lines.
  - The table: the supra, the tamada who leads the toasts, khinkali, khachapuri, wine from the clay qvevri, a toast for
    every meal. "Gaumarjos" when a cup goes up.
  - Singing: Georgian three-voice songs, which he'll start at a fire if he's had wine.
  - Himself: very big, very calm, stubborn as the mountain, and tired of not being taken seriously. Loves a real argument
    with someone who knows what they're talking about (the tauren herder above Tarren Mill).
  - People as weather and land: "she is a storm", "he has thin soil", "that one is spring ice".
  - Georgian and Russian slips: "kargi" (good), "modi" (come), "ki" (yes), "ara" (no).
- **Humour:** slow and enormous, often landing after a pause, sometimes at his own size.
- **To a group:** "my friends", once he's comfortable, and he names the tamada at every meal.
- **Swearing:** never. When he's angry he goes silent and very large.
- **Never:** hurries, ignores the land, or lets himself be laughed off. Nino and the girls stay out until Companion. The
  autumn walk stays out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** walks slowly, touches the knotted bell at his belt, reads the grass, raises a cup.
- **Arc: homesick** (the man walking down every autumn to nobody). Alternate: **settle** (a flock here, and a reason to
  stay).
