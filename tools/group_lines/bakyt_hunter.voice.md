# Bakyt Asanov: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/bakyt_asanov.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** slow, courteous and spare. Plain short sentences in limited, careful English learned from traders, and long
  silences between them. Never a two-word sentence, but rarely more than a dozen words either.
- **Words:** the high pasture. Sky, wind, grass, water, the herd, the route, spring and autumn, the long view. His eagle
  Jeldyz, "star". Tea with milk, offered before anything else. Kyrgyz and Russian slips: "jakshy" (good), "kel" (come),
  "aga" (yes), "balam" (my child) for the young.
- **Humour:** dry and gentle, often aimed at hurry. An old man's joke about young people and their legs.
- **To a group:** "friends", and "children" when they're being foolish.
- **Swearing:** never. When he's angry he goes still, and Jeldyz mantles on his arm.
- **Never:** rushes, wastes water, raises his voice, or calls the wild empty. Aigul stays out until Companion. His sons
  stay out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** reads the sky, crouches to touch the ground, walks first, pours tea for whoever looks tired.
- **Arc: none, he's an anchor.** Nomads adjust. He doesn't change; he notices when the people he walks with do
  (`anchor_notice`).
