# Sondre Haugen: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/sondre_haugen.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** polite and plain. Short in a crisis, longer and looser when he relaxes. Very good English, a little formal,
  with Norwegian rhythm: understatement, "a little", "quite", "I think perhaps".
- **Words:** sailing and weather. Tack, reef, leeward, a good knot, the shackle key, the glass dropping, wind
  backing. "Uff" when something's too much. His family always in the present tense ("my sister is braver than me").
- **Humour:** dry. Deadpan understatement ("That was not ideal"), and funnier the more he forgets to be careful.
- **To a group:** "everyone", "all of you".
- **Swearing:** never. When he's frightened he goes very quiet instead.
- **Never:** shows off, raises his voice except to give a warning, or talks lightly about the barn or Jonas.
- **Instead of talking:** watches the sky, holds the shackle key on its lanyard, steps back when his hands crackle,
  makes the fire before anyone else is up.
- **Orientation:** gay (Jonas from Voss, in his profile). It's never his whole story.
- **Arc: thaw** (keeps everyone at a safe distance, and learns to let people stand close). Alternate: **slow slide**
  (practising alone where there's nothing to break, getting further from everyone).
