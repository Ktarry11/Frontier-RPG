# Matías Fuentes: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/matias_fuentes.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** quiet, careful and short. Simple words chosen exactly, with a pause while he finds the next one. English three
  years old and getting better; still a whole sentence, never a fragment. Says very little in a fight, all of it useful.
- **Words:** the bus. The route, the fare box, the stops, the same forty faces every morning, who gets off where, who is
  having a bad week. Football, his mother's cooking, early mornings. Chilean Spanish slips when tired or with friends:
  "ya", "cachai" (you get it?), "weón" (mate), "po". The ticket punch, clicked twice before a fight.
- **Humour:** rare and very dry, landing late because nobody expects it from the quiet one.
- **To a group:** "cabros" (lads), once he's comfortable.
- **Swearing:** Chilean, under his breath, often: "chucha", "puta". Never at anyone who's down.
- **Never:** pushes in, talks loudly about nothing, or explains the ticket punch. His mother and Benjamín stay out until
  Companion. His father stays out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** stands at the front, watches every face, gets up for older people, clicks the punch twice.
- **Arc: voice** (the man who went silent for a year finds his words again). Alternate: **slide** (the quiet gets comfortable,
  and he stops coming back from it).
