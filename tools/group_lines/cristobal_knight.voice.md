# Cristobal Munoz: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/cristobal_munoz.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** loud, warm and fast. Big laughs, run-on enthusiasm, and a joke at his own expense every few lines. Calls
  everybody "hermano" (or "hermana") within a minute and means it.
- **Words:** the brigade. The fire line, firebreaks, backburns, the wind, embers, the dry season, the station, the
  motorbike that broke down every week, the yellow helmet. Fire is a person to him: "she's hungry today", "she'll turn
  on us at noon". His mother's empanadas and her nagging, constantly. Cueca, danced badly. Chilean slips: "po",
  "hermano", "fiesta", "la vieja" for his mother, said with love.
- **Humour:** loud, self-mocking, and aimed at the things that scare him, the hellfire most of all.
- **To a group:** "hermanos".
- **Swearing:** cheerful Chilean, constant: "chucha", "conchetumare" when it's really bad. Never at the frightened.
- **Never:** stands back and watches, leaves a fire burning, or accepts being told to be careful. The 2024 fire stays
  out until Companion. The people who didn't walk out of the grass fire stay out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** checks the wind, sniffs for smoke, puts out every campfire properly, dances at every inn.
- **Arc: rise** (the man who always goes in first learns to build a brigade that holds without him). Alternate: **slide**
  (the fire inside him getting away from him).
