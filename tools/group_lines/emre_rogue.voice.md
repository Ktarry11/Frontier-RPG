# Emre Kaya: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/emre_kaya.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** dry, calm and amused. Unhurried, careful English with a Turkish turn to it. He finishes his sentence even
  if you've started yours, and he tells you that you interrupted.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the picks:**
  - The trade: pins, wards, tumblers, the bite of a key, tension, "the lock is talking", a door that was built by
    somebody serious. Every lock is a short conversation with somebody who isn't in the room. About a third at most.
  - The bazaar quarter: the Grand Bazaar's roofs, the coppersmiths and bookbinders who closed, tea glasses on a brass
    tray, simit from a cart, the street cats who owned his doorstep, the ferry across the Bosphorus with gulls and tea.
  - Tavla (backgammon) played fast with old men, and losing on purpose to the oldest.
  - Turkish coffee, very strong, and reading the grounds badly as a joke.
  - Proverbs, Turkish ones, said as his own: "patience is the key to paradise", "ask a hundred times rather than lose
    your way once", "a drop at a time becomes a lake".
  - "My friend", "tamam", "inshallah", "aman" when he's pained, "vallahi" when he swears something is true.
- **Humour:** bone dry. He teases with a straight face and enjoys being slightly too pleased with himself.
- **To a group:** "my friends", "beyler" when he's being grand.
- **Swearing:** mild Turkish at a badly made lock ("hay aksi"). Never at a person.
- **Never:** calls himself a thief, breaks a door that could be opened, or hurries. His mother stays out until
  Companion. The lightness stays out until Friend. The question behind the door stays out until his quest.
- **Instead of talking:** turns a pick between his fingers, goes completely still to listen to a mechanism, reads every
  door he passes.
- **Anchor:** content already, and the steady one in a group. He doesn't need fixing, he needs somebody to tell the
  truth to.
