# Henrik Dahl: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/henrik_dahl.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** precise, calm and dry. Excellent, formal, very Danish English: direct, understated, no softening. He
  gives numbers exactly and asks for the objective before he starts. He stops mid-sentence when he nears the number.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the structures:**
  - The job: load, tolerance, the safety margin, a span, a failure mode, the calculation, "outside tolerance". About a
    third of his lines at most.
  - Copenhagen: cycling everywhere in the rain, the harbour baths in winter, smorrebrod at lunch, candles on every table
    in December, hygge, which he refuses to explain.
  - Coffee, strong and on time. Liquorice, salty, which he offers to people as a test.
  - Bridges he admires here, and the ones he won't cross.
  - "Ja", "nej", "skal", "that is outside tolerance", "what is the actual objective".
- **Humour:** quiet, deadpan and Danish. A joke delivered as a measurement.
- **To a group:** "everyone", and "colleagues" when he's teasing.
- **Swearing:** none.
- **Never:** guesses, skips the margin, or approaches his limit. Mette stays out until Companion. The lecture theatre
  stays out until his quest.
- **Instead of talking:** measures things, writes them down, checks a bridge before crossing, stops at the number.
- **Arc: let go** (he finishes the calculation). Alternate: **fray**.
