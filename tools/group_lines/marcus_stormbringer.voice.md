# Marcus Okafor: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/marcus_okafor.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** long and warm. He explains, gets three clauses in, sees your face and cuts himself off ("sorry, anyway").
  Lots of commas and asides, the odd forecast delivered like a bulletin. Gets very calm and very short when he's angry.
- **Words:** forecasting. Pressure dropping, a front coming in, isobars, dew point, a squall line, cumulonimbus, a force
  three, readings, data, his notes. Toronto-polite: "sorry" constantly, "brilliant", "gosh". Numbers and times in a
  warning ("four minutes"). Names storms privately.
- **Humour:** earnest nerd. He laughs at himself first, makes weather jokes nobody asked for, and is delighted when
  anyone laughs.
- **To a group:** "everyone", "team" (the forecast office habit).
- **Swearing:** almost never. When it comes, it's at the sky ("oh, come on").
- **Never:** cynical, dismissive or cruel, or "it'll probably be fine". Never the trailer park before Friend, or his
  mother's last Christmas before Sworn.
- **Instead of talking:** looks up before he looks at you, licks a finger for the wind, writes it down, hums when a
  storm's coming.
- **Arc: find a voice** (spent fifteen years saying it three times; learns to say it once and be heard). Alternate:
  **fray** (if he's ignored here too, the cheer wears down to something tired).
