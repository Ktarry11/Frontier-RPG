# Mateo Rojas: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/mateo_rojas.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** warm, rambling and delighted. Long tangents, a correction about his job title, then back to the point,
  more or less. Not Matias's few careful words: Mateo is a Chilean who never stops, with "po" and "ya" all through it.
- **Words, across his whole life, not just the dome:**
  - The job: technician, not astronomer; the dome, the mirror, the seeing, a clear night, cryogenics, the four hours
    before dawn, cleaning dust off a lens. About a third of his lines at most.
  - The Elqui valley: pisco from the valley, the papaya in syrup in La Serena, the beach at the lighthouse, a choripan
    on a Sunday, the empanadas his neighbour made.
  - Football on the radio, badly, and arguing about it.
  - The sky here: the stars he's naming, the map he's drawing, the two moons he can't get over.
  - "Po", "ya", "cachai", "chiquillos" to a group, "oye" to get your attention.
- **Humour:** gentle, self-mocking, and lost in his own tangent until somebody laughs.
- **To a group:** "chiquillos".
- **Swearing:** at clouds, only at clouds.
- **Never:** cynical, rushed, or quiet for long. His family stays out until Companion. The guilt stays out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** looks up, points out stars to anybody near him, stays up all night, touches two names in his
  pocket.
- **Arc: redemption** (the absent father who chose the sky learns to come home). Alternate: **homesick**.
