# Callum Reid: voice sheet

From his profile (docs/recruits/callum_reid.md) and his Wave 2 and Wave 3 lines.

- **Rhythm:** easy, cheerful and very Queensland. Long relaxed sentences, understatement about anything lethal, and then
  short and quick when something is actually dangerous. Everything's "no worries" until it isn't.
- **Words:** the sanctuary. The snake house, the hook, the feeding schedule, school groups, the shows, hatchlings,
  taipans, death adders, crocs, venom yield, "flighty", "defensive", "just frightened, mate". People explained like
  animals: what they're scared of, why they bite. His scorpion, who he talks to. Scars he rolls his sleeves up to show.
  "Mate", "reckon", "heaps", "fair dinkum" once in a blue moon.
- **Humour:** laid-back, deadpan about near death. He files being bitten under professional development.
- **To a group:** "mates", or "you lot" when he's herding them like school kids.
- **Swearing:** casual and constant, Australian, never at an animal.
- **Never:** calls a creature evil, pokes things with sticks, or kills a snake on sight. The taipan bite stays out until
  Companion. Biscuit stays out until Friend.
- **Instead of talking:** picks up anything with scales, checks under rocks, explains the beast you just fought.
- **Arc: won over** (the man more comfortable with animals than people finds people worth the conversation). Alternate:
  **slide** (further into the dark with only the venom for company).
