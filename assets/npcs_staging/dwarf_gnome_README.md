# Generated Dwarf & Gnome sprite sheet — raw, not yet cropped

Imported 2026-09-10. Same AI-generation pipeline as every other sheet
this session. 24 characters in one generation (12 Dwarf: 6 male/6
female, 12 Gnome: 6 male/6 female) - one of the strongest results of the
whole batch, per Kyle: "This one's the best yet."

## What's here

- `dwarf_gnome_contact_sheet_raw.png` — 12 Dwarf + 12 Gnome, clearly
  distinct in scale and design language per the prompt's explicit rules:
  Dwarves stout/bearded/earth-toned with mountain-clan gear (chainmail,
  plate, pickaxes, war-hammers, rune staves); Gnomes dramatically
  smaller with big eyes, bright varied hair (pink, green, purple, teal,
  orange, red), goggles, gadgets, and a genuinely warmhearted/friendly
  read matching real WoW gnome lore (Kyle was explicit: keep them
  lore-accurate and NOT sinister, even though he personally jokes about
  finding them "vile little Chihuahuas" - that's his own read on them,
  not a design direction).

Kyle's ask was for BOTH races on a single sheet (not split into two
generations like some earlier sheets) since only 12 of each were needed
- correctly scoped smaller than the 20-24 character sheets since dwarves
and gnomes are Alliance-flavored minor/rare NPCs in this Horde-territory
game, not a major playable race needing the same volume as Orc/Troll/Elf.

## Known issues before this is usable (same as every prior sheet)

1. **No real transparency** - RGB, checkerboard baked into pixels.
2. **Not a uniform grid** - 2 rows of 6 per race (Dwarf male/female,
   Gnome male/female), no numbered per-character labels this time (only
   the two race-level headers "DWARF" and "GNOME" are text-labeled) -
   closer to the Tauren/Troll/Forsaken sheets' cropping difficulty than
   the numbered Orc/Elf sheet.
3. **Single front-facing pose only** - fine for the confirmed NPC/
   portrait use case.
