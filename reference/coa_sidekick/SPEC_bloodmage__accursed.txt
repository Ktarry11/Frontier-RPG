RETAIL FEEL
Closest to a Feral/Guardian Druid's form-dancing bolted onto a Blood DK self-sustain layer. Like a shapeshifter, the whole kit re-labels itself between your human caster mode (ranged Shadow bolts: Bloodbolt, Veinburst, Hemoburst) and Accursed Form (melee: Reave, Ravenous Strike, Puncturing Fangs), and you pick the form the fight wants. The transformed half is pure Blood DK. Ravenous Strike healing you off the damage it deals is a dead ringer for Death Strike, and Reave's conditional bonus (bleeding, below 35%, or hitting from behind) plays like an execute-flavored Blood strike. No retail spec fuses ranged caster and melee lifesteal natively. Bloodmage-Accursed time-slices between caster bolting from range and Blood DK smashing face depending on which form you're in.

PLAYSTYLE

You flip between two modes and the kit changes meaning under you. Human caster form is your ranged mode: Bloodbolt, Veinburst and Hemoburst are Shadow bolts that build Rage, and Aortic Assault seeds your transformation. Accursed Form flips you into a melee bruiser. Reave, Ravenous Strike (which feeds you health off the damage it deals), Puncturing Fangs and the rest all require the form, and the spec passive strips the Rage cost off self-damaging spells and form abilities entirely. Once shifted you're spamming your hardest-hitting and highest-lifesteal buttons off cooldown, gated only by the GCD. Self-sustain rides your own output: Ravenous Strike lifesteal while transformed, plus Blood Curse trickling health and Rage for as long as you hold the form. The decision is always the same one. Bolt from range and build Rage, or get in someone's face and turn swings into health.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Ravenous Strike heals off its own damage and the spec passive strips its Rage cost, so you spam it on the GCD while transformed. Blood Curse trickles health and Rage the whole time you stay shifted.
Caster form is a ranged Shadow rotation of Bloodbolt and Veinburst; Accursed Form is a lifestealing melee bruiser running Reave and Puncturing Fangs. No respec between them, so you fight at whatever range the situation wants.
In Accursed Form your hardest-hitting melee buttons cost no Rage, so the GCD is the only limit. Bloodsurge and Final Embrace stack burst on top.
WEAKNESSES
Every point of sustain is damage-gated. Ravenous Strike's lifesteal and Blood Curse's trickle both need you landing hits and holding form, so a stun, fear, kite, or LOS drops your healing to zero instantly.
No stun, interrupt, or silence in the spec kit. Control is The Hunter and the Hunted's form-swap charge-and-root, with Crimson Maw and Perilous Ascent for mobility. Class-kit picks patch around it (Hemostasis, Scarlet Delirium, Adrenaline, Bloodmoon Power) but none is a stun-break, and a timed lockdown chain kills your sustain loop.
Ravenous Strike only lifesteals transformed and in melee. Ranged classes that kite you, or burst you before Hunt is up, out-race your sustain.
Not a party healer. Group contribution is Dark Frenzy's spell haste, Dark Sigil's crit buff, and whatever splash healing Insatiable Appetite happens to proc off a crit. Fleshcraft from the class kit is one Rage-costly targeted heal that applies Sated. Spot patches only.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open at range in your caster form with Bloodbolt and Veinburst to build Rage while the mob walks to you, then Hemoburst as it closes. When it reaches melee, hit Accursed Form and Hunt in. Position yourself behind the target for Reave's bonus; Cruel Intent adds extra auto strikes as you engage. Priority once transformed: Reave (best value when the target is bleeding, below 35%, or you're behind it, so stack conditions for the extra-damage-per-condition bonus) > cleave freely when multiple mobs are tagged; your form abilities cost no Rage, so AoE liberally > Ravenous Strike spammed in the gaps. Ravenous Strike is free and up every GCD, and it doubles as your sustain — you should rarely need to sit and drink between pulls. Save Bloodsurge or Final Embrace as a burst window for a tough single target, and lean on Blood Curse's health-and-Rage trickle to top yourself off while shifted.

DUNGEON

Ranged phases: stay in your caster form and weave Bloodbolt, Veinburst and Hemoburst on cooldown to build Rage and chip the pack. Melee phases, or when you want throughput: Accursed Form, then cycle your free Reave / Ravenous Strike / Puncturing Fangs. Ravenous Strike's lifesteal keeps you healthy without a healer babysitting you. Cooldowns: pop Bloodsurge (an enrage, Shadow damage and spell crit up) or Final Embrace (a dramatically improved version of your transformation) for burst windows. Endure the Curse is for when you are the one being trained. It reduces all damage taken and can one-time-save you below 10%. Remember that your caster-form casts are Rage builders, not filler. They fund the free transformed spam.
