RETAIL FEEL
Closest to retail Vengeance Demon Hunter. The Soul Fragment into consume-for-self-heal loop is nearly identical in shape: Fracture/Shear into Soul Cleave becomes Reap/Murder/Dirge/auto-procs into Soul Harvest into Reaped Soul spenders. Both are melee specs that heal themselves off their own damage rather than through direct healing spells. Both lean on short defensive cooldowns, Demon Spikes and Metamorphosis versus Limbo and Shade, rather than a steady HoT or shield toolkit. It's Vengeance's fragment engine wearing a rogue-flavored stealth-and-mobility duelist chassis, thanks to Shade's improved stealth and the Underwalk/Beyond the Veil movement layer.

PLAYSTYLE

You build Soul Fragments through weapon swings and generators (Reap, Murder, Dirge, Wraithblade), and fragments cascade into a Reaped Soul plus a burst hit. Reaped Souls stack toward the Soul Infusion state that powers your strongest windows, and Purgatory ramps your Soulrend and Reliquary of the Lost damage each time you gain it. On top of that sits a defensive-and-mobility toolkit that fires independent of the fragment engine: Limbo, Shade (an improved stealth that drops you into Underwalk and removes all movement-impairing effects), and Spiritual Reflexes, a below-35%-HP dodge boost that unleashes Soul Harvest on the attacker. So you weave Dirge, Reap and Wraithblade to keep the fragment cascade running, spend Reaped Souls into Soulslam and Reliquary of the Lost when Soul Infusion is up, and bank Limbo and Shade as separate answers to magic burst and to shake a lockdown.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
The Soul Harvest cascade heals off your own fragment generation and Lamenting converts Ghostly Weapon's Frost damage into healing. Spiritual Reflexes fires Soul Harvest when you dodge below 35% health. No dedicated heal spell anywhere, and it still heals like this.
Limbo covers magic burst and Shade drops you into improved stealth while removing all movement-impairing effects. Two different outs, and Sepulchral Renewal resets both.
Spectre Stride closes, Shade, Underwalk and Beyond the Veil give speed and water-walking, and Ghost Claw with Deathchaser's sub-20% reapplication holds a target through the kill.
WEAKNESSES
Death's Presence, a static party damage buff, is the entire party toolkit. A partner under pressure gets no heal, no shield and no CC-break from you, only whatever pressure you put on their attacker.
Limbo and Shade are finite cooldowns. Spend both and there is a dead window with no CC-break left between you and the next burst.
Soul Fragments into Reaped Souls into Soul Infusion into Reliquary of the Lost and Soulslam: every step needs hits landing in melee. A kite, root, or slow stalls damage, self-healing and Runic Power together.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Deathchaser (repeating Shadowfrost damage, generates Runic Power, reapplies on sub-20% targets) to soften and mark the target. Then Wraithblade as your hardest hitting opener: armor-ignoring weapon damage, and with The End Is Near it spawns a spectral copy casting Dirge and Murder. Loop Dirge (dual-weapon strike, generates 2 Soul Fragments) as your main sustained button. Fill gaps with Reap, the cheap generator. Whenever Soul Infusion comes up, fire Reliquary of the Lost (Purgatory has ramped its damage), or dump Reaped Souls into Soulslam if 2+ mobs are clustered. Spend Murder off cooldown for the extra Reaped Soul on a crit (Soul Generator). Lean on The Jailer's Call's bonus damage to finish targets under 20% HP, keep Ghostly Weapon imbued for the Frost damage (and Lamenting's self-heal off it), and travel between pulls in Underwalk/Beyond the Veil for the speed and water-walk.

DUNGEON

This spec has no real heal-the-group kit, so 'support' in a dungeon means holding your own aggro and survival while adding sustained AoE pressure. Pre-pull, keep Death's Presence active for the party's flat damage buff. On the pull, open Deathchaser on the priority target. As adds cluster, fire Soulslam and Reliquary of the Lost whenever Soul Infusion is up. That is your best AoE damage, and your leech lets you shrug off stray aggro without a healer's help (Ghastly Hilt makes your melee abilities cleave nearby adds). Bank Limbo for magic burst and AoE spike phases. Shade resets movement-impairing effects and repositions you when you eat unavoidable pressure. Use Deathchaser and Ghost Claw to lock down a dangerous add, Wraithblade/Dirge/Reap to keep the Fragment engine running between cooldowns, and lean on Lamenting's Ghostly Weapon self-heal if the group's healing falls behind.
