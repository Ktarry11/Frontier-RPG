Closest to Augmentation Evoker. Both are fundamentally damage-dealers whose support value comes through buff and debuff windows and redirect-style cooldowns rather than sustained HPS. Voodoo's party-damage auras (Dark Loa's Blessing) and stacking self-buff De Other Side mirror Ebon Might/Prescience-style empowerment, and War Golem's ally-spell-redirect is functionally the same idea as an Evoker soaking damage for the group via a temporary construct. PLAYSTYLE

Voodoo is a ranged Shadow/Nature/Fire caster that builds a small pile of stacks off Shadow Puppets impacts through De Other Side, which increases your own damage and crit as it stacks. You keep the Hex of Malice DoT rolling, with Zalazane's Malice amplifying it and stretching your Jinxes, and chain nukes: Bad Juju, Malefic Wrath, Hexfire. Puppeteer's Threads left on enemies by your abilities are the connective tissue. Hexfire snaps your threads to unleash their damage instantly, Voodoo Strings makes Bad Juju hits on thread-affected enemies echo extra Nature damage over time, and Overflowing Juju lets Shadow Puppets and Puppeteer's Threads reset Bad Juju's cooldown. Its support identity comes less from throughput healing (its only ally heal is the class kit's Healing Ward, a stationary drop that pulses healing to nearby allies) and more from redirect-and-absorb protection. War Golem is a personal damage absorb, scaling off Intellect, that also redirects harmful spells cast on nearby allies onto itself, and Dark Loa's Blessing buffs the group's damage. Malignant Jinx curses an enemy so their next cast silences them — a soft interrupt. Self-sustain is thin and comes from Spirit generation (Shadow Puppets each collect a Spirit, and Puppeteer's Grasp grants them periodically) and mana returns rather than a heal. Puppeteer's Grasp itself is a mobility and uptime window that lets you cast Malefic Wrath and Bad Juju while moving. Mana is spent aggressively, and Voice of Bwonsamdi is your big damage cooldown.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Zalazane's Malice spreads through Bad Juju and Hexfire and amplifies under Ritual Hexing and Growing Malice, punishing anyone stacked or turtling. Dark Loa's Blessing buffs the whole group's damage on top.
War Golem is an Intellect-scaling personal absorb that also redirects harmful spells off nearby allies, and Malignant Jinx counters a dangerous cast with a silence. You peel for a partner without being a full healer.
Loa Spirits and Puppeteer's Grasp feed a Spirit-generation loop that pays for a mana-hungry rotation and scales your own Spirit stat. Puppeteer's Grasp also casts Jin'do's Wrath and Bad Juju while moving.
WEAKNESSES
No ally heal in the spec at all: no HoT, no direct heal, no off-heal filler. The closest is Healing Ward from the class kit, a stationary drop pulsing to nearby allies. Your own recovery stays self-facing, and a partner under sustained pressure does not get topped.
No anti-CC and no hard CC-break, and Soul Marionette self-roots you for its duration. Chain-CC, or getting caught mid-cooldown, has almost no answer.
De Other Side builds off repeated Shadow Puppets impacts, and the payoff abilities want Zalazane's Malice and Puppeteer's Threads already applied. Early focus fire or forced repositioning resets those layers and guts your output right when the fight needs swinging.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open at range by seeding Zalazane's Malice on the pull point, then loop: Bad Juju on cooldown (main nuke; it hits extra targets, spreads Zalazane's Malice via Malignant, and gets a bonus effect versus Zalazane's Malice targets) → Shadow Puppets on cooldown (summoned burst that collects a Spirit and stacks De Other Side) → Jin'do's Wrath and Hexfire to cash in on your Zalazane's Malice and Ritual Hexing amplification → let Overflowing Juju resets and Voodoo Unleashed feed extra Bad Juju / Shadow Puppets. Keep Zalazane's Malice up (Growing Malice makes each tick hit harder, Prolonged Malice extends it), since Bad Juju, Jin'do's Wrath, and Hexfire all deal more to Zalazane's Malice targets. Detonate Puppeteer's Threads with Hexfire (Voodoo Spirits) for burst and a Spirit. Lean on Spirit generation and Puppeteer's Grasp for between-pull recovery and uptime rather than a heal. Reapply your ground AoE for extra damage when facing a pack.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: summon War Golem before a hard-hitting pull so its absorb-and-redirect is up, and let your party-damage auras (Dark Loa's Blessing) ride on the group. Sustain: Voodoo has no ally heal, so your "support" is protection and pressure. Keep Zalazane's Malice applied to the dangerous add or boss (spread by Bad Juju/Hexfire via Malignant, amplified by Ritual Hexing), and pour AoE into trash to speed clears: your ground damage, plus Bad Juju/Hexfire hitting extra targets via Dark Effigy. Use Malignant Jinx to counter a dangerous caster's next cast with a silence. Emergency: War Golem when the group is about to eat a big targeted or AoE magic hit. It absorbs for you, scaling off Intellect, and redirects harmful spells off nearby allies. Keep your De Other Side stacks alive off Shadow Puppets so your damage stays high, since damage IS the support here, and use Voice of Bwonsamdi as your burst window on priority targets.
