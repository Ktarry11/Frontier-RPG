RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Vengeance Demon Hunter's self-sustain tank chassis. Demonic Will is a Soul Cleave-style spender that heals you off your own output. Tyrannical Resolve, which ignores a large share of damage taken and pays a Felfire bonus when it ends, reads like a Fiery Brand / metamorphosis-mitigation identity. Inner Demon is the timed Demon Form damage and mitigation window (Bulwark of Azzinoth, Grommash's Folly). Where it diverges is the external-buff layer. Infernal Whipcrack enrages allies with reduced damage taken plus haste, Eye of the Tyrant grants party/raid haste and boosts your attack power, and Betrayer enrages your group on your dodges. That's an Augmentation-Evoker-style external-cooldown flavor Vengeance DH doesn't have. Net: a Vengeance-DH self-sustain tank with a raid-buff layer stapled on.

PLAYSTYLE

A Felfury builder-spender loop fed partly by getting hit. Illidari Training turns dodges into Energy, and Demonic Fortitude generates Felfury off damage taken or avoided while a defensive is up. You spend Felfury on Carve (a channeled frontal cleave that charges up for more damage and dodge) and Felrend (a Felfury-generating whirlwind cleave) while tanking and soaking threat. The self-heal engine is deep and layered: Demonic Will reduces physical damage taken and heals you off your direct damaging abilities, Burning Hatred is an enrage that instantly generates 6 Felfury and makes your melee leech health as Chaos damage, Hateforged Barrier is a Stamina- and dodge-scaling absorb that regenerates your health while it holds, Skull of Gul'dan is a burst of extra maximum health and Energy, and Tyrant's Gaze is an intimidate-and-combust that deals Shadowflame damage. Big cooldowns sit on top. Tyrannical Resolve is a taunt that ignores a large share of damage taken and ends in a Felfire burst, and the Inner Demon window brings Bulwark of Azzinoth's reduced damage taken and Grommash's Folly's haste and crit-heal over the always-on Dark Lord damage-up and damage-taken-down above 75% health. Group-facing tools (Infernal Whipcrack, Eye of the Tyrant, Betrayer, Doomscarring) make you as much a raid-buff bot as a brawler. You end fights still standing far more often than with a kill. That's the design.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Tyrannical Resolve ignores damage for a window and pays out in Felfire, Demonic Will cuts physical damage and heals, and Bulwark of Azzinoth reduces damage taken during Inner Demon. Dark Lord sits on top above 75% health. Nothing bursts through that stack.
Betrayer and Tyrannical Resolve pull threat and enrage your group off dodges. Infernal Whipcrack enrages allies for reduced damage taken and haste, Eye of the Tyrant hands out party haste, and Doomscarring scars enemies to raise ally attack power.
Demonic Will heals on damage, Burning Hatred leeches in melee, Hateforged Barrier absorbs and regens, and Grommash's Folly heals off crits during Inner Demon. Long brawls rarely need a healer.
WEAKNESSES
Damage is modest. You win by not dying, not by killing. Solo kills crawl.
The defense is anchored to dodge and parry: Illidari Training, Burning Commander's armor and magic resist off dodge rating, Nether Disciple, Vengeance Is Mine. It only ramps while something is meleeing you, so ranged and casters sidestep most of it.
Resource income comes from being hit, through Illidari Training's dodge-to-Energy and Demonic Fortitude's Felfury off damage taken and avoided. Stop being the focus and your Energy and Felfury dry up right when you want to fight back.
Felfury overcaps. Twin Slice, Felrend, Demonic Will and Bane of Betrayal each add 1 and Burning Hatred dumps 6, while Carve, Sargeron Smite, Sunder and Inner Demon are all you have to spend it on. Extended fights waste resource.
Sustain hangs on channeling Carve, your biggest heal window through Straight to the Nether's leech and Demonic Will's heal-on-damage. A knockback or hard stun cuts the channel and your healing with it, which is why anything that displaces or stuns, Scarlet Crusade especially, feels so bad.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Keep AoE pressure up with Felrend against packs, and Carve channeled when you can afford to charge it for a big frontal cleave (longer channel = more damage and dodge via Battle Finesse) → Demonic Will whenever HP dips, since it both cuts physical damage and heals you off your own hits → Burning Hatred to enrage, dump 6 Felfury, and turn your melee into a leech on a tougher fight → Hateforged Barrier for its Stamina/dodge-scaling absorb-plus-regen when you're taking real damage → Tyrant's Gaze to intimidate and combust a dangerous target → Tyrannical Resolve as your emergency button against anything threatening to kill you, riding its ignore-damage window into the Felfire burst when it ends.

DUNGEON

Pull: open with Tyrant's Gaze or Tyrannical Resolve to taunt/lock threat and intimidate the pull. Then Felrend and Carve for AoE threat and damage (Unholy Endurance boosts Carve's threat). Drop Whispers of the Pit to fear/scatter a caster pack or peel adds off the group. Sustain: Demonic Will on cooldown for its physical DR and heal-on-damage. Burning Hatred's leech-enrage and Hateforged Barrier's absorb-plus-regen cover upkeep. On a long or rough pull, drop Infernal Whipcrack to enrage the group (reduced damage taken plus haste). Emergency: Tyrannical Resolve when you, or whoever you taunted for, are in trouble; its ignore-damage window ends in a Felfire burst. Hateforged Barrier for a fast top-off. Skull of Gul'dan to spike your maximum health and Energy on a rough pull. Fel Bargain trades banked Energy into health in a pinch. Lean on Betrayer's dodge-triggered off-hand strikes and group enrage on avoidance-heavy pulls, and save Eye of the Tyrant's party haste for the pull or burn phase you want to reinforce.
