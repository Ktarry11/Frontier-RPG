RETAIL FEEL
Closest to retail Arcane Mage. The core loop is a near-1:1 mechanical match for Arcane Blast stacking into a big spender: cast to build a stacking resource (glyphs, like Arcane Charges/Blast stacks), then detonate it. The Glyphic Overload plus Eye of the Beholder burst window plays like layering Arcane Surge/Radiant Spark. Silencing Rune is a school-lock control tool like Counterspell, Frost Glyph's slow is a Mage-style kite tool, and Ley Walker/Warpdagger covering repositioning is the same reposition-mid-fight-via-Blink pattern Arcane Mages use. The analogy holds cleanly on identity too: a fragile burst caster with strong control and no sustain. Arcane Mage has zero self-heal either.

PLAYSTYLE

Build glyph stacks off Elemental Burst, Primordial Blast and Runic Obliteration. The chain is sequential: casting grants Frost Glyph, casting again while Frost Glyph is up grants Flame Glyph, casting again while that's up grants Arcane Glyph. You detonate whichever glyph is active with Glyphic Ruin, a bigger cast-time hit, or the cheaper instant Thaumaturgy, for its bonus effect. Frost Glyph Unleash hits and applies a slow, Flame Glyph Unleash hits plus a Fire DoT, and Arcane Glyph Unleash via Primordial Salvos splashes nearby targets. An economy note from the class's players: you rarely want all three glyphs on a single target. Arcane Glyph's unleash is an AoE splash, so on single target you run a tighter loop and keep Flame Glyph's Fire DoT rolling, saving the full three-glyph spread for AoE. Runic Obliteration is a short-CD instant filler that both hits and feeds a glyph. Primordial Pulse (a runed AoE ground zone) and Manuscription (a strong Arcane damage-over-time that envelops the target and nearby enemies, a standout pick for both single-target and AoE) are your pack-clear and sustained-damage tools. Cooldowns stack for burst windows: Glyphic Overload empowers your next glyph unleashes, Eye of the Beholder adds crit and boosts Glyphic Ruin and Primordial Blast damage, and Kirin Tor Agent then rapidly cools your Rune spells and Phase Out for its duration. Defense is thin and kite-based. Frigid Blast is a repeatable frost nuke that the Cold Elements talent can reset and make instant-cast, Frost Glyph Unleash slows, Ley Walker's Warpdagger reactivation strips roots and snares, Phase Rush grants a speed burst after Thaumaturgy, and Silencing Rune drops a delayed area silence. There is no damage-reduction cooldown, so surviving means never being caught in melee.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Layer Eye of the Beholder and Glyphic Overload into one nova and the target is gone, with Arcane Student guaranteeing the Glyphic Ruin crit behind it.
Silencing Rune area school-locks, Frost Glyph Unleash slows, Ley Walker turns Warpdagger into a root and snare break, and Phase Rush bursts your speed. You make distance and hold it.
Cold Elements resets Frigid Blast and makes it instant on a 20% chance off Elemental Burst and Primordial Blast damage, so a hard frost nuke stays available alongside the glyph chain.
WEAKNESSES
No healing and no damage-reduction cooldown anywhere in the kit. You cannot mitigate or out-heal a single hit, so being caught in melee is usually fatal. Burst and kite or lose outright.
The glyph chain runs Frost to Flame to Arcane and all three stages have to be tracked. Mistime the sequence and the glyph payload is wasted.
Most abilities cost a percentage of base mana and the big cooldowns take large chunks. Focusing Crystals' partial regen is your only in-combat relief: no mana-return burst, no mana-burn.
The control is strong on paper and clunky in play. Glacial Rune arms after about a second, Silencing Rune has its own delay, Cryobrand is a short-range single-target freeze, and repositioning is Warpdagger's throw-then-teleport, not a Blink. None of it reacts fast enough to gap-closing melee.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open at range building a glyph with Elemental Burst or Runic Obliteration (instant, glyph-feeding). On single target, the players who main the spec (MarkTheRed, Exasperated) agree you do NOT push the full Frost/Flame/Arcane chain. You run a tight ~2-glyph loop and keep Flame Glyph's Fire DoT (the 'fire debuff') stacked, because Arcane Glyph's unleash splashes nearby targets via Primordial Salvos and is largely wasted on one target. The core single-target loop is Elemental Burst -> Glyphic Ruin -> Primordial Blast, so a fresh Flame Glyph is up as Ruin resolves, chaining Primordial Blast on cooldown as your main filler nuke. Dump the active glyph with the cheap, fast Thaumaturgy when up, or Glyphic Ruin (bigger cast-time hit) otherwise. Manuscription is a standout damage talent for both single-target and AoE (per Maniac); its Arcane damage-over-time envelops the target and nearby enemies. For AoE, run all three glyphs and add Primordial Pulse's ground zone or Manuscription on packs. Every Arcane-glyph unleash already splashes via Primordial Salvos. Drop Silencing Rune under a caster mob to shut down a dangerous school mid-cast. Save Eye of the Beholder for elites and tough single targets to boost the Glyphic Ruin/Primordial Blast burst window, and Glyphic Overload to empower your next glyph unleashes before a hard pull.

DUNGEON

On pull, drop Silencing Rune under the first caster pack to blunt their casts. During AoE trash, keep all three glyphs rolling with Elemental Burst/Runic Obliteration. Dump preferentially through the instant Thaumaturgy rather than the longer Glyphic Ruin so you're not locked into a cast mid-trash. On a single priority target, hold to a tight ~2-glyph loop and keep Flame Glyph's Fire DoT stacked, since Arcane Glyph's unleash splashes via Primordial Salvos and is wasted on one target. Frost Glyph Unleash's slow peels a mob running down a squishy party member, and Primordial Pulse/Manuscription cleaves clumped adds. On yourself there's no damage-reduction button. Stay at max range and kite with Frigid Blast, Frost Glyph's slow, Ley Walker's root-break, and Phase Rush's speed burst rather than trying to tank a hit. On boss pulls, stack Eye of the Beholder / Glyphic Overload for the burst window during a vulnerability phase, letting Kirin Tor Agent cool your Rune spells and Phase Out. Keep Silencing Rune ready for any interruptible cast.
