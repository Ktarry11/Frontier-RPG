RETAIL FEEL
Closest to a Fistweaver-flavored melee hybrid crossed with Retribution Paladin. Solar Power functions like Chi or Holy Power, and it comes out of standing in melee and swinging: auto attacks via Vow of the Valkyr, Sunwalker stacks, and ability hits all feed it. Building toward the Dawn empowered state plays like a Serenity/Thunder Focus Tea window. Its support is a party damage buff in March of the Valkyr plus passive self-sustain (Light of the Sun's parry heal, Arbiter of Light's Vow return, Glorious Execution's mana refund), not a heal spell. Layered on top is a Retribution-Paladin-flavored burst and CC kit. Judgement Day reads like a Repentance-into-finisher window, echoing Ret's Judgement-into-burst, with Sunslam, Valkyr's Calling and Divine Retribution's stored-damage detonation as the signature Holy cooldowns.

PLAYSTYLE

Melee combat is the engine room. You swing two-handers, or dual-wield via Valkyr's Grip's off-hand bonus. Auto attacks build Solar Power (Vow of the Valkyr generates it and buffs their damage) alongside your melee-damage stacks from Sunwalker, and you weave Horusath Blast in as a ranged filler and extra Solar Power. You dump into Glorious Execution, a dual-weapon strike that regenerates mana equal to a share of the damage dealt, and Justice, a dual-weapon spin/cleave. Periodically you burn resource on Champion of the Sun to make your autos deal bonus Holy damage for a window. Solar Power builds toward a Dawn empowered state, refreshed by Pendant of the Sun and with cooldowns cut by It's Always Sunny, that auto-crits Horusath Blast and unlocks Sunslam. Playing it well is about timing that Dawn window and Judgement Day's disorient-and-detonate, not following a flat priority list. The spec line has no direct in-combat heal spell. Sustain is Glorious Execution's mana refund, Sunsworn's auto-attack mana returns, Light of the Sun's parry-triggered self-heal, and Arbiter of Light's Vow of Light health return, so the class kit's Solar Prayer, an instant self-heal usable even while stunned, is the one heal button worth the Essence.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Champion of the Sun's Holy-damage window into Judgement Day's disorient, detonated with Glorious Execution or Sunslam and topped by Divine Retribution's stored-damage burst. A real kill setup.
Glorious Execution refunds mana as a share of its damage and Sunsworn returns more off autos, while Light of the Sun and Arbiter of Light heal you passively. You rarely stop swinging. The only direct heal button is the class kit's Solar Prayer, at the cost of an Essence pick.
Valkyr's Calling leaps you up immune to all physical and spell damage for its duration, then lands for AoE Holy damage. Sun Stride is a shorter-cooldown mobility leap on top. Most melee never get an escape and reset like that.
WEAKNESSES
Glorious Execution, Justice, Champion of the Sun, Judgement Day and Sunslam all require weapon range. Against a kiter you have Horusath Blast as filler and nothing else.
Judgement Day is a single disorient that breaks on damage, Chains of Light is an anti-escape snare rather than a hard root, and Sunslam's stun is gated behind Dawn being active. Nothing here reliably forces a fight.
No direct in-combat heal in the spec line. Survivability is Valkyr's Calling plus passive returns from Light of the Sun's parry heal, Arbiter of Light and Glorious Execution's mana refund, none of which help mid-burst. Without Solar Prayer, a focus that waits out Valkyr's Calling drops you.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open at range with Horusath Blast to start building Solar Power before the mob closes. Once in melee, keep your auto attacks up (Vow of the Valkyr generates Solar Power and Sunwalker stacks) and spend on Justice for cleave. Glorious Execution on cooldown gives both burst damage and the mana refund that keeps you from needing to drink. Pop Champion of the Sun before or during a tough pull to build the Holy-damage window. Drop Judgement Day on anything dangerous or elite to disorient it, then detonate with Glorious Execution/Sunslam once adds are clear. Use Sunslam for its stun while Dawn is active, and Chains of Light to keep a runner from fleeing. You have no direct heal spell, so if HP dips hard, lean on Light of the Sun's parry heal, Arbiter of Light's Vow return, and Valkyr's Calling's immunity rather than trying to heal through it. Use Justice on packs for AoE.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: March of the Valkyr for the raid-wide damage buff, plus whichever set-and-forget Vow (Vow of Grace/Vow of Light/Vow of the Valkyr) fits the pull. In combat: keep autos and Horusath Blast flowing to build Solar Power. Fire Glorious Execution and Justice on cooldown for damage plus the mana refund. Drop Judgement Day to disorient the most dangerous single add, then detonate it. Stack Champion of the Sun ahead of burn phases, and hold Valkyr's Calling as your own immunity/reset cooldown if you get focused or a raid-wide hits hard. Note the spec has no interrupt, no direct in-combat heal, no cleanse, and no combat rez. Chains of Light only snares, and your only healing is passive: Light of the Sun's parry heal, Arbiter of Light, Glorious Execution's mana refund. Don't rely on it for those group duties.
