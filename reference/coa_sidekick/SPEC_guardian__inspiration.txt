RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Augmentation Evoker. Both are melee-adjacent support units whose primary value is buffing an ally's damage output rather than direct healing, with Song of Steel and Inspiring Presence scaling off your attack power ≈ Ebon Might scaling off the Evoker. Both keep personal DPS uptime while doing it (the Ballad/Broad Sweep/Ram loop ≈ the Evoker's own spellcasting). Both bring hard utility on top: Liberation / Cavalry Charge / Song of Battle's stun ≈ Landslide root / Wing Buffet / Time Stop. The divergence is the frame. Inspiration is literally standing in melee with a shield and a block-rating stat, so it plays like an Augmentation Evoker that decided to also gear like a Protection Warrior.

PLAYSTYLE

You open by weaving Broad Sweep and Ram with your Ballads off their shared charge bar. Inspiring Leader turns Pulverize and Broad Sweep into Ballads, each Ballad cast stacks Tempo, and the 3rd stack consumes to perform a bonus Sound of War. Spend charges, get a free nuke. Underneath that you're planting your Standard of Valiance and Banners on cooldown: Standard of Valiance with Stand Your Ground! for allied physical damage reduction and You Shall Not Fall! for stun and grip resist, plus Banner of Swiftness for haste and Banner of Conquest for crit. You layer party buffs like Song of Steel (attack/spell power), Champion's Presence (haste enrage), Spirit of Battle (hit) and Battle Drums onto whoever needs them. That's where your support comes from, since you're a buff and mitigation unit wearing a shield, not a direct-heal caster. Defensively you lean on Veteran of the Third War's on-block self-heal and Fearless (damage reduction that scales with nearby enemies) to solo-sustain, then spend Liberation and Cavalry Charge (which prevents root and slow on allies via Cavalry Tactics) to peel CC off whoever you're protecting.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Liberation breaks stuns, fears and polymorph, grants immunity, and dispels magic. Cavalry Charge stops roots and slows landing on allies, and Standard of Valiance's You Shall Not Fall! gives the group passive stun and grip resistance.
Veteran of the Third War heals on block and Fearless stacks damage reduction while you are surrounded. Voice of an Angel resets your defensive cooldowns, so you outlast focus fire alone.
Song of Battle is AoE damage into an AoE stun and Harrowing Melody adds AoE damage with anti-heal. On a shield frame you stand in the middle of a scrum and still matter.
WEAKNESSES
No hard single-target heal. Standard of Recovery's ground HoT and Knight's Calling's self-restore only drip, which does nothing for a partner being trained down fast.
Anti-magic is Little Drummer's curse resist and Dissonance's resistance shred. Sustained caster pressure is your worst matchup.
Ram and Ballad charges plus Song of Battle and Champion's Presence cooldowns make your damage and CC windows finite. Caught with charges down, you are auto-attacking and weaving fillers.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Ram on cooldown (big single-target hit) > cast Ballads on cooldown (instant damage + builds Tempo toward a free Sound of War) > Broad Sweep as your energy-dump filler and cleave (Inspiring Leader also turns it into a Ballad), chasing the 3-stack Tempo proc. Open packs with Cavalry Charge to close distance, and use Song of Battle for 3+ mobs, where damage ticks into an AoE stun finisher. Pop Champion's Presence for a haste burst window. Minstrel's stacking haste builds as you cast Ballads. Use Veteran of the Third War's on-block heal and Fearless while surrounded. Voice of an Angel instantly resets Liberation, Harrowing Melody, and Hero's March when you need them back.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: drop Standard of Valiance (Stand Your Ground! group physical DR + You Shall Not Fall! stun/grip resist), then apply your party buffs on whoever needs them: Song of Steel (attack/spell power), Banner of Conquest (crit) or Banner of Swiftness (haste), Spirit of Battle (hit). During the pull: keep the Standard and Banners refreshed. Weave Ballads + Broad Sweep + Ram for your own damage and Tempo/Sound of War procs. Fire Song of Battle on add-heavy pulls for AoE-damage-into-stun, and use Hero's March for the party haste and Sound of War trigger. Field Commander makes your next Song of Steel unleash a bonus effect after placing a Standard. Hold Liberation and Cavalry Charge in reserve as emergency CC-breaks and peels rather than spending them on cooldown.
