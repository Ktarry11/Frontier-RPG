RETAIL FEEL
Closest to a self-sufficient ranged burst-caster with a built-in immunity panic button: Fire Mage's ramp-and-crit identity crossed with the CC-proof defensive of a Fel-shaped caster. Survivability is Earth's Embrace, a short-cooldown full physical/spell immunity that heals for spell power every 2 sec and is explicitly usable while stunned, feared or polymorphed. That's its Ice Block / Nether Ward moment, and it punches straight through a CC chain. Damage ramps through Earthshaping stacks the way a Fire Mage banks crit and Hot Streak, Crushing the Earth converts armor penetration into spell power, and Volcanic Blast layers fire splash off your crits. It carries real disruption too. Magma Fissure sends nearby enemies fleeing in horror, Seismic Grasp dismounts and slows a target, and Golem Form is a second defensive and haste cooldown. Its spec line has no ally heal or cleanse beyond Molten Fervor's raid haste. Outward tools like Grove Guardian's healing/cleansing zone or Protective Roar's group heal have to be bought from the class kit with Ability Essence. On its own picks it stays a self-reliant DPS caster rather than any kind of support.

PLAYSTYLE

You fight at range as an Intellect caster on a dual resource. Rage is fed by Geode Barrage and your Seismic abilities, with Rupturer generating extra off Seismic Spike/Crash. Mana pays for nearly every hard-hitting nuke: Lithic Lance, Seismic Tremor, Cracking the Earth, Eruption, Magma Fissure, Terrasurge. The core loop is Geode Barrage and your Seismic abilities feeding Earthshaping stacks; at 15 stacks that's 15% haste plus up to ~60% more spender damage and crit damage, which you cash into Stoneshard and Terrasurge. Eruption comes in once Earthshaping is stacked, its magma splash hitting above-75%-health targets harder via Magmatism. Nearly everything you hit with is Physical damage, so armor penetration is a core scaling stat, not spell penetration. Survival isn't a separate healing rotation. It's the full-immunity-plus-heal-over-time of Earth's Embrace, usable even while stunned, feared or polymorphed, backstopped by Golem Form's armor and haste transformation. Your reach outside your own health bar is an interrupt in Cave In, which school-locks the target, and the raid spell-haste buff Molten Fervor. Not a heal.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Earth's Embrace is full immunity plus a heal-over-time and it casts while stunned, feared, or polymorphed. Most casters never get a panic button that works through CC. Golem Form is a second armor and haste layer behind it.
Earthshaping stacks ramp Lithic Lance and Terrasurge, Cataclysm resets the Seismic family, and Volcanic Blast splashes fire off crits. The longer you hold a window the bigger it gets.
Cave In is a real interrupt with a school lock, Magma Fissure knocks back a line, and Cracking the Earth is a heavy AoE slow. Molten Fervor hands the raid spell haste. Rare utility on a durable chassis.
WEAKNESSES
Every hard nuke taxes mana: Seismic Tremor, Lithic Lance, Eruption, Magma Fissure, Terrasurge. A long fight or an extended 1vX runs you dry unless you lean on One With The Earth's Lithic Lance mana return.
The spec line gives buffs and externals, Molten Fervor's raid haste and Essence of Dispersion's spell-damage reduction, not healing or cleansing. Ally healing and poison or disease cleansing are class-kit picks costing Ability Essence against damage nodes. Nothing removes a magic effect or a curse.
Lithic Lance, Geode Barrage and Eruption are all interruptible and silenceable. With Earth's Embrace and Golem Form down you get locked off the ramp and worn out.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Geode Barrage or Seismic Tremor to bank rage and start Earthshaping stacks. Build Earthshaping with Geode Barrage, your main spammable filler, watching for the Lithic Lance conversion and Primal Shaman's Mask procs. Lithic Lance itself is a hard-hitting nuke that procs off Geode Barrage. Spend Earthshaping into Stoneshard, your main spender, which One With The Earth also refunds mana on. Fire Terrasurge as a big armor-shredding hit once stacks are high; under Seismic Tremor it always crits, and it pierces armor and absorbs. Keep Seismic Tremor's ramping DoT rolling on a single target. Weave in Eruption once Earthshaping is stacked (its magma splash hits above-75%-health targets harder via Magmatism), and lean on Cracking the Earth for packs. Let One With The Earth's Stoneshard mana return keep you casting rather than sitting to drink. Hold Earth's Embrace (immunity + heal) for a dangerous pull, and Golem Form for a haste/armor burst window.

DUNGEON

Pull with Cracking the Earth (AoE damage + slow) or Magma Fissure, a delayed Fire burst that fears nearby enemies. Build damage by cycling Geode Barrage -> Lithic Lance -> Seismic Tremor on cooldown to keep Earthshaping ramped, and layer Eruption once stacks are up. Support the group with what you have: keep Molten Fervor up for the raid's spell haste, and use Cave In to interrupt and school-lock a dangerous caster add. This spec does have a real interrupt. If you get focused or eat a bad mechanic, pop Golem Form for armor/haste, or, worst case, Earth's Embrace (immunity + self-heal, works even through a stun or fear). Manage mana with One With The Earth's Stoneshard return so you don't dry out mid-pull.
