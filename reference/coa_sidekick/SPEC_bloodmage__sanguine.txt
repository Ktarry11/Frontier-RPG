RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Affliction Warlock crossed with a Blood DK personal-survival layer. Like Affliction you pay your own health to cast, stack ramping debuffs (Thirst, Twisted Magic's Shadow-damage-taken stacks, Malediction's damage-taken DoT), and grind a target down over time rather than bursting it. The health economy is pure Blood DK. Vampiric Fang is a Death Strike-shaped dump that steals health straight out of the target, and it must be used on a timer, before Thirst caps, or you punish yourself. Shadowfang Shield's steady self-heal tick and Aneurysm's heal on a successful interrupt bleed health back into you as you play. Blood Craving (uninterruptible, unsilenceable, self-healing for its duration) reads like a self-only Anti-Magic Shell layered on top of a caster kit. Group healing is a footnote: the class kit's Fleshcraft can spot-heal an ally and Shadowfang Ritual patches one up. Virtually every drop of sustain is aimed at keeping you alive.

PLAYSTYLE

You live in caster form spending your own health bar to cast. Bloodmoon Blast, Atherann's Anguish (a ground-target hemoplague that banks a share of your damage then detonates), Sanguine Rupture, Valanar's Vengeance and Keleseth's Calamity all cost a percentage of your health, and every one builds Rage and a stack of Thirst. Rage goes back into your nukes and into Malediction's damage-taken DoT. Thirst has to go into Vampiric Fang, which lifesteals and scales harder per stack, before it hits 10 and flips into a self-inflicted output penalty ticking every 1.5 sec. That's a three-way balancing act: spend health to make Rage and Thirst, spend Rage to keep the nukes flowing, spend Thirst on Vampiric Fang to heal back up. Valanar's Vengeance healing you for a share of its damage, Vampiric Fang's stack-scaled lifesteal and passives like Vampirism top you off in the background. When it goes wrong, bank Blood Craving or Sacrificial Rite and ride it out.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Vampiric Fang lifesteal and Vampirism healing you off your own damage stack under Blood Craving's uninterruptible self-heal window. It adds up to something close to unkillable.
DoTs like Atherann's Anguish and Shadow Hemorrhage grind under Twisted Magic's ramping Shadow debuff, with Sanguine Rupture for the pack. The same casts that kill also heal you.
Blood Craving makes you uninterruptible and unsilenceable while it runs, and heals you through it. Almost nothing else escapes an interrupt chain.
WEAKNESSES
Real CC, no clean lockdown. Aneurysm is only an interrupt and school-lock; Hemostasis and Hemophobia are fear-type, which scatters targets and breaks on damage. A DoT-covered kill target does not stay pinned, so kill speed lags well behind survivability.
The Rage and Thirst engine runs on casting. Silence, interrupts, or forced movement stall your damage and your regen together.
The cost model bites back. Health-cost spells drain you directly, and Thirst capped at 10 flips into an output penalty ticking every 1.5 sec. Play it badly and the group's survivor is the first one in trouble.
Not a party healer. Fleshcraft spot-heals one ally for Rage and applies Sated, and Shadowfang Ritual patches someone up, but the spec carries itself and nobody else.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open in your caster form: Atherann's Anguish on the ground to bank damage for its detonation, then Bloodmoon Blast as the main filler to build Thirst and Rage while chipping damage. Sanguine Rupture on any pack of 2+. Dump Thirst via Vampiric Fang around 3-5 stacks for a lifesteal burst that also tops your own health back up, and never let it hit 10 and flip into the self-punishing penalty. Use Malediction on anything tanky for the damage-taken debuff plus DoT. Valanar's Vengeance and Keleseth's Calamity are your Thirst-cheapened burst nukes, and Valanar's Vengeance also heals you for a share of its damage as it hits. Aneurysm interrupts a dangerous cast. Hold Blood Craving or Sacrificial Rite in reserve for an 'about to die' spike.

DUNGEON

You have no ally-targeted heals, so everything below is damage and self-preservation. Keep your DoTs rolling: Atherann's Anguish planted for its detonation, Malediction on the priority or tanky target for the damage-taken debuff, and Shadow Hemorrhage ticking on whatever lives longest. Filler: Bloodmoon Blast and Sanguine Rupture on cooldown. That is what keeps Rage and Thirst flowing, dumped into Valanar's Vengeance and Keleseth's Calamity as your Thirst stacks cheapen them. Dump Thirst via Vampiric Fang before it caps at 10, both to lifesteal yourself up and to avoid the self-inflicted penalty. Blood Craving and Sacrificial Rite are your emergency layer for damage-spike phases. Aneurysm is your interrupt for dangerous caster mobs.
