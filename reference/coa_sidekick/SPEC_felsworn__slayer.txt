RETAIL FEEL
Closest to a Fury Warrior chassis with Demon Hunter flavor bolted on. Felguard's Inner-Demon melee leech plus Inner Demon's dodge/crit-damage window is a self-sustain and burst engine. The class kit's Hateforged Barrier, an absorb that regenerates while it holds, mirrors Demon Hunter mitigation stacking. Net: a Fury-Warrior-tempo cleave bruiser with Demon Hunter self-leech and a channeled AoE spin.

PLAYSTYLE

Felsworn Slayer is a dual-wield melee brawler built on a Felfury builder-spender loop and a constant stream of off-hand triggers off crits. Dancing Blades turns melee crits into extra off-hand hits on nearby enemies. You open with Bane of Frailty to strip a chunk of the target's armor, then Azzinoth's Assault, an armor-shredding spender whose Fel Blades stacks keep peeling armor while Azzinoth's Rage adds passive Armor Penetration on top. Those three stack into a large share of ignored armor, which is where much of the spec's physical damage comes from. Chain into Annihilan Strike, a guaranteed-crit two-weapon hit that generates 2 Felfury, and weave Glaiving and Felhoof Charge into the loop for reach and cleave. Fighting fuels you. While Inner Demon is active, Felguard turns your melee damage into a heal, and the class kit's Hateforged Barrier layers an absorb that regenerates health while it holds, so the harder you fight inside an Inner Demon window the more self-sufficient you get. That window is the periodic power spike: True Self grants dodge and crit damage, Blighted Strikes applies healing-reduction to your target, and it enables the leech-and-mitigation riders on top of the always-on Pit Lord's Strength bonuses (extra damage and reduced damage taken above 75% health).

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
While Inner Demon is up, Felguard heals you for a cut of all your melee damage, and the class kit's Hateforged Barrier adds an absorb that regenerates while it holds. That grinds out a long 1vX with little outside healing. It is window-gated, so time the aggression to it.
Felguard's Inner Demon damage reduction and True Self's dodge and crit damage layer inside the same window, with Fury Unleashed and Fury of the Illidari spiking on top. Pit Lord's Strength holds above 75% health. A high anti-focus floor for a melee class.
Felhoof Charge generates Felfury on the way in and Reachwalker's dash heals you there. Once you are on someone, shaking you is hard.
WEAKNESSES
Pure melee with only Felhoof Charge and Reachwalker to close. A competent kiter turns off the crit and leech loop entirely.
Group contribution is passive auras and one interrupt: Demonfire Pact's crit aura, the Illidari Intuition Agility buffs, Felbreak. Illidari Barrier, the one ally shield, has to be talented in off Hateforged Barrier, and you cannot heal or dispel anyone. A bruiser, not a support.
No reliable stun against players. You lean on gap-closers and mitigation windows instead, and once Fury of the Illidari and Fury Unleashed are burned you meet the next wave on baseline damage reduction.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

1) Open pulls with Immolation Aura for its recurring Fire pulse on nearby enemies. 2) Azzinoth's Assault on cooldown as your armor-shred spender (Azzinoth's Rage/Fel Blades; consumes 2 Felfury and seeds the loop). 3) Follow with Annihilan Strike as your main hard hit. It generates 2 Felfury and cleaves both weapons into your target, with a guaranteed crit above 75% health. 4) Keep Glaiving in the rotation for off-hand damage and Felfury (Twin Fury generates extra Felfury on Annihilan Strike crits). 5) Dump Fury Unleashed on 3+ mob pulls: the movement-speed AoE spin, striking all nearby enemies with both weapons. 6) Use Felhoof Charge to gap-close on casters and runners, and Reachwalker to reposition and heal. 7) Lean on Felguard's Inner-Demon melee leech and Hateforged Barrier to sustain you. Fury of the Illidari and Fury Unleashed are your big burst windows for a dangerous fight.

DUNGEON

Your dungeon value is a self-sufficient melee presence plus utility: Scars of Suffering's ally attack-power scar and Nether Champion buffs. 1) Open with Felhoof Charge to close on the priority target, or straight into Azzinoth's Assault into Annihilan Strike for instant cleave and off-hand triggers. 2) Immolation Aura immediately after for sustained AoE pulse damage on trash. 3) Apply Scars of Suffering (via Azzinoth's Assault) so your allies hit the scarred target harder. That plus Dancing Blades' cleave is your real group-facing contribution — the kit has no dispel or interrupt. 4) Sustain yourself through Felguard's Inner-Demon melee leech and Hateforged Barrier so the healer's mana goes to everyone else. 5) Save Fury of the Illidari and Fury Unleashed for the pull that's dangerous, and lean on Inner Demon's dodge (True Self) as a personal survival window on rough trash.
