RETAIL FEEL
Closest to a Guardian Druid / Blood Death Knight hybrid. Ravenous Bite is a Death Strike analog, a melee swing that heals a share of the damage it deals. Thick Pelt and Shadows In The Night stack Guardian-style passive mitigation. Eternal Curse's tradeoff, item armor and Stamina and threat up with damage down, is exactly Blood/Bear presence stance math, backed by a real taunt in Blood Howl. Rage generated by landing and eating hits, rather than a clean builder/spender like Runic Power, mirrors the combat-contact resource loop. Where it deviates from a pure tank is the self-sustain-through-damage layer: Ravenous Bite lifesteal, Monstrous Hunger's enraged Bloodfang Bites, and Enraging Wounds' Stamina-scaling melee-crit leech. That lets it stay up on its own without a healer. No single retail spec does both the deep-mitigation tanking and the vampiric self-heal at once.

PLAYSTYLE

Eternal is a shapeshifting bruiser/tank that lives in two stances. Human form is where you build up before committing. The real power sits on the other side of Eternal Curse: threat, item armor and Stamina go up, damage goes down, and the melee/tank half of the kit unlocks. That gets you Ravenous Bite (which heals you off the damage it deals), Bloodfang Bite, Claw Sweep, Rotclaw, Night Hunter's Howl and Eternal Resolve, plus an AoE taunt in Blood Howl. Monstrous Hunger turns your next 6 Bloodfang Bites into halved-GCD enrage hits, and Enraging Wounds makes every melee crit leech health scaling with your Stamina. Feral's flat 4-rage-per-4-sec floor means you're never fully dry. The hard part is holding Eternal Curse for its stacked mitigation and lifesteal, timing Monstrous Hunger and Blood Pact windows, and using Night Hunter's Howl to control the fight instead of hopping stances mid-burst.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Eternal Curse adds item armor and Stamina, Thick Pelt and Shadows In The Night mitigate on top, and Blood Pact is there on demand. Full focus does not burst you down.
Ravenous Bite heals off the damage it deals and Enraging Wounds leeches on every melee crit. Uncoordinated pressure cannot out-damage that.
Blood Howl is a real AoE taunt, Night Hunter's Howl an AoE snare. You can peel for a partner and lock down adds, not just save yourself.
WEAKNESSES
Eternal Curse taxes your damage. Bruiser, not assassin, and your fights run long.
Rage comes from landing and eating melee hits, so a disciplined kiter throttles your whole resource engine. Night Hunter's Howl is your only answer.
Nothing in the spec or class kit locks a spell school. Fear like Terrify is the only way you stop a caster, and every defensive and lifesteal tool needs Eternal Curse plus melee contact. In-spec you protect people by body-blocking, taunting and snaring.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Stay transformed via Eternal Curse as your default grinding stance for the built-in lifesteal. Filler on single targets: Ravenous Bite (self-heals off the damage dealt) and Bloodfang Bite carry the rotation, with Rotclaw for its bleed and Rage. On 2+ mobs, weave in Claw Sweep on cooldown for the cleave. If HP dips, pop Monstrous Hunger to turn your next 6 Bloodfang Bites into halved-GCD enrage hits instead of sitting to eat and drink. Use Night Hunter's Howl to snare adds that get too many, Blood Pact to eat a spike hit, and save Eternal Resolve for a tough single target as a max-health/attack-speed/healing-received frenzy.

DUNGEON

Pull: shift with Eternal Curse for the item-armor and Stamina bump, taunt the pack with Blood Howl to lock threat, and let the curse's threat bonus hold the pull. Sustain: Ravenous Bite and Bloodfang Bite carry both your threat and your self-healing. Rotclaw keeps a bleed and your Rage up, and Claw Sweep spreads threat across the pack. Pop Monstrous Hunger when you need to self-heal through a spike. Emergency: Blood Pact to survive a burst window, Eternal Resolve for the frenzy heal, and Night Hunter's Howl to snare adds gone wrong off the group. You have no ally-targeted heal, though Rejuvenating Shadows does passively boost the healing your allies receive from elsewhere.
