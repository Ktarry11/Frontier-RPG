PLAYSTYLE

The engine is Runeblade weaving into elemental weapon strikes and cooldown detonations. This is a straight two-handed melee spec, not an attunement or sigil builder. Runeblade is your core filler; Primordial Blast and Smolder each refresh a Runeblade charge via Riftblade, and every third Runeblade restores resources and hits harder through Runic Omen and Surging Slash. You layer your elemental strikes: Smolder (Fire weapon strike), Fracture (Frost strike that also burns the target's mana), Hoarfrost (frontal Frost cleave with a lingering tick) and Hurricane (a multi-strike Nature channel). Genesis brands a target, accumulating a share of your damage and detonating it as Elemental Damage. Primordial Blast is your always-on instant nuke between strikes. Zenith and Primordial Fury are your burst cooldowns, with Primordial Fury flaring your runic tattoos to cleave extra enemies and pump Runeblade. Turbulence drops a Nature AoE zone for packs, Warpdagger is your gap-closer and can reset Smolder via Spellfire Runes, and Ley Lock is your interrupt, instant-cast under Battle Engravings. Windsage, Surging Slash and Spellfire Runes resets keep the Runeblade proc chain rolling.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Smolder, Fracture, Hoarfrost and Hurricane feed a resource-returning Runeblade proc chain, so single-target melee damage pays for itself. Zenith and Primordial Fury are the burst layer on top.
Ley Lock is a hard interrupt, instant under Battle Engravings. Magebreaker self-dispels and Fracture burns mana off enemy casters.
Primordial Fury cleaves your strikes into extra enemies and Turbulence's AoE zone pays you for being surrounded. Phase Out with Rift Clones is a strong juke and disengage.
WEAKNESSES
No repeatable self-healing. Echo Rune's teleport-heal and Riftblade's small resource return are all of it, so with Echo Rune and Phase Out down you get health back by disengaging to food or a bandage.
Nothing clears a stun or silence once it lands and there is no passive duration reduction, so a coordinated CC chain strips your burst window. The counterplay is preventive and all class-kit: Warding Rune pre-cast to make incoming magic CC fizzle, Granite Resolve to mitigate physical damage while stunned.
Nearly every ability costs mana. Keep the Runeblade every-third-cast return and Fracture's mana burn flowing or sustained cooldown use strands you empty mid-fight.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

1) Open with Primordial Blast while closing distance; it also refreshes a Runeblade charge via Riftblade. 2) Weave Runeblade as your filler, letting Primordial Blast/Smolder top its charges and every third cast pay back resources. 3) Layer your elemental strikes on cooldown: Smolder (Fire), Fracture (Frost + mana burn), and Hoarfrost (Frost cleave). Keep Hurricane rolling for its multi-strike and the Swift Etching haste/crit window after it ends. 4) Fire Zenith and Primordial Fury as your burst cooldowns on a tough target. 5) Drop Genesis early on an elite so its accumulated-damage detonation lands during the fight. 6) On packs, use Turbulence for the AoE tick zone and Warpdagger to leap in and tag multiple targets on arrival, and Ley Lock any dangerous cast.

DUNGEON

This spec has no heal to prioritize, so its "support" seat is interrupt-and-DPS. Ley Lock is a real hard interrupt: time it onto dangerous casts. Around that, run sustained personal damage through the Runeblade / elemental-strike / Primordial Blast loop. Use Fracture to drain a caster add's mana, and Magebreaker's Hurricane strikes to shed a harmful magic effect from yourself. Drop Turbulence on clumped trash and layer Zenith/Primordial Fury for burst on the priority target. If you pull unwanted attention, fall back on Phase Out (with Rift Clones) to shed aggro and Echo Rune's teleport-heal to reset your position and health, rather than expecting an external heal to save you.
