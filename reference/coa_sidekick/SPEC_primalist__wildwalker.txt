Closest to retail Beast Mastery Hunter played as a melee bruiser. It's a pet class at its core: Spirit Beast Master lets it tame beasts, and Bestial Wrath, Legacy of Rexxar and Rylak's Blessing all revolve around the pet. PLAYSTYLE

Wildwalker is a rage-fueled melee weapon-basher with a tamed beast pet, built around Wildclaw as its main dual-weapon strike and Rylak's Bite, a 2-charge gap-close strike, as its filler. You dump rage into Totemic Smash (weapon damage + AoE + bleed) and Primal Shred, where your pet cleaves and bleeds up to several targets. Crit-fishing is central. Legacy of Rexxar, Rexxar's Might, Lacerations and Torn to Shreds turn crits into bleeds and pet procs, keeping the bleed engine rolling. On top sits a per-situation Boon you swap, Bear for offense with Totem Warrior making Wildclaw strike an extra time under it, plus Frenzied Roar (an attack-speed cooldown that also generates rage) and Savage Frenzy (an enrage for damage, attack speed and movement that, with Son of Ursoc, frees you of movement-impairing effects). Your pet's Throat Clamp is a dash-in interrupt and school-lock. Party support comes from Killer's Path and the Flourishing-style attack-power buffs, with Rips and Tears feeding both your rage and your pet's Focus off periodic damage.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Legacy of Rexxar, Rexxar's Might and Lacerations stack constant bleed pressure and pet procs with no healer behind you. It grinds out 1v1s and skirmishes alone.
Throat Clamp sends the pet in to interrupt and school-lock, and Rylak's Bite closes on two charges. Lockout and stickiness most bruiser kits never get.
Killer's Path and the party attack-power buffs sit on top of full melee damage. You swing fights beyond your own numbers.
WEAKNESSES
No ranged attack past Rylak's Bite's charge. Anyone who keeps distance wins on attrition.
This is not a healer. Protective Roar's group heal is one roar on a cooldown, and any dispel or cleanse means taking Grove Guardian's zone from the class kit. A duo partner gets self-sufficiency, an interrupt and AP buffs, never external healing.
Anti-CC is Savage Frenzy's snare-break and nothing else. Between windows you have no stun or root immunity, so a well-timed opener or a second CC chain locks you down.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Rylak's Bite to close distance (a gap-close strike, and Wild Rage removes its rage cost) -> spend rage into Totemic Smash on cooldown (biggest hit + bleed via Torn to Shreds) -> Primal Shred instead when 2+ mobs are up (pet cleave + bleed) -> Rylak's Bite on its charges to strike and stay glued to the target -> keep Wildclaw as your main filler strike to keep the crit-driven bleeds rolling -> run Boon of the Bear for sustained fights (Totem Warrior makes Wildclaw hit an extra time under it) -> Frenzied Roar as an attack-speed-and-rage cooldown -> Savage Frenzy to break a snare or root (with Son of Ursoc) and burst. Lean on the crit-triggered bleed pressure and Bestial Wrath's mana/Focus returns to stay in the fight rather than sitting to drink.

DUNGEON

Pull with Totemic Smash to start pack cleave and its bleed, and Primal Shred to spread pet bleeds across the group -> keep your attack-power buffs (Killer's Path and the party AP boosts) up -> sustain priority: keep Wildclaw and Rylak's Bite flowing to drive the crit-based bleed loop, and use your pet's Throat Clamp to interrupt a dangerous caster add, since it is your only interrupt -> run Boon of the Bear for damage -> Savage Frenzy to break out of a snare or root and burst -> Frenzied Roar for attack speed and rage on a longer fight. Note: no cleanse, dispel, or targeted ally heal here. A healer must cover those. Your group contribution is damage, AP buffs, and the pet interrupt.
