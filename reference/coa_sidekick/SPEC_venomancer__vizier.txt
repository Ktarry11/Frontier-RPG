Vizier does what any pure healer does: keeps a frontline ally, or itself, alive through the trade.

RETAIL FEEL
Closest to a HoT-and-cooldown healer like Restoration Druid or Preservation Evoker. Serpent's Fang blankets the group with Shadra's Vigil the way those specs blanket a group with HoTs and Echo. The rest of the kit is a spread of rolling heals (Shadra's Prayer, Balm, Mending Mist) plus targeted charge heals (Alkahest) and a single-ally save (Shadra's Aid). Like Preservation, Vizier's power is almost entirely in smart HoT-timing and target selection rather than raw throughput. It's weak at personal damage and self-peel because the whole toolkit is externally focused. Its one offensive cast, Serpent's Fang, exists to heal allies, not to threaten anyone.

PLAYSTYLE

Vizier is a pure mana healer built around Shadra's Prayer and a spread of HoT and AoE heals. Its group opener and AoE heal is Serpent's Fang, an offensive cast that heals several nearby allies as it strikes the enemy (Lifemender and Venom Fanatic widen it) and applies Shadra's Vigil to those allies through the Shadra's Vigil passive. From there you weave the heal toolkit: Shadra's Prayer (single-target heal + HoT), Shadra's Balm (rolling HoT), Green Salve (a heal that, via Book of Shadra, seeks another wounded ally), Mending Mist (spread HoT + burst), and Alkahest (a charge-based heal that extends Shadra's Prayer). The passive Ritual Healing makes Shadra's Prayer also restore mana, energy or rage to the target. One healing Venom sits on top: Rejuvenating Venom, which procs a random-ally and self HoT off your casts. The one big single-ally save is Shadra's Aid, damage reduction plus heal-on-damage-taken. So a hard pull is HoT uptime across the party plus watching for whoever needs it.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Serpent's Fang into Shadra's Prayer, Shadra's Balm, Green Salve, Mending Mist and Alkahest, with Rejuvenating Venom procs and Ritual Healing's resource return. The HoT economy is deep, cheap, and it spreads across the party.
Shadra's Aid cuts damage and heals on damage taken, a real save button for a partner or a tank, with fast Alkahest charge heals behind it for follow-up spikes.
Antivenom cures poison and Shadra's Presence widens it, Blight Antidote dispels curses, and Toxic Communion extends your HoT effects across nearby allies.
WEAKNESSES
Serpent's Fang is your only offensive cast, and it exists to heal. You threaten nobody.
Anti-CC is the passive root and snare break on shifting into Vizier Form, plus class-kit borrows: Toxic Stride for silence and interrupt immunity while casting on the move, Burrow as an immune-but-inert stall. No stun immunity, no interrupt, so hard stun chains lock you down.
Shadra's Aid is single-target and cooldown-gated. Spend it and fast Alkahest charges are the only personal safety net left against sustained focus fire.
STAT PRIORITY
Haste (Vizier Form explicitly lets its HoT effects benefit from spell haste — faster ticks mean more allies topped off per GCD)

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Vizier has almost no damage kit — solo play is slow and defensive. Shift into Vizier Form and keep Rejuvenating Venom active for its passive random-ally (and self) HoT procs. Use Serpent's Fang on your target to both chip damage and blanket yourself with Shadra's Vigil and its self-heal. Chain your heals (Shadra's Prayer, Shadra's Balm, Green Salve, Alkahest) to stay topped, and lean on Shadra's Aid only when a pull goes bad. You cannot out-damage anything quickly, so favor pulling single, low targets and grind them down with Serpent's Fang and auto-attacks while your HoTs keep you full.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: Vizier Form up, Rejuvenating Venom active. Opener: Serpent's Fang on the pull to AoE-heal the group and blanket it with Shadra's Vigil. Sustain: weave Shadra's Balm and Shadra's Prayer as your rolling heals; via the Ritual Healing passive, Shadra's Prayer also feeds the target resources. Mending Mist covers spread AoE, Green Salve is a fast heal that seeks a second wounded ally, and Alkahest's charges top and extend Shadra's Prayer. Single-target spike: stack HoTs and use Toxic Communion to extend Balm/Mending Mist on the group. Incoming burst: Shadra's Aid on the focused target. Mana management: Serpent Lord's Amulet during heavy sustained damage (spell haste + reduced mana costs), Extraction (doubled Int/Spirit + crit-heal mana return), and Mycelial Replenishment for an instant chunk of mana. Utility as needed: Antivenom (poison cure) and Blight Antidote (curse dispel). Note this spec has no battle-rez or enrage-strip of its own.
