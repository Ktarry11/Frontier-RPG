RETAIL FEEL
Closest to retail Marksmanship Hunter. Advantage stacks feeding Precision Shot, Skullpiercer and Incendiary Shot is structurally the same builder-into-big-spender loop as MM's Precise Shots feeding Aimed Shot. Deadshot mirrors Kill Shot as the low-HP execute, and the single active Quiver slot plays like MM's short burst windows (Trick Shots/Careful Aim). Layered on top is an Elude-gated mobility and opener kit: Elude stealth-window abilities, Adrenaline Rush, and Boots of Elvenkind's movement-slow reduction. Unlike its sibling specs there is no party-buff or support layer at all here. No auras, no haste or crit sharing, no healing whatsoever. Despite the class name, judge it as a pure MM-Hunter DPS spec.

PLAYSTYLE

A Focus/Advantage builder-spender loop. Spam Quick Shot and Hunting Shot, which ricochets between enemies, at range while banking Advantage stacks off crits (Archery Master gives Quick Shot crits a bonus stack) and off Hunting Shot's per-enemy generation. Then dump the bank into the Advantage spenders: Precision Shot, Skullpiercer, Serrated Shot, Incendiary Shot, Skirmish, each scaling harder per stack spent. Only one Quiver buff, Poison or Searing, can be active at a time, so a lot of the play is picking the right one for the moment and refreshing it. Openers lean on Neurotoxin Arrow's silence. Finish with the sub-20%-HP execute Deadshot, which Silent, But Deadly can occasionally unlock early.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Neurotoxin Arrow's silence into Precision Shot, Skullpiercer and a Deadshot execute deletes a caught target on its own. Skirmish's crit and Focus-cost window sharpens the whole sequence.
Elude's stealth window and Adrenaline Rush open distance whenever you need it, with Boots of Elvenkind cutting movement slows.
No auras, no party upkeep. The whole budget goes into a tight builder-spender loop built for picking off priority targets.
WEAKNESSES
No heal anywhere in the spec: no shield, no HoT, no proc-heal. You live on positioning.
Neurotoxin Arrow's silence and Crippling Shot's snare are your only control. No stun, no CC-break, no damage-reduction cooldown, so once those are spent nothing stops a train.
Advantage generation through Archery Master and Hunting Shot depends on crits and hits landing, and Precision Shot, Skullpiercer, Incendiary Shot, Serrated Shot and Skirmish all draw from the same bank. A bad crit streak stalls the burst plan.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

1) Keep one Quiver active: Poison Quiver for sustained leveling DoT pressure, or Searing Quiver for more burst. 2) Open with Hunting Shot on cooldown; it ricochets between enemies for Advantage. 3) Fill gaps with Quick Shot (crits proc Archery Master for a bonus Advantage stack). 4) At a few banked Advantage stacks, spend into Precision Shot (main nuke, scales per stack) or Serrated Shot (bleed). 5) Fire Deadshot on cooldown once the target is under 20% HP (Advantage-generating execute; Silent, But Deadly can unlock it early). 6) On packs, swap to Incendiary Shot/Brutal Shot and use Hunting Shot's ricochet to tag multiple mobs. 7) Lean on Sniper's Focus (Focus refund when consuming Advantage) and Superb Shot (Auto Shot restores Focus) to keep Focus flowing rather than sitting to regen.

DUNGEON

1) This spec brings no group buffs. There are no Horn auras, party haste/crit, or heals to manage, so your job is damage plus the occasional peel. 2) Open pulls with Hunting Shot (AoE tag + Advantage), then Brutal Shot to cleave the pack in a line, adding Incendiary Shot for fire splash. 3) Neurotoxin Arrow silences a casting add; Crippling Shot snares a dangerous add off the healer or tank. 4) Run single-target priority (Precision Shot dump > Skullpiercer > Serrated Shot > Deadshot execute) on the designated kill target once Advantage is banked. 5) There is no heal here and no self-sustain item — rely on the group's actual healer. 6) Keep Auto Shot rolling (Superb Shot) and spend Advantage on cooldown (Sniper's Focus) to sustain Focus through longer fights.
