RETAIL FEEL
Closest to retail Fire Mage. The core loop is mechanically Fire Mage's Ignite/Combustion snowball, not a Discipline/Resto-style healing loop. You build periodic Ignite and Blaze pressure, let crits via Lighting the Fuse fuel a stacking haste proc (Flamecasting, functionally a Hot-Streak-esque state), and that empowers your spenders, with Flames of the Firelord supercharging them and making them cleave. The sliver of passive party utility, Fiery Passion raid haste, is bolted on, closer to what a support-flavored DPS gives a group. There's still no healer identity here. The spec itself has no heal, and the closest it gets is taking the class kit's Ember Touch, a single spot heal rather than a healing toolkit.

PLAYSTYLE

A DoT-stacking caster. Lay Ignite and Blaze on the target, weave Flare Bolt and Lava Shard as filler, and let Lighting the Fuse (periodic crits) and Infernus/Dancing Flames (Lava Shard applying Burn, direct Fire damage spreading your DoTs to a nearby enemy) snowball damage while Flamecasting and Wreath of Flames convert those periodic crits into stacking haste. Once you're rolling, dump the pile of periodic effects into spenders for the burst: Pillar of Flame, Explode, Slag Barrage, and Pyroclasm, which detonates your Burning effects. Flames of the Firelord supercharges your Ember spenders during its window and makes them cleave. The support side is thin and almost entirely passive. Fiery Passion is a raid haste aura, and the closest things to a heal are class-kit picks: Ember Touch, a single Ember-costing spot cast, or the niche Ignis Ultimatus damage-plus-heal channel. You're a fire mage who brings an aura, not a healer who occasionally does damage.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Meteor's AoE disorient locks down a clump or pulls one off a teammate. It is the spec's only real crowd control, and in an uncoordinated skirmish that is enough.
Infernus and Destroyer's Legacy spread Burn and Lava Shard blast damage across a pack. Once the periodic effects are rolling the cleave costs you almost nothing.
Ignite and Blaze stack, Lighting the Fuse periodic crits amplify them, Flamecasting snowballs haste, and Cataclysmic Power buffs Flare Bolt per periodic effect. Once it is rolling a target or a clump melts.
WEAKNESSES
The spec kit contains no heal at all. Ember Touch from the class kit is one Ember-costing spot heal, with Ignis Ultimatus as a niche damage-plus-heal channel, and neither is a sustain engine. You live on positioning and your one disorient.
Meteor's disorient is the only real CC. No silence, no knockback, no pull.
No spec-native absorb and no panic button. Volcanic Shell's self-absorb and Inferno Barrier are class-kit picks competing with damage picks, so a committed focus with any CC attached ends the fight fast.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Ignite and Blaze to get DoTs rolling. Weave Flare Bolt/Lava Shard as your filler while periodic crits build Flamecasting stacks, then dump into Pillar of Flame -> Explode (scales with your periodic-effect count) -> Flames of Neltharion / Pyroclasm as they come off cooldown. Use Meteor to disorient-and-tag a pull, and let Infernus/Destroyer's Legacy spread your Lava Shard damage for cleave on 2-3 packs. Fired Up! turns your Ember spenders instant when it procs, so cash those windows in. You have no heal, so drink between pulls when your HP dips.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: nothing to hand out. Fiery Passion's raid haste is passive, so it's always on. On pull, open with Meteor for the disorient and cleave, then run your normal DoT-and-spend rotation: Ignite/Blaze -> Flare Bolt/Lava Shard filler -> Pillar of Flame / Explode / Flames of Neltharion / Pyroclasm spenders, letting Infernus and Destroyer's Legacy spread damage across the pack. You are pure DPS here. The spec has no heal and no silence — you can't hard-interrupt casters. Leave healing to the healer, focus on damage, and use Meteor's disorient to peel dangerous adds.
