RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Blood Death Knight. Your own damage is your healing, packaged in an AoE cleave-and-leech loop, where Harvester's 20%-of-damage heal plus Crimson Thirst is like Death Strike. The resource economy gates your strongest windows: Soul Fragments and Reaped Souls feeding Soul Infusion is like Bone Shield-esque banked charges. Put that on a Strength-scaling brawler chassis and you have the same fantasy Blood DK plays in retail PvP. Dive in, keep swinging, out-sustain the burst, be nearly impossible to solo down. The Soul Fragment generate-then-consume rhythm also borrows straight from Vengeance Demon Hunter's Soul Fragments. Blood DK is still the better full-kit analog, since like Harvest none of its sustain is an external heal. It's all self-directed toughness, not party support.

PLAYSTYLE

A two-handed weapon melee/Shadow hybrid whose offense doubles as your healthbar. The passive Harvester heals you for 20% of all damage dealt and Crimson Thirst stacks on top, so the loop is hit things, stay full. You build Soul Fragments off Crow's Harvest (and Reap/Murder), and bank Reaped Souls off Slaughter and Blood Knight's Doomrend crits. Openers lean on Crow's Harvest, an AoE spin that harvests up to 3 Soul Fragments, and Doomrend, which applies a dark anti-heal shield to the target and, with Darkrend Scythe, a rolling bleed. Keep Souls for the Slaughter buffing your next auto attacks, drop Harvesting Grounds to slow the pack, and cash Slaughter as your execute under 35% HP. Harvest Time (spikes damage and crit, and lets Soul Infusion casts skip consuming the effect) and Blood Frenzy (unleashed by Harvest Time near an enemy) are your all-in buttons for extended trades.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Harvester turns 20% of all your damage into healing and Crimson Thirst stacks leech on top. Sustained melee trades go your way with no healing rotation to run.
Crow's Harvest hits the whole cluster and Harvesting Grounds holds them in it, so diving a clustered BG scrum nukes their group and keeps you topped at the same time. One player swings the fight.
Slaughter executes under 35% and refunds a Reaped Soul, Hemorrhage boosts damage in the same range, and Doomrend's anti-heal shield stops them stabilizing. Once you are on someone low, they stay low.
WEAKNESSES
All that sustain is yours alone. A duo partner gets body-blocking, Harvesting Grounds slows and Sever interrupts, never a heal.
No blink, no vanish, no bubble, no CC-break in the spec line. The closest analogs are class-kit Essence picks: Limbo, and Jailer's Bargain's absorb shield. Without them a coordinated CC chain, or a kite at range before you close, turns the sustain engine off completely.
Soul Infusion powers your strongest windows and has to be built off Reaped Souls first. Caught cold before Crow's Harvest and Slaughter have banked any, your damage and your healing are both well below mid-fight.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

1) Open with Crow's Harvest to leap into the pull, deal weapon damage in an arc, and harvest up to 3 Soul Fragments. 2) Doomrend to apply its anti-heal shield and, with Darkrend Scythe, start the bleed. 3) Reap as your basic filler between cooldowns. It feeds Soul Fragment generation, and Redshade turns it into Thresh for a stretch. 4) Murder on cooldown while closing, or on a second target, for free extra Soul Fragments and to apply Ruin (Shudder Scythe upgrades it into a bigger fragment-generating hit). 5) Keep Souls for the Slaughter rolling so your autos add Shadow damage. 6) Slaughter the moment a target drops below 35% HP for the execute plus a Reaped Soul. 7) Harvesting Grounds on a fleeing target, or a pack you want slowed. 8) Sever to interrupt a dangerous cast. 9) Harvest Time (and the Blood Frenzy it unleashes) as your burst window when you can commit to a heavy trade.

DUNGEON

This spec has no ally-targeted heal. Its role in a group is soaking and holding pressure off the healer with sustained AoE, not topping party HP. 1) Pull with Crow's Harvest into the pack to tag every add, harvest Soul Fragments, and start the Darkrend Scythe bleeds. 2) Sustain-tank the pack: keep Harvester's 20%-of-damage leech flowing off AoE while Crimson Thirst stacks, and park Doomrend's anti-heal shield and bleed on the hardest-hitting add. 3) Drop Harvesting Grounds under the pack to slow adds and keep them clustered in your cleave. 4) Use Murder (or Shudder Scythe) to keep Ruin applied on the primary target. 5) Pop Harvest Time on a big damage-taken spike, boss burn or add swarm, to spike your damage and crit and drive a few big extra leeches. 6) Sever any dangerous cast the group's actual interrupts can't reach. 7) Slaughter every add that drops below 35% for the execute and Reaped Soul. 8) Save Blood Frenzy (via Harvest Time) as an all-in in a burn phase, not as a defensive.
