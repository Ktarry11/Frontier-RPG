RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Assassination Rogue crossed with Outlaw's utility toolkit. The builder/finisher loop is structurally Mutilate-into-Envenom with a bleed-consumption twist: Ravage builds Advantage and lays a bleed, Wild Strike layers the Bounty debuff and Barbed Quills bleed, and Assault, Viper's Bite and Skullpiercer dump Advantage for bonus damage, with Viper's Bite's bonus scaling off the target already bleeding. Bushwhack (teleport-behind plus stun) plays like Shadowstep-into-Cheap-Shot. Knockout plays like Blind that also cleanses the target's bleeds and poisons. Outmaneuver's teleport-in, decoy, teleport-back is a Shadowstep-with-Preparation-reset analog. The party-buff layer, Guile of the Cutthroat's AP aura, has no Rogue analog at all. That's a Warrior-Battle-Shout-style support bolt-on, nothing resembling an actual healer's kit.

PLAYSTYLE

Brigand is a dagger-wielding melee assassin, not a healer despite the archetype tag. Its only support tool is the passive Guile of the Cutthroat party/raid attack-power aura. Combat runs on Focus feeding a builder/finisher loop. Ravage lays a bleed and generates an Advantage stack. Wild Strike (the Ravager passive makes every one generate a stack too) layers on the Bounty debuff via Bounty Hunter, from Flank, and Barbed Quills' stacking bleed. Then Assault, Viper's Bite and Skullpiercer dump Advantage for burst, with Viper's Bite hitting harder against a bleeding target and Rub It In giving each stack spent a chance to refund. Its trump card is a hard-CC suite bolted onto the DPS loop: Bushwhack (teleport-behind + stun), Knockout (an incap that also strips bleeds and poisons off the target), and Throatpunch (spell-school interrupt/lockout). That's more control than most pure-DPS kits get.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Bushwhack teleports behind and stuns, Knockout incapacitates while stripping bleeds and poisons off the target, and Throatpunch interrupts and locks a spell school. Deep hard CC for a DPS spec.
Barbed Quills and Toxic Dart bleeds feed Viper's Bite's bonus-damage strikes, then Assault under 20% and Skullpiercer at 5 Advantage cash in the bank.
Bushwhack teleports behind, Outmaneuver goes in and back behind a decoy, and Hookshot pulls you to a bleeding target. You are not limited to whatever stands still.
WEAKNESSES
No real self-heal. The small Pilfering and Dirty Blades leech will not carry you through focus fire.
Nothing breaks a stun, root, or fear, and Dirty Fighter's passive root-resist chance is the whole answer. A chain-CC opener kills you outright.
Built entirely around daggers in melee range with no ranged pressure. Lose Bushwhack, Outmaneuver and Hookshot to CC or terrain and a kiter leaves you with nothing to do.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Bushwhack, or Flank from behind for the Beatdown/Bounty payoff, to close the gap and land the stun. Ravage first to lay the bleed and bank an Advantage stack. Weave Wild Strike to build further Advantage (Ravager makes it generate an extra stack) and stack Barbed Quills' bleed and the Bounty debuff. Dump with Assault once a few Advantage stacks are banked, especially below 20% health where it hits hardest. Fire Viper's Bite whenever the target is bleeding to cash in its bonus-damage strikes. Spend Skullpiercer at 5 Advantage as your hardest scaling nuke (Underhanded boosts its AP scaling, and Stonemason's Secret extends Dirty Blades off it). Finish low-health mobs with Assault once they drop below 20%.

DUNGEON

There is no direct heal spell anywhere in this spec, and no Horn auras either. Its only "support" contribution is the passive Guile of the Cutthroat party attack-power aura, which is always on. No crafted party buffs, no ally-heal item: you bring damage and control, not sustain. Otherwise run the normal Ravage → Wild Strike → Assault/Viper's Bite/Skullpiercer melee chain for damage. Save Knockout/Throatpunch/Bushwhack to peel or lock down adds that go after the healer, and Hookshot to yank a breakaway bleeding add back.
