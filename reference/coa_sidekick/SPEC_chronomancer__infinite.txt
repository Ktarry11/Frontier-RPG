PLAYSTYLE

A Chaos-damage caster running on mana plus a burst window rather than a dual resource economy. Your core spender is Chromatic Shard, a chaos bolt that pierces immunities and resistances, layered over Melt Reality (a hard-hitting Chromatic damage-over-time channel) with Timerend and Unmake as fillers and Discordance for cleave. Periodic damage feeds procs: Anomaly Spikes fling extra bolts off your DoTs, Hourglass of Eternity grants stacking haste, and Infinite Power shaves your damaging cooldowns. Burst hinges on Incarnation of Chaos. It transforms you into an Incarnate of Chaos, instantly resets Chromatic Shard's cooldown, makes your next cast free of cooldown, and boosts your damage. Inside that window Chaos Fusion lets you keep firing Chromatic Shard while moving and Chaotic Time amps and replicates Melt Reality. Most of the work is sequencing your DoTs (Melt Reality, Timerend, Unmake, Decomposition) so Anomaly Spikes and the haste and cooldown procs are always rolling, then cashing that into the Incarnation of Chaos window.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Chromatic Shard pierces immunities and resistances outright. Time Means Nothing and Destabilize Time amplify and slow the focus target on top, so there is not much left to shrug off with.
Incarnation of Chaos resets Chromatic Shard, frees your next cast and raises damage, and Chaos Fusion lets you keep firing while moving. Infinite Power shaves the cooldowns down.
Rolling DoTs drive Anomaly Spikes and stacking haste with almost no input from you. Infinite Horizon adds a raid-wide damage increase.
WEAKNESSES
Reverse Wound is your only real self-heal. No CC-break, no damage immunity; chain-CC or a melee train kills you.
Damage ramps through DoTs and procs before the Incarnation of Chaos window pays off, so you cannot spike a fresh target instantly. The burst is only good once the proc engine is already running.
No stun. Slipstream's banish, Clasp of Infinity's root and Destabilize Time's cast-slow let you peel and kite, but nothing hard-locks a determined attacker.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open at range by seeding your damage-over-time: Melt Reality on the primary target, with Timerend and Unmake kept rolling. Use Unmake into a Timerend'd target to trigger Infinite Keeper's sand-vortex burst. Fire Chromatic Shard as your main nuke. Anomaly Spikes will fling extra bolts off your ticking DoTs, and Discordance cleaves packs. When Incarnation of Chaos is up, pop it to reset Chromatic Shard and free your next cast, then dump Chromatic Shard and Melt Reality into the boosted-damage window (Chaos Fusion lets you keep casting Chromatic Shard while repositioning). Use Slipstream to remove a dangerous add from the fight, Clasp of Infinity to root a runner, and Reverse Wound to top off between pulls. Infinite Power quietly pulls your cooldowns forward the more you cast, so never stop pressing damage.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull, position at range and pop Infinite Horizon's group damage buff for the pull. On the pull, seed Melt Reality and Timerend on priority targets, then spread Decomposition/Discordance for cleave so Anomaly Spikes and your haste procs (Hourglass of Eternity) stay rolling. Use Unmake into Timerend'd targets for Infinite Keeper's burst. Bank your Incarnation of Chaos for a heavy single-target window or a big pull, then reset-and-free-cast Chromatic Shard and Melt Reality through it. Layer utility as needed: Slipstream to neutralize a dangerous add, Clasp of Infinity to root a caster or runner, Destabilize Time to slow an add's casts. Keep Chromatic Shard and your DoTs cycling constantly, since Infinite Power feeds off damaging casts to pull your cooldowns forward.
