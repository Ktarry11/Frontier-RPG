RETAIL FEEL
Closest to retail Retribution Paladin. The builder-into-spender loop, the 2H gate on its best finishers (Titanstrike, Righteous Tempest, Argent Blade) and the Holy/Physical hybrid damage split all read as Holy Power analogs. The resemblance stops at the surface, though. Holy Power is fungible and patient, whereas Crusader's Oaths are typed, each builder granting a different buff, and they decay on a shared Oath Chain. That makes it a stack-maintenance spec far more than a bank-and-dump one. Argent Blade and Titanstrike's self-heals are its Word of Glory analogs, mitigation folded into damage rather than a real healing kit. The other departure is the pure utility it carries: Interdict silence, Crusader's Brand, Divine Charge's outdoor party-speed burst. That reads more like bolted-on PvP and world kit than anything in Ret's retail toolkit.

PLAYSTYLE

Crusader runs a typed-Oath engine, and reading it as a generic combo-point pool is the most common mistake. Three abilities each grant their own distinct Oath: Holy Cleave's makes your abilities strike an additional nearby target, Righteous Lunge's makes them damage and heal for more, and Condemn's raises your critical strike chance. Condemn itself is also a stacking Holy damage-over-time. Those Oaths stack simultaneously and all ride one shared Oath Chain timer, so the job is keeping the Chain alive. Argent Blade extends it and heals you for the damage dealt; Titanstrike extends it too, but is execute-only below 35% health unless Testament of Strength is up. Let the Chain lapse and every stack goes with it. The twist is that your finishers are Oath Breakers. Chastise, Righteous Tempest and Blade of Faith all consume your Oaths for bonus damage per Oath spent, so spending is a choice against holding a fat bank of multipliers. Around that, Scourgebane procs extra Holyflame off your direct damage, Scarlet Champion turns your crits into a stacking Holy-damage buff, and Energy is the fuel that Improved Scarlet Hammer and Warrior of Tyr feed back.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Stacked Oaths run at once: an extra target struck, more damage and healing, more crit. Protect the Oath Chain and everything you do scales together, rather than one big button getting bigger.
Argent Blade heals for the damage it deals and Titanfall makes Titanstrike heal for twice its damage, folding recovery into the rotation. On a big pull Libram of Grace turns every damaging ability into party healing.
Interdict silences a target and locks Undead and Demons out of healing. Crusader's Brand pins a runner so they cannot flee, mount, or speed away, and reveals them through stealth. Divine Charge gives the party a mounted speed burst outdoors that does not break on casting.
WEAKNESSES
The healing is incidental self-heal off Argent Blade and Titanstrike, not throughput, and it cannot cover a group. Worse, Testament of Fortitude and Testament of Strength both apply Sacred Restraint for a minute, blocking further Testaments, so the defensive and offensive ones compete directly.
Everything rides one timer. Let the Oath Chain lapse and all three stacked Oaths vanish together and you rebuild from zero, so a long run between packs, a kite, or a CC chain costs far more than its seconds. The finisher kit is 2H-weapon-gated.
Righteous Lunge is the only real engage. Divine Charge is an outdoors-only party movement cooldown, not a targeted gap-closer, and there is no blink or escape, so sustained kiting shuts off your damage and the self-heals it feeds. No dispel-all, no magic-immunity button.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Righteous Lunge to close the gap; it grants its own Oath on the way in. Build the Oath layer: Holy Cleave for the extra-strike Oath, Righteous Lunge for the damage/heal Oath, Condemn for the crit Oath and its stacking Holy damage-over-time. Keep the Oath Chain alive with Argent Blade, which heals you for the damage it deals. Solo you can afford to spend. Dump into Chastise for the Holy nuke (transformed into Scarlet Hammer if you cast Argent Blade while Warrior of Dawn is active, via Scarlet Training) or Blade of Faith for the damage-over-time, both scaling with how many Oaths they consume. Titanstrike is your execute below 35% health and heals you for the damage dealt, twice that with Titanfall talented. Argent Blade's self-heal means a bad pull is usually recovered just by keeping the rotation going. Always fight with a 2H weapon equipped for the full finisher kit.

DUNGEON

Divine Charge only works outdoors, so it is not your dungeon opener. Close with Righteous Lunge, and use Temple Guardian to charge to an ally rather than an enemy (it heals you both, 3 charges on a 35 sec recharge). With the tank on a focus macro, that becomes your catch-up tool whenever a pull outruns you. The mythic-plus pattern is stack-and-hold rather than spend. Build Holy Cleave for the extra-target Oath; around 10 stacks covers a normal pull. Then layer Righteous Lunge and Condemn so the damage/heal and crit Oaths ride alongside it, and keep Argent Blade flowing to extend the Oath Chain. Deliberately hold your Oath Breakers here. Righteous Tempest consumes the entire bank for a 3-second whirl, trading a sustained multiplier on every ability for a short spike and a full rebuild. Between packs, the Chain is what you protect. Testament of Strength makes your next 3 Titanstrikes usable regardless of target health (+25% damage, 1 minute), which converts Titanstrike into a bankable Oath Chain extension you can fire while repositioning. The competing pick, Legacy of the Silver Hand, is the crit/Scourgebane build instead. Only one Libram can be active at a time. Libram of Consecration adds Holy damage to your damaging abilities; Libram of Grace makes each of them restore health to you and nearby allies, which on a large pull is party throughput. Holy Edge (2 ranks) makes Scourgebane and Scarlet Hammer each strike an additional enemy. Interdict silences a caster add and locks Undead/Demons out of healing. Crusader's Brand pins a runner. All of it depends on a tank who chains pulls so you always have something to hit — this build's enemy is downtime, not damage.
