Pyromancer

⚡ Mana + Breath Charges
Pyromancers are mages that have devoted their studies to the school of Fire. Some have a draconic lineage that allows them to harness the power of the dragonflights in battle; others are simple fire magi who use their power to incinerate foes. (Class Leader: Kael'thas Sunstrider.)
