The Necromancer is a form of mage specialising in the dark arts. Many tend toward a less-than-good alignment and most served the Lich King before his fall; now rejoining the Horde and Alliance, they spread terrible plagues, conjure icy magic, and summon legions of undead to overwhelm their enemies.
