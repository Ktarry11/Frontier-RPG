RETAIL FEEL
Closest to a Frost Death Knight reskinned as a pure ranged caster with an Unholy DK pet layer bolted on. The naming and math are a direct homage. Rime is Frost DK's Rime/Killing Machine payoff logic, proccing a guaranteed-crit frost hit off frozen setup. Runic Power is literally the same resource and terminology, and Lich Form is the Undead-transform become-the-Lich cooldown. The pet upkeep, where Skeletal Mage occupies Life Force and is raised like Unholy's ghoul, is the Unholy DK minion layer grafted onto a Frost DK's damage and control identity, delivered at range instead of melee.

PLAYSTYLE

Rime is a dual-resource frost caster. It spends a % of base mana on Lichfrost, Ice Barrage and Glacial Impact, and those same casts (plus Glacial Impact via Freeze Dry and Lich Form's passive tick) generate Runic Power. That Runic Power goes into Command abilities, Command: Bonefreeze and its raised Skeletal Mage's Command, which are usable while casting or channeling, so you weave burst and control on top of your own cast bar instead of stopping to use them. The payoff loop is freeze-then-detonate. You apply Frozen states: Ice Barrage entombs its target at the end of the channel, Command: Bonefreeze freezes a location, Necrotic Chains freezes every 3rd Command spell, and Permafrost treats your next spells as if the target were Frozen. Then you drop Glacial Impact, guaranteed to crit on Frozen targets via Glacier Lord, for a large spike, with Soulfreeze and Frigidness amplifying frost damage against Frozen enemies. Alongside this you maintain raised Skeletal Mages that eat Life Force and give you persistent extra bodies and damage even mid-cast.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Ice Barrage entombs, Command: Bonefreeze freezes, and Necrotic Chains lands every third Command. Staggered like that, you chain-lock or peel almost without a gap.
Set up a Frozen target and Glacial Impact auto-crits through Glacier Lord, splashing out with Harrowing Winds. It hits like a truck once the setup is done.
A raised Skeletal Mage keeps working and Command abilities fire mid-cast, so you pressure and control while your own cast bar runs.
WEAKNESSES
No on-demand self-heal, no HoT, no life-drain. Recovery is all conditional: Cryoshroud's ice-tomb heal needs Lich Form and only fires below a third of your health, Phylactery's Shade has to be pre-placed, Vampiric Aura drips only below half. Pressure that never trips those thresholds leaves you nothing to press.
Mana, Runic Power, Life Force minion upkeep, several separately-timed freeze cooldowns. Sequencing the burst combo has a high floor.
No gap-closer, no escape, no stun. Your peels are offensive freezes that get resisted or trinketed, and the backups are all defensive: Phylactery's Shade, Cryoshroud's ice tomb, Bone Tithe's shield castable while stunned. They buy seconds. Nothing moves you, and you are still a caster with a cast bar.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Raise a Skeletal Mage first for pet upkeep and pull damage. Open at range with Lichfrost (builder, generates Runic Power, slows). Channel Ice Barrage to add damage and set up its end-of-channel entomb; Permafrost procs let non-frost setups still count as hitting a Frozen target. Use Glacial Impact on any Frozen target for its guaranteed crit (Glacier Lord). Tap into your Runic Power on Command: Bonefreeze to freeze a group, or on the raised Skeletal Mage's Command for extra burst on tougher single targets. On packs, open with Command: Bonefreeze to freeze the group, then land Glacial Impact into the frozen clump (Harrowing Winds splashes its damage to nearby enemies). Re-raise a fresh Skeletal Mage if you lose your pet.

DUNGEON

There is no heal to prioritize. "Support" here means Chilling Presence for the party crit aura, Icecrown to raise your allies' crit chance against the enemy as you deal frost damage, a Skeletal Mage raised for extra threat and damage, and Command: Bonefreeze to freeze loose adds off the group. Otherwise run your DPS/control loop around the pull. Lichfrost to open and slow, Ice Barrage to entomb its target, Heartchill to interrupt a caster. Then land Glacial Impact on any Frozen target for its guaranteed crit, once your Frozen setup is up. Army of the North gives a Rotting Frost Giant cooldown for burst windows.
