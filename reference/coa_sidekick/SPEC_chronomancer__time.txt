Closest to Preservation Evoker. Like Preservation, this spec is a hybrid that heals through hard-hitting direct and HoT spells: Epoch, Accelerated Recovery, Correct the Mistake, Ripple. PLAYSTYLE

Core loop is Epoch (a heal that builds Sands of Time stacks) and Accelerated Recovery (a HoT) for sustained healing, with Correct the Mistake and Ripple as your party-wide tools. Sands of Time stacks buff Epoch's healing through Carbon Dating. On top of that you pick one of four Aeons (Oblivion, Protection, Renewal, Resilience) that share a cooldown and reshape what Epoch does: burst crit healing that buffs the target, a mitigating absorb shield, a delayed-HoT version, or a cheap fast-cast version. You re-toggle constantly to match the moment, and Fabric of Time lets your next Aeon fire free of cost or cooldown. Defensively you layer Shield of the Ages onto yourself, which triggers a stacking damage-reduction-plus-healing bonus when you're crit, and lean on Aeon of Protection's absorb with Time Out! as a personal damage-cut. Control comes from Buy Time (an AoE stasis freeze), Continuum Restoration and Displacement for dispels and repositioning, and Roll Back to cleanse harmful effects, so you can peel or reset a fight without a partner's CC. The Vast Infinite is your raid-wide cooldown — it pools a share of party damage taken, then heals the shared total.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Shield of the Ages stacks damage reduction and a healing bonus off crits, and Aeon of Protection's absorb layers onto Time Out!'s damage cut. Bursting you or your partner takes real effort.
Buy Time's AoE stasis freeze swings a fight on its own, with no ally CC needed to set it up. The Vast Infinite pools and heals party damage as a raid-wide save.
The four Aeons and Fabric of Time reshape Epoch into burst, absorb, HoT, or cheap-cast healing. You retune to the fight instead of playing one pattern.
WEAKNESSES
Every strong tool sits on a cooldown and costs mana: the four Aeons, Buy Time, Shield of the Ages, Temporal Anomaly. Dump them at the wrong moment and the next stretch is rough. Timeguard and Infinity's cooldown-free Infinite Shield are single-target stopgaps eating Ability Essence picks.
Buy Time's stasis makes the target invulnerable, so anyone low you freeze walks away alive. Nothing in the kit stops a determined physical stunlock either.
Continuum Restoration and Displacement only handle magic and roots or snares. There is no real CC-break, so a chain-stun or silence landing while your cooldowns are down kills you.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open a pull by putting your control where it helps most. In a double-pull, freeze the extra mobs with Buy Time so they're locked out while you kill the first. Keep Epoch and Accelerated Recovery rolling for self-healing; Epoch builds Sands of Time, which buffs its own healing via Carbon Dating. Reach for Correct the Mistake or a Ripple channel when you need a bigger top-up. Pick the Aeon that fits the fight: Aeon of Protection's absorb or Aeon of Resilience's cheap fast heals for spike windows, Aeon of Renewal for grind-it-out phases. Fabric of Time fires a second Aeon free during a tough beat. If a pull goes bad, layer Shield of the Ages and Time Out! and let your absorbs carry you until it stabilizes.

DUNGEON

On the pull, select the Aeon that fits the incoming damage profile. Aeon of Protection (absorb shield) or Aeon of Resilience (cheap fast heals) for burst windows, Aeon of Renewal for sustained HoT-heavy phases. Then weave Epoch and Accelerated Recovery as your core heal loop, letting the Sands of Time stacks Epoch generates buff its own healing (Carbon Dating). Pre-emptively layer Shield of the Ages onto yourself, or Aeon of Protection's absorb onto the tank, before a known big hit. Fabric of Time heals and fires your next Aeon free during a cooldown-heavy burn phase. Use Correct the Mistake and Ripple for party-wide top-ups, Fortify Timeline for an instant group heal. Hold The Vast Infinite for a heavy raid-wide damage window: it pools party damage, then heals the shared total. Continuum Restoration strips magic debuffs off the party and Roll Back cleanses harmful effects. If you get in trouble yourself, Time Out! and your absorb stack are the emergency layer.
