RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Brewmaster Monk. Exposed Flesh is a stacking debuff you periodically purify for mitigation and healing, a near 1:1 analog of Stagger plus Purifying Brew. Incoming damage feeds a resource pool (Exposed Flesh stacks, rage generation), then you spend a cooldown to convert that pool into a burst of mitigation and, in Beetle Form, a heal. Regrow Exoskeleton, Barbed Stinger, Expulsion and Harden stand in for Purifying Brew. Like Brewmaster, the spec is built to be extremely hard to burst down rather than to top DPS charts. Both use a stance/form gimmick, Beetle Form here, where Brew's Stagger is form-agnostic but the loop feels identical. Both layer dodge and avoidance stacking (Reinforced Exoskeleton and Alacrity's Chitin Rush dodge burst) rather than a flat damage-reduction shield.

PLAYSTYLE

Fortitude fights in Beetle Form, its tank stance, and in an exposed aggressive state when its exoskeleton shatters. Its buttons all spend Rage: Chitin Rush, Carapace Crash, Hivebreak, Venomtip Poison, Barbed Stinger, Expulsion, Regrow Exoskeleton, Molt, Carapace Regeneration, Harden. The core loop is reactive. Taking a hit has a chance to grant a stack of Exposed Flesh, the Fortitude spec passive, which pumps Rage and stacks increased physical damage taken. You deliberately let yourself get hit to fuel offense and cooldowns, then shed the stacks on Regrow Exoskeleton, Barbed Stinger or Expulsion for a burst of damage reduction and, in Beetle Form via Reformed, a heal based on stacks cleared. Layered on top are passive self-heal procs (Charm of Warding on Exposed Flesh application, Reformed's shed-heal, Protogenesis, Carapace Regeneration) and passive mitigation procs (Regenerative Properties, Reinforced Exoskeleton, Charmed Plating, Slimy's Beetle Slime pools) that fire constantly without any input. Offense is wide, sticky Nature poison pressure (Venomtip Poison, Expulsion and Locust Swarm AoE, Carapace Crash cleave) rather than single-target burst. Beetle Form gives a large health and armor boost, extra threat and reduced damage taken, and unlocks Harden, the biggest single defensive cooldown in the kit. It holds threat with Vile Sting, a single-target taunt that also fires your active Venoms. Lifeblood is a second active-mitigation cooldown that reduces damage taken and turns every hit into a Rejuvenating Venom self-heal.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Getting hit builds Rage and Exposed Flesh, which pay out as mitigation and heal dumps: Regrow Exoskeleton, the Barbed Stinger and Expulsion shed, Beetle Form's Reformed heal, Harden, Lifeblood turning damage taken into healing. Charm of Warding and Carapace Regeneration proc underneath. Focus fire does not wear you down.
Barbed Stinger's throw-and-rip pull drags a fleeing enemy back or yanks whoever is on your healer. The Nature vulnerability it applies through Shadra's Gift softens that target for the whole team.
Venomtip Poison, Expulsion and Locust Swarm blanket a fight in DoT, leech and cleave, and Slimy's Beetle Slime pools spread by Carapace Crash hold ground. None of it needs a healer behind it.
Spider Form shifts for move speed, Skitter dashes on three charges, Chitin Rush closes gaps and Molt bursts. You chain-pull and reposition far faster than most tanks.
WEAKNESSES
Every spec-line heal points at yourself: Regrow Exoskeleton, Beetle Form's Reformed shed heals, Regenerative Properties, Protogenesis, Carapace Regeneration. Green Salve and Shadra's Balm heal an ally if you spend the picks, but they are spot patches. You cannot keep a duo partner up.
Unshed Exposed Flesh stacks raise your physical damage taken, so mistiming the shed turns your core defensive mechanic into a liability. Single-target burst stays mediocre as the price of all that survivability.
Harden, Carapace Regeneration and avoidance cover physical damage and do little against magic burst. The control is real, Nullifying Toxin's interrupt and Impale's uncapped line stun, but it lives in cooldowns and Spider-Form weaves, not the Beetle-Form tank loop. A caster train that kites you with Harden down grinds you out.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Chitin Rush to close and generate threat and damage, then keep Chitin Rush rolling as your main rage-spender. Apply Venomtip Poison for its wide DoT and attack-speed slow. Use Carapace Crash and Hivebreak for cleave; Hivebreak can consume Venomtip Poison duration via Toxic Expulsion to gush AoE. Drop Locust Swarm on a pack for AoE leech, and apply Barbed Stinger on a tough single target to raise its Nature damage taken (via Shadra's Gift). Shed Exposed Flesh via Regrow Exoskeleton / Barbed Stinger / Expulsion whenever stacks are high or HP dips. On a hard pull, or when spike-tanking, swap to Beetle Form for Harden and the Reformed shed-heal, then keep chaining Chitin Rush / Venomtip / Hivebreak through the rest of the pull.

DUNGEON

Default in Beetle Form for the threat and Harden access; its damage-taken reduction and threat boost make it the tanking stance. On the pull: open damage and threat with Chitin Rush, then Venomtip Poison and Barbed Stinger for wide DoT/debuff coverage (Barbed Stinger also raises Nature damage taken via Shadra's Gift). Use Claw Strike, a Stamina-scaling AoE that also builds Rage, plus Carapace Crash and Hivebreak to cleave the pack, and let Slimy Stingers' Beetle Slime pools zone adds. Drop Locust Swarm and Expulsion on stacked trash for AoE. Burn Lifeblood, Regrow Exoskeleton / Barbed Stinger shed, or Harden defensively whenever Exposed Flesh is stacked high or you are spike-tanking damage. Taunt loose adds with Vile Sting. The spec has its own taunt (Vile Sting) and interrupt (Nullifying Toxin). What it lacks is a battle-rez and an enrage-strip.
