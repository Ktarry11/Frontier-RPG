RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Unholy/Blood Death Knight, with an Affliction Warlock veneer. Like a DK it runs on Runic Power, applies diseases, and builds around periodic Plague damage rather than casting discrete heal spells on others. Flesh to Worms and Plague of Undeath are a disease engine, not an Atonement-style damage-becomes-ally-healing loop. The DoT-stacking (Crypt Plague to its cap, Necrosis duration extension), spreading (Crypt Explosion, Pandemic) and disease-refresh (Virulency) layer is pure Affliction Warlock. It is not mechanically close to any real allied-healer spec (Disc/Resto/Mistweaver/Holy/Preservation), because it has no external heal in the kit at all.

PLAYSTYLE

You layer diseases onto a target (Flesh to Worms, Lichplague, Plague of Undeath) and let Crypt Plague stack: Crypt Swarm applies 1 stack, Lichfrost applies 2, stacking up through Withering. Meanwhile you spend Runic Power on Command spells, which apply Expunge to diseased targets via the Expunge passive and drive Crypt Explosion's AoE plague. The loop is self-referential and disease-driven. Rot Fetishist replicates a share of your periodic damage as direct damage, Muck Summoner and Wormfood spawn exploding Rotlings off your ticks and Crypt Swarm channel, and Virulency refreshes and copies your whole disease stack for a burst of pressure. Damnation and Plague Science make everything hit harder as the target drops below execute health. Spread comes from Virulency and from Crypt Explosion and Putricide's Formula, which fan Crypt Plague and Plague damage out to nearby enemies.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Rot Fetishist replicates a share of your periodic damage as direct damage and Virulency copies the whole disease stack, with Damnation and Plague Science amplifying against low-health targets. It snowballs the longer your DoTs stay up.
Muck Summoner and Wormfood spawn exploding Rotlings off your periodic damage. Free cleave, no resources spent.
Crypt Explosion and Putricide's Formula spread Crypt Plague and Plague damage to everything nearby, and Virulency duplicates the package. A clustered fight takes all of it, not one target.
WEAKNESSES
No external heal anywhere in the kit. A duo partner gets Apothecary's Cauldron's haste buff and nothing else, so a teammate dying beside you stays dying. Harvest Plague's siphon, Plague Doctor and Plague Drinker only ever feed your own health bar.
Core tools split across Runic Power for the Command spenders, Expunge and Crypt Explosion, and heavy mana for Flesh to Worms, Lichplague, Plague of Undeath and Virulency. Long fights drain both pools.
Damage is stack-based through Crypt Plague, Necrosis and Expunge, so the first seconds of a burst opener find you weak. There is no interrupt, and Ghoulify's fear and Lingering Blight's root need you free to act. Once a stunlock lands, Bone Tithe's shield and a pre-placed Phylactery are all that work.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open by applying Plague of Undeath (Plague DoT + reduced enemy Holy healing) → Flesh to Worms and let Crypt Plague build (Crypt Swarm applies stacks, Lichfrost applies two) → Lichplague on top so it scales up per disease already on the target → spend Runic Power on Command spells to apply Expunge and trigger Crypt Explosion's AoE plague → Virulency to refresh and copy the whole disease stack when it's fully ramped. Let Muck Summoner/Wormfood Rotlings and Rot Fetishist's damage replication do passive work while the diseases tick. Damnation and Plague Science make the last 20-35% of the target's health melt as your DoTs amplify.

DUNGEON

Pull: Plague of Undeath / Flesh to Worms / Lichplague to get diseases rolling on the pack, then Crypt Swarm and Command spells to fan Crypt Plague out via Crypt Explosion for AoE plague damage. Sustain: keep the disease engine running so Crypt Swarm's Runic Power and your DoTs never stop. Refresh the whole package with Virulency on cooldown, and let Rotlings (Muck Summoner/Wormfood) add cleave. Support: Apothecary's Cauldron gives the party a spell-haste raid buff and boosts your own Magic damage. That, plus Plague of Undeath's Holy-healing debuff on the enemy, is the closest thing to "support" you bring. There is no ally-heal, taunt, silence, interrupt or hard CC to fall back on.
