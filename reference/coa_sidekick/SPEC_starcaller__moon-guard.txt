RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Protection Paladin. The fingerprint matches mechanically, not just in feel. Blanket of Stars is a shield-gated cooldown, like Paladin's Avenger's Shield/Holy Shield lineage. Chosen of the Moon bundles a taunt with mitigation, taunting everything nearby while cutting physical damage taken, like Hand of Reckoning fused with a damage-reduction cooldown. Moonflares fire only off a mitigation event and deal unavoidable AoE damage, with Nebula seeding stars off blocks, similar to old Reckoning/Judgment procs. Moonlit Bulwark is the large emergency shield that ignores the exact CC that usually kills a tank, usable while stunned, like Ardent Defender/Guardian of Ancient Kings. Font of Magic converts cumulative mana spent into a heal, a small self-heal the kit funds itself, like Word of Glory funded by Holy-Power-adjacent generation. The Energy/Mana dual-resource split, fillers on Energy and everything impactful on percentage mana, is the one place it diverges from Paladin's single-resource model.

PLAYSTYLE

You alternate Energy-funded filler swings (Celestial Strike, Celestial Cleave, and Moonblade once Blade of the Moon is talented) and Starburst, which applies a Scattered Star per hit and cleaves for Arcane damage that scales with your block value and max mana. Mana-funded tools carry the impactful damage: Celestial Cleave and Starsunder. Stacks also build passively, from blocking (Nebula seeds Scattered Stars onto the attacker) and melee crits (Vengeful Inspiration). Once stacked you dump them into Starsweep, an up-to-15-target cleave that consumes stars and whose cooldown shrinks per star spent. Every block, parry or dodge feeds the kit. Avoiding a melee attack calls down a Moonflare, an unavoidable Arcane nuke, once per second via Moonflares. Parrying refunds Energy and hits the attacker back through Dancing in the Moonlight, and any block or parry buffs your damage via Starlight Duelist, so positioning and gear that raises block and parry directly increase your damage. Survivability is a toolbox rather than passive tankiness: Chosen of the Moon (flat physical damage reduction plus an AoE taunt), Moonlit Bulwark (a max-mana damage shield explicitly usable while stunned), Astral Reconstitution (converts a share of damage taken into mana), Blanket of Stars (shield-gated block-chance/value burst), plus the passive layer of Coalesced Resilience and Shrouded Stars' party-wide DR. The spec identity passive, Moon Guard, grants Intellect equal to your Strength. Asteroid Belt cycles you on a timer into a reduced-damage-taken / reduced-damage-dealt phase, so the kit has a built-in tank/DPS duty cycle instead of one flat stance. Layered on top is an Aspect stance system, with Aspects of the Moonwell, Warden and Cosmos sharing one cooldown. Aspect of the Moonwell doubles the damage and mana from consuming Scattered Stars, so you swap into it before dumping Lunar Lance, your Scattered-Star spender, buffed further by Lunar Anomaly. Aspect of the Warden makes your autos multi-strike and turns Warden's Blade into a healing-reduction hit. Aspect of the Cosmos adds a slow and seeds extra Scattered Stars. Celestial Aegis is a flat damage-reduction cooldown that sits on top of Moonlit Bulwark and Blanket of Stars.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Chosen of the Moon's flat physical reduction and AoE taunt, Moonlit Bulwark's stun-proof mana shield, Blanket of Stars' shield-gated block wall and Astral Reconstitution's damage-to-mana conversion all stack, with Coalesced Resilience and party-wide Shrouded Stars underneath. You eat focus fire far longer than melee should.
Avoided attacks call down Moonflares for AoE Arcane damage that cannot be dodged, blocks seed Scattered Stars through Nebula, and parries refund Energy while punishing the attacker via Dancing in the Moonlight. Tanking well is how you deal damage.
Parries refund Energy and Celestial Cleave, and Starsweep's star consumption and Astral Reconstitution refund mana per hit. Font of Magic trickles health back as you spend, so you rarely stop swinging.
WEAKNESSES
No direct heal, no battle-rez, no true cleanse; Vial of Moonwell Water strips crowd control, not poison or magic. You live on mitigation cooldowns and Starbound's healing bonus. The only heal buttons are Touch of Moonlight and the Moonwell Tides talent, a sizable but occasional emergency.
No charge, no gap-closer of any kind. Chosen of the Moon's taunt drags the target to you but never moves you, so ranged classes that respect your cooldowns kite you.
The payoffs are proc-gated. Vengeance of Elune needs a real parried attack, Nebula seeds stars off blocks, Star Pupil wants damaging crits. Starslip sheds slows and roots and Celestial Impact stuns, but no hard-CC break exists in the spec, and the only one, Vial of Moonwell Water, costs a class pick.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Two parallel builder/spender pairs, and the spec clicks once you stop treating them separately. AoE is the money loop: Starburst to build, Starsweep to spend, with Celestial Cleave woven in to refund mana per enemy struck. Starburst > Starsweep > Celestial Cleave > Starsweep, repeated. Starsweep also pulls Starburst's cooldown down, so the rhythm feeds itself and the AoE damage is large. Single target runs the same shape with the other pair: Starsunder to build, Lunar Lance to spend. Lunar Lance cuts Starburst's cooldown exactly as Starsweep does. Mixing Starsunder in during AoE is the release valve when Starsweep is still cooling. Every 10th Scattered Star consumed drops a comet through Second Moon, so the spender you press is also your burst.

DUNGEON

Open a pack with Starburst, then hold the Starburst/Starsweep loop and use Celestial Cleave to keep mana funded. Threat and damage both come from the same rotation, so there is no separate threat routine to maintain. Keep Blanket of Stars up as much as the talents allow. It is the core mitigation, and it wants to be running before a big pull lands. Even fully talented, there is roughly an 8-10 second window with no Aegis where the spec feels noticeably squishy. That gap is what you plan around: cover it with Starshatter's line stun, Reverse Magic to bounce a dangerous cast back at its caster, Vial of Moonwell Water to break a stun or root, and trinkets. Enduring Moon means your crits keep a 10% spell-damage-taken debuff rolling on the pack, so the group benefits from you doing your rotation. Line-of-sight pulls around corners to strip ranged damage, as with any tank.
