RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Discipline Priest, mechanically. The core identity is Disc's damage-that-heals loop, not a pure-HoT or pure-direct-heal kit. Gaze of C'Thun is a single cast that either damages an enemy or heals an ally. Malevolence damages one target and heals a cleave of the lowest-HP allies in the same GCD, echoing Atonement/Power Word: Radiance. Layer on a Shadow Priest-style Insanity meter with a capped Total Madness penalty you either manage or talent away (Herald of the Depths), the Abyssal Covenant damage-link, and a melee-weapon-gated damage kit that no retail healer has, with Blade of the Empire and Malevolence requiring a 2H weapon. The result plays like Disc Priest's shield-and-atonement fundamentals wearing a melee-hybrid, Insanity-resource skin.

PLAYSTYLE

A dual-resource loop. Mana funds almost every heal and utility cast (Eldritch Mending, Void Shield, Dark Veil), while Insanity is a separate build-and-spend meter. You fill it with Blade of the Empire (+20), Malevolence (+10) or a Tentacle summon, then spend on Sanity Tap (dump 20 Insanity for 20% of your missing mana, your constant in-combat top-up), Entropic Slam for AoE or Hammer of Twilight for single target. The healing comes out of your damage. Malevolence hits an enemy and applies Black Blood, a heal-over-time, to nearby allies, and it is your main throughput. Around that sit three systems most guides skip. Herald of the Depths is not just the passive that neutralises Total Madness; it teaches an active window that amplifies your output and stretches your heal-over-times and shields while it runs, then drains your Insanity when it ends, so Sanity Tap before you pop it. Void-Touched (Level 30 passive) marks every ally you heal directly, stacking to 10 for 30 seconds, and Gaze of C'Thun consumes every stack at once to heal all marked allies scaled by how many they had. Gaze is a bank you fill with direct heals and cash in — not a spam button. Presences are a stance system where only one can be active: Presence of N'Zoth spawns tentacles off your damage (which Twisted Sanity turns into healing for Black Blood targets), Presence of Y'Shaarj curses the ground for AoE, Presence of C'Thun grants haste procs, and Presence of Yogg-Saron slows enemy casting. Your signature link is Abyssal Covenant. Tether one ally to soak a share of their damage while a slice of your damage converts into healing for them. Dark Veil channels a stacking self-and-ally shield that grants stun and silence immunity while it channels, so standing in the fight and taking hits is part of the healing model, not a liability.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Trading hits is how you heal. Blade of Yogg-Saron's on-hit heal, Shadow Pennant, and As The Prophecy Foretold's crit mana refund carry you through most 1v1s and 1vX without outside help.
Abyssal Covenant soaks an ally's damage and turns your damage into their healing. Dark Veil channels through stuns and silences while stacking a shield on you and an ally. Few healers protect a focus target this well.
Malevolence and Gaze of C'Thun hit an enemy and heal allies off the same cast. You apply pressure instead of standing there supporting, which is what wins a sloppy skirmish.
Whispers of N'zoth, amplified by the Shadow Words talent, is an all-stats party and raid buff in the Blessing of Kings mould, with Whispers of C'thun and Whispers of Y'shaarj as spell power and mana regen alternates. Void Resilience's shadow-resistance aura runs alongside.
WEAKNESSES
You juggle mana and Insanity at once, most spells costing a share of base mana while Insanity builds and spends against the Total Madness cap. Misplay either one and both your damage and your healing stall.
No interrupt at all. Mass Nightmare only interrupts non-player spellcasting and Corrupt Mind is a fear, so a caster who has to be stopped is your group's problem. Devour Magic and Devour Curse are single-target, roughly a second to cast, and expensive.
Dark Veil and Satiate are channels. The instant, guaranteed-crit Eldritch Mendings that answer a spike need Eldritch Eye or a Malevolent Power proc to be up, and with those down a sudden burst window outruns your raw throughput.
Malevolence is a 5-yard spell that fails outright if the target dies or leaves range, and Blade of the Empire needs melee too, so most of your healing happens standing in cleave and stun range. It works while moving, but holding a target in range is its own skill.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Malevolence (usable while moving, +10 Insanity) to apply Black Blood, a heal-over-time on nearby allies, and chip the mob until it closes. Don't re-cast it on a timer to 'keep the heal up'. Black Blood's duration doesn't refresh; you re-cast for the damage and Insanity, not to maintain the HoT. Then weave Blade of the Empire (dump charges, +20 Insanity) in melee. Blade of Yogg-Saron and As The Prophecy Foretold turn those hits into healing and mana refunds. Between pulls, self-target Eldritch Mending or Gaze of C'Thun to top off. Refill mana in combat by dumping built Insanity into Sanity Tap (20 Insanity → 20% of your missing mana, castable on the move). Save the Satiate channel for a bigger top-up when it's safe to eat its increased-damage-taken tax. Spend built Insanity down before it caps into Total Madness. The exception is Herald of the Depths — with that talented, let it ride to 100 for the free self-buff instead of a penalty.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: put Abyssal Covenant on the main tank. It drops when you change instance, so recast it every time, including on a requeue with the same tank. Pick your Presence (N'Zoth for single target, Y'Shaarj for packs). Place a Tentacle: Yogg-Saron's for the party healing-received aura, C'Thun's for damage, though C'Thun's can pull if you are careless. Pre-shield with Forbidden Ritual and spread Void Shield. Consider a Satiate channel to top mana and bank Insanity for Herald of the Depths. On pull: Hand of Yogg-Saron on the tank if it is needed, then Malevolence twice to lay Black Blood (in a raid, make sure the tanks get it), then Sermon of Dread to cut enemy melee attack power for the pack. Sustain priority for group damage runs Herald of the Depths > Black Blood kept up > Blade of the Empire > Malevolence > Gaze of C'Thun > instant Eldritch Mending > Void Shield. If the tank is the one dying, move instant Eldritch Mending and Gaze higher. Cast Malevolence to apply the heal-over-time and build Insanity, not on a refresh timer. Extra applications stack with Dark Chants but do not refresh the duration. Eldritch Mending is a trap to hard-cast. Fire it off Malevolent Power's every-third-melee proc, or off Eldritch Eye, which makes your next several Eldritch Mendings or Malevolences both instant and guaranteed critical strikes. Refresh your Tentacle when it is inside 10 seconds of cooldown during a healing lull. For control, the spec has no true interrupt. Mass Nightmare horrifies a group and interrupts non-player casting, and Corrupt Mind fears up to three. Both are there to take pressure off the tank on a big pull. Isolate drops a target's threat entirely and cancels casts aimed at them. Use it on a DPS who pulled aggro, or on yourself to heal safely from behind line of sight. Never on the tank. Emergency: Dark Infusion, Dark Veil, Abyssal Ward for yourself (50% reduction, usable while stunned), and Embrace the Void as an outright cheat death.
