RETAIL FEEL
Closest to retail Protection Warrior played in an offensive/PvP posture, a DPS-Prot hybrid. Arms Warrior burst identity is layered strongly on top. Shield Slam/Revenge map onto Ram/Pulverize/Broad Sweep, Shield Block maps onto the spec's block-scaling mitigation (Shield Training, Shield Combat, Bulwark Rush), and Charge maps onto Battle Rush. Spear Throw's healing-reduction debuff is Mortal Strike's anti-heal identity transplanted onto a shield-tank frame. Mechanically this is a block-rating melee kit built around reactive mitigation, not passive sustain or HoTs, which is exactly how Protection Warrior plays defense in retail.

PLAYSTYLE

Energy-based generator/spender loop. Broad Sweep and Reprisal apply cheap pressure, with Reprisal a counterattack you spend after a block (Valiance feeds extra Energy back each time it fires). Centurion Strike is your bigger, weapon-dependent hit that extends Raise Shield via Champion, and Ram and Pulverize are your priority swings; Shield Combat turns casting them into a block-rating-scaled attack-power window. The burst comes from banking Glory off Ram, where three stacks fuel a heavily increased Ram, and from the Favor crit engine, which stacks off critical strikes and killing blows toward a large bonus auto attack. Reaching 3 Glory or 20 Favor also makes your next couple of Pulverizes or Broad Sweeps free via Heroism. Defense is proactive rather than restorative. Raise Shield's block window, Bulwark Rush's block-rating spike off Battle Rush, and the Shield Training and Duty passives are the survival layer, while self-healing is thin: Counter Stance heals you when a parry charge fires, Veteran of the Third War restores health on blocks, and the one heal you can press is the class kit's Knight's Calling, a periodic health-and-Energy restore that costs a class pick. It closes gaps with Battle Rush (charge + stun) and locks targets down with Net Throw, a ranged root that also stops the target dodging your attacks, with No Escape cutting Battle Rush's cooldown against a rooted target and your Standards' slow on top. Against roots and snares it does carry an answer. Shrug It Off instantly breaks them and grants brief immunity to slows, and Grand Entrance's leap is immune to crowd control while airborne, though nothing in the kit breaks a stun that has already landed. Play it by sequencing: hoard Glory and Favor into a burst window, spend Reprisal charges cleanly off blocks, keep Spear Throw's healing-reduction debuff up.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Glorious Arena's punishing ring plus a Glory-empowered Ram and the Favor bonus auto swings node fights and clumped teamfights, not only duels.
Net Throw roots at range and Pinned Down! refreshes it, while Spear Throw cuts the target's healing. You can kill someone who still has a healer on them.
Glory into an empowered Ram is the burst, fed by the Favor and Heroism crit-and-free-cast engines.
WEAKNESSES
No lifesteal, and the self-healing is trickles: Veteran of the Third War on block, Counter Stance when a parry charge fires. The only heal you can press is Knight's Calling. Once Raise Shield and Bulwark Rush are spent you are on base armor and block.
Shrug It Off breaks roots and slows and briefly grants immunity to movement-slowing effects, and Unstoppable shortens stuns, roots and slows. Nothing breaks a stun, fear, or sleep, and there is no interrupt or silence anywhere, so a stunlock or fear chain goes largely unanswered.
Battle Rush is your only gap-closer, on a real cooldown. A disciplined kiter locks you out.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Battle Rush (charge + stun) or Net Throw to close or lock a mob, and apply Broad Sweep as a cheap opening builder. Spend Ram and Pulverize on cooldown as your priority damage, banking Glory off Ram toward the increased hit. Use Spear Throw when you need the healing-reduction debuff. It matters even against self-healing mobs. Fill remaining energy with Reprisal (spend charges after a block) and Centurion Strike (bigger hit, weapon-dependent bonus, extends Raise Shield). For groups, open with Broad Sweep (cleave) and Glorious Arena before mopping up with Ram/Reprisal, and lean on Heroism's free Pulverizes/Broad Sweeps once Glory or Favor peaks. Keep Raise Shield up for the block window while questing, since incoming damage is manageable solo. Save Final Verdict to finish an enemy below the execute threshold for the self-heal.

DUNGEON

Pull: Battle Rush into the pack to engage, immediately Glorious Arena to zone the group, then Broad Sweep to cleave threat and damage onto adds. Sustain: weave Ram/Pulverize on cooldown for damage, since each keeps Shield Combat's block-scaled attack-power window rolling. Apply Spear Throw's healing-reduction debuff on the priority target. Cycle Reprisal into Centurion Strike to keep charges refreshed (via Supremacy) and Raise Shield extended (via Champion). Emergency: keep Raise Shield up and lean on Bulwark Rush's block spike off Battle Rush through heavy incoming-damage phases. Use Final Verdict on a low target for the 15% self-heal. There is no interrupt, silence, or CC-break in the kit — caster adds must be handled positionally or by the group.
