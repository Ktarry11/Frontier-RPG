RETAIL FEEL
Closest to retail Shadow Priest. The core identity is the same. A resource literally named Insanity, built by a spam-filler (Horrorbolt ≈ Mind Flay/Mind Blast) and spent on big finishers once you cross a threshold (Wrath of the Black Empire and the empowered Obliteration Beam ≈ Devouring Plague/Void Eruption payoff). A proc that rewards sitting high on the bar (Corrupting Whispers ≈ Voidform's escalating buff). A hard control lift as signature (Psychic Suppression ≈ Psychic Horror). It diverges by spending mana on every cast, a dual-resource system Shadow Priest doesn't have, and by leaning on a cheat-death rather than any Atonement-style healing. Corruption is Shadow Priest damage without the Priest utility, a fragile ranged nuker whose only lifeline is the class tree's Embrace the Void.

PLAYSTYLE

A builder-into-spender Insanity loop. Horrorbolt is the core filler, with a chance via Dark Revelation to reset Gaze of C'Thun's cooldown and generate Insanity, and Horrorbolt Volley transforming it after three casts. Around it sit the Darkwither DoT (which also cuts the target's periodic healing taken), the AoE Obliteration Beam (Glimpse of Madness cuts its cooldown and makes it stamp Madness on everything it hits, a group-wide damage-taken debuff), the nukes Wrath of the Black Empire and Gaze of C'Thun (which feeds Presence of C'Thun for a self-haste burst), and the chaining channel Eldritch Devastation for clustered packs. Nearly every offensive spell feeds Insanity. Darkwither, Obliteration Beam and Gaze all generate it, and Ancient Curse gives a big chunk, so you want to live high on the bar. Corrupting Whispers is a proc that boosts your magic damage, and above 60 Insanity two things come online at once: your Obliteration Beam upgrades, and the Corruption tree's Embrace the Void adds a flat damage buff. This is a hover-near-the-top loop, not a spend-to-zero one, with the class-wide Total Madness backfire waiting at the cap (cleansed by an ally's Restore Sanity). Every spell also costs a mana percentage on top of Insanity, so you tap Sanity Tap to bridge mana troughs. Don't budget for a panic button. The Corruption tree's Embrace the Void is that Insanity-threshold damage buff, not a defensive, and this pure ranged caster's only self-recovery is the slow Psychic Leech health drain: a trickle, not a heal to fall back on.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Obliteration Beam and the chaining Eldritch Devastation swing a clustered BG fight in one or two GCDs once Insanity is banked.
Nearly every spell feeds Insanity, so Corrupting Whispers procs and an empowered Obliteration Beam keep the damage climbing the longer you free-cast.
The class tree's Embrace the Void is a real cheat-death. Take it and you can commit to a burst window, then live through a spike you would otherwise have no answer to.
WEAKNESSES
Horrorbolt, Wrath of the Black Empire and the Psychic Suppression channel are all long and interruptible. Moment of Clarity only trims pushback, and your lone interrupt is the class kit's Mass Nightmare, which hard-interrupts non-player casts only. Any coordinated interrupt chain shuts you off.
Almost no self-healing, and nothing defensive in the spec tree itself. Corruption's own Embrace the Void is an Insanity-threshold damage buff, not a cheat-death, and it shares its name with the class-tree talent that is one. Recovery is the slow Psychic Leech drain plus the Lost in the Void pick, which heals back part of the damage taken. Trickles, not a button.
Every spell costs mana on top of Insanity, so you can go oom mid-fight. Sanity Tap trades Insanity back for mana, which is a bad rate while you are being trained.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open at range with Darkwither to get the DoT rolling and start your Insanity; it also cuts the target's periodic healing taken. Horrorbolt is your filler to build, with free Gaze resets via Dark Revelation. Weave Gaze of C'Thun on cooldown, and Ancient Curse for a burst of Insanity and a damage-taken debuff. Drop Wrath of the Black Empire as your big hit, and use Obliteration Beam once you're above 60 Insanity for its empowered version. Bank toward the top for the Corrupting Whispers proc, and past 60 for Embrace the Void's damage buff. Refill mana with Sanity Tap when you dip. There is no panic button in the spec tree itself. No real self-heal, and Corruption's own Embrace the Void is a damage buff rather than a defensive. The cheat-death of the same name is a class-tree pick you have to spend a point on. Respect pulls and kite rather than expecting a bail-out.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull, buff the group with Visions' party haste and Shadow damage. During the pull, keep Darkwither rolling on the primary target; Unrelenting Void's stacking damage-taken debuff benefits the whole group. Horrorbolt is your filler, and Ancient Curse and Gaze of C'Thun go on cooldown. Obliteration Beam is the pack button: it hits everything nearby, upgrades above 60 Insanity, and with Glimpse of Madness comes off cooldown faster and stamps Madness on every target for a group-wide damage-taken debuff. Save Psychic Suppression's stun for a dangerous caster or a kill window. Bank Insanity toward the top for the Corrupting Whispers proc when it's safe to sit there, but spend down via Wrath of the Black Empire and Obliteration Beam rather than idle-capping into Total Madness, which needs an ally's Restore Sanity to clear. You bring no healing and no panic button. Corruption's own Embrace the Void is a damage buff at 60 Insanity, not a defensive — so play around positioning and Psychic Suppression's stun.
