Closest to Vengeance Demon Hunter. The Soul Fragment to Reaped Soul economy is almost a direct port of Vengeance's Soul Fragments: generated passively off your own attacks and mitigation, then consumed at thresholds into a self-heal and a mitigation buff. Eater of Souls mirrors Fracture/Shear feeding Spirit Bomb and Fiery Brand-esque sustain, and Soul Slip's avoidance-into-fragments echoes the same loop. The party utility lines up too, with Ethereal Guard's raid-wide damage reduction and Spectral Warden's group absorb shield reading like Vengeance's cooldown-based party mitigation. The one piece that isn't pure Vengeance is the support tilt. Ethereal Guard and Soulsight hand out raid-wide damage reduction and crit, leaning Blood-DK-style toughness-and-anchor onto the DH-shaped chassis.

PLAYSTYLE

A 2H-weapon melee tank running on Runic Power generated off your own attacks and defense. Reap and Soul Strike are your builders, Bolstered Form generates Runic Power on activation, and Soul Slip turns your parries and dodges into Soul Fragments. Harvesting a Reaped Soul mints your defensive stacks. Reaching 3 Reaped Souls triggers Eater of Souls (physical mitigation plus a per-second heal of your max HP), while Fatesealer stacks flat damage reduction each time you generate a soul. Rotationally you cycle Reap, whose every 6th cast turns into a Decimate via Decimation, the cone Dreadwake, and the AoE Requiem, which slows enemies' melee and ranged attack speed. Off your banked souls you summon Spectral Scythes and Spectral Warden, with Deathwind laid down as a self-healing zone that Spirit Realm boosts healing taken inside. None of this is burst damage — it's attrition, cleave and sustain that snowballs the longer the fight runs, backed by overlapping damage-reduction layers (Bolstered Form, Soul Knight, Fatesealer, Ethereal Guard) you weave to survive spikes.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Bolstered Form, Soul Knight, Fatesealer, Dark Soldier and Ethereal Guard overlap into mitigation that deepens the longer the fight runs. Bursting you down is not realistic.
Deathwind lays a healing zone through Spirit Realm, Siphon Anima leeches off Spectral Scythe, and Eater of Souls regens. It all scales with time spent in the fight, so you stay and tank rather than disengage.
Ethereal Guard cuts raid-wide damage, Spectral Warden shields the group, Soulsight buffs raid crit. Soul Slip turns your avoidance straight into resource. Rare support from a tank.
WEAKNESSES
Requiem, Deathwind and Dreadwake are all short-range. A kiter kills your damage and sustain at once.
Control is blunt. Requiem's attack-speed slow and the Spectral Scythe and Spectral Warden bodies do most of your peeling, and hard CC stops at Mark of Terror's fear and Soulstone Lure's horrify. Shadow's Embrace cleanses fear, charm and sleep one effect at a time. Your only personal CC-out is Limbo.
No instant burst and no finisher. Everything is builder, DoT and cleave, so you swing fights by attrition and mitigation, and a healer or a good sustain target can refuse to die.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Reap (builder, feeds Soul Fragments; every 6th cast becomes a Decimate via Decimation) -> Soul Strike as your Runic Power spender and health/Strength steal (Essence Binder) -> Dreadwake (cone, Weapon plus Frost damage, generates a Reaped Soul) as your main filler once you're on a 2H weapon -> Requiem (AoE swing, attack-speed slow) for multi-mob pulls -> Deathwind for sustain on a tough single target or when you've overpulled -> Spectral Scythe to cash banked Reaped Souls into extra bodies, or hold them to let Eater of Souls proc at 3 stacks -> Bolstered Form when you need the armor/parry cushion and a Runic Power dump.

DUNGEON

Pull: pop Bolstered Form if you're holding threat, open Dreadwake into the pack, and keep Reap flowing so every 6th becomes a Decimate. Sustain: keep Reap and Soul Strike generating Soul Fragments and Reaped Souls. Fatesealer stacks damage reduction, and Eater of Souls gives physical mitigation plus regen at 3 stacks. Layer Requiem/Deathwind over the pack for AoE damage that self-heals via Spirit Realm's healing zone and Siphon Anima's Spectral Scythe leech. Drop Spectral Warden for the group absorb shield on a hard pull, and keep Ethereal Guard's raid damage reduction up for the whole party. Emergency: Bolstered Form on a damage spike for armor and parry, Soul Knight's magic reduction and increased healing off a lucky parry or dodge, Spectral Warden to shield the group. Hold Reaped Souls to bank the Eater of Souls proc before you dump them into Spectral Scythe.
