PLAYSTYLE

A dual-wield melee spec that builds and spends. Main-hand auto-attacks stack Purity & Wickedness on the target while your strikes build Dawn/Dusk stacks and grant Flames of Sin, which Torchlight and your fire strikes cash in. Dusk Blade is your health siphon, healing for a share of its damage. Pyro Tonic buffs your fire output, and Hunt (charge + slow) plus Burrow Bolt are your gap-closers.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Dusk Blade siphons health, healing for a share of its damage, and Pyro Tonic opens a fire-damage window. You out-trade a prolonged fight on your own resources.
Purity & Wickedness plus the Dawn and Dusk stacks build into big Torchlight, Purifier's Edge and Flourish payoffs through Flames of Sin. Uninterrupted uptime is what it pays for.
Hunt charges and slows on two charges and Burrow Bolt hooks and pulls, so you close and stay on a kiter. Darkslayer's Lantern is an AoE stun for locking down a pack.
WEAKNESSES
In-spec control is all offensive, Darkslayer's Lantern and Trap spells, and Witching Idol only dispels fear, sleep and charm on a slow periodic tick. Your one true out is Witching Shroud, and it opens only briefly after a magical critical strike hits you. Otherwise stuns, silences and roots stick.
Smite Evil, Fiery Judgement, Purifier's Edge, Flourish and Dusk Blade all require one-handed melee weapons in range, and Hunt and Burrow Bolt, your only answers to a kiter, sit on real cooldowns rather than being spammable.
The payoff is ramping stacks and grinding a fight out with Dusk Blade's siphon, not ending it fast. You lose straight damage races to dedicated burst specs even while outliving them.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Hunt (charge + slow) or Burrow Bolt to close. Build Purity & Wickedness with main-hand auto-attacks and Dawn/Dusk stacks with your strikes, then spend through Torchlight, Purifier's Edge, and Flourish while Flames of Sin fuels your auto-attacks. Weave Dusk Blade for its heal, keep Pyro Tonic up, and use Darkslayer's Lantern to stun a pack.

DUNGEON

Run the Purity & Wickedness build-and-spend loop with Flames of Sin feeding your auto-attacks. Keep Dusk Blade in the rotation for its self-heal. Darkslayer's Lantern (AoE stun) and Trap spells cover control. Hunt's slow and Burrow Bolt's pull handle stragglers, and Monster Hunting adds a party-wide haste aura.
