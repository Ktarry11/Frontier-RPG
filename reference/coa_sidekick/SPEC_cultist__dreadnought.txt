RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Blood Death Knight. Both are self-sufficient melee bruisers with a swing-and-block-generated secondary resource, Insanity here and Runic Power there, plus scaling absorbs and mitigation. Both bake self-healing into their own offensive kit instead of relying on an external healer: Twilight Shieldtoss's damage-to-health leech via General of Y'Shaarj, plus Eldritch Bastion's emergency mitigation ≈ Death Strike / Vampiric Blood. Its Strength-into-spell-damage scaling (Strength of the Sha) plus the Strength of the Black Empire void-monstrosity damage-reduction transform mirror Blood's identity. This is a shield tank that holds threat and refuses to die, not a pure caster.

PLAYSTYLE

Dreadnought is a sword-and-board (1H + shield) shadow-tank that weaves instant shadow abilities into melee swings. You build Insanity mainly off blocking (Inner Darkness) and your strikes, and spend it with Void Strikes. Twilight Shieldtoss hurls a bouncing cursed shield for Shadow damage that grows with the Insanity you're holding and heals you for a share of its damage through General of Y'Shaarj. Your shield is the engine. Blocking generates Insanity and feeds critical-block and crit procs (Shadowy Symbiosis, Entropic Retaliation), and above 60-80 Insanity your Void-Enhanced Shield absorb and block value scale up. Void-Enhanced Shield is your absorb layer, Bulwark of Shadow your defensive cooldown, and Eldritch Bastion the last-ditch mitigation against incoming burst. In a fight you charge in with Dreadfall, keep Void Strikes and Twilight Shieldtoss cycling, and lean on Deep Secrets (each point of Insanity cuts the mana cost of your instant abilities) to keep from going dry. At high level the Dreadnought line sheds your mortal form into a void monstrosity that reduces the damage you take.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Void-Enhanced Shield's absorb, plus block value and critical block that climb with Insanity, make you very hard to burst. The void-monstrosity transform cuts damage taken further.
Armageddon leech-heals and Embrace the Void holds off death, then pays back part of the damage taken when it ends. Deep Secrets discounts mana off Insanity. You outlast long skirmishes with no healer.
Crushing Dissonance is an AoE interrupt that locks out the school it hits, and Dreadfall's shield charge closes on kiters. Rare shutdown for a bruiser.
WEAKNESSES
No stun-break, no trinket-style CC escape, no self-cleanse. Abyssal Ward goes up while stunned to blunt the damage, but the CC itself sticks, so a timed stun into a physical train chunks you before absorbs matter.
Mitigation leans on absorbs and block that need Insanity uptime to be worth full value. Caught low on Insanity, Void-Enhanced Shield scaling and the Shieldtoss self-heal fall off at the same time.
Everything costs a share of base mana with no rage or energy pool to fall back on. Deep Secrets' Insanity discount is the only offset, so long fights leave you dry exactly when sustain matters.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open on approach with Dreadfall to charge in, deal Shadow damage to everything you pass through, and grab threat. Build Insanity by getting into melee and blocking (Inner Darkness), keeping Twilight Shieldtoss cycling as your bounce filler. It hits harder the more Insanity you're holding, and heals you via General of Y'Shaarj. Spend Insanity with Void Strikes once you have it. Void-Enhanced Shield's absorb and Bulwark of Shadow are your defensive layers. Eldritch Bastion is the panic emergency cooldown if a pull goes wrong. Between pulls you top off from Twilight Shieldtoss's leech rather than sitting to drink, and Deep Secrets keeps your instant-ability mana costs down as your Insanity climbs.

DUNGEON

Pull: Dreadfall into the pack to charge through it for threat and Shadow damage, then keep aggro sticky with Void Strikes and Twilight Shieldtoss (Eldritch Strength makes them generate heavy threat). Sustain: keep Twilight Shieldtoss on cooldown as your heal-while-you-fight, since its leech carries your bar via General of Y'Shaarj. Against magic-heavy packs, lean on Void-Enhanced Shield and Bulwark of Shadow. Control: Crushing Dissonance to interrupt-and-lockout a dangerous caster pack. Support: Voidwarding for the raid's resist and damage-reduction aura. Emergency: Eldritch Bastion the instant you're about to be spiked out, layered under your absorbs.
