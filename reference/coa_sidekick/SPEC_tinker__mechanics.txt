PLAYSTYLE

Mechanics is a Scrap-fueled ranged/pet hybrid built around the Mechsuit. You build Scrap by casting Scrap Shot and Sticky Bomb and by landing crits through Cogmaster, while Makeshift Dynamite is your charge-based hard hitter that generates 10 Scrap a cast and marks targets for Arclight Core, which your pet consumes for bonus damage. Your permanent Scrapmaw, plus proc'd Clockwork Guardians, fights alongside you and is amplified by Junker, Overclocked Machine and the Master Technician turret stacks. Periodically you dump Scrap into Build: Mechsuit, a self-transformation that drains Scrap over time, boosts your damage via Augmechtation and armor via Defensive Utility, and unlocks a new ability bar: Mechsuit: Combustion (a moving fireball), Mechsuit: Laser Beam (a weapon-damage laser that charges your pet in), and Mechsuit: Activate Jets (a launch-and-slam mobility/AoE tool). Inside the suit you also gain damage reduction and self-sustain, with Blazewrought Titan cutting all damage taken and Nano-Repair Tech regenerating health for you and your pet. Overload procs make Combustion and Laser Beam strike extra times, and Sprocket Loaded lets Makeshift Dynamite reset your Mechsuit abilities. The whole kit juggles mana (percentage costs on nearly everything), Scrap, and Sentry Turret uptime.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Inside the Mechsuit, Blazewrought Titan reduces all damage taken, Defensive Utility adds armor, Mechanical Enhancements cut bleeds and Nano-Repair Tech regenerates health. Focus fire does not get through that.
Scrapmaw, proc'd Clockwork Guardians and an upgradeable Sentry Turret keep dealing damage while you kite, reposition, or sit locked down. Deploy the board early and it swings a skirmish for you.
Combustion and Laser Beam spike inside the Mechsuit, amplified by Overload multi-strikes and reset by Makeshift Dynamite through Sprocket Loaded. That sits on top of your pet's damage.
WEAKNESSES
Vanguard X-42: Fuselight Furnace only works inside the Mechsuit. Out of the suit you are on class-kit picks like Med Pack or Nanobot Reconstruction for slow drip-heals. No battle-rez either, and Auto Resuscitation Device only pre-empts a death if it was attached in advance.
Mana and Scrap both have to be tracked, and running Scrap to zero mid-transformation ejects you from the Mechsuit. Activate Jets needs the suit already up. The class kit's Rocket Boots is the one disengage that still works ejected, and it costs an Essence pick.
Scrapmaw, the Sentry Turret and your Scrap bank all have to be built before your power comes online. Get jumped or interrupted before setup and you play from baseline for a while.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Pre-pull: Build: Scrapmaw so your pet is out, and Build: Sentry Turret near the pull. Open at range with Scrap Shot to build Scrap and, via Makeshift Dynamite, apply Arclight Core's mark so your pet consumes it for burst. Weave Sticky Bomb on cooldown for more Scrap and its explosion. Spend Makeshift Dynamite charges as your hard hitter; each resets your Mechsuit abilities via Sprocket Loaded once you're suited. When you have Scrap banked, enter Build: Mechsuit and cycle Mechsuit: Combustion and Mechsuit: Laser Beam, chasing Overload extra-hit procs, while Nano-Repair Tech regenerates your health and Blazewrought Titan cuts incoming damage. That is also your only real heal window, so duck into the suit to top off. Use Mechsuit: Activate Jets to reposition or slam a clustered pull.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: drop your Scrapmaw and a Sentry Turret, and pre-stack turret buffs (Master Technician). On the pull, build Scrap with Scrap Shot and Makeshift Dynamite, then enter the Mechsuit. Use Mechsuit: Combustion for AoE damage on adds threatening the tank or healer; Junkyard Loiter drops Lesser Sticky Bombs on everything Combustion/Laser Beam hits. Overclock your pet (Overclocked Machine) during the pull's damage burn to spike its output. If you're eating stray damage, lean on the suit's Blazewrought Titan damage reduction and Nano-Repair Tech regen as your emergency defensive. There is no battle-rez in this kit — a dead ally waits for a run-back.
