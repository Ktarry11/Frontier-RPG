PLAYSTYLE

A mobile crossbow skirmisher. Build Bounty Hunter stacks with Darkslayer and Witchbane, then spend them on a charged Heartseeking Bolt for burst. Keep Tormentor's DoT and Witchbane rolling, and use Twilight Frenzy as a moving AoE spin. Survival is avoidance, not absorb-tanking: Daring Escape (jump back, ranged attacks miss you), Vault (a dash you can reset off Daring Escape), and Pavise (a deployed shield). Kingdom Hearts gives a conditional execute self-heal below 20% health.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Daring Escape jumps back and makes ranged attacks miss you, with a knockback rider on your next Bolt. Vault dashes and Escape Artist resets it off Daring Escape, and Pavise deploys a shield. Few specs reset a fight this well.
Bounty Hunter stacks feed a charged Heartseeking Bolt that reduces the target's healing received, and Kingdom Hearts turns that bolt into a guaranteed-crit, self-healing execute under 20% health.
Twilight Frenzy gives movement speed through Crossbow Frenzy and damage reduction through Evasive while it spins. With the escape layer and the Kingdom Hearts execute heal you survive a 1v1 or 1vX without a healer.
WEAKNESSES
Damage keys off building Bounty Hunter first. Interrupt the Darkslayer and Witchbane feed, or let the stacks fall off, and your Heartseeking Bolt window stalls.
Witchbane, Tormentor, Heartseeking Bolt and Twilight Frenzy all want a ranged crossbow equipped, and Pavise additionally needs a shield trained through Pavise Crossbowman. Throughput hinges on the loadout.
Sustain is thin and conditional. Kingdom Hearts' sub-20% execute is a mana refund, not a true heal, and real healing costs a class pick on Vampiric Tonic's lifesteal. Stuns and silences come from class-kit traps an enemy has to walk into. Cooldowns down, you cannot top off or pin anyone.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open with Tormentor to lay its DoT, keep Witchbane channeling, and build Bounty Hunter stacks to dump into a charged Heartseeking Bolt (guaranteed to crit and to heal you once the target is below 20% via Kingdom Hearts). Use Twilight Frenzy for packs; it also grants damage reduction and movement speed while spinning. Daring Escape and Vault reset you when something closes.

DUNGEON

Survival is avoidance-based: Pavise, Daring Escape, Vault, and Evasive's damage reduction during Twilight Frenzy. Your only heal is Kingdom Hearts' sub-20% execute restore. Prioritize Tormentor spread via crits, keep Witchbane and Heartseeking Bolt up for single-target, and pop Slayer's raid crit buff for the group.
