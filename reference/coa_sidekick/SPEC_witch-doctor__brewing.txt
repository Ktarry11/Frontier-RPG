RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Preservation Evoker, mechanically rather than by vibe. Spirit in a Bottle heals an ally while chipping and debuffing nearby enemies off the same cast, functionally the same trick as Living Flame's dual heal/damage identity. Mojo Beam's channel-then-weave-instant-heals-through-it is the same shape as Dream Breath/Spiritbloom empowerment weaving, and the Cauldron's rolling Jungle Shrooms heal-over-time plus Master Mixologist's amplify window play like a cheaper, more sustained Reversion/Emerald Blossom setup. Its group mitigation is distributed across the Cauldron Bases and Ingredients, with Beast Blood converting physical hits into damage-over-time and Crystal Water and Frog Bones shielding allies. That parallels Preservation's spread-the-mitigation approach rather than one big raid button. Where it diverges from Evoker is hard single-target CC, of which it has none of its own. Its group protection is the placed Voodoo Cauldron and the Spice: Peacebloom CC-duration cut rather than a Hex-style lockdown.

PLAYSTYLE

The engine is a heal-throw loop built around Loa's Brew, a 2-sec-cast main heal scaling off Spell Power and Spirit, filled in with the instant, short-cooldown Potion Toss. Splash On 'Em chips that cooldown down further every time you land a heal with Loa's Brew or Mojo Beam. Spirit in a Bottle is the signature button: a heal that also, via Touch of the Spirits, chips at nearby enemies and softens their damage, so healing and pressure share a cast. Mojo Beam is the channel-weave tool. While channeling it you can instantly cast Potion Toss, Splash Potion and Spirit in a Bottle, so a good Brewing WD plants a channel and fires off-heals through it rather than standing still doing nothing else. Around that core sits the Cauldron. You brew Ingredients (Jungle Shrooms for a rolling group heal-over-time, Bloodthistle for increased healing, Frog Bones for reduced damage taken) and Spices (Earthroot to amplify all Cauldron effects, Peacebloom to shorten roots, stuns and snares on the group), and you can swap a Base into it: Beast Blood converts a slice of allies' incoming physical damage into damage-over-time, Crystal Water shields allies for a portion of the healing they take. Voodoo Cauldron is your placed idol, a heals-received-up zone with a heal-over-time pulse, and Master Mixologist is the big amplify window, boosting every Cauldron effect and letting you cast Spirit in a Bottle while moving. Dispel duty is single-curse removal through Hexbreak. Your anti-burst layer is Voodoo Cauldron and the Cauldron and Base ingredients that reduce or convert incoming damage, not a single immunity button.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Base: Beast Blood turns allies' physical hits into damage-over-time, Base: Crystal Water and Ingredient: Frog Bones shield and soften what comes in, and Spice: Peacebloom shortens roots, stuns and snares. The group soaks pressure without one all-or-nothing immunity button.
A rolling Jungle Shrooms heal-over-time and Voodoo Cauldron's heals-received zone and heal pulse sit on top of the Loa's Brew, Potion Toss and Splash Potion loop. Master Mixologist amplifies all of it on demand.
Spirit in a Bottle turns a heal into chip damage and a weaken through Touch of the Spirits, so you pressure while you protect. Hexbreak strips a dangerous curse.
WEAKNESSES
Your escapes drop threat, they do not move you: Mirage's invisibility and decoy, Death Draught's feign death, Slither's root-break in a serpent form that cannot attack or cast. No combat blink, no leap, no hard single-target CC. A train that AoEs through the vanish runs you down.
Every dispel costs a real cast. Hexbreak strips curses, Allcure Elixir clears poison, disease and curse effects off one ally (For Da Loa! upgrades it into the group-wide Mass Allcure Elixir), and Cleansing Idol pulses around its drop point. Against DoT-heavy comps that is globals not spent healing.
Voodoo Cauldron is a placed ground zone that people walk out of or focus down, and the whole Cauldron and Base setup takes brewing time up front. Fast-moving BG fights punish that.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Brewing has no real damage nuke of its own — its solo game leans on the heal/damage hybrid rather than a DPS rotation. Keep your Cauldron brewed with Jungle Shrooms (rolling heal-over-time) and a Spice, then pressure through Spirit in a Bottle, whose heal also chips and weakens nearby enemies via Touch of the Spirits. Keep Voodoo Cauldron down for its heal-over-time pulse and healing-received zone so you can keep casting. Swap a mitigation Base (Beast Blood or Crystal Water) or brew Frog Bones in if a pull hits hard. Master Mixologist is your amplify button if a pull goes wrong. Between pulls, top off with Loa's Brew or Potion Toss rather than sitting to drink. Potion Toss's short cooldown (chipped further by Splash On 'Em) and Spirit in a Bottle's heal both keep out-of-mana downtime rare while questing. Expect kills to be slow: this is a healer chassis, so solo content is about surviving and out-lasting mobs, not bursting them down.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: brew your Cauldron (Jungle Shrooms for the rolling group heal-over-time plus a Spice) and drop Voodoo Cauldron, a heals-received zone with a heal pulse, on the tank's stack point. Sustain phase: Loa's Brew as the primary heal filler, backed by the instant Potion Toss whenever its cooldown is up. Watch for the free-cast window from Mojo Beam channel-weaving, and note Splash On 'Em shaving Potion Toss/Splash Potion cooldown off every effective Loa's Brew/Mojo Beam heal. Use Splash Potion for stacked-group healing, and Spirit in a Bottle on tank or cleave so the heal doubles as chip-and-weaken on whatever's stacked on the tank. Hexbreak on cooldown for a dangerous curse. Swap in a mitigation Base (Beast Blood / Crystal Water) or brew Frog Bones ahead of a heavy physical or magic pull. Hold Master Mixologist as the group heal-burst and wipe-prevention amplify window for a burst mechanic the group needs to survive.
