RETAIL FEEL
Closest to Affliction Warlock. Its kit is pure DoT-stacking poison and disease (Wilt, Spore, Decay, Venom Bolt, Serpent's Fang with the Fungal Growth ramp), structurally identical to Affliction's Agony/Corruption/UA ramp-and-refresh game. That extends right down to a party spell-haste aura it provides in groups (Fungal Link) and mushroom detonations layered on for burst (Decay, Spore, Suffocating Coil). Like Affliction, it uses shapeshifting (Weaver Form) mainly for form identity and a thin anti-CC shift-break rather than for defense, and its damage is a ramping DoT machine rather than upfront burst. Where a Warlock's soul-tap sustain is self-only, this does carry real ally healing. Serpent's Fang splashes heals onto allies near its target, Mycelial Ring heals allies standing in it when it expires, and the class kit adds Green Salve and Shadra's Balm. That's modest off-healing rather than a healer's engine, but not all of its power points outward at the enemy.

PLAYSTYLE

DoT-stacking poison and disease from range. Apply Wilt, Spore and Decay, then cast Venom Bolt and Serpent's Fang while Fungal Growth stacks periodic-damage amplification on the target. Mycosis is a big direct Nature hit plus a healing-absorb shield on the enemy. This is a pure ranged damage spec. Its support is a group spell-haste aura, Fungal Link, that you provide by being in the party, not any ally healing. Proc chains reward keeping DoTs rolling on cooldown: Rot Lich can reset and grant an instant Serpent's Fang, Cycle of Decay procs off casting, Infestation shaves cooldowns off Serpent's Fang and Fungal Assailant, and mushrooms (Blightfang, Decay, Spore, Fungal Assailant via Sporelord) feed extra Decay cooldown reduction through Prophetic Speaker and From Death Comes Life. You can run up to 2 active Venoms, Debilitating Venom's slow and Blight Venom's DoT, to color your casts, and Serpent's Fang stacks its own damage the more you chain it via A Pit of Snakes. Weaver Form is the spec's form, and shifting frees roots and snares via the class's shift-break. That shift is your only real anti-CC.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Wilt, Spore, Decay, Venom Bolt and Serpent's Fang ramp under Fungal Growth amplification, with Blightfang and Sporelord mushroom detonations behind them. Strong sustained single-target and AoE from range.
Rot Lich resets Serpent's Fang and makes it instant, Cycle of Decay and Infestation cut cooldowns, and A Pit of Snakes stacks Serpent's Fang's own damage the more you chain it.
Sepsis Bloom disorients and purges your poisons for a burst, Suffocating Coil webs a target, and Mycosis's healing-absorb shield turns an enemy's healing off while you ramp them down.
WEAKNESSES
The damage is a DoT ramp, not burst. A target that survives the ramp window pressures you first.
Nearly every spell costs a percentage of base mana, and Serpent Lord's Ring plus Shadra's Gift's Int-into-haste is the only real relief. A long 1vX or extended teamfight runs you dry.
The Weaver Form shift-break clears roots and snares and nothing else: no stuns, no silences, no fears. A focused melee train grinds you down through that gap, because DoT damage will not out-race a hard commit.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Open at range: apply Wilt and Spore, then Venom Bolt and Serpent's Fang to build Fungal Growth and start the ramp. Cast Mycosis for a big direct hit plus the healing-absorb shield. Layer Decay for the AoE mushroom burst, and on packs Suffocating Coil to spread pressure and Wilting Away to strike an extra enemy. Keep up to 2 Venoms active: Debilitating Venom's slow for kiting, Blight Venom for extra DoT. Use Debilitating Venom's slow and Decay/Mycelial Ring zones as kite tools. Lean on Serpent's Fang chains for their stacking damage (A Pit of Snakes) and the Rot Lich reset. Pop Serpent Lord's Ring when you need mana back and free Mycosis casts, and keep Fungal Assailant on cooldown to summon a Fungarian and apply Debilitating Venom.

DUNGEON

Pre-pull: nothing spec-unique to hand out beyond your party aura, Fungal Link's spell haste, which you give just by being in the group. Damage: keep Wilt/Spore/Venom Bolt/Serpent's Fang rolling with Fungal Growth stacks. Use Mycosis for the healing-absorb debuff on a healing enemy, and Decay/Suffocating Coil/Wilting Away for AoE, plus mushroom detonations (Blightfang, Decay, Fungal Assailant via Sporelord) for burst. Cooldowns: Fungal Assailant to summon a Fungarian (Fungal Allies/Sporelord scale it up), Serpent Lord's Ring for mana and free Mycosis during a heavy phase. Utility: Sepsis Bloom to disorient a dangerous add, which also purges your poisons for burst, and the Weaver Form shift to clear a root or snare and keep moving. Note this spec has no battle-rez, poison cure, or group cleanse of its own. Those belong to the Vizier (healer) spec.
