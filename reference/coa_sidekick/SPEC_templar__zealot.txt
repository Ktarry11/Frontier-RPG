RETAIL FEEL
Closest to a Retribution Paladin's Holy Power loop, where builders bank Oaths and a finisher spends them. The differences are dual-wield and DoT-forward (Condemn plus Blade of Faith) with a Zealotry proc engine off the off-hand rather than one clean nuke finisher. It can optionally bolt on a Discipline-Priest Atonement overlay, since Chakra of Light makes its Oath Breakers heal allies. At the far end that plays like a Ret Paladin who can spec its finishers into Word of Glory. Its default is straight melee damage.

PLAYSTYLE

Zealot runs a builder into Oath into Oath Breaker loop. Righteous Lunge and Vindication are your Oath builders, each granting an Oath, and Vindication triggers Zealotry; your off-hand auto attacks also trigger Zealotry via the Zealotry passive. With Oaths banked you dump an Oath Breaker: Chastise, Blade of Faith, Divine Fury or Righteous Upheaval. Take Righteous Upheaval when you've triggered enough Zealotry to max its stacks, otherwise Blade of Faith, a long, hard-critting DoT. You chain the two ~20s finishers rather than spamming one. Keep Condemn and Blade of Faith rolling, and spread Condemn across a pack with Holy Cleave; there's no ranged spread, you cleave it out. Iron Penance stacks a physical-damage-taken debuff as Zealotry fires. Every ~10 Zealotries your Chastise becomes Divine Fury, a cleave holy hit that shreds armor, and Force of Golganneth is a two-minute burst that explodes for holy damage and triggers Zealotry on everything nearby. Cycle your Librams (Consecration into Fervor into Zeal) for a rolling steroid, and Chakra of Power refunds Energy on melee hits so the loop rarely starves. If you'd rather support, Chakra of Light turns those same Oath Breakers into ally heals. Identical rotation, pointed at healing.

STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
STRENGTHS
Condemn carries a huge share of your damage per press and Blade of Faith adds a hard-critting 21s DoT. Off-hand swings fish Zealotry procs all fight, bringing holy damage plus Iron Penance's stacking physical-damage-taken debuff.
Force of Golganneth is a two-minute cooldown that explodes for holy damage and triggers Zealotry on every nearby enemy for several seconds, feeding Divine Fury procs and Iron Penance across a whole pack.
Chakra of Light turns your Oath Breakers into ally heals. Same rotation, pure DPS or damage-into-healing bruiser, nothing to relearn.
WEAKNESSES
Every tool closes distance, Norgannon's Wrath and Force of Golganneth included, and none of them gets you out. A kiting comp that keeps you off shuts down the Zealotry engine and the whole Oath economy at once.
The builder into Oath into Oath Breaker chain is fragile under pressure. A miss or a CC strands you with no finisher up and drops your Condemn and Blade of Faith uptime, which is where most of your damage lives.
Tuning inside the kit is uneven. Divine Fury scales poorly in single target and you press it for the free global and energy, not the damage, and Righteous Upheaval needs its Zealotry stacks built first. A chopped-up chain loses a lot of the payoff.

ROTATIONS
SOLO / QUESTING

Stack Condemn early. It's your primary DoT and huge damage per press, so keep it up. Build Oaths with Righteous Lunge and Vindication; Vindication and your off-hand swings both trigger Zealotry. Spend into a finisher: Righteous Upheaval when you've triggered enough Zealotry to max its stacks, otherwise Blade of Faith. Chain the two ~20s finishers rather than spamming one. Cycle your Librams on cooldown (Consecration -> Fervor -> Zeal), and pop Force of Golganneth on a tough target or pack for a big Zealotry-fuelled burst. Chakra of Power refunds Energy on melee hits so you rarely starve. There's no self-heal to lean on — the Chakra of Light heal is a support-spec choice. Use cooldowns and positioning rather than expecting to out-sustain a bad pull.

DUNGEON

Cycle a Libram from the start: Consecration -> Fervor -> Zeal on a rolling ~15s timer for a near-permanent damage buff. On a pack, Holy Cleave up to the target cap, then spread Condemn across everything by cleaving; Zealot has no ranged Condemn spread. Keep Condemn and Blade of Faith rolling on the priority target and spend finishers (Righteous Upheaval at max Zealotry stacks, else Blade of Faith). Force of Golganneth is your best AoE button. Its repeated explosions trigger Zealotry on every nearby enemy, feeding Divine Fury procs and Iron Penance stacks across the pack. If you've specced Chakra of Light for support, aim your Oath Breakers at the group's center so the heal reaches the most allies.
